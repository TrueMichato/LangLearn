## Verdict

The remediation "fixed the front door and made the core loop genuinely kind." Onboarding now ends by handing off into real content, the zero-data dashboard is a path instead of a scoreboard, and the design system is back in force.

But the app **still stops sequencing at the exact two handoffs a beginner leans on**: immediately *after the first activity*, and *after the first session*.

## P0 — found this round

**1. The recommendation led to a reference wall.** The on-ramp told ja/ru/ar learners to start with Letters, then delivered them to the character *chart* — 46 unfamiliar glyphs in a grid. That is the reference you consult after you know the script, not the way in. The guided column-by-column flow existed and sat behind a tab the newcomer had no reason to press.

**2. Day-2 amnesia.** Finishing a letter column writes `characterProgress` but no words, and the dashboard's single `hasData` gate counted only words and study time. A learner who did *exactly* what the on-ramp asked was greeted the next morning by the same zero state — the app forgot them for following its own advice.

## P1 — found this round

**3. The review session was a cul-de-sac.** "Session Complete!" with no exit, while the header timer kept counting.

**4. The first review was gated behind a settings question.** Four practice modes, no basis to choose, before a single card.

**5. Contrast defects the earlier pass missed.** The Phase-3 audit walked neither ancestor backgrounds nor SVG `fill`. A stricter pass found the Analytics back arrow with no `dark:` variant at all (black on slate-900, 1.18:1), selected segmented-control labels at 3.31:1 across 10 sites, chart axis labels at 9–10px, the onboarding overlay rooted on `.app-canvas` (3.86:1), and white-on-yellow at 1.91:1.
