# Spelling the Hamza

**هَمْزَةٌ** (hamzatun) — hamza is a real consonant, the glottal stop. Arabic may write it on a seat: **أ** (ʾalifun) — alif seat, **إ** (ʾalifun taḥtahā hamzatun) — alif with hamza below, **ؤ** (wāwun maʿa hamzatin) — wāw seat, **ئ** (yāʾun maʿa hamzatin) — tooth or yāʾ seat, or **ء** (hamzatun ʿalā s-saṭri) — hamza on the line.

> The sound is one; the spelling depends on position and neighboring vowels.

## Core idea

At the beginning of a word, hamza normally sits on alif: **أَكَلَ** (ʾakala) — he ate, **أُمٌّ** (ʾummun) — mother, **إِيمَانٌ** (ʾīmānun) — faith. Inside a word, the strongest nearby vowel usually chooses the seat: **كَسْرَةٌ** (kasratun) — i is strongest, then **ضَمَّةٌ** (ḍammatun) — u, then **فَتْحَةٌ** (fatḥatun) — a.

## Seats in practice

| Situation | Common spelling | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Initial fatḥa or ḍamma | **أَ / أُ** | **أَخٌ** (ʾakhun) — brother; **أُسْتَاذٌ** (ʾustādhun) — teacher |
| Initial kasra | **إِ** | **إِسْمٌ** (ʾismun) — name; **إِذَا** (ʾidhā) — if / when |
| Kasra nearby | **ئ** | **بِيئَةٌ** (bīʾatun) — environment; **سُئِلَ** (suʾila) — he was asked |
| Ḍamma nearby | **ؤ** | **سُؤَالٌ** (suʾālun) — question; **مُؤْمِنٌ** (muʾminun) — believer |
| Fatḥa nearby | **أ** | **سَأَلَ** (saʾala) — he asked; **رَأْسٌ** (raʾsun) — head |
| Long vowel before final hamza | **ء** | **سَمَاءٌ** (samāʾun) — sky; **شَيْءٌ** (shayʾun) — thing |

## Usage notes

Do not guess from pronunciation alone. **سُؤَالٌ** (suʾālun) — question and **سَأَلَ** (saʾala) — he asked both contain hamza, but the seat differs because the vowels around it differ. Final hamza is especially variable: **شَيْءٌ** (shayʾun) — thing writes hamza on the line after a long-like glide, while **مَبْدَأٌ** (mabdaʾun) — principle writes it on alif after fatḥa.

## Practice patterns

Read the vowel before and after the hamza, then decide:

- **سَأَلَ** (saʾala) — he asked: fatḥa favors **أ**.
- **سُئِلَ** (suʾila) — he was asked: kasra favors **ئ**.
- **مُؤْمِنٌ** (muʾminun) — believer: ḍamma favors **ؤ**.
- **قِرَاءَةٌ** (qirāʾatun) — reading: long ā before hamza leaves it on the line.
