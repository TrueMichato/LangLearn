# Expressing Ability, Permission & Obligation (yastaṭīʿu, yajibu, yumkinu, lā budda)

Arabic often expresses ability, permission, and obligation with a main verb or fixed expression followed by أَنْ (an) — to / that and a subjunctive verb. The idea is familiar, but the case and mood endings matter in fully vocalized MSA.

## Core idea

اِسْتَطَاعَ (istaṭāʿa) — to be able has the imperfect يَسْتَطِيعُ (yastaṭīʿu) — he is able / can. It is commonly followed by أَنْ (an) and a subjunctive verb.

- أَسْتَطِيعُ أَنْ أَقْرَأَ الْجَرِيدَةَ (astaṭīʿu an aqraʾa al-jarīdata) — I can read the newspaper.
- لَا يَسْتَطِيعُ الطِّفْلُ أَنْ يَفْتَحَ الْبَابَ (lā yastaṭīʿu aṭ-ṭiflu an yaftaḥa al-bāba) — the child cannot open the door.
- اِسْتَطَاعَتْ هِنْدٌ أَنْ تَحْفَظَ الْقَصِيدَةَ (istaṭāʿat Hindun an taḥfaẓa al-qaṣīdata) — Hind was able to memorize the poem.

## Possibility and permission

يُمْكِنُ (yumkinu) — it is possible can be impersonal or can take an attached pronoun for the person involved. It often translates as can, may, or it is possible for.

| Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| يُمْكِنُ أَنْ نَبْدَأَ الآنَ (yumkinu an nabdaʾa al-āna) — it is possible to begin now | general possibility |
| يُمْكِنُكَ أَنْ تَدْخُلَ (yumkinuka an tadkhula) — you may enter / you can enter | permission or ability |
| هَلْ يُمْكِنُنِي أَنْ أَسْأَلَ سُؤَالًا؟ (hal yumkinunī an asʾala suʾālan?) — may I ask a question? | polite request |

## Obligation

يَجِبُ (yajibu) — it is necessary / must commonly uses عَلَى (ʿalā) with the person who has the obligation. لَا بُدَّ (lā budda) — there is no escape / it is necessary often takes مِنْ (min) plus a verbal noun or أَنْ (an) plus a subjunctive verb.

- يَجِبُ عَلَى الطَّالِبِ أَنْ يَدْرُسَ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ (yajibu ʿalā aṭ-ṭālibi an yadrusa kulla yawmin) — the student must study every day.
- يَجِبُ عَلَيْنَا أَنْ نَحْتَرِمَ الْوَقْتَ (yajibu ʿalaynā an naḥtarima al-waqta) — we must respect time.
- لَا بُدَّ مِنْ مُرَاجَعَةِ الدَّرْسِ (lā budda min murājaʿati ad-darsi) — the lesson must be reviewed.

## Practice patterns

Choose the expression by nuance: يَسْتَطِيعُ (yastaṭīʿu) for personal ability, يُمْكِنُ (yumkinu) for possibility or permission, يَجِبُ (yajibu) for obligation, and لَا بُدَّ (lā budda) for necessity.

- هَلْ تَسْتَطِيعِينَ أَنْ تُسَاعِدِينِي؟ (hal tastaṭīʿīna an tusāʿidīnī?) — can you help me?
- يُمْكِنُ لِلْمُسَافِرِ أَنْ يَنْتَظِرَ هُنَا (yumkinu lil-musāfiri an yantaẓira hunā) — the traveler may wait here.
- لَا بُدَّ أَنْ نَنْتَبِهَ (lā budda an nantabiha) — we must pay attention.
