# kāna & inna Sisters in Depth

The sisters of **كَانَ** (kāna) — was and **إِنَّ** (inna) — indeed/that reshape nominal sentences by changing case and nuance.

> kāna changes the predicate to accusative. inna changes the subject to accusative. Both keep the nominal sentence at the center.

## kāna and her sisters

A **كَانَ وَأَخَوَاتُهَا** (kāna wa-akhawātuhā) — kāna and her sisters enter a nominal sentence. The subject is **اسْمُ كَانَ** (ismu kāna) — the noun of kāna and stays nominative. The predicate is **خَبَرُ كَانَ** (khabaru kāna) — the predicate of kāna and becomes accusative.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| **الجَوُّ بَارِدٌ** (al-jawwu bāridun) — the weather is cold | **كَانَ الجَوُّ بَارِدًا** (kāna al-jawwu bāridan) — the weather was cold |
| **الطَّالِبُ مُجْتَهِدٌ** (aṭ-ṭālibu mujtahidun) — the student is hardworking | **أَصْبَحَ الطَّالِبُ مُجْتَهِدًا** (aṣbaḥa aṭ-ṭālibu mujtahidan) — the student became hardworking |

## Common kāna sisters

| Sister | Transliteration | Core meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **أَصْبَحَ** | aṣbaḥa | became in the morning / became | **أَصْبَحَ الوَلَدُ طَوِيلًا** (aṣbaḥa al-waladu ṭawīlan) — the boy became tall |
| **صَارَ** | ṣāra | became | **صَارَ الأَمْرُ وَاضِحًا** (ṣāra al-amru wāḍiḥan) — the matter became clear |

## inna and her sisters

**إِنَّ وَأَخَوَاتُهَا** (inna wa-akhawātuhā) — inna and her sisters enter a nominal sentence. The subject becomes **اسْمُ إِنَّ** (ismu inna) — the noun of inna and is accusative. The predicate, **خَبَرُ إِنَّ** (khabaru inna) — the predicate of inna, remains nominative.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| **الجَوُّ بَارِدٌ** (al-jawwu bāridun) — the weather is cold | **إِنَّ الجَوَّ بَارِدٌ** (inna al-jawwa bāridun) — indeed, the weather is cold |
| **الوَلَدُ مَرِيضٌ** (al-waladu marīḍun) — the boy is sick | **لَعَلَّ الوَلَدَ مَرِيضٌ** (laʿalla al-walada marīḍun) — perhaps the boy is sick |

## Common inna sisters

| Sister | Transliteration | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **أَنَّ** | anna | that | **عَلِمْتُ أَنَّ الخَبَرَ صَحِيحٌ** (ʿalimtu anna al-khabara ṣaḥīḥun) — I knew that the report was true |
| **كَأَنَّ** | kaʾanna | as if | **كَأَنَّ القَمَرَ مِصْبَاحٌ** (kaʾanna al-qamara miṣbāḥun) — as if the moon were a lamp |
| **لَكِنَّ** | lākinna | but/however | **لَكِنَّ الطَّرِيقَ طَوِيلٌ** (lākinna aṭ-ṭarīqa ṭawīlun) — but the road is long |
| **لَيْتَ** | layta | if only | **لَيْتَ الوَقْتَ كَافٍ** (layta al-waqta kāfin) — if only the time were enough |
| **لَعَلَّ** | laʿalla | perhaps | **لَعَلَّ الدَّرْسَ سَهْلٌ** (laʿalla ad-darsa sahlun) — perhaps the lesson is easy |
