# Everyday Phrases, Politeness & Greetings

Polite phrases make grammar feel human. Many Arabic greetings are shared across regions, and even when dialects adapt pronunciation, the core expressions are widely understood.

## Core idea

The most important greeting is **السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ** (as-salāmu ʿalaykum) — peace be upon you. The reply is **وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَامُ** (wa-ʿalaykumu as-salāmu) — and upon you be peace.

## Forms and examples

| Phrase | Use |
|---|---|
| **السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ** (as-salāmu ʿalaykum) — peace be upon you | greeting |
| **وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَامُ** (wa-ʿalaykumu as-salāmu) — and peace be upon you | reply |
| **صَبَاحُ الْخَيْرِ** (ṣabāḥu al-khayri) — good morning | morning greeting |
| **مَسَاءُ الْخَيْرِ** (masāʾu al-khayri) — good evening | evening greeting |
| **شُكْرًا** (shukran) — thank you | thanks |
| **عَفْوًا** (ʿafwan) — you are welcome / excuse me | response or polite attention |
| **مِنْ فَضْلِكَ** (min faḍlika) — please, to a male | polite request |
| **مِنْ فَضْلِكِ** (min faḍliki) — please, to a female | polite request |
| **مَعَ السَّلَامَةِ** (maʿa as-salāmati) — goodbye | farewell |

## Usage notes

Arabic politeness often uses blessings and warm formulas. **جَزَاكَ اللهُ خَيْرًا** (jazāka allāhu khayran) — may God reward you with good, to a male, is a heartfelt thank-you in many contexts.

## Practice patterns

Mini-dialogue:

> **السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ** (as-salāmu ʿalaykum) — peace be upon you.
> **وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَامُ** (wa-ʿalaykumu as-salāmu) — and upon you be peace.
> **كَيْفَ حَالُكَ؟** (kayfa ḥāluka?) — how are you, to a male?
> **أَنَا بِخَيْرٍ، شُكْرًا** (anā bikhayrin, shukran) — I am well, thank you.
