# The Mīmī Verbal Noun & Nouns of Place/Time (mafʿal)

Arabic often builds nouns with an initial **مِيمٌ** (mīmun) — the letter mīm. These can name an action, a place, or a time, and context decides the meaning.

> The same shape can mean “entry,” “entrance,” or “time/place of entering.” Do not translate the pattern mechanically.

## Three related terms

| Term | Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| mīmī verbal noun | **المَصْدَرُ المِيمِيُّ** (al-maṣdaru al-mīmiyyu) | an action noun beginning with mīm |
| noun of place | **اسْمُ المَكَانِ** (ismu al-makāni) | place where an action occurs |
| noun of time | **اسْمُ الزَّمَانِ** (ismu az-zamāni) | time when an action occurs |

## Form I patterns

For many Form I verbs, the useful patterns are **مَفْعَلٌ** (mafʿalun) and **مَفْعِلٌ** (mafʿilun). The exact vowel often depends on the verb and usage.

| Verb | Derived noun | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **دَخَلَ** (dakhala) — to enter | **مَدْخَلٌ** (madkhalun) | entrance; entry |
| **خَرَجَ** (kharaja) — to go out | **مَخْرَجٌ** (makhrajun) | exit; articulation point |
| **جَلَسَ** (jalasa) — to sit | **مَجْلِسٌ** (majlisun) | sitting place; council |
| **وَعَدَ** (waʿada) — to promise | **مَوْعِدٌ** (mawʿidun) | appointment; appointed time |
| **نَزَلَ** (nazala) — to descend/stay | **مَنْزِلٌ** (manzilun) | house; stopping place |

## Context decides action, place, or time

The same form may be understood differently in different sentences.

- **مَدْخَلُ البِنَايَةِ وَاسِعٌ** (madkhalu al-bināyati wāsiʿun) — the entrance of the building is wide.
- **كَانَ مَدْخَلُهُ إِلَى المَوْضُوعِ لَطِيفًا** (kāna madkhaluhu ilā al-mawḍūʿi laṭīfan) — his entry into the topic was graceful.
- **لَنَا مَوْعِدٌ فِي الصَّبَاحِ** (lanā mawʿidun fī aṣ-ṣabāḥi) — we have an appointment in the morning.

## A useful contrast with ordinary maṣdar

An ordinary **مَصْدَرٌ** (maṣdarun) — verbal noun may have many patterns: **دُخُولٌ** (dukhūlun) — entering, **خُرُوجٌ** (khurūjun) — going out, **جُلُوسٌ** (julūsūn) — sitting. A mīmī form can overlap in meaning but sounds like a different lexical noun.

| Ordinary maṣdar | Mīmī-related noun |
|---|---|
| **دُخُولٌ** (dukhūlun) — entering | **مَدْخَلٌ** (madkhalun) — entry/entrance |
| **خُرُوجٌ** (khurūjun) — going out | **مَخْرَجٌ** (makhrajun) — exit |
| **جُلُوسٌ** (julūsūn) — sitting | **مَجْلِسٌ** (majlisun) — sitting place/council |
