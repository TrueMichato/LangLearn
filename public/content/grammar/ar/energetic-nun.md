# The Energetic Mood (nūn al-tawkīd)

The energetic nūn adds strong confirmation to a verb. In careful MSA it is mostly seen in oaths, solemn promises, emphatic commands, and emphatic prohibitions.

## Core idea

نُونُ التَّوْكِيدِ (nūn at-tawkīd) — the nūn of emphasis has two shapes: the heavy نَّ (nūn thaqīla) and the lighter نْ (nūn khafīfa). The heavy form is the one you will meet most often in reading: لَأَكْتُبَنَّ (la-aktubanna) — I will surely write.

The meaning is not a new tense. It is an emphatic mood: the speaker presents the action as certain, urgent, or strongly commanded.

## Forms and examples

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Oath or promise with لَـ | لَأَكْتُبَنَّ الرِّسَالَةَ (la-aktubanna ar-risālata) — I will surely write the letter |
| Emphatic command | اُدْرُسَنَّ الدَّرْسَ (udrusanna ad-darsa) — Do study the lesson |
| Emphatic prohibition | لَا تُهْمِلَنَّ الْوَقْتَ (lā tuhmilanna al-waqta) — Do not neglect time |
| Emphatic plural promise | لَنُسَاعِدَنَّ الْجَارَ (la-nusāʿidanna al-jāra) — We will certainly help the neighbor |

## Usage notes

Energetic forms are formal and rhetorical. They are common in Qurʾānic, literary, legal, and solemn styles, but ordinary speech usually prefers adverbs such as حَقًّا (ḥaqqan) — truly or بِالتَّأْكِيدِ (bi-t-taʾkīdi) — certainly.

When a verb already has a strong context, the energetic nūn makes the force explicit: لَا تَخُونَنَّ الْأَمَانَةَ (lā takhūnanna al-amānata) — Do not ever betray the trust.

## Practice patterns

Compare the plain verb with the emphatic verb:

- أَحْفَظُ الْوَعْدَ (aḥfaẓu al-waʿda) — I keep the promise.
- لَأَحْفَظَنَّ الْوَعْدَ (la-aḥfaẓanna al-waʿda) — I will certainly keep the promise.
- لَا تَتْرُكِ الْعَمَلَ (lā tatruki al-ʿamala) — Do not leave the work.
- لَا تَتْرُكَنَّ الْعَمَلَ (lā tatrukanna al-ʿamala) — Do not leave the work at all.

## Watch out

Do not confuse energetic نَّ with the feminine plural ending ـنَ in forms such as يَكْتُبْنَ (yaktubna) — they write. Energetic نَّ is an added marker of emphasis and is usually pronounced with a doubled n sound.
