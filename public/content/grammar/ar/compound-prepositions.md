# Compound & Locative Prepositions (fawqa, taḥta, amāma, bayna…)

Arabic has many location words that act like prepositions in meaning but still behave like nouns. Words such as فَوْقَ (fawqa) — above, تَحْتَ (taḥta) — under, أَمَامَ (amāma) — in front of, خَلْفَ (khalfa) — behind, and بَيْنَ (bayna) — between are often called locative adverbs or ẓurūf.

## Core idea

A locative word normally comes before a genitive complement, much like the first word of an iḍāfa. The locative word itself often has fatḥa when it functions as an adverbial of place.

| Locative phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| فَوْقَ الطَّاوِلَةِ (fawqa aṭ-ṭāwilati) — above the table | الطَّاوِلَةِ (aṭ-ṭāwilati) is genitive |
| تَحْتَ السَّرِيرِ (taḥta as-sarīri) — under the bed | السَّرِيرِ (as-sarīri) is genitive |
| أَمَامَ الْمَدْرَسَةِ (amāma al-madrasati) — in front of the school | الْمَدْرَسَةِ (al-madrasati) is genitive |
| خَلْفَ الْبَيْتِ (khalfa al-bayti) — behind the house | الْبَيْتِ (al-bayti) is genitive |
| بَيْنَ الْمَكْتَبَيْنِ (bayna al-maktabayni) — between the two offices | dual genitive ending -ayni |

## Compound prepositional phrases

A simple preposition can come before a locative noun. Then the first preposition governs the locative noun, and the locative noun still governs its own complement.

- مَرَرْتُ مِنْ أَمَامِ الْمَسْجِدِ (marartu min amāmi al-masjidi) — I passed from in front of the mosque.
- خَرَجَتِ الْقِطَّةُ مِنْ تَحْتِ الْكُرْسِيِّ (kharajati al-qiṭṭatu min taḥti al-kursiyyi) — the cat came out from under the chair.
- سَمِعْتُ الصَّوْتَ مِنْ فَوْقِ السَّطْحِ (samiʿtu aṣ-ṣawta min fawqi as-saṭḥi) — I heard the sound from above the roof.

## Attached pronouns

These locative nouns also take attached pronouns: فَوْقَهُ (fawqahu) — above it/him, تَحْتَهَا (taḥtahā) — under it/her, أَمَامَكَ (amāmaka) — in front of you. The pronoun is the genitive complement.

- اَلْمِفْتَاحُ تَحْتَهَا (al-miftāḥu taḥtahā) — the key is under it/her.
- وَقَفَ الْمُدَرِّسُ أَمَامَنَا (waqafa al-mudarrisu amāmanā) — the teacher stood in front of us.

## Practice patterns

Use these words to make precise spatial descriptions:

- اَلْبَيْتُ قُرْبَ النَّهْرِ (al-baytu qurba an-nahri) — the house is near the river.
- اَلْحَدِيقَةُ بَيْنَ الْمَدْرَسَةِ وَالْمَكْتَبَةِ (al-ḥadīqatu bayna al-madrasati wa-l-maktabati) — the garden is between the school and the library.
- اَلْوَلَدُ دَاخِلَ الْغُرْفَةِ (al-waladu dākhila al-ghurfati) — the boy is inside the room.
