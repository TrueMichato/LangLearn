# Exception Sentence Types with illā

<!-- vocab-inventory: {"mode":"explicit"} -->

Arabic exceptions with **إِلَّا** (illā) depend on the shape of the whole sentence. The three useful labels are complete affirmative, complete negative, and incomplete negative.

## Core idea

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"complete-vs-incomplete","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Core idea","rule":"A complete exception names the group from which something is excluded.","example":"What makes an exception construction “complete” rather than “incomplete” here?","answer":"It explicitly names the group before إِلَّا.","explanation":"This completeness label concerns the expressed group, not whether the utterance communicates a complete thought."} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"rule-incomplete-role","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Core idea","rule":"In an incomplete negative exception, the noun after إِلَّا takes the case of its normal role in the sentence.","example":"In an incomplete negative exception (no group named before إِلَّا), what decides the case of the noun after إِلَّا?","answer":"Its normal grammatical role in the sentence, such as subject or object.","explanation":"مَا حَضَرَ إِلَّا طَالِبٌ has a nominative subject; لَمْ أَرَ إِلَّا طَالِبًا has an accusative object."} -->

A complete sentence names the group before **إِلَّا** (illā). An incomplete negative sentence does not name that group, so the noun after **إِلَّا** (illā) simply fills the normal grammatical role needed by the sentence.

## Forms and examples

<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-illa","word":"إِلَّا","reading":"illā","meaning":"except; only in a negative restrictive construction","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Forms and examples"} -->

| Type | Arabic label | Pattern | Case after إِلَّا | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| complete affirmative | **تَامٌّ مُوجَبٌ** (tāmmun mūjabun) | group is named; no negation | usually accusative | **حَضَرَ الطُّلَّابُ إِلَّا طَالِبًا** (ḥaḍara aṭ-ṭullābu illā ṭāliban) — the students attended except one student |
| complete negative | **تَامٌّ مَنْفِيٌّ** (tāmmun manfiyyun) | group is named; negation present | accusative or substitute case | **مَا حَضَرَ الطُّلَّابُ إِلَّا طَالِبٌ** (mā ḥaḍara aṭ-ṭullābu illā ṭālibun) — no students attended except one student |
| incomplete negative | **نَاقِصٌ مَنْفِيٌّ** (nāqiṣun manfiyyun) | no group before إِلَّا | normal role in sentence | **مَا حَضَرَ إِلَّا طَالِبٌ** (mā ḥaḍara illā ṭālibun) — only one student attended |
| incomplete with object | **نَاقِصٌ مَنْفِيٌّ** (nāqiṣun manfiyyun) | noun is an object | accusative as object | **لَمْ أَرَ إِلَّا طَالِبًا** (lam ara illā ṭāliban) — I saw only one student |

## Usage notes

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"complete-negative-choice","kind":"rule","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Usage notes","rule":"A connected complete negative exception allows exception accusative or matching badal case.","example":"In مَا حَضَرَ الطُّلَّابُ إِلَّا طَالِبٌ, what alternative case can the excluded student take, and why is nominative allowed?","answer":"طَالِبًا is also allowed; طَالِبٌ can be a substitute matching the nominative group.","explanation":"This is a connected exception, with a member of the named group. Do not extend the substitute option indiscriminately to disconnected exceptions."} -->

In a complete affirmative exception, the excluded noun is the **مُسْتَثْنَى** (mustathnā) and is usually accusative. In a complete negative sentence, Arabic allows either accusative exception or a substitute matching the group’s case. In an incomplete negative sentence, do not force an accusative; read what the sentence still needs.

The substitute option here concerns a connected exception, where the excluded member belongs to the named group; do not extend it indiscriminately to disconnected exceptions.

## Practice patterns

Notice how the case changes with the sentence type:

- **قَرَأْتُ الْكُتُبَ إِلَّا كِتَابًا** (qaraʾtu al-kutuba illā kitāban) — I read the books except one book.
- **مَا قَرَأْتُ الْكُتُبَ إِلَّا كِتَابًا** (mā qaraʾtu al-kutuba illā kitāban) — I read no books except one book.
- **مَا قَرَأْتُ إِلَّا كِتَابًا** (mā qaraʾtu illā kitāban) — I read only one book.
