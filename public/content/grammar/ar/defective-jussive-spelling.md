# Defective Verb Spelling in the Jussive

Defective verbs end in a weak letter: **و** (wāw), **ي** (yāʾ), or **ى** (alif maqṣūra). In the jussive, that final weak letter is normally removed, and the remaining vowel hints at what disappeared.

## Core idea

After jussive particles such as **لَمْ** (lam), the sign of jazm for a defective verb is deletion of the final weak letter. Compare **يَدْعُو** (yadʿū) “he calls” with **لَمْ يَدْعُ** (lam yadʿu) “he did not call.”

## Forms and examples

| Final weak type | Indicative | Jussive | Remaining vowel | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| final wāw | **يَدْعُو** (yadʿū) | **لَمْ يَدْعُ** (lam yadʿu) | **ـُ** for lost wāw | **لَمْ يَدْعُ الْمُدِيرُ أَحَدًا** (lam yadʿu al-mudīru aḥadan) — the manager did not call anyone |
| final yāʾ | **يَرْمِي** (yarmī) | **لَمْ يَرْمِ** (lam yarmi) | **ـِ** for lost yāʾ | **لَمْ يَرْمِ الطِّفْلُ الْكُرَةَ** (lam yarmi aṭ-ṭiflu al-kurata) — the child did not throw the ball |
| final alif | **يَخْشَى** (yakhshā) | **لَمْ يَخْشَ** (lam yakhsha) | **ـَ** for lost alif | **لَمْ يَخْشَ الْجُنْدِيُّ الْخَطَرَ** (lam yakhsha al-jundiyyu al-khaṭara) — the soldier did not fear danger |
| another alif verb | **يَنْسَى** (yansā) | **لَمْ يَنْسَ** (lam yansa) | **ـَ** for lost alif | **لَمْ يَنْسَ الطَّالِبُ الْقَاعِدَةَ** (lam yansa aṭ-ṭālibu al-qāʿidata) — the student did not forget the rule |

## Usage notes

The spelling can look short because the weak letter disappears. Do not add an extra silent letter in singular forms: write **لَمْ يَدْعُ** (lam yadʿu), not **لَمْ يَدْعُو** for “he did not call.” With plural masculine forms, the final **ـُوا** belongs to the plural ending, so patterns like **لَمْ يَدْعُوا** (lam yadʿū) follow a different agreement rule.

## Practice patterns

Match the vowel to the deleted weak letter:

- **لَمْ يَمْشِ الرَّجُلُ بَعِيدًا** (lam yamshi ar-rajulu baʿīdan) — the man did not walk far.
- **لَمْ يَرْضَ الْقَاضِي بِالْجَوَابِ** (lam yarḍa al-qāḍī bi-al-jawābi) — the judge was not satisfied with the answer.
- **لِيَدْعُ الْإِمَامُ لِلنَّاسِ** (liyadʿu al-imāmu li-an-nāsi) — let the imam pray for the people.
