# Verb–Subject Agreement & Word-Order Effects

<!-- vocab-inventory: {"mode":"explicit"} -->

In standard MSA, verb agreement depends strongly on word order. With the human subjects illustrated here, a verb before an explicit subject normally stays singular while agreeing in gender. A preceding human topic normally has a following verb showing full agreement in person, gender, and number. Non-human plurals normally take feminine singular agreement in either order; these MSA defaults should not be imposed as universal dialect rules.

## Core idea

In the standard verb-first pattern illustrated here, the verb agrees with the human subject in gender but remains singular even if that subject is plural or dual.

## Subject-first sentences

When the subject comes before the verb, the verb has an expressed pronoun inside it and normally agrees fully.

- اَلطُّلَّابُ جَاءُوا (aṭ-ṭullābu jāʾū) — the students came.
- اَلطَّالِبَاتُ جِئْنَ (aṭ-ṭālibātu jiʾna) — the female students came.
- اَلْمُعَلِّمَانِ شَرَحَا الدَّرْسَ (al-muʿallimāni sharaḥā ad-darsa) — the two teachers explained the lesson.

## Gender still matters

In these standard verb-first examples, a feminine singular or feminine human plural subject has a feminine singular verb.

- دَرَسَ الطَّالِبُ (darasa aṭ-ṭālibu) — the male student studied.
- دَرَسَ الطُّلَّابُ (darasa aṭ-ṭullābu) — the male students studied.
- دَرَسَتِ الطَّالِبَةُ (darasati aṭ-ṭālibatu) — the female student studied.
- دَرَسَتِ الطَّالِبَاتُ (darasati aṭ-ṭālibātu) — the female students studied.

## Practice patterns

The explicit subject after a verb is nominative. In traditional analysis, the initial noun in the subject-first examples is a nominative topic (mubtadaʾ), and the verb contains its own agreeing subject pronoun. In fully vowelled MSA, watch for ـُ (-u), ـَانِ (-āni), and ـُونَ (-ūna) or broken plural endings.

- كَتَبَتِ الْبِنْتُ الرِّسَالَةَ (katabati al-bintu ar-risālata) — the girl wrote the letter.
- اَلْبِنْتُ كَتَبَتِ الرِّسَالَةَ (al-bintu katabati ar-risālata) — the girl wrote the letter.
- سَافَرَ الرِّجَالُ صَبَاحًا (sāfara ar-rijālu ṣabāḥan) — the men traveled in the morning.
- اَلرِّجَالُ سَافَرُوا صَبَاحًا (ar-rijālu sāfarū ṣabāḥan) — the men traveled in the morning.
