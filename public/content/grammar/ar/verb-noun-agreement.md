# Verb–Subject Agreement & Word-Order Effects

Arabic verb agreement depends strongly on word order. When an explicit subject follows the verb, the verb normally stays singular while agreeing in gender. When the subject comes first, the following verb usually shows full agreement in person, gender, and number.

## Core idea

In a verb-first sentence, the verb agrees with the subject in gender, but it remains singular even if the subject is plural or dual.

| Verb-first sentence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| جَاءَ الطَّالِبُ (jāʾa aṭ-ṭālibu) — the male student came | masculine singular subject |
| جَاءَ الطُّلَّابُ (jāʾa aṭ-ṭullābu) — the male students came | plural subject, singular verb |
| جَاءَتِ الطَّالِبَةُ (jāʾati aṭ-ṭālibatu) — the female student came | feminine singular subject |
| جَاءَتِ الطَّالِبَاتُ (jāʾati aṭ-ṭālibātu) — the female students came | plural subject, singular feminine verb |

## Subject-first sentences

When the subject comes before the verb, the verb has an expressed pronoun inside it and normally agrees fully.

- اَلطُّلَّابُ جَاءُوا (aṭ-ṭullābu jāʾū) — the students came.
- اَلطَّالِبَاتُ جِئْنَ (aṭ-ṭālibātu jiʾna) — the female students came.
- اَلْمُعَلِّمَانِ شَرَحَا الدَّرْسَ (al-muʿallimāni sharaḥā ad-darsa) — the two teachers explained the lesson.

## Gender still matters

Even in verb-first order, the verb should match the gender of a feminine singular or feminine human plural subject.

- دَرَسَ الطَّالِبُ (darasa aṭ-ṭālibu) — the male student studied.
- دَرَسَ الطُّلَّابُ (darasa aṭ-ṭullābu) — the male students studied.
- دَرَسَتِ الطَّالِبَةُ (darasati aṭ-ṭālibatu) — the female student studied.
- دَرَسَتِ الطَّالِبَاتُ (darasati aṭ-ṭālibātu) — the female students studied.

## Practice patterns

The subject of a verb is nominative whether it comes before or after the verb. In fully vowelled MSA, watch for ـُ (-u), ـَانِ (-āni), and ـُونَ (-ūna) or broken plural endings.

- كَتَبَتِ الْبِنْتُ الرِّسَالَةَ (katabati al-bintu ar-risālata) — the girl wrote the letter.
- اَلْبِنْتُ كَتَبَتِ الرِّسَالَةَ (al-bintu katabati ar-risālata) — the girl wrote the letter.
- سَافَرَ الرِّجَالُ صَبَاحًا (sāfara ar-rijālu ṣabāḥan) — the men traveled in the morning.
- اَلرِّجَالُ سَافَرُوا صَبَاحًا (ar-rijālu sāfarū ṣabāḥan) — the men traveled in the morning.
