# Nuances of the Core Prepositions

Arabic prepositions are short, but each one has a range of meanings. In MSA, the same preposition can mark place, direction, cause, possession, comparison, or instrument depending on the verb and context.

## Core meanings

Every preposition makes the following noun genitive. For example, **فِي الْبَيْتِ** (fī al-bayti) — in the house, and **بِالْقَلَمِ** (bil-qalami) — with the pen.

## Nuance table

| Preposition | Common nuance | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **مِنْ** (min) | from, some of, starting point | **خَرَجَ مِنَ الْبَيْتِ** (kharaja mina al-bayti) — He left the house. |
| **إِلَى** (ilā) | to, toward, endpoint | **ذَهَبَ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ** (dhahaba ilā al-madrasati) — He went to the school. |
| **عَنْ** (ʿan) | away from, about | **سَأَلَ عَنِ الطَّرِيقِ** (saʾala ʿani aṭ-ṭarīqi) — He asked about the road. |
| **عَلَى** (ʿalā) | on, against, responsibility | **الْوَاجِبُ عَلَى الطَّالِبِ** (al-wājibu ʿalā aṭ-ṭālibi) — The duty is upon the student. |
| **فِي** (fī) | in, within, during | **دَرَسَ فِي الصَّبَاحِ** (darasa fī aṣ-ṣabāḥi) — He studied in the morning. |
| **بِـ** (bi-) | with, by means of, because of | **كَتَبَ بِالْقَلَمِ** (kataba bil-qalami) — He wrote with the pen. |
| **لِـ** (li-) | for, belonging to, purpose | **الْكِتَابُ لِلطَّالِبِ** (al-kitābu liṭ-ṭālibi) — The book belongs to the student. |
| **كَـ** (ka-) | like, as | **هُوَ كَالْأَسَدِ** (huwa kal-asadi) — He is like a lion. |

## Verb-preposition pairings

Some meanings are tied to specific verbs. **بَحَثَ عَنِ الْحَقِيقَةِ** (baḥatha ʿani al-ḥaqīqati) means “he searched for the truth,” while **رَغِبَ فِي الْعِلْمِ** (raghiba fī al-ʿilmi) means “he desired knowledge.” Learn the verb and its preposition together.

## Practice patterns

Compare direction and location: **سَافَرَ إِلَى مِصْرَ** (sāfara ilā miṣra) — He traveled to Egypt, but **أَقَامَ فِي مِصْرَ** (aqāma fī miṣra) — He stayed in Egypt. The case after both prepositions is genitive, even when a diptote place name shows fatḥa.
