# Reading Romanized Arabic & the Chat Alphabet

<!-- vocab-inventory: {"mode":"explicit"} -->

**عَرَبِيزِيٌّ** (ʿarabīziyyun) — Arabizi is informal Romanized Arabic used in texting. It mixes Latin letters with numbers to represent Arabic sounds that English spelling cannot easily show.

## Core idea

Arabizi is not one official system. It is a practical chat habit. Numbers usually stand for Arabic letters by visual resemblance or community convention: **3 = ع** (thalāthatun tusāwī ʿayn) — 3 represents ʿayn, **7 = ح** (sabʿatun tusāwī ḥāʾ) — 7 represents ḥāʾ, **2 = ء** (ithnatāni tusāwī hamzah) — 2 represents hamza, **9 = ص** (tisʿatun tusāwī ṣād) — 9 represents ṣād.

## Common symbols

| Arabizi | Arabic sound | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | **هَمْزَةٌ** (hamzatun) — hamza | **2ana** → **أَنَا** (ʾanā) — I |
| 3 | **عَيْنٌ** (ʿaynun) — ʿayn | **3arabī** → **عَرَبِيٌّ** (ʿarabiyyun) — Arabic |
| 7 | **حَاءٌ** (ḥāʾun) — ḥ | **7abībī** → **حَبِيبِي** (ḥabībī) — my dear, masculine |
| 5 / kh | **خَاءٌ** (khāʾun) — kh | **5alas** → **خَلَاصْ** (khalāṣ) — enough / done in chat |
| 6 | **طَاءٌ** (ṭāʾun) — ṭ | **6ālib** → **طَالِبٌ** (ṭālibun) — student |
| 9 | **صَادٌ** (ṣādun) — ṣ | **9aḥḥ** → **صَحّْ** (ṣaḥḥ) — correct in chat |

## Reading strategy

First replace numbers with likely Arabic sounds, then identify the dialect or MSA word. **salaam 3alaykum** maps to **سَلَامٌ عَلَيْكُمْ** (salāmun ʿalaykum) — peace be upon you. **2ana mish 3ārif** maps to **أَنَا مِشْ عَارِفْ** (ʾana mish ʿārif) — I do not know, masculine in Egyptian-style speech. Colloquial chat forms do not acquire MSA case tanwīn simply by being written in Arabic script.

## Cautions

Arabizi spelling depends on the writer. The same word may appear as **khalaas**, **5alas**, or **7'alaas** for **خَلَاصْ** (khalāṣ) — enough / finished. Do not use Arabizi in formal Arabic writing; use it to recognize messages and dialect examples.

> Arabizi helps you read chats. It does not replace Arabic script practice.
