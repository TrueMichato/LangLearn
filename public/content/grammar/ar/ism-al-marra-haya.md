# Nouns of Instance and Manner (ism al-Marra & ism al-Hayʾa)

اِسْمُ الْمَرَّةِ (ismu al-marrati) names one occurrence of an action, while اِسْمُ الْهَيْئَةِ (ismu al-hayʾati) names the manner or shape of doing it.

## Core idea

For many triliteral verbs, the instance pattern is فَعْلَةٌ (faʿlatun), and the manner pattern is often فِعْلَةٌ (fiʿlatun). A tiny vowel change can move from “one act” to “the way the act is done.”

## Forms and examples

| Verb | One instance | Manner or shape | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| جَلَسَ (jalasa) — to sit | جَلْسَةٌ (jalsatun) | جِلْسَةٌ (jilsatun) | جَلَسْتُ جَلْسَةً قَصِيرَةً (jalastu jalsatan qaṣīratan) — I sat for one short sitting |
| ضَرَبَ (ḍaraba) — to strike | ضَرْبَةٌ (ḍarbatun) | ضِرْبَةٌ (ḍirbatun) | سَمِعْتُ ضَرْبَةً قَوِيَّةً (samiʿtu ḍarbatan qawiyyatan) — I heard one strong blow |
| وَقَفَ (waqafa) — to stand | وَقْفَةٌ (waqfatun) | وِقْفَةٌ (wiqfatun) | كَانَتْ وِقْفَتُهُ هَادِئَةً (kānat wiqfatuhu hādiʾatan) — his stance was calm |
| مَشَى (mashā) — to walk | مَشْيَةٌ وَاحِدَةٌ (mashyatun wāḥidatun) | مِشْيَةٌ (mishyatun) | مِشْيَتُهَا سَرِيعَةٌ (mishyatuhā sarīʿatun) — her gait is fast |

## Usage notes

Not every verb has a common everyday noun for both categories. When the special form sounds unclear, MSA can use the maṣdar plus وَاحِدَةٌ (wāḥidatun) or وَاحِدًا (wāḥidan): اِنْطَلَقَ اِنْطِلَاقًا وَاحِدًا (inṭalaqa inṭilāqan wāḥidan) — he set out one time.

## Practice patterns

Watch the vowel after the first consonant:

- جَلْسَةٌ وَاحِدَةٌ (jalsatun wāḥidatun) — one sitting.
- جِلْسَةٌ مُسْتَقِيمَةٌ (jilsatun mustaqīmatun) — an upright posture.
- ضَرْبَةٌ خَفِيفَةٌ (ḍarbatun khafīfatun) — one light strike.
