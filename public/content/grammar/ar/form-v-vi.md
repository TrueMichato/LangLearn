# Verb Forms V & VI (tafaʿʿala, tafāʿala): reflexive & reciprocal

Forms V and VI are built from earlier forms by adding تَـ (ta-) — a prefix often linked with reflexive, middle, or shared action.

## Core idea

Form V تَفَعَّلَ (tafaʿʿala) — he did for himself / became is closely related to Form II فَعَّلَ (faʿʿala). Form VI تَفَاعَلَ (tafāʿala) — they interacted / he acted mutually is closely related to Form III فَاعَلَ (fāʿala).

| Form | Pattern | Related form | Typical clue | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V | تَفَعَّلَ (tafaʿʿala) — he did reflexively | II | self-directed, acquired state | تَعَلَّمَ (taʿallama) — he learned |
| V | تَفَعَّلَ (tafaʿʿala) — he did reflexively | II | trained oneself | تَدَرَّبَ (tadarraba) — he trained |
| VI | تَفَاعَلَ (tafāʿala) — they interacted | III | reciprocal action | تَقَابَلَ (taqābala) — they met each other |
| VI | تَفَاعَلَ (tafāʿala) — they interacted | III | cooperative action | تَعَاوَنَ (taʿāwana) — he cooperated |

## Form V: inward or acquired action

عَلَّمَ (ʿallama) — he taught is Form II. تَعَلَّمَ (taʿallama) — he learned is Form V: the action turns inward toward the subject. Likewise دَرَّبَ (darraba) — he trained someone and تَدَرَّبَ (tadarraba) — he trained / practiced.

## Form VI: shared action

Form VI often implies two sides or a group. تَقَابَلَ الطُّلَّابُ (taqābala aṭ-ṭullābu) — the students met one another. تَعَاوَنَ الْفَرِيقُ (taʿāwana al-farīqu) — the team cooperated.

- تَشَاوَرَ (tashāwara) — he consulted / they consulted.
- تَبَادَلَ (tabādala) — he exchanged / they exchanged.
- تَفَاهَمَ (tafāhama) — they reached understanding.

> The تَـ (ta-) prefix does not always mean “self,” but it often tells you the verb has moved away from a simple one-way action.
