# Conditional Particles in Depth (in, idhā, law, lawlā)

Arabic conditionals are not all the same. The particle you choose tells the listener whether the condition is open, expected, counterfactual, or prevented by something.

## Core idea

أَدَاةُ الشَّرْطِ (adātu ash-sharṭi) — conditional particle introduces a condition. إِنْ (in) — if often marks an open condition and can make two present verbs jussive. إِذَا (idhā) — when / if often points to an expected event. لَوْ (law) — if only / if had often marks counterfactual meaning. لَوْلَا (lawlā) — were it not for introduces a preventing factor.

| Particle | Typical meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| إِنْ (in) — if | open condition | إِنْ تَدْرُسْ تَنْجَحْ (in tadrus tanjaḥ) — if you study, you succeed |
| إِذَا (idhā) — when / if | expected condition | إِذَا دَرَسْتَ نَجَحْتَ (idhā darasta najaḥta) — when you study, you succeed |
| لَوْ (law) — if had / if were | counterfactual | لَوْ دَرَسْتَ لَنَجَحْتَ (law darasta la-najaḥta) — if you had studied, you would have succeeded |
| لَوْلَا (lawlā) — were it not for | prevented result | لَوْلَا الْمَطَرُ لَخَرَجْنَا (lawlā al-maṭaru la-kharajnā) — were it not for the rain, we would have gone out |

## In and idhā

إِنْ (in) is common for open, uncertain conditions: إِنْ تَفْتَحِ النَّافِذَةَ يَدْخُلِ الْهَوَاءُ (in taftaḥi an-nāfidhata yadkhuli al-hawāʾu) — if you open the window, the air enters. إِذَا (idhā) often expects the event: إِذَا وَصَلَ الْقِطَارُ سَافَرْنَا (idhā waṣala al-qiṭāru sāfarnā) — when the train arrives, we will travel.

## Law and lawlā

لَوْ (law) often imagines the opposite of reality: لَوْ كَانَ الْوَقْتُ طَوِيلًا لَقَرَأْتُ (law kāna al-waqtu ṭawīlan la-qaraʾtu) — if the time had been long, I would have read. لَوْلَا (lawlā) names what prevents the result: لَوْلَا الْمُعَلِّمُ لَمَا فَهِمْنَا (lawlā al-muʿallimu la-mā fahimnā) — if not for the teacher, we would not have understood.

- إِنْ سَافَرْتَ فَاتَّصِلْ بِي (in sāfarta fa-ittaṣil bī) — if you travel, contact me.
- إِذَا جِئْتَ فَسَنَبْدَأُ (idhā jiʾta fa-sanabdaʾu) — when you come, we will begin.
- لَوْلَا الْمَاءُ لَهَلَكَ الزَّرْعُ (lawlā al-māʾu la-halaka az-zarʿu) — were it not for water, the crops would perish.

> The English word “if” hides several Arabic choices. Pick the particle according to the reality status of the condition.
