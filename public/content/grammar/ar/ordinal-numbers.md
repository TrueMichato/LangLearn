# Ordinal Numbers (al-awwal, ath-thānī…) & Dates

Ordinal numbers behave more like adjectives than cardinal numbers. They describe order: first, second, third, and so on.

> Cardinal numbers count things; ordinal numbers rank things. In Arabic, ordinals usually agree with the noun they describe.

## Core forms

An ordinal is **عَدَدٌ تَرْتِيبِيٌّ** (ʿadadun tartībiyyun) — ordinal number. The first ten are especially common.

| Number | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| first | **الأَوَّلُ** (al-awwalu) — the first | **الأُولَى** (al-ūlā) — the first |
| second | **الثَّانِي** (ath-thānī) — the second | **الثَّانِيَةُ** (ath-thāniyatu) — the second |
| third | **الثَّالِثُ** (ath-thālithu) — the third | **الثَّالِثَةُ** (ath-thālithatu) — the third |
| fourth | **الرَّابِعُ** (ar-rābiʿu) — the fourth | **الرَّابِعَةُ** (ar-rābiʿatu) — the fourth |
| tenth | **العَاشِرُ** (al-ʿāshiru) — the tenth | **العَاشِرَةُ** (al-ʿāshiratu) — the tenth |

## Agreement with nouns

Because ordinals are adjective-like, they commonly agree in gender, definiteness, number, and case with the noun.

- **الدَّرْسُ الأَوَّلُ** (ad-darsu al-awwalu) — the first lesson.
- **الوَحْدَةُ الأُولَى** (al-waḥdatu al-ūlā) — the first unit.
- **الصَّفْحَةُ الثَّانِيَةُ** (aṣ-ṣafḥatu ath-thāniyatu) — the second page.
- **البَابُ الثَّالِثُ** (al-bābu ath-thālithu) — the third chapter.

## Dates

Dates often use masculine ordinals because the understood noun is **اليَوْمُ** (al-yawmu) — the day. After **فِي** (fī) — in/on, the ordinal becomes genitive.

| Date phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **فِي اليَوْمِ الأَوَّلِ مِنْ مَارِسَ** (fī al-yawmi al-awwali min mārisa) | on the first day of March |
| **فِي الثَّالِثِ مِنْ أَبْرِيلَ** (fī ath-thālithi min abrīla) | on the third of April |
| **فِي العَاشِرِ مِنْ يُونْيُو** (fī al-ʿāshiri min yūnyū) | on the tenth of June |

## Compound ordinals

For eleventh and beyond, Arabic often combines a unit ordinal with ten: **الحَادِي عَشَرَ** (al-ḥādī ʿashara) — the eleventh masculine, **الحَادِيَةَ عَشْرَةَ** (al-ḥādiyata ʿashrata) — the eleventh feminine.

- **الفَصْلُ الحَادِي عَشَرَ** (al-faṣlu al-ḥādī ʿashara) — the eleventh chapter.
- **اللَيْلَةُ الثَّانِيَةَ عَشْرَةَ** (al-laylatu ath-thāniyata ʿashrata) — the twelfth night.
- **القَرْنُ الوَاحِدُ وَالعِشْرُونَ** (al-qarnu al-wāḥidu wa-l-ʿishrūna) — the twenty-first century.
