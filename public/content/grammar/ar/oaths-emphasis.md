# Oaths, Emphasis & the Energetic Nūn

Formal Arabic has several ways to strengthen a statement: oath particles, emphatic particles, and the energetic nūn attached to verbs.

## Core idea

قَسَمٌ (qasamun) — oath often uses وَ (wa-) — by, بِـ (bi-) — by, or تَـ (ta-) — by before the sworn name, followed by a response that may contain لَـ (la-) — emphatic lām. نُونُ التَّوْكِيدِ (nūn at-tawkīd) — energetic nūn adds strong verbal emphasis.

| Tool | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Oath with وَ | وَٱللّٰهِ (wallāhi) — by God | common oath expression |
| Oath response | لَأَكْتُبَنَّ (la-aktubanna) — I will surely write | lām plus energetic nūn |
| Heavy nūn | نَّ (nna) — heavy energetic nūn | strong emphasis |
| Light nūn | نْ (n) — light energetic nūn | lighter / rarer emphasis |

## Oath particles and the answer

An oath often places the sworn word in the genitive: وَٱللّٰهِ (wallāhi) — by God, بِٱللّٰهِ (billāhi) — by God, تَٱللّٰهِ (tallāhi) — by God. The answer may be strengthened: وَٱللّٰهِ لَأَقُولَنَّ الْحَقَّ (wallāhi la-aqūlanna al-ḥaqqa) — by God, I will surely tell the truth.

## Other emphasis tools

Arabic also uses إِنَّ (inna) — indeed, قَدْ (qad) — indeed / already, and لَـ (la-) — surely with nouns or verbs. إِنَّ الطَّالِبَ مُجْتَهِدٌ (inna aṭ-ṭāliba mujtahidun) — indeed the student is hardworking. قَدْ نَجَحَ الطَّالِبُ (qad najaḥa aṭ-ṭālibu) — the student has indeed succeeded.

- إِنَّ الْخَبَرَ صَحِيحٌ (inna al-khabara ṣaḥīḥun) — indeed the report is correct.
- لَلْحَقُّ وَاضِحٌ (la-l-ḥaqqu wāḍiḥun) — surely the truth is clear.
- اُكْتُبَنَّ الدَّرْسَ (uktubanna ad-darsa) — surely write the lesson!

> Emphasis is powerful. In ordinary prose, too many emphatic markers can sound heavy or dramatic.
