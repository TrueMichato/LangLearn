# Form I Maṣdar Patterns

The Form I verbal noun, or **الْمَصْدَرُ** (al-maṣdaru), names the action of a basic verb. Unlike derived forms, Form I has several common patterns, so learners should recognize patterns and check dictionaries.

## Core idea

A maṣdar behaves like a noun: it can be the subject, object, or part of an iḍāfa. In **أُحِبُّ قِرَاءَةَ الْكُتُبِ** (uḥibbu qirāʾata al-kutubi) — “I love reading books,” **قِرَاءَةَ** (qirāʾata) is the maṣdar of **قَرَأَ** (qaraʾa).

## Forms and examples

| Verb | Meaning | Maṣdar | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **كَتَبَ** (kataba) | wrote | **كِتَابَةٌ** (kitābatun) | **فِعَالَةٌ** (fiʿālatun) | **الْكِتَابَةُ مُهِمَّةٌ** (al-kitābatu muhimmatun) — writing is important |
| **دَرَسَ** (darasa) | studied | **دِرَاسَةٌ** (dirāsatun) | **فِعَالَةٌ** (fiʿālatun) | **بَدَأْتُ دِرَاسَةَ النَّحْوِ** (badaʾtu dirāsata an-naḥwi) — I began studying grammar |
| **دَخَلَ** (dakhala) | entered | **دُخُولٌ** (dukhūlun) | **فُعُولٌ** (fuʿūlun) | **مُنِعَ دُخُولُ السَّيَّارَاتِ** (muniʿa dukhūlu as-sayyārāti) — the entry of cars was forbidden |
| **فَهِمَ** (fahima) | understood | **فَهْمٌ** (fahmun) | **فَعْلٌ** (faʿlun) | **فَهْمُ السُّؤَالِ سَهْلٌ** (fahmu as-suʾāli sahlun) — understanding the question is easy |

## Usage notes

Form I maṣdars are partly lexical. Many verbs of motion use **فُعُولٌ** (fuʿūlun), such as **دُخُولٌ** (dukhūlun) and **خُرُوجٌ** (khurūjun). Many activity verbs use **فِعَالَةٌ** (fiʿālatun), such as **كِتَابَةٌ** (kitābatun) and **دِرَاسَةٌ** (dirāsatun). Short maṣdars like **فَهْمٌ** (fahmun) and **فَتْحٌ** (fatḥun) are also common.

## Practice patterns

Read the maṣdar as “the act of doing” the verb:

- **أَحْتَاجُ إِلَى فَهْمِ الْقَاعِدَةِ** (aḥtāju ilā fahmi al-qāʿidati) — I need to understand the rule.
- **قِرَاءَةُ الصُّحُفِ مُفِيدَةٌ** (qirāʾatu aṣ-ṣuḥufi mufīdatun) — reading newspapers is useful.
- **يَسْهُلُ حِفْظُ الْأَمْثِلَةِ** (yashulu ḥifẓu al-amthilati) — memorizing examples becomes easy.
