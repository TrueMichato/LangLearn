# The Many Uses of Mā

**مَا** (mā) is short, common, and flexible. It can ask a question, negate a verb, introduce a relative clause, turn a clause into a time expression, or combine with another particle.

## Core idea

Do not translate **مَا** automatically as “what.” First identify its job in the sentence. In **مَا هٰذَا؟** (mā hādhā?) it asks “what?” In **مَا سَافَرْتُ** (mā sāfartu) it negates: “I did not travel.”

## Forms and examples

| Use | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Interrogative **مَا الِاسْتِفْهَامِيَّةُ** | **مَا هٰذَا؟** (mā hādhā?) | what is this? |
| Negating **مَا النَّافِيَةُ** | **مَا ذَهَبْتُ أَمْسِ** (mā dhahabtu amsi) | I did not go yesterday |
| Relative **مَا الْمَوْصُولَةُ** | **أَحْفَظُ مَا تَقُولُ** (aḥfaẓu mā taqūlu) | I remember what you say |
| Verbal-noun/time **مَا الْمَصْدَرِيَّةُ** | **سَأَزُورُكَ بَعْدَمَا يَنْتَهِي الدَّرْسُ** (sa-azūruka baʿdamā yantahī ad-darsu) | I will visit you after the lesson ends |
| Restrictive **إِنَّمَا** | **إِنَّمَا الْعِلْمُ نُورٌ** (innamā al-ʿilmu nūrun) | knowledge is only light |

## Usage notes

The words around **مَا** often decide its meaning. A question mark and a following demonstrative suggest a question: **مَا هٰذَا؟** (mā hādhā?) — what is this? A past-tense verb after it often gives negation: **مَا فَهِمْتُ** (mā fahimtu) — I did not understand. After words such as **بَعْدَ** (baʿda) — after, it can help form a time clause.

## Practice patterns

Compare these minimal pairs:

- **مَا قَرَأْتَ؟** (mā qaraʾta?) — what did you read?
- **مَا قَرَأْتُ الْخَبَرَ** (mā qaraʾtu al-khabara) — I did not read the news.
- **أُحِبُّ مَا تَكْتُبِينَ** (uḥibbu mā taktubīna) — I like what you write.
