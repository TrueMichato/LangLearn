# Intensive Actor Patterns (ṣīghat al-mubālagha)

Arabic can intensify an active meaning with special patterns called **صِيَغُ الْمُبَالَغَةِ** (ṣiyaghu al-mubālaghati). They often describe someone who does an action strongly, repeatedly, or as a stable trait.

## Core idea

The ordinary active participle **كَاتِبٌ** (kātibun) means “writer” or “writing.” Intensive patterns go further: **صَبُورٌ** (ṣabūrun) means very patient, and **غَفَّارٌ** (ghaffārun) means very forgiving or repeatedly forgiving.

## Forms and examples

| Pattern | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **فَعَّالٌ** | **الرَّجُلُ غَفَّارٌ** (ar-rajulu ghaffārun) | the man is very forgiving |
| **فَعُولٌ** | **الْمُعَلِّمُ صَبُورٌ** (al-muʿallimu ṣabūrun) | the teacher is very patient |
| **فَعِيلٌ** | **الْبَاحِثُ عَلِيمٌ** (al-bāḥithu ʿalīmun) | the researcher is very knowledgeable |
| **مِفْعَالٌ** | **الْقَائِدُ مِقْدَامٌ** (al-qāʾidu miqdāmun) | the leader is very bold |
| **فَعِلٌ** | **الطَّفْلُ حَذِرٌ** (aṭ-ṭiflu ḥadhirun) | the child is very cautious |

## Usage notes

Intensive patterns are lexical: you cannot always invent one freely and expect it to sound natural. Many are common adjectives or names of professions and traits. Agreement works like normal adjectives: **امْرَأَةٌ صَبُورَةٌ** (imraʾatun ṣabūratun) — a very patient woman, and **رِجَالٌ صَبُورُونَ** (rijālun ṣabūrūna) — very patient men.

## Practice patterns

Watch for the vowel shape:

- **صَدُوقٌ** (ṣadūqun) — very truthful, on **فَعُولٌ**.
- **سَمِيعٌ** (samīʿun) — very hearing or attentive, on **فَعِيلٌ**.
- **مِعْطَاءٌ** (miʿṭāʾun) — very generous, on a pattern related to **مِفْعَالٌ**.
