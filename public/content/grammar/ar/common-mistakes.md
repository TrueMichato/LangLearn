# Common Learner Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

Arabic rewards noticing small endings and relationships. Many mistakes come from applying an English habit too quickly. Build a pause: ask whether the noun is definite, whether an adjective agrees, and whether a particle changes case.

## Core idea

Most beginner errors are not random. They cluster around **تَعْرِيفٌ** (taʿrīfun) — definiteness, **اِتِّفَاقٌ** (ittifāqun) — agreement, **إِضَافَةٌ** (ʾiḍāfatun) — construct possession, and **حَرْفُ جَرٍّ** (ḥarfu jarrin) — preposition.

## Frequent traps

| Mistake | Better MSA | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **الْبَيْتٌ** (al-baytun) | **الْبَيْتُ** (al-baytu) — the house | The article removes tanwīn. |
| **بَيْتُ الْكَبِيرُ** (baytu l-kabīru) | **الْبَيْتُ الْكَبِيرُ** (al-baytu l-kabīru) — the big house | An adjective follows definiteness. |
| **كِتَابُ الطَّالِبُ** (kitābu ṭ-ṭālibu) | **كِتَابُ الطَّالِبِ** (kitābu ṭ-ṭālibi) — the student's book | The second iḍāfa noun is genitive. |
| **فِي الْبَيْتُ** (fī l-baytu) | **فِي الْبَيْتِ** (fī l-bayti) — in the house | A preposition takes genitive. |
| **الْكُتُبُ جَدِيدُونَ** (al-kutubu jadīdūna) | **الْكُتُبُ جَدِيدَةٌ** (al-kutubu jadīdatun) — the books are new | Non-human plural often agrees as feminine singular. |

## Usage notes

When you see **الْـ** (al-) — the, remove tanwīn. When you see a noun followed by a describing adjective, check gender, number, case, and definiteness: **الْمَدْرَسَةُ الْجَدِيدَةُ** (al-madrasatu l-jadīdatu) — the new school. When you see a preposition, expect genitive: **مَعَ الطَّالِبِ** (maʿa ṭ-ṭālibi) — with the student.

## A quick checking routine

1. Find particles: **فِي** (fī) — in, **مِنْ** (min) — from, **إِلَى** (ʾilā) — to.
2. Mark noun relationships: adjective, iḍāfa, subject, object.
3. Check definiteness and endings last.

> Accuracy comes from a checklist, not from rushing.
