# Question Words Across Dialects (ēh, shū, wēn, laysh…)

MSA question words are stable in writing, while spoken dialects use shorter regional forms. Learning the common forms helps you recognize conversations without replacing formal MSA in careful writing.

## Core idea

Dialect question words vary by country and city. Still, several forms are widespread: إِيهْ (ēh) in Egyptian for what, شُو (shū) in Levantine for what, وَيْنْ (wēn) for where, and لَيْشْ (lēsh or laysh) for why.

## Common correspondences

| Meaning | MSA | Egyptian | Levantine and common regional forms |
|---|---|---|---|
| What | مَاذَا؟ (mādhā) or مَا؟ (mā) | إِيهْ؟ (ēh) | شُو؟ (shū) |
| Where | أَيْنَ؟ (ayna) | فِينْ؟ (fēn) | وَيْنْ؟ (wēn) |
| Why | لِمَاذَا؟ (limādhā) | لِيهْ؟ (lēh) | لَيْشْ؟ (lēsh) |
| How | كَيْفَ؟ (kayfa) | إِزَّايْ؟ (izzāy) | كِيفْ؟ (kīf) |
| When | مَتَى؟ (matā) | إِمْتَى؟ (imtā) | إِيمْتَى؟ (ēmtā) |

## What questions

MSA places مَاذَا (mādhā) or مَا (mā) according to the style and sentence. Dialects often put the question word where the missing information would naturally appear.

- مَاذَا تُرِيدُ؟ (mādhā turīdu) — What do you want? MSA.
- إِنْتَ عَايِزْ إِيهْ؟ (inta ʿāyiz ēh) — What do you want? Egyptian.
- شُو بَدَّكْ؟ (shū baddak) — What do you want? Levantine.

## Where, why, and how

Many dialects use وَيْنْ (wēn) for where and لَيْشْ (lēsh) for why. Egyptian commonly uses فِينْ (fēn) for where, لِيهْ (lēh) for why, and إِزَّايْ (izzāy) for how.

- وَيْنْ بَيْتَكْ؟ (wēn bētak) — Where is your house? Common Levantine-style form.
- لَيْشْ تَأَخَّرْتَ؟ (lēsh taʾakhkharta) — Why were you late? Common Levantine-style form.
- إِزَّايْ أَرُوحْ لِلْمَحَطَّةِ؟ (izzāy arūḥ li-l-maḥaṭṭati) — How do I go to the station? Egyptian-style form.

## Register control

Use dialect forms when speaking a dialect or quoting dialogue. Use MSA forms in formal writing, exams, news, and fully vocalized reading practice.

- Formal: أَيْنَ الْمَحَطَّةُ؟ (ayna al-maḥaṭṭatu) — Where is the station?
- Dialectal: فِينْ الْمَحَطَّةْ؟ (fēn il-maḥaṭṭa) — Where is the station? Egyptian-style form.
