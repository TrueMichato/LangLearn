# Lā Siyyamā: Saying Especially

MSA often uses **لَا سِيَّمَا** (lā siyyamā) to mean especially or in particular. It is common in essays, reports, and formal speech.

## Core idea

The phrase **وَلَا سِيَّمَا** (wa-lā siyyamā) highlights one member of a larger group. It often follows a plural or general noun, then names the item that deserves special attention.

## Forms and examples

| Pattern | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Definite highlighted noun | **أُحِبُّ اللُّغَاتِ، وَلَا سِيَّمَا الْعَرَبِيَّةُ** (uḥibbu al-lughāti, wa-lā siyyamā al-ʿarabiyyatu) | I love languages, especially Arabic. |
| Indefinite highlighted noun | **نُكْرِمُ الضُّيُوفَ، وَلَا سِيَّمَا ضَيْفًا بَعِيدًا** (nukrimu aḍ-ḍuyūfa, wa-lā siyyamā ḍayfan baʿīdan) | We honor guests, especially a distant guest. |
| With a profession | **أُقَدِّرُ الْعُلَمَاءَ، وَلَا سِيَّمَا الطَّبِيبُ الْأَمِينُ** (uqaddiru al-ʿulamāʾa, wa-lā siyyamā aṭ-ṭabību al-amīnu) | I value scholars, especially the trustworthy doctor. |
| With an abstract noun | **نَحْتَاجُ إِلَى الصَّبْرِ، وَلَا سِيَّمَا فِي الشِّدَّةِ** (naḥtāju ilā aṣ-ṣabri, wa-lā siyyamā fī ash-shiddati) | We need patience, especially in hardship. |

## Usage notes

With a definite noun after **لَا سِيَّمَا** (lā siyyamā), formal Arabic allows more than one case analysis, and modern writers often use a nominative form: **وَلَا سِيَّمَا الْعَرَبِيَّةُ** (wa-lā siyyamā al-ʿarabiyyatu). With an indefinite noun, accusative is a common clear learner choice: **وَلَا سِيَّمَا ضَيْفًا بَعِيدًا** (wa-lā siyyamā ḍayfan baʿīdan).

Do not translate it word by word. In English, especially is usually enough.

## Practice patterns

Use this frame when writing formal MSA:

- **أُحِبُّ ...، وَلَا سِيَّمَا ...** (uḥibbu ..., wa-lā siyyamā ...) — I love ..., especially ....
- **نَحْتَاجُ إِلَى ...، وَلَا سِيَّمَا ...** (naḥtāju ilā ..., wa-lā siyyamā ...) — We need ..., especially ....
- **يَجِبُ احْتِرَامُ ...، وَلَا سِيَّمَا ...** (yajibu iḥtirāmu ..., wa-lā siyyamā ...) — It is necessary to respect ..., especially ....
