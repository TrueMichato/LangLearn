# Hamzat al-Waṣl vs Hamzat al-Qaṭʿ

Arabic has two kinds of initial hamza behavior. **هَمْزَةُ الْوَصْلِ** (hamzatu al-waṣli) is a helping vowel heard only when you start from that word, while **هَمْزَةُ الْقَطْعِ** (hamzatu al-qaṭʿi) is a real glottal stop heard both at the start and after another word.

## Core idea

Hamzat al-waṣl connects speech smoothly. In **ٱسْمُ الطَّالِبِ جَمِيلٌ** (ismu aṭ-ṭālibi jamīlun) — the student's name is beautiful, the first vowel is heard because the word begins the phrase. After another word, it drops: **قُلِ ٱسْمَكَ** (quli-smaka) — say your name.

## Forms and examples

| Type | Written sign | Pronunciation rule | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **هَمْزَةُ الْوَصْلِ** (hamzatu al-waṣli) | often **ٱ** or plain initial alif | pronounced only at the beginning | **ٱسْتَمَعَ الطَّالِبُ** (istamaʿa aṭ-ṭālibu) — the student listened |
| connected waṣl | **وَٱسْتَمَعَ** | initial vowel disappears after **وَ** | **وَٱسْتَمَعَ الطَّالِبُ** (wa-stamaʿa aṭ-ṭālibu) — and the student listened |
| **هَمْزَةُ الْقَطْعِ** (hamzatu al-qaṭʿi) | **أَ، إِ، أُ** | always pronounced | **أَكَلَ الْوَلَدُ** (ʾakala al-waladu) — the boy ate |
| connected qaṭʿ | **وَأَكَلَ** | hamza remains after **وَ** | **وَأَكَلَ الْوَلَدُ** (wa-ʾakala al-waladu) — and the boy ate |

## Usage notes

Common waṣl words include the article **ٱلْـ** (al-) — the, some basic nouns such as **ٱسْمٌ** (ismun) — name and **ٱبْنٌ** (ibnun) — son, and many imperatives or derived verb forms such as **ٱكْتُبْ** (uktub) — write and **ٱسْتَغْفَرَ** (istaghfara) — he sought forgiveness. Qaṭʿ appears in many nouns, particles, and Form IV verbs: **إِنَّ** (inna) — indeed, **أَنَا** (anā) — I, and **أَكْرَمَ** (akrama) — he honored.

## Practice patterns

Read aloud and decide whether the first vowel stays:

- **فَٱفْتَحِ الْبَابَ** (fa-ftaḥi al-bāba) — so open the door.
- **فَأَخَذَ الْكِتَابَ** (fa-ʾakhadha al-kitāba) — so he took the book.
- **مِنَ ٱلْمَدِينَةِ** (mina al-madīnati) — from the city.
