# Fractions, Percentages & Telling Time in Words (Tafqit-informed)

Fractions and time expressions reuse ordinary nouns, ordinals, and number agreement. Many forms are fixed phrases, so learn them as chunks.

> In formal writing, Arabic often spells numbers out for clarity. Tafqit-style wording helps you see how the grammar fits together.

## Common fractions

A fraction is **كَسْرٌ** (kasrun) — fraction. The everyday fractions are short nouns.

| Fraction | Arabic | Example |
|---|---|---|
| one half | **نِصْفٌ** (niṣfun) — half | **نِصْفُ سَاعَةٍ** (niṣfu sāʿatin) — half an hour |
| one third | **ثُلُثٌ** (thuluthun) — third | **ثُلُثُ الكِتَابِ** (thuluthu al-kitābi) — a third of the book |
| one quarter | **رُبْعٌ** (rubʿun) — quarter | **رُبْعُ الكَمِّيَّةِ** (rubʿu al-kammiyyati) — a quarter of the amount |
| two thirds | **ثُلُثَانِ** (thuluthāni) — two thirds | **ثُلُثَا الطَّرِيقِ** (thuluthā aṭ-ṭarīqi) — two thirds of the road |
| three quarters | **ثَلَاثَةُ أَرْبَاعٍ** (thalāthatu arbāʿin) — three quarters | **ثَلَاثَةُ أَرْبَاعِ السَّاعَةِ** (thalāthatu arbāʿi as-sāʿati) — three quarters of the hour |

## Percentages

A percentage is **نِسْبَةٌ مِئَوِيَّةٌ** (nisbatun miʾawiyyatun) — percentage. You will see both **فِي المِئَةِ** (fī al-miʾati) — percent, literally “in the hundred,” and **بِالمِئَةِ** (bi-l-miʾati) — percent.

- **خَمْسَةٌ فِي المِئَةِ** (khamsatun fī al-miʾati) — five percent.
- **خَمْسَةٌ وَعِشْرُونَ فِي المِئَةِ** (khamsatun wa-ʿishrūna fī al-miʾati) — twenty-five percent.
- **سَبْعَةٌ وَنِصْفٌ فِي المِئَةِ** (sabʿatun wa-niṣfun fī al-miʾati) — seven and a half percent.

## Telling time

Time uses **السَّاعَةُ** (as-sāʿatu) — the hour/clock. Because **سَاعَةٌ** (sāʿatun) — hour is feminine, hour numbers one through twelve often appear as feminine ordinals.

| Time | Arabic |
|---|---|
| one o'clock | **السَّاعَةُ الوَاحِدَةُ** (as-sāʿatu al-wāḥidatu) |
| two o'clock | **السَّاعَةُ الثَّانِيَةُ** (as-sāʿatu ath-thāniyatu) |
| three fifteen | **السَّاعَةُ الثَّالِثَةُ وَالرُّبْعُ** (as-sāʿatu ath-thālithatu wa-r-rubʿu) |
| three thirty | **السَّاعَةُ الثَّالِثَةُ وَالنِّصْفُ** (as-sāʿatu ath-thālithatu wa-n-niṣfu) |
| four forty-five | **السَّاعَةُ الخَامِسَةُ إِلَّا رُبْعًا** (as-sāʿatu al-khāmisatu illā rubʿan) — a quarter to five |

## Minutes in words

Minutes are **دَقَائِقُ** (daqāʾiqu) — minutes. The number before them follows normal number rules.

- **السَّاعَةُ السَّادِسَةُ وَخَمْسُ دَقَائِقَ** (as-sāʿatu as-sādisatu wa-khamsu daqāʾiqa) — six oh five.
- **السَّاعَةُ السَّابِعَةُ وَعَشْرُ دَقَائِقَ** (as-sāʿatu as-sābiʿatu wa-ʿashru daqāʾiqa) — seven ten.
- **السَّاعَةُ الثَّامِنَةُ وَخَمْسٌ وَعِشْرُونَ دَقِيقَةً** (as-sāʿatu ath-thāminatu wa-khamsun wa-ʿishrūna daqīqatan) — eight twenty-five.
