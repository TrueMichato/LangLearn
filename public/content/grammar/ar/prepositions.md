# Prepositions (fī, ʿalā, min, ilā, maʿa, bi-, li-)

Arabic prepositions are small but powerful. They link nouns to location, direction, source, company, instrument, and purpose. A noun after a preposition is normally genitive.

## Core idea

Core prepositions include **فِي** (fī) — in, **عَلَى** (ʿalā) — on / upon, **مِنْ** (min) — from, **إِلَى** (ilā) — to, **مَعَ** (maʿa) — with, **بِـ** (bi-) — with / by / in, and **لِـ** (li-) — to / for. After them, definite nouns take genitive **ـِ** (-i) or indefinite nouns take **ـٍ** (-in).

## Forms and examples

| Preposition | Example |
|---|---|
| **فِي** (fī) — in | **فِي الْبَيْتِ** (fī al-bayti) — in the house |
| **عَلَى** (ʿalā) — on | **عَلَى الطَّاوِلَةِ** (ʿalā aṭ-ṭāwilati) — on the table |
| **مِنْ** (min) — from | **مِنَ الْمَدْرَسَةِ** (mina al-madrasati) — from the school |
| **إِلَى** (ilā) — to | **إِلَى السُّوقِ** (ilā as-sūqi) — to the market |
| **بِـ** (bi-) — with / by | **بِقَلَمٍ** (biqalamin) — with a pen |
| **لِـ** (li-) — to / for | **لِلطَّالِبِ** (liṭ-ṭālibi) — for the male student |

## Usage notes

Short prepositions attach in writing: **بِـ** (bi-) — with / by and **لِـ** (li-) — to / for are prefixes. Before **الْـ** (al-) — the, **لِـ** (li-) — to / for becomes **لِلْـ** (lil-) — for the.

## Practice patterns

Sentence examples:

- **الْكِتَابُ عَلَى الْمَكْتَبِ** (al-kitābu ʿalā al-maktabi) — the book is on the desk.
- **ذَهَبْتُ إِلَى الْمَدِينَةِ** (dhahabtu ilā al-madīnati) — I went to the city.
- **كَتَبْتُ بِقَلَمٍ** (katabtu biqalamin) — I wrote with a pen.
