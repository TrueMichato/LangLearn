# Exception Verbs: khalā, ʿadā, ḥāshā

خَلَا (khalā), عَدَا (ʿadā), and حَاشَا (ḥāshā) can introduce exceptions meaning “except” or “apart from.” They are special because they may behave like verbs or like prepositions.

## Core idea

When these words act as verbs, the exception after them is accusative. When they act as prepositions, the exception after them is genitive. With مَا before خَلَا or عَدَا, the verbal reading is required.

## Forms and examples

| Tool | Verbal example | Prepositional example | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| خَلَا (khalā) | حَضَرَ الطُّلَّابُ خَلَا طَالِبًا (ḥaḍara aṭ-ṭullābu khalā ṭāliban) — the students attended except one student | حَضَرَ الطُّلَّابُ خَلَا طَالِبٍ (ḥaḍara aṭ-ṭullābu khalā ṭālibin) — the students attended except one student | Accusative or genitive is possible |
| عَدَا (ʿadā) | قَرَأْتُ الصُّحُفَ عَدَا صَحِيفَةً (qaraʾtu aṣ-ṣuḥufa ʿadā ṣaḥīfatan) — I read the newspapers except one newspaper | قَرَأْتُ الصُّحُفَ عَدَا صَحِيفَةٍ (qaraʾtu aṣ-ṣuḥufa ʿadā ṣaḥīfatin) — I read the newspapers except one newspaper | Accusative or genitive is possible |
| حَاشَا (ḥāshā) | أُكْرِمُ الْجَمِيعَ حَاشَا ظَالِمًا (ukrimu al-jamīʿa ḥāshā ẓāliman) — I honor everyone except an oppressor | أُكْرِمُ الْجَمِيعَ حَاشَا ظَالِمٍ (ukrimu al-jamīʿa ḥāshā ẓālimin) — I honor everyone except an oppressor | Often treated as a particle in modern explanation |
| مَا خَلَا (mā khalā) | حَضَرُوا مَا خَلَا طَالِبًا (ḥaḍarū mā khalā ṭāliban) — they attended except one student | — | With مَا, use accusative |

## Usage notes

These exception tools are more formal than simple إِلَّا (illā). In careful reading, the final case ending tells you which analysis is being used: طَالِبًا (ṭāliban) as an object of a verb, or طَالِبٍ (ṭālibin) after a prepositional use.

## Practice patterns

Compare the endings aloud:

- سَافَرَ الْأَصْدِقَاءُ عَدَا صَدِيقًا (sāfara al-aṣdiqāʾu ʿadā ṣadīqan) — the friends traveled except one friend.
- سَافَرَ الْأَصْدِقَاءُ عَدَا صَدِيقٍ (sāfara al-aṣdiqāʾu ʿadā ṣadīqin) — the friends traveled except one friend.
- نَجَحُوا مَا عَدَا طَالِبًا (najaḥū mā ʿadā ṭāliban) — they succeeded except one student.
