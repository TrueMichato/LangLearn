# Collective & Unit Nouns (ism al-jins & ism al-waḥda)

Arabic often has one word for a class or mass, then a related word for a single item from that class. This pair is useful for food, plants, animals, and many everyday nouns.

## Core idea

**اِسْمُ الْجِنْسِ** (ism al-jins) — generic or collective noun names a kind, material, or group. **اِسْمُ الْوَحْدَةِ** (ism al-waḥda) — unit noun names one individual item. A common way to make the unit noun is to add **تَاءٌ مَرْبُوطَةٌ** (tāʾ marbūṭa) — tied tāʾ.

## Forms and examples

| Collective | Unit noun |
|---|---|
| **تَمْرٌ** (tamrun) — dates as a collective | **تَمْرَةٌ** (tamratun) — one date |
| **شَجَرٌ** (shajarun) — trees as a collective | **شَجَرَةٌ** (shajaratun) — one tree |
| **سَمَكٌ** (samakun) — fish as a collective | **سَمَكَةٌ** (samakatun) — one fish |
| **بَقَرٌ** (baqarun) — cattle | **بَقَرَةٌ** (baqaratun) — one cow |

## Usage notes

Use the collective when you mean the kind or material in general: **أُحِبُّ التَّمْرَ** (uḥibbu at-tamra) — I like dates. Use the unit noun when you count or point to one item: **أَكَلْتُ تَمْرَةً وَاحِدَةً** (akaltu tamratan wāḥidatan) — I ate one date.

The unit noun can take normal singular agreement: **هٰذِهِ شَجَرَةٌ طَوِيلَةٌ** (hādhihi shajaratun ṭawīlatun) — this is a tall tree. The collective can often behave like a singular idea: **الشَّجَرُ كَثِيفٌ** (ash-shajaru kathīfun) — the trees are dense.

## Collective, unit, and plural

The unit noun is not always the same as an ordinary plural. **شَجَرٌ** (shajarun) — trees collectively, **شَجَرَةٌ** (shajaratun) — one tree, and **أَشْجَارٌ** (ashjārun) — trees as countable plural can all appear in MSA. The choice depends on whether you mean a class, one item, or several countable items.

- **زَرَعَ الْفَلَّاحُ شَجَرًا** (zaraʿa al-fallāḥu shajaran) — the farmer planted trees.
- **زَرَعَ الْفَلَّاحُ شَجَرَةً** (zaraʿa al-fallāḥu shajaratan) — the farmer planted one tree.
- **زَرَعَ الْفَلَّاحُ أَشْجَارًا** (zaraʿa al-fallāḥu ashjāran) — the farmer planted several trees.
