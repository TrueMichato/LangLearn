# Predicate Types in Nominal Sentences

A nominal sentence, **جُمْلَةٌ اِسْمِيَّةٌ** (jumlatun ismiyyatun), has a topic, **مُبْتَدَأٌ** (mubtadaʾun), and a predicate, **خَبَرٌ** (khabarun). The predicate does not have to be one adjective or noun; it can be a phrase or a whole sentence.

## Core idea

The predicate tells us something about the topic. In **الْبَيْتُ كَبِيرٌ** (al-baytu kabīrun) — the house is big, the predicate is one word. In **الْكِتَابُ عَلَى الطَّاوِلَةِ** (al-kitābu ʿalā aṭ-ṭāwilati) — the book is on the table, the predicate is a prepositional phrase.

## Forms and examples

| Predicate type | Arabic name | Link to topic | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| single word | **خَبَرٌ مُفْرَدٌ** (khabarun mufradun) | agrees as needed | **الطَّرِيقُ طَوِيلٌ** (aṭ-ṭarīqu ṭawīlun) — the road is long |
| nominal sentence | **جُمْلَةٌ اِسْمِيَّةٌ** (jumlatun ismiyyatun) | often has a pronoun link | **الطَّالِبُ أَبُوهُ مُهَنْدِسٌ** (aṭ-ṭālibu abūhu muhandisun) — the student's father is an engineer |
| verbal sentence | **جُمْلَةٌ فِعْلِيَّةٌ** (jumlatun fiʿliyyatun) | verb may contain the link | **الطَّائِرُ يُغَرِّدُ** (aṭ-ṭāʾiru yugharridu) — the bird is singing |
| semi-sentence | **شِبْهُ جُمْلَةٍ** (shibhu jumlatin) | preposition or adverbial phrase | **الْقَلَمُ فَوْقَ الْمَكْتَبِ** (al-qalamu fawqa al-maktabi) — the pen is above the desk |

## Usage notes

When the predicate is a whole sentence, Arabic usually needs a link back to the topic. In **الطَّالِبُ أَبُوهُ مُهَنْدِسٌ** (aṭ-ṭālibu abūhu muhandisun) — the student's father is an engineer, the attached pronoun **ـهُ** (hu) — his links the inner sentence to **الطَّالِبُ** (aṭ-ṭālibu) — the student.

## Practice patterns

Identify the predicate in each nominal sentence:

- **الْحَدِيقَةُ جَمِيلَةٌ** (al-ḥadīqatu jamīlatun) — the garden is beautiful.
- **الْمُدِيرُ يَتَكَلَّمُ بِهُدُوءٍ** (al-mudīru yatakallamu bihudūʾin) — the director speaks calmly.
- **السَّيَّارَةُ أَمَامَ الْبَيْتِ** (as-sayyāratu amāma al-bayti) — the car is in front of the house.
