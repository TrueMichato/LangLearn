# Agreement Rules: Gender, Number & the Non-Human Plural

Arabic agreement is precise, but one rule makes reading much easier: non-human plurals are often treated as feminine singular.

> Human plurals usually get real plural agreement. Non-human plurals often get feminine singular agreement, especially with adjectives and demonstratives.

## What agrees?

A **صِفَةٌ** (ṣifatun) — adjective commonly agrees with the noun in gender, number, definiteness, and case.

| Noun | Adjective phrase | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **طَالِبٌ** (ṭālibun) — male student | **طَالِبٌ مُجْتَهِدٌ** (ṭālibun mujtahidun) — a hardworking student | masculine singular |
| **طَالِبَةٌ** (ṭālibatun) — female student | **طَالِبَةٌ مُجْتَهِدَةٌ** (ṭālibatun mujtahidatun) — a hardworking female student | feminine singular |
| **طُلَّابٌ** (ṭullābun) — students | **طُلَّابٌ مُجْتَهِدُونَ** (ṭullābun mujtahidūna) — hardworking students | human masculine plural |
| **طَالِبَاتٌ** (ṭālibātun) — female students | **طَالِبَاتٌ مُجْتَهِدَاتٌ** (ṭālibātun mujtahidātun) — hardworking female students | human feminine plural |

## The non-human plural rule

A **جَمْعٌ لِغَيْرِ العَاقِلِ** (jamʿun li-ghayri al-ʿāqili) — non-human plural is often treated as feminine singular for agreement.

- **الكُتُبُ الجَدِيدَةُ** (al-kutubu al-jadīdatu) — the new books.
- **هَذِهِ البُيُوتُ كَبِيرَةٌ** (hādhihi al-buyūtu kabīratun) — these houses are big.
- **الأَبْوَابُ مَفْتُوحَةٌ** (al-abwābu maftūḥatun) — the doors are open.
- **الأَوْرَاقُ سَقَطَتْ** (al-awrāqu saqaṭat) — the papers fell.

## Human plural contrast

For humans, use plural agreement when the group is clearly human.

| Human noun | Agreement |
|---|---|
| **المُهَنْدِسُونَ الجُدُدُ** (al-muhandisūna al-jududu) — the new engineers | masculine plural |
| **المُعَلِّمَاتُ الجَدِيدَاتُ** (al-muʿallimātu al-jadīdātu) — the new female teachers | feminine plural |
| **هَؤُلَاءِ أَطِبَّاءُ مَاهِرُونَ** (hāʾulāʾi aṭibbāʾu māhirūna) — these are skilled doctors | human plural demonstrative |

## Broken plurals and meaning

The shape of the plural is not the main issue; meaning is. **مَسَاجِدُ** (masājidu) — mosques is non-human, so **مَسَاجِدُ قَدِيمَةٌ** (masājidu qadīmatun) — old mosques. **رِجَالٌ** (rijālun) — men is human, so **رِجَالٌ أَقْوِيَاءُ** (rijālun aqwiyāʾu) — strong men.
