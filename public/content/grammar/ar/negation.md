# Negation (lā, mā, lam, lan, laysa)

Arabic has several negation tools. Each one has a job, and learning the job is easier than trying to translate every “not” the same way.

## Core idea

Use **لَا** (lā) — not / no for present-tense verbs and general negation, **مَا** (mā) — not for past in many simple MSA sentences, **لَمْ** (lam) — did not before jussive present, **لَنْ** (lan) — will not before subjunctive present, and **لَيْسَ** (laysa) — is not for nominal sentences.

## Forms and examples

| Negator | Example |
|---|---|
| **لَا** (lā) — not | **لَا أَفْهَمُ** (lā afhamu) — I do not understand |
| **مَا** (mā) — not | **مَا ذَهَبْتُ** (mā dhahabtu) — I did not go |
| **لَمْ** (lam) — did not | **لَمْ أَذْهَبْ** (lam adhhab) — I did not go |
| **لَنْ** (lan) — will not | **لَنْ أَذْهَبَ** (lan adh-haba) — I will not go |
| **لَيْسَ** (laysa) — is not | **لَيْسَ الْبَيْتُ بَعِيدًا** (laysa al-baytu baʿīdan) — the house is not far |

## Usage notes

After **لَمْ** (lam) — did not, the verb is jussive: **أَذْهَبْ** (adhhab) — I go, jussive. After **لَنْ** (lan) — will not, the verb is subjunctive: **أَذْهَبَ** (adh-haba) — I go, subjunctive.

## Practice patterns

Kind shortcut:

- Present not: **لَا أَدْرُسُ** (lā adrusu) — I do not study.
- Past not: **لَمْ أَدْرُسْ** (lam adrus) — I did not study.
- Future not: **لَنْ أَدْرُسَ** (lan adrusa) — I will not study.
