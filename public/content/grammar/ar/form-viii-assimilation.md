# Form VIII Assimilation with tāʾ

Form VIII normally has an inserted **ت** after the first root consonant. With some roots, that ت changes its sound to fit the neighboring consonant.

## Core idea

The basic Form VIII pattern is **اِفْتَعَلَ** (iftaʿala). When the first root consonant is emphatic, dental, or weak, the inserted **ت** may become **ط**, **د**, or merge into a doubled consonant.

## Forms and examples

| Root type | Form VIII | Imperfect | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initial wāw | **اِتَّصَلَ** (ittaṣala) | **يَتَّصِلُ** (yattaṣilu) | **اِتَّصَلَ الطَّالِبُ بِالْمُعَلِّمِ** (ittaṣala aṭ-ṭālibu bi-l-muʿallimi) — the student contacted the teacher. |
| Emphatic ṣād | **اِصْطَبَرَ** (iṣṭabara) | **يَصْطَبِرُ** (yaṣṭabiru) | **اِصْطَبَرَ الْمُؤْمِنُ عَلَى الشِّدَّةِ** (iṣṭabara al-muʾminu ʿalā ash-shiddati) — the believer endured hardship. |
| Emphatic ḍād | **اِضْطَرَبَ** (iḍṭaraba) | **يَضْطَرِبُ** (yaḍṭaribu) | **اِضْطَرَبَ الْبَحْرُ** (iḍṭaraba al-baḥru) — the sea became turbulent. |
| Initial zāy | **اِزْدَادَ** (izdāda) | **يَزْدَادُ** (yazdādu) | **اِزْدَادَ الْعِلْمُ** (izdāda al-ʿilmu) — knowledge increased. |

## Usage notes

Do not try to rebuild these forms from English each time. Recognize the common written clusters: **اِصْطَـ** (iṣṭa-), **اِضْطَـ** (iḍṭa-), **اِزْدَـ** (izda-), and **اِتَّـ** (itta-). They still belong to Form VIII.

The meaning often has a reflexive, middle, or acquired sense, but the exact meaning depends on the root: **اِتَّصَلَ** (ittaṣala) means contacted, while **اِزْدَادَ** (izdāda) means increased.

## Practice patterns

Read these aloud and identify the changed ت:

- **اِتَّفَقَ الطَّرَفَانِ** (ittafaqa aṭ-ṭarafāni) — the two sides agreed.
- **اِصْطَفَى اللهُ الرَّسُولَ** (iṣṭafā allāhu ar-rasūla) — God chose the messenger.
- **اِزْدَحَمَ الشَّارِعُ** (izdaḥama ash-shāriʿu) — the street became crowded.
- **اِدَّعَى الشَّاهِدُ الْحَقَّ** (iddaʿā ash-shāhidu al-ḥaqqa) — the witness claimed the truth.
