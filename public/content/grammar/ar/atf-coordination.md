# Coordination with ʿAṭf: Shared Case and Meaning

اَلْعَطْفُ (al-ʿaṭfu) links one word or phrase to another with a coordinating particle. The coordinated word, اَلْمَعْطُوفُ (al-maʿṭūfu), usually follows the case of the word before it.

## Core idea

If the first noun is nominative, the coordinated noun is nominative. If it is accusative or genitive, the coordinated noun follows. This is why endings change in careful MSA.

## Forms and examples

| Case of first noun | Coordinated example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative | جَاءَ الطَّالِبُ وَالْمُعَلِّمُ (jāʾa aṭ-ṭālibu wa-l-muʿallimu) — the student and the teacher came | Both are subjects |
| Accusative | رَأَيْتُ الطَّالِبَ وَالْمُعَلِّمَ (raʾaytu aṭ-ṭāliba wa-l-muʿallima) — I saw the student and the teacher | Both are objects |
| Genitive | مَرَرْتُ بِالطَّالِبِ وَالْمُعَلِّمِ (marartu bi-ṭ-ṭālibi wa-l-muʿallimi) — I passed by the student and the teacher | Both are governed by بِـ |
| After إِنَّ | إِنَّ الطَّالِبَ وَالْمُعَلِّمَ حَاضِرَانِ (inna aṭ-ṭāliba wa-l-muʿallima ḥāḍirāni) — the student and the teacher are present | Both follow إِنَّ in accusative |

## Usage notes

The particle وَ (wa) simply joins, فَـ (fa-) suggests sequence or immediate result, and ثُمَّ (thumma) suggests sequence with delay. The syntax of shared case is separate from the exact meaning of each particle.

## Practice patterns

Read the first noun, then copy its syntactic role to the second:

- وَصَلَتِ الْمُدِيرَةُ فَالْمُوَظَّفَةُ (waṣalati al-mudīratu fa-l-muwaẓẓafatu) — the director arrived, then the employee.
- سَأَلْتُ الْمُدِيرَ ثُمَّ الْمُوَظَّفَ (saʾaltu al-mudīra thumma al-muwaẓẓafa) — I asked the director, then the employee.
- سَلَّمْتُ عَلَى الْمُدِيرِ وَالْمُوَظَّفِ (sallamtu ʿalā al-mudīri wa-l-muwaẓẓafi) — I greeted the director and the employee.
