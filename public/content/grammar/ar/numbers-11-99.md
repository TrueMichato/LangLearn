# Numbers 11–99 & Their Endings

Numbers from eleven to ninety-nine combine agreement, fixed endings, and **تَمْيِيزٌ** (tamyīzun) — specification. The counted noun usually becomes singular accusative.

> Once you pass ten, the noun no longer stays plural genitive. It becomes a singular accusative “specifier.”

## 11 and 12

**أَحَدَ عَشَرَ** (aḥada ʿashara) — eleven masculine and **إِحْدَى عَشْرَةَ** (iḥdā ʿashrata) — eleven feminine agree with the counted noun. The number parts are mostly indeclinable in ordinary parsing.

| Meaning | Arabic |
|---|---|
| eleven books | **أَحَدَ عَشَرَ كِتَابًا** (aḥada ʿashara kitāban) |
| eleven cars | **إِحْدَى عَشْرَةَ سَيَّارَةً** (iḥdā ʿashrata sayyāratan) |
| twelve books, nominative | **اثْنَا عَشَرَ كِتَابًا** (ithnā ʿashara kitāban) |
| twelve books, accusative/genitive | **اثْنَيْ عَشَرَ كِتَابًا** (ithnay ʿashara kitāban) |
| twelve cars, nominative | **اثْنَتَا عَشْرَةَ سَيَّارَةً** (ithnatā ʿashrata sayyāratan) |

## 13 through 19

For **ثَلَاثَةَ عَشَرَ** (thalāthata ʿashara) — thirteen through **تِسْعَةَ عَشَرَ** (tisʿata ʿashara) — nineteen, the unit part follows the old polarity rule, while the ten part agrees with the counted noun.

| Counted noun | Phrase | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **كِتَابٌ** (kitābun) — book, masculine | **ثَلَاثَةَ عَشَرَ كِتَابًا** (thalāthata ʿashara kitāban) | unit opposite, ten masculine |
| **سَيَّارَةٌ** (sayyāratun) — car, feminine | **ثَلَاثَ عَشْرَةَ سَيَّارَةً** (thalātha ʿashrata sayyāratan) | unit opposite, ten feminine |
| **طَالِبٌ** (ṭālibun) — student | **تِسْعَةَ عَشَرَ طَالِبًا** (tisʿata ʿashara ṭāliban) | singular accusative tamyīz |

## The tens: 20, 30, 40...

The tens behave like sound masculine plurals in case:

| Case | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| nominative | **عِشْرُونَ** (ʿishrūna) — twenty | **جَاءَ عِشْرُونَ طَالِبًا** (jāʾa ʿishrūna ṭāliban) — twenty students came |
| accusative/genitive | **عِشْرِينَ** (ʿishrīna) — twenty | **رَأَيْتُ عِشْرِينَ طَالِبًا** (raʾaytu ʿishrīna ṭāliban) — I saw twenty students |
| with preposition | **عِشْرِينَ** (ʿishrīna) — twenty | **مَعَ عِشْرِينَ طَالِبًا** (maʿa ʿishrīna ṭāliban) — with twenty students |

## 21 through 99

Compound numbers use **وَ** (wa-) — and. The unit comes first, then the tens. The counted noun remains singular accusative.

- **وَاحِدٌ وَعِشْرُونَ يَوْمًا** (wāḥidun wa-ʿishrūna yawman) — twenty-one days.
- **اثْنَانِ وَثَلَاثُونَ شَهْرًا** (ithnāni wa-thalāthūna shahran) — thirty-two months.
- **خَمْسَةٌ وَأَرْبَعُونَ كِتَابًا** (khamsatun wa-arbaʿūna kitāban) — forty-five books.
- **خَمْسٌ وَأَرْبَعُونَ سَيَّارَةً** (khamsun wa-arbaʿūna sayyāratan) — forty-five cars.
