# Likening & Comparison Structures (ka-, mithl, ka-anna)

Arabic comparison is not only about more and most. It also has compact ways to say like, similar to, and as if. Three common tools are كَـ (ka-) — like / as, مِثْلَ (mithla) — like / the same as, and كَأَنَّ (ka-anna) — as if.

## Core idea

The prefix كَـ (ka-) is a preposition, so the noun after it is genitive. It attaches directly in writing.

- اَلْعِلْمُ كَالنُّورِ (al-ʿilmu ka-n-nūri) — knowledge is like light.
- وَجْهُهَا كَالْقَمَرِ (wajhuhā ka-l-qamari) — her face is like the moon.
- يَجْرِي الْوَلَدُ كَالرِّيحِ (yajrī al-waladu ka-r-rīḥi) — the boy runs like the wind.

## Using mithl

مِثْلَ (mithla) is a noun meaning likeness or equivalent. It can take different case endings according to its role, but its complement is genitive because it behaves like an iḍāfa.

- اَلْوَلَدُ شُجَاعٌ مِثْلَ أَبِيهِ (al-waladu shujāʿun mithla abīhi) — the boy is brave like his father.
- هٰذَا الْقَلَمُ مِثْلُ ذٰلِكَ الْقَلَمِ (hādhā al-qalamu mithlu dhālika al-qalami) — this pen is like that pen.
- لَيْسَ هٰذَا مِثْلَ ذٰلِكَ (laysa hādhā mithla dhālika) — this is not like that.

## As if with ka-anna

كَأَنَّ (ka-anna) means as if. It belongs to the family of إِنَّ (inna), so its noun is accusative and its predicate is usually nominative.

- كَأَنَّ الْقَمَرَ مِصْبَاحٌ (ka-anna al-qamara miṣbāḥun) — it is as if the moon were a lamp.
- كَأَنَّ الْبَيْتَ قَلْعَةٌ (ka-anna al-bayta qalʿatun) — the house is as if it were a fortress.
- كَأَنَّ صَوْتَهُ بَحْرٌ هَادِئٌ (ka-anna ṣawtahu baḥrun hādiʾun) — his voice is as if it were a calm sea.

## Practice patterns

Use كَـ (ka-) for quick similes, مِثْلَ (mithla) when you want a fuller noun meaning like or equal to, and كَأَنَّ (ka-anna) when the comparison imagines a situation.

- اَلْمُعَلِّمُ كَالْبَحْرِ فِي الْعِلْمِ (al-muʿallimu ka-l-baḥri fī al-ʿilmi) — the teacher is like the sea in knowledge.
- هٰذِهِ الْمَدِينَةُ مِثْلُ قَرْيَةٍ كَبِيرَةٍ (hādhihi al-madīnatu mithlu qaryatin kabīratin) — this city is like a large village.
- كَأَنَّ النُّجُومَ عُيُونٌ صَغِيرَةٌ (ka-anna an-nujūma ʿuyūnun ṣaghīratun) — it is as if the stars were small eyes.
