# The Object of Accompaniment (al-Mafʿūl Maʿahu)

The object of accompaniment, **الْمَفْعُولُ مَعَهُ** (al-mafʿūlu maʿahu), is a noun after the wāw of accompaniment. It means that something happened together with, alongside, or at the same time as another thing.

## Core idea

In ordinary coordination, **وَ** (wa) — and links two items that share the same role. With accompaniment, **وَ** means “with,” and the noun after it is usually accusative. In **سَافَرْتُ وَطُلُوعَ الْفَجْرِ** (sāfartu wa-ṭulūʿa al-fajri) — I traveled at daybreak, the rising of dawn is not another traveler; it accompanies the action.

## Forms and examples

| Pattern | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Verb + subject + وَ + accusative noun | **سَارَ الطَّالِبُ وَالنَّهْرَ** (sāra aṭ-ṭālibu wa-n-nahra) | the student walked along the river |
| Verb + pronoun + وَ + time/event noun | **جِئْتُ وَغُرُوبَ الشَّمْسِ** (jiʾtu wa-ghurūba ash-shamsi) | I came at sunset |
| Coordination, not accompaniment | **جَاءَ زَيْدٌ وَعَمْرٌو** (jāʾa zaydun wa-ʿamrun) | Zayd and ʿAmr came |
| Accompaniment with a non-actor | **وَصَلْنَا وَاشْتِدَادَ الْمَطَرِ** (waṣalnā wa-shtidāda al-maṭari) | we arrived as the rain intensified |

## Usage notes

A quick test: ask whether the noun after **وَ** can logically perform the same verb. In **جَاءَ زَيْدٌ وَعَمْرٌو** (jāʾa zaydun wa-ʿamrun), both people can come, so it is coordination. In **جِئْتُ وَغُرُوبَ الشَّمْسِ** (jiʾtu wa-ghurūba ash-shamsi), sunset cannot “come” as a person comes, so the phrase is accompaniment.

## Practice patterns

Try reading **وَ** in two ways:

- **رَجَعَ الْأَبُ وَالِابْنُ** (rajaʿa al-abu wa-l-ibnu) — the father and the son returned.
- **رَجَعَ الْأَبُ وَطُلُوعَ الْقَمَرِ** (rajaʿa al-abu wa-ṭulūʿa al-qamari) — the father returned as the moon rose.
- **قَرَأْتُ وَهُدُوءَ اللَّيْلِ** (qaraʾtu wa-hudūʾa al-layli) — I read during the stillness of the night.
