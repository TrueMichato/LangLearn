# The Ten Verb Forms (al-Awzān)

Arabic verb forms are patterns that reshape a root’s meaning. You do not need to master all ten immediately; recognize that extra letters and doubled consonants often carry predictable shades of meaning.

## Core idea

Traditional Form I through Form X patterns are often shown with the model root **ف-ع-ل** (f-ʿ-l) — doing. For example, **فَعَلَ** (faʿala) — Form I pattern is basic, while **فَعَّلَ** (faʿʿala) — Form II pattern often intensifies or causes.

## Forms and examples

| Form | Pattern | Typical meaning clue |
|---|---|---|
| I | **فَعَلَ** (faʿala) — he did | basic action |
| II | **فَعَّلَ** (faʿʿala) — he caused / intensified | teaching, making do |
| III | **فَاعَلَ** (fāʿala) — he interacted | mutual action |
| IV | **أَفْعَلَ** (afʿala) — he caused / entered state | causative |
| V | **تَفَعَّلَ** (tafaʿʿala) — he did reflexively | related to Form II |
| VI | **تَفَاعَلَ** (tafāʿala) — he interacted mutually | related to Form III |
| X | **اِسْتَفْعَلَ** (istafʿala) — he sought / considered | seeking / requesting |

## Usage notes

Real examples help: **عَلِمَ** (ʿalima) — he knew, **عَلَّمَ** (ʿallama) — he taught, and **تَعَلَّمَ** (taʿallama) — he learned. The doubled **لّ** (ll) and the **تَـ** (ta-) reshape the root.

## Practice patterns

More high-frequency forms:

- **دَرَسَ** (darasa) — he studied; **دَرَّسَ** (darrasa) — he taught.
- **قَابَلَ** (qābala) — he met / interviewed.
- **اِسْتَعْمَلَ** (istaʿmala) — he used.

Notice patterns first; details can deepen over time.
