# The Five Nouns (al-asmāʾ al-khamsa: abū, akhū, ḥamū, fū, dhū)

The **الأَسْمَاءُ الخَمْسَةُ** (al-asmāʾu al-khamsatu) — five nouns are a small classical set whose case endings appear as long vowels instead of short final vowels.

> They are rare as a group, but the words “father” and “brother” are common enough that the pattern is worth learning early.

## The set

| Noun | Transliteration | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **أَبٌ** (abun) | abun | father |
| **أَخٌ** (akhun) | akhun | brother |
| **حَمٌ** (ḥamun) | ḥamun | father-in-law or male in-law |
| **فَمٌ / فُو** (famun / fū) | famun / fū | mouth |
| **ذُو** (dhū) | dhū | possessor of |

## Case with long vowels

When these nouns are singular, not diminutive, and in iḍāfa, they often show case with long vowels: **وَاوٌ** (wāwun) — wāw for nominative, **أَلِفٌ** (alifun) — alif for accusative, and **يَاءٌ** (yāʾun) — yāʾ for genitive.

| Case | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| nominative | **جَاءَ أَبُوكَ** (jāʾa abūka) | your father came |
| accusative | **رَأَيْتُ أَبَاكَ** (raʾaytu abāka) | I saw your father |
| genitive | **مَرَرْتُ بِأَبِيكَ** (marartu bi-abīka) | I passed by your father |

## ذُو (dhū) — possessor of

**ذُو** (dhū) — possessor of is used in elevated or formal style. It agrees for case like the other five nouns and is followed by a genitive noun.

- **رَجُلٌ ذُو عِلْمٍ** (rajulun dhū ʿilmin) — a man of knowledge.
- **رَأَيْتُ رَجُلًا ذَا عِلْمٍ** (raʾaytu rajulan dhā ʿilmin) — I saw a man of knowledge.
- **تَحَدَّثْتُ مَعَ رَجُلٍ ذِي عِلْمٍ** (taḥaddathtu maʿa rajulin dhī ʿilmin) — I spoke with a man of knowledge.

## Conditions and caveats

The special endings are most visible when the noun is:

- singular, as in **أَبُوكَ** (abūka) — your father;
- not a diminutive, so not **أُبَيٌّ** (ubayyun) — little father;
- in iḍāfa to something other than the speaker's **يَاءُ المُتَكَلِّمِ** (yāʾu al-mutakallimi) — first-person yāʾ.

If the conditions are not met, ordinary case endings may appear: **هَذَا أَبٌ طَيِّبٌ** (hādhā abun ṭayyibun) — this is a good father.
