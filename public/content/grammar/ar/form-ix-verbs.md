# Verb Form IX for Colors and Defects

Form IX, **اِفْعَلَّ** (ifʿalla), is a specialized derived verb form. It most often describes becoming a color or acquiring a visible quality or defect.

## Core idea

Form IX is usually intransitive: the subject itself becomes red, green, white, black, or similar. **اِحْمَرَّ وَجْهُهُ** (iḥmarra wajhuhu) means “his face became red,” not “he reddened something.”

## Forms and examples

| Root/quality | Form IX past | Imperfect | Verbal noun | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| red, **ح-م-ر** | **اِحْمَرَّ** (iḥmarra) | **يَحْمَرُّ** (yaḥmarru) | **اِحْمِرَارٌ** (iḥmirārun) | **اِحْمَرَّتِ الْوَرْدَةُ** (iḥmarrati al-wardatu) — the rose became red |
| green, **خ-ض-ر** | **اِخْضَرَّ** (ikhḍarra) | **يَخْضَرُّ** (yakhḍarru) | **اِخْضِرَارٌ** (ikhḍirārun) | **اِخْضَرَّتِ الْحَدِيقَةُ** (ikhḍarrati al-ḥadīqatu) — the garden became green |
| white, **ب-ي-ض** | **اِبْيَضَّ** (ibyaḍḍa) | **يَبْيَضُّ** (yabyaḍḍu) | **اِبْيِضَاضٌ** (ibyiḍāḍun) | **اِبْيَضَّ الثَّوْبُ** (ibyaḍḍa ath-thawbu) — the garment became white |
| black, **س-و-د** | **اِسْوَدَّ** (iswadda) | **يَسْوَدُّ** (yaswaddu) | **اِسْوِدَادٌ** (iswidādun) | **اِسْوَدَّ اللَّيْلُ** (iswadda al-laylu) — the night became black |

## Usage notes

The doubled final consonant is a key sign: **اِحْمَرَّ** (iḥmarra), **اِسْوَدَّ** (iswadda), **اِعْوَجَّ** (iʿwajja) — became crooked. Because Form IX is usually intransitive, use a causative form or another verb if someone makes something change color.

## Practice patterns

Change the subject and keep the Form IX meaning:

- **اِصْفَرَّتِ الْوَرَقَةُ** (iṣfarrati al-waraqatu) — the leaf became yellow.
- **اِعْوَجَّ الطَّرِيقُ** (iʿwajja aṭ-ṭarīqu) — the road became crooked.
- **اِبْيَضَّ شَعْرُهُ** (ibyaḍḍa shaʿruhu) — his hair turned white.
