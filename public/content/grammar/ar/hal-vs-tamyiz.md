# Ḥāl vs Tamyīz: Two Accusatives Compared

Both **حَالٌ** (ḥālun) — circumstantial accusative and **تَمْيِيزٌ** (tamyīzun) — specification are often indefinite accusatives. They answer different questions.

> Ḥāl tells the state of someone or something during an action. Tamyīz clarifies an unclear measure, number, or quality.

## Ḥāl: state or circumstance

The **حَالٌ** (ḥālun) — state describes how the subject or object is at the time of the action. The person or thing described is **صَاحِبُ الحَالِ** (ṣāḥibu al-ḥāli) — the possessor of the state.

- **جَاءَ الطَّالِبُ مُبْتَسِمًا** (jāʾa aṭ-ṭālibu mubtasiman) — the student came smiling.
- **رَأَيْتُ الوَلَدَ نَائِمًا** (raʾaytu al-walada nāʾiman) — I saw the boy sleeping.
- **دَخَلَتِ المُدَرِّسَةُ مُسْرِعَةً** (dakhalati al-mudarrisatu musriʿatan) — the teacher entered quickly/in a hurry.

## Tamyīz: specification

The **تَمْيِيزٌ** (tamyīzun) — specification clarifies what a number, measure, comparative, or vague quality refers to.

| Trigger | Example | What is clarified? |
|---|---|---|
| number | **عِنْدِي عِشْرُونَ كِتَابًا** (ʿindī ʿishrūna kitāban) — I have twenty books | twenty what? |
| measure | **اشْتَرَيْتُ كِيلُوغْرَامًا عِنَبًا** (ishtaraytu kīlūghrāman ʿinaban) — I bought a kilogram of grapes | a kilogram of what? |
| comparative | **هِيَ أَكْثَرُ مِنِّي صَبْرًا** (hiya aktharu minnī ṣabran) — she is more patient than I am | more in what respect? |
| vague quality | **طَابَ المَكَانُ هَوَاءً** (ṭāba al-makānu hawāʾan) — the place is pleasant in air | pleasant in what respect? |

## Side-by-side test

If the accusative describes a participant, it is probably ḥāl. If it completes the meaning of a quantity or comparative, it is probably tamyīz.

| Sentence | Accusative | Type |
|---|---|---|
| **قَرَأْتُ الكِتَابَ جَالِسًا** (qaraʾtu al-kitāba jālisan) — I read the book sitting | **جَالِسًا** (jālisan) — sitting | ḥāl |
| **قَرَأْتُ عِشْرِينَ صَفْحَةً** (qaraʾtu ʿishrīna ṣafḥatan) — I read twenty pages | **صَفْحَةً** (ṣafḥatan) — pages | tamyīz |
