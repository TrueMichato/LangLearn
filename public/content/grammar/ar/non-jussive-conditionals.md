# Non-Jussive Conditionals: lammā and kullamā

Not every Arabic conditional particle makes the verb jussive. Particles such as **لَمَّا** (lammā) “when/as soon as” and **كُلَّمَا** (kullamā) “whenever/every time” usually introduce past-time or repeated situations without changing the verb into the jussive.

## Core idea

Compare **إِنْ تَدْرُسْ تَنْجَحْ** (in tadrus tanjaḥ) — “if you study, you succeed,” where the verbs are jussive, with **كُلَّمَا دَرَسْتَ نَجَحْتَ** (kullamā darasta najaḥta) — “whenever you studied, you succeeded,” where the verbs are past. The meaning is conditional, but the grammar is not jussive.

## Forms and examples

| Particle | Meaning | Common verb form | Example | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **لَمَّا** (lammā) | when / as soon as | past + past | **لَمَّا وَصَلَ الْمُدِيرُ بَدَأَ الِاجْتِمَاعُ** (lammā waṣala al-mudīru badaʾa al-ijtimāʿu) — when the manager arrived, the meeting began | time link |
| **كُلَّمَا** (kullamā) | whenever / every time | past + past | **كُلَّمَا سَمِعْتُ الْأَذَانَ ذَكَرْتُ الصَّلَاةَ** (kullamā samiʿtu al-adhāna dhakartu aṣ-ṣalāta) — whenever I heard the call to prayer, I remembered prayer | repeated link |
| **لَوْلَا** (lawlā) | if not for | noun + la-answer | **لَوْلَا الْمَطَرُ لَخَرَجْنَا** (lawlā al-maṭaru lakharajnā) — if not for the rain, we would have gone out | prevention |
| **لَمَّا** negative | not yet | jussive imperfect | **لَمَّا يَحْضُرِ الطَّبِيبُ** (lammā yaḥḍuri aṭ-ṭabību) — the doctor has not arrived yet | different use |

## Usage notes

The same written particle can have different grammar. **لَمَّا** (lammā) with past verbs is temporal: **لَمَّا دَخَلْتُ رَأَيْتُهُ** (lammā dakhaltu raʾaytuhu) — “when I entered, I saw him.” **لَمَّا** (lammā) meaning “not yet” behaves like **لَمْ** (lam) and jussivizes the imperfect: **لَمَّا أَفْهَمْ** (lammā afham) — “I have not yet understood.”

## Practice patterns

Use these particles to connect events:

- **لَمَّا فَتَحْتُ النَّافِذَةَ دَخَلَ الْهَوَاءُ** (lammā fataḥtu an-nāfidhata dakhala al-hawāʾu) — when I opened the window, the air entered.
- **كُلَّمَا قَرَأَتِ الطَّالِبَةُ صَفْحَةً كَتَبَتْ مُلَاحَظَةً** (kullamā qaraʾati aṭ-ṭālibatu ṣafḥatan katabat mulāḥaẓatan) — every time the student read a page, she wrote a note.
- **لَوْلَا النُّورُ لَمَا رَأَيْنَا الطَّرِيقَ** (lawlā an-nūru lamā raʾaynā aṭ-ṭarīqa) — if not for the light, we would not have seen the road.
