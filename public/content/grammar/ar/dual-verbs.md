# Dual Verbs & Pronoun Agreement (yafʿalāni, faʿalā)

Arabic does not only distinguish singular and plural; it also marks exactly two in verbs. Dual verb forms are especially important when pronouns are attached or when a dual subject comes before the verb.

## Core idea

The dual marker in many present-tense verbs is **أَلِفُ الِاثْنَيْنِ** (alif al-ithnayn) — the alif of two plus **نُونٌ** (nūnun) — nūn in the indicative: **يَفْعَلَانِ** (yafʿalāni) — they two masculine do, and **تَفْعَلَانِ** (tafʿalāni) — they two feminine or you two do. In the past, the third masculine dual is **فَعَلَا** (faʿalā) — they two did, and the third feminine dual is **فَعَلَتَا** (faʿalatā) — they two feminine did.

## Forms and examples

| Meaning | Past | Present |
|---|---|---|
| they two masculine | **كَتَبَا** (katabā) — they two wrote | **يَكْتُبَانِ** (yaktubāni) — they two write |
| they two feminine | **كَتَبَتَا** (katabatā) — they two feminine wrote | **تَكْتُبَانِ** (taktubāni) — they two feminine write |
| you two | **كَتَبْتُمَا** (katabtumā) — you two wrote | **تَكْتُبَانِ** (taktubāni) — you two write |

## Pronoun agreement and word order

When the subject is a pronoun, the dual ending is required: **هُمَا يَدْرُسَانِ** (humā yadrusāni) — they two study. When the dual noun comes before the verb, the verb also agrees fully: **الطَّالِبَانِ يَدْرُسَانِ** (aṭ-ṭālibāni yadrusāni) — the two male students study.

When the verb comes before an explicit dual noun subject, MSA usually keeps the verb singular while matching gender: **جَاءَ الطَّالِبَانِ** (jāʾa aṭ-ṭālibāni) — the two male students came, and **جَاءَتِ الطَّالِبَتَانِ** (jāʾati aṭ-ṭālibatāni) — the two female students came.

## The five verbs and dropped nūn

Dual present forms belong to **الْأَفْعَالُ الْخَمْسَةُ** (al-afʿāl al-khamsa) — the five verbs. In the indicative they keep final **نِ** (ni), but after **لَنْ** (lan) — will not or **لَمْ** (lam) — did not, that nūn drops.

- **هُمَا يَكْتُبَانِ** (humā yaktubāni) — they two write.
- **لَنْ يَكْتُبَا** (lan yaktubā) — they two will not write.
- **لَمْ يَكْتُبَا** (lam yaktubā) — they two did not write.
