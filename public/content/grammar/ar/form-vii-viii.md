# Verb Forms VII & VIII (infaʿala, iftaʿala): passive-reflexive

Forms VII and VIII often describe a change that happens to the subject, or an action the subject undertakes for itself. They are common in reading and news Arabic.

## Core idea

Form VII اِنْفَعَلَ (infaʿala) — it was affected / it happened to itself often has a passive-reflexive meaning. Form VIII اِفْتَعَلَ (iftaʿala) — he took on / did for himself places تَاءٌ (tāʾun) — tāʾ after the first root consonant.

| Form | Pattern | Meaning clue | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| VII | اِنْفَعَلَ (infaʿala) — it was affected | passive-reflexive change | اِنْكَسَرَ (inkasara) — it broke |
| VII | اِنْفَعَلَ (infaʿala) — it was affected | opening / being opened | اِنْفَتَحَ (infataḥa) — it opened |
| VIII | اِفْتَعَلَ (iftaʿala) — he undertook | acquired or self-involved action | اِجْتَمَعَ (ijtamaʿa) — he met / gathered |
| VIII | اِفْتَعَلَ (iftaʿala) — he undertook | moving toward | اِقْتَرَبَ (iqtaraba) — he approached |

## Form VII: change undergone

اِنْكَسَرَ الزُّجَاجُ (inkasara az-zujāju) — the glass broke does not name an outside breaker. اِنْفَتَحَ الْبَابُ (infataḥa al-bābu) — the door opened has the same inward focus.

## Form VIII: inserted tāʾ and assimilation

In Form VIII, تَاءٌ (tāʾun) — tāʾ enters after the first root consonant: اِجْتَمَعَ (ijtamaʿa) — he met / gathered, اِقْتَرَبَ (iqtaraba) — he approached. With some sounds, the تَاءٌ (tāʾun) — tāʾ can assimilate, so you may see spellings such as اِضْطَرَبَ (iḍṭaraba) — he was disturbed.

- اِحْتَفَلَ (iḥtafala) — he celebrated.
- اِعْتَمَدَ (iʿtamada) — he relied on.
- اِتَّصَلَ (ittaṣala) — he contacted / was connected.

> Form VIII is not simply passive. It often means the subject is actively involved, but the action is internally directed or acquired.
