# Shared Relative Nouns: man and mā

Most Arabic relative pronouns change for gender and number, but **مَنْ** (man) and **مَا** (mā) are shared relative nouns, **أَسْمَاءٌ مَوْصُولَةٌ مُشْتَرَكَةٌ** (asmāʾun mawṣūlatun mushtarakatun). They stay the same while the surrounding sentence tells you who or what is meant.

## Core idea

Use **مَنْ** (man) for people and **مَا** (mā) for things, ideas, or actions. Each introduces a relative clause called **صِلَةُ الْمَوْصُولِ** (ṣilatu al-mawṣūli), and that clause often contains a returning pronoun when needed.

## Forms and examples

| Relative noun | Typical referent | Agreement behavior | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **مَنْ** (man) | a person or people | fixed form, meaning from context | **أُحِبُّ مَنْ يُسَاعِدُ النَّاسَ** (uḥibbu man yusāʿidu an-nāsa) — I love whoever helps people |
| **مَنْ** with feminine meaning | a female person | verb may follow meaning | **جَاءَتْ مَنْ فَازَتْ بِالْجَائِزَةِ** (jāʾat man fāzat bil-jāʾizati) — the one who won the prize came |
| **مَا** (mā) | a thing or idea | fixed form | **اِخْتَرْ مَا يُنَاسِبُكَ** (ikhtar mā yunāsibuka) — choose what suits you |
| **مَا** with a return pronoun | thing needed by clause | pronoun links back | **اِشْتَرَيْتُ مَا تَحْتَاجُ إِلَيْهِ** (ishtaraytu mā taḥtāju ilayhi) — I bought what you need |

## Usage notes

Do not confuse relative **مَنْ** (man) — whoever with question **مَنْ؟** (man?) — who? Word order and punctuation help. In **مَنْ يَجْتَهِدْ يَنْجَحْ** (man yajtahid yanjaḥ) — whoever works hard succeeds, **مَنْ** is conditional rather than relative, but it still shows how one fixed form can carry broad meaning.

## Practice patterns

Build relative meanings with a full clause after **مَنْ** or **مَا**:

- **سَأَقْرَأُ مَا كَتَبْتَ** (saʾaqraʾu mā katabta) — I will read what you wrote.
- **نَحْتَرِمُ مَنْ يَصْدُقُ فِي كَلَامِهِ** (naḥtarimu man yaṣduqu fī kalāmihi) — we respect whoever is truthful in his speech.
- **هَذَا مَا بَحَثْتُ عَنْهُ** (hādhā mā baḥathtu ʿanhu) — this is what I searched for.
