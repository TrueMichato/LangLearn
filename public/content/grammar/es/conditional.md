# Conditional Mood (Condicional Simple)

The **condicional simple** is the "would" tense in Spanish — used for hypothetical situations, polite requests, and reported speech. Its formation is closely linked to the futuro simple: same irregular stems, and the endings attach to the full infinitive.

## Formation — Regular Verbs

Add these endings to the **full infinitive** (just like the future tense, but with different endings):

| Pronoun | hablar | comer | vivir |
|---------|--------|-------|-------|
| yo | **hablaría** | comería | viviría |
| tú | **hablarías** | comerías | vivirías |
| él/ella/usted | **hablaría** | comería | viviría |
| nosotros | **hablaríamos** | comeríamos | viviríamos |
| ustedes / ellos | **hablarían** | comerían | vivirían |

All endings carry a written accent on the **í**: -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían.

- **Hablaría** más despacio (ah-blah-REE-ah) — I would speak more slowly
- **Comeríamos** juntos (koh-meh-REE-ah-mos) — We would eat together
- **¿Vivirías** en otro país? (vee-vee-REE-ahs) — Would you live in another country?

## Irregular Stems

The **same twelve irregular stems** from the futuro simple apply here — just swap the endings:

| Infinitive | Stem | Conditional yo |
|------------|------|---------------|
| tener | tendr- | **tendría** (ten-DREE-ah) |
| hacer | har- | **haría** (ah-REE-ah) |
| poder | podr- | **podría** (poh-DREE-ah) |
| decir | dir- | **diría** (dee-REE-ah) |
| querer | querr- | **querría** (keh-RREE-ah) |
| saber | sabr- | **sabría** (sah-BREE-ah) |
| venir | vendr- | **vendría** (ven-DREE-ah) |
| salir | saldr- | **saldría** (sal-DREE-ah) |
| poner | pondr- | **pondría** (pon-DREE-ah) |
| haber | habr- | **habría** (ah-BREE-ah) |
| caber | cabr- | **cabría** (kah-BREE-ah) |
| valer | valdr- | **valdría** (val-DREE-ah) |

## Uses of the Conditional

### 1. Hypothetical Situations (with si-clauses)

Paired with the **imperfect subjunctive** in the si-clause:

- **Yo iría si tuviera tiempo** (ee-REE-ah / too-VYEH-rah) — I would go if I had time
- **Compraría esa casa si pudiera** — I would buy that house if I could
- **¿Qué harías si ganaras la lotería?** — What would you do if you won the lottery?

### 2. Polite Requests

Much softer than the present indicative — use this to sound courteous:

- **¿Podrías ayudarme?** (poh-DREE-ahs) — Could you help me? (polite)
- **¿Me traerías un café?** — Would you bring me a coffee?
- **¿Sabría usted dónde está el banco?** — Would you know where the bank is?

### 3. Future-in-the-Past (Reported Speech)

When the main verb is in the past and you report what someone said about the future:

- **Dijo que vendría** (ven-DREE-ah) — He said he would come
- **Prometió que llamaría** — She promised she would call
- **Creíamos que habría tiempo** — We thought there would be time

### 4. Probability in the Past

Just as the future expresses conjecture about the present, the conditional expresses conjecture about the **past**:

- **Serían las diez** (seh-REE-an) — It must have been ten o'clock
- **Tendría unos treinta años cuando llegó** — He must have been about thirty when he arrived

## The si-Clause Pattern

| si-clause (condition) | Result clause |
|-----------------------|---------------|
| **si + imperfect subjunctive** | **conditional** |
| Si tuviera dinero... | ...viajaría. |
| Si pudiera volar... | ...vería el mundo. |
| Si supiera la respuesta... | ...te la diría. |

⚠️ Never use the conditional in the si-clause itself: ~~Si tendría~~ → Si tuviera.
