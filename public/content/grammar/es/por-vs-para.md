# Por vs Para (The Two "Fors")

Both **por** and **para** can translate as "for" in English, but they cover very different semantic territory. Mixing them up can change your meaning entirely — "Lo hago por ti" (I do it because of you) vs "Lo hago para ti" (I do it for you / I do it as a gift to you). Learning their core concepts makes the choice click.

## Por — The Core Idea: Cause, Exchange, Motion Through

**Por** looks backward (cause, origin, exchange) or around (movement through/along).

### Cause or Reason ("because of / on behalf of")

- **Lo hago por ti** (por tee) — I do it because of you / for your sake
- **Gracias por tu ayuda** — Thanks for your help (the reason for thanks)
- **Lucharon por la libertad** — They fought for (the sake of) freedom

### Exchange or Substitution

- **Pagué veinte dólares por el libro** (POR el LEE-broh) — I paid twenty dollars for the book
- **Te cambio esto por aquello** — I'll trade you this for that
- **Vendieron la casa por poco dinero** — They sold the house for little money

### Duration of Time

- **Estudié por dos horas** (por dohs OH-rahs) — I studied for two hours
- **Vivió en México por diez años** — She lived in Mexico for ten years
- **Puedo esperar por un rato** — I can wait for a while

### Means, Agent, or Mode

- **Hablamos por teléfono** (por teh-LEH-foh-noh) — We spoke by phone
- **El paquete llegó por correo** — The package arrived by mail
- **Escrito por Cervantes** — Written by Cervantes (passive agent)

### Movement Through or Along

- **Caminamos por el parque** (por el PAR-keh) — We walked through the park
- **El tren pasa por Guadalajara** — The train passes through Guadalajara
- **Paseamos por la playa** — We strolled along the beach

### Per / Rate

- **Tres veces por semana** (por seh-MAH-nah) — Three times per week
- **Gana diez dólares por hora** — She earns ten dollars per hour

## Para — The Core Idea: Purpose, Destination, Deadline

**Para** looks forward (goal, destination, deadline, recipient).

### Purpose or Goal ("in order to")

- **Estudio para aprender** (PA-rah ah-prehn-DEHR) — I study in order to learn
- **Ahorré dinero para el viaje** — I saved money for the trip
- **Este botón es para pausar** — This button is for pausing

### Recipient ("for someone")

- **Este regalo es para ti** (PA-rah tee) — This gift is for you
- **Hice la cena para mi familia** — I made dinner for my family
- **¿Hay un mensaje para mí?** — Is there a message for me?

### Deadline

- **Necesito el informe para el lunes** (PA-rah el LOO-nehs) — I need the report by Monday
- **Termina esto para mañana** — Finish this by tomorrow

### Direction Toward a Destination

- **Salgo para Madrid mañana** (PA-rah mah-DRID) — I'm leaving for Madrid tomorrow
- **Este vuelo va para Buenos Aires** — This flight goes to Buenos Aires

### Opinion or Point of View

- **Para mí, es muy difícil** (PA-rah mee) — For me / In my opinion, it's very difficult
- **Para ella, no es un problema** — For her, it's not a problem

### Employment

- **Trabajo para Google** (PA-rah) — I work for Google
- **Ella diseña para una marca española** — She designs for a Spanish brand

### Comparison / Unexpected Standard

- **Para un niño, lee muy bien** — For a child, he reads very well
- **Para ser principiante, tocas muy bien** — For a beginner, you play very well

## Side-by-Side Contrast

| Por (cause / exchange / through) | Para (purpose / destination / recipient) |
|-----------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Lo hago **por** dinero (motivated by money) | Lo hago **para** ganar dinero (in order to earn) |
| Gracias **por** el regalo (for = because of) | Este es **para** ti (for = intended for you) |
| Estudié **por** una hora (duration) | Estudio **para** el examen (goal) |
| Voy **por** el parque (through) | Voy **para** el parque (heading toward) |
| Escrito **por** García Márquez (agent) | Un libro **para** estudiantes (recipient) |

## Memory Hooks

- **Para →** a forward arrow **→** (purpose, destination, deadline, recipient)
- **Por →** a cycle or exchange **⇄** (cause, exchange, means, movement through/along)
