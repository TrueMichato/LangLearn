# Pretérito Indefinido (Simple Past)

The *pretérito indefinido* is used for **single, completed actions in the past** — events that happened once, at a specific time, and are now finished. It is the main past tense for describing what you did yesterday, last year, or at any defined point in the past. Regular verbs follow predictable patterns; a handful of high-frequency verbs are irregular and must be memorized.

## Regular -AR Verbs: hablar (to speak)

| Pronoun | Ending | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|--------|-------------|--------------|
| yo | **-é** | hablé | ah-BLEH |
| tú | **-aste** | hablaste | ah-BLAHS-teh |
| él / ella / usted | **-ó** | habló | ah-BLOH |
| nosotros | **-amos** | hablamos | ah-BLAH-mohs |
| vosotros | **-asteis** | hablasteis | ah-BLAHS-tehs |
| ellos / ustedes | **-aron** | hablaron | ah-BLAH-rohn |

> 📌 The *nosotros* form of -ar verbs is identical in present and preterite: **hablamos** can mean "we speak" or "we spoke" — context and time expressions tell them apart.

## Regular -ER / -IR Verbs (Shared Endings)

-ER and -IR verbs share the same preterite endings. Example: **comer** (to eat) and **vivir** (to live):

| Pronoun | Ending | comer | vivir |
|---------|--------|-------|-------|
| yo | **-í** | comí | viví |
| tú | **-iste** | comiste | viviste |
| él / ella / usted | **-ió** | comió | vivió |
| nosotros | **-imos** | comimos | vivimos |
| vosotros | **-isteis** | comisteis | vivisteis |
| ellos / ustedes | **-ieron** | comieron | vivieron |

- **Comí pizza anoche** (koh-MEE PEET-sah ah-NOH-cheh) — I ate pizza last night
- **Mis abuelos vivieron en Colombia** — My grandparents lived in Colombia

## Common Trigger Words

These time expressions typically signal the pretérito indefinido:

| Spanish | English |
|---------|---------|
| **ayer** | yesterday |
| **anoche** | last night |
| **la semana pasada** | last week |
| **el mes pasado** | last month |
| **el año pasado** | last year |
| **hace dos días** | two days ago |
| **en 2010** | in 2010 |
| **de repente** | suddenly |
| **una vez** | once |

- **Ayer fui al médico** (ah-YEHR fwee) — Yesterday I went to the doctor
- **El año pasado viajé a Argentina** — Last year I traveled to Argentina
- **De repente empezó a llover** — Suddenly it started to rain

## Irregular Verbs — High-Priority List

These high-frequency verbs have completely irregular preterite stems. Their endings carry no accent marks: -e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron/-eron.

### Ser and Ir — Identical Forms (Context Distinguishes)

| Pronoun | ser / ir |
|---------|---------|
| yo | **fui** |
| tú | **fuiste** |
| él / ella | **fue** |
| nosotros | **fuimos** |
| vosotros | **fuisteis** |
| ellos | **fueron** |

- **Fui al mercado** (fwee) — I went to the market *(ir)*
- **Fue un gran día** (fweh) — It was a great day *(ser)*

### Hacer (to do / make)

| yo | tú | él | nosotros | ellos |
|----|----|----|----------|-------|
| hice | hiciste | **hizo** | hicimos | hicieron |

> ⚠️ Third-person singular is **hizo** (not *hizó*, not *hicó*) — c → z before *o* to preserve the /s/ sound.

### More Irregular Stems

| Infinitive | Stem | yo | él | ellos |
|------------|------|----|----|-------|
| **tener** | tuv- | tuve | tuvo | tuvieron |
| **estar** | estuv- | estuve | estuvo | estuvieron |
| **poder** | pud- | pude | pudo | pudieron |
| **poner** | pus- | puse | puso | pusieron |
| **saber** | sup- | supe | supo | supieron |
| **querer** | quis- | quise | quiso | quisieron |
| **venir** | vin- | vine | vino | vinieron |
| **decir** | dij- | dije | dijo | **dijeron** |
| **traer** | traj- | traje | trajo | **trajeron** |

> ⚠️ **Decir** and **traer**: because the stem ends in **j**, the third-person plural drops the *i* → **dijeron** (not *dijieron*), **trajeron** (not *trajieron*).

### Dar and Ver — Short but Irregular

| Pronoun | dar | ver |
|---------|-----|-----|
| yo | **di** | **vi** |
| tú | diste | viste |
| él | dio | vio |
| nosotros | dimos | vimos |
| ellos | dieron | vieron |

*Dar* uses -er/-ir endings despite being an -ar verb. *Ver* is irregular only in losing the accent marks (vi not *ví*, vio not *vió*).

## Spelling-Change Verbs (yo Form Only)

Verbs ending in **-car, -gar, -zar** change spelling in the *yo* form to preserve the original pronunciation:

| Type | Change | Example |
|------|--------|---------|
| -car | c → qu | **buscar** → **busqué** (not *buscé*) |
| -gar | g → gu | **llegar** → **llegué** (not *llegé*) |
| -zar | z → c | **empezar** → **empecé** (not *empezé*) |

All other forms are regular: llegaste, llegó, llegamos, llegasteis, llegaron.
