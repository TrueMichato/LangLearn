# Gender & Number Agreement

Every Spanish noun has a grammatical **gender** (masculine or feminine) and a **number** (singular or plural). Articles, adjectives, and pronouns must all **agree** with the noun they refer to — matching its gender and number. Internalizing these patterns early makes the rest of Spanish grammar click into place.

## Noun Gender: The Basics

Most nouns follow predictable patterns based on their ending:

| Ending | Usually | Examples |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **-o** | Masculine | **el libro** (book), **el chico** (boy), **el año** (year) |
| **-a** | Feminine | **la casa** (house), **la chica** (girl), **la mesa** (table) |
| **-ción / -sión** | Feminine | **la nación** (nah-SYOHN) — nation; **la televisión** — TV |
| **-dad / -tad** | Feminine | **la ciudad** (syoo-DAHD) — city; **la libertad** — freedom |
| **-or** | Masculine | **el color** — color; **el calor** — heat |

## Important Exceptions

Some very common nouns break the pattern and must be memorized.

### Masculine Nouns Ending in -a (Greek-origin -ma words)

Words borrowed from Greek ending in **-ma / -ema / -ama / -oma** are masculine:

- **el problema** (ehl proh-BLEH-mah) — the problem
- **el tema** (ehl TEH-mah) — the topic / theme
- **el sistema** (ehl sees-TEH-mah) — the system
- **el programa** (ehl proh-GRAH-mah) — the program
- **el idioma** (ehl ee-DYOH-mah) — the language
- **el poema** (ehl poh-EH-mah) — the poem

### Other Common Exceptions

| Word | Gender | Why it's tricky |
|------|--------|----------------|
| **el día** (ehl DEE-ah) | Masculine | Ends in -a but masculine |
| **la mano** (lah MAH-noh) | Feminine | Ends in -o but feminine |
| **el mapa** (ehl MAH-pah) | Masculine | Ends in -a but masculine |
| **la foto** (lah FOH-toh) | Feminine | Short for *fotografía* |
| **la radio** (lah RAH-dyoh) | Feminine | Short for *radiodifusión* |
| **la moto** (lah MOH-toh) | Feminine | Short for *motocicleta* |

## Forming the Plural

### Rule 1 — Nouns ending in a vowel: add -s

- **libro** → **libros** (LEE-brohs)
- **casa** → **casas** (KAH-sahs)
- **estudiante** → **estudiantes** (ehs-too-DYAHN-tehs)

### Rule 2 — Nouns ending in a consonant: add -es

- **ciudad** → **ciudades** (syoo-DAH-dehs) — cities
- **árbol** → **árboles** (AHR-boh-lehs) — trees
- **profesor** → **profesores** (proh-feh-SOH-rehs) — teachers

### Special Rule: Z → C before -es

Nouns ending in **-z** change the z to c before adding -es:

- **lápiz** → **lápices** (LAH-pee-sehs) — pencils
- **vez** → **veces** (BEH-sehs) — times
- **pez** → **peces** (PEH-sehs) — fish (plural)
- **nariz** → **narices** (nah-REE-sehs) — noses

## Adjective Agreement

Adjectives in Spanish must agree in **gender AND number** with the noun they modify.

### Adjectives ending in -o/-a (four forms)

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| **Masculine** | el libro **rojo** | los libros **rojos** |
| **Feminine** | la casa **roja** | las casas **rojas** |

- **El gato negro** (ehl GAH-toh NEH-groh) — The black cat
- **La falda negra** (lah FAHL-dah NEH-grah) — The black skirt
- **Los zapatos rojos** (lohs sah-PAH-tohs RROH-hohs) — The red shoes
- **Las flores rojas** (lahs FLOH-rehs RROH-hahs) — The red flowers

### Invariable Adjectives (two forms: singular and plural only)

Adjectives NOT ending in -o do not change for gender — only for number:

| Adjective | Masc. Singular | Fem. Singular | Plural |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **azul** (blue) | el coche azul | la bici azul | los coches azules |
| **verde** (green) | el parque verde | la planta verde | los parques verdes |
| **fácil** (easy) | el examen fácil | la pregunta fácil | los exámenes fáciles |
| **grande** (big) | el país grande | la ciudad grande | los países grandes |

- **El cielo azul** / **La camisa azul** — The blue sky / The blue shirt (same form)
- **Los cielos azules** / **Las camisas azules** — plurals add -es

## Mixed-Gender Groups

When a group contains both masculine and feminine nouns, the **masculine plural** is used:

- **Pedro y María son simpáticos** — Pedro and María are friendly (masculine plural)
- **El libro y la pluma son azules** — The book and the pen are blue (invariable, plural)

## Summary: Plural Formation

| Noun ending | Plural rule | Example |
|------------|------------|---------|
| Vowel (-o, -a, -e) | + **s** | libro → libros |
| Consonant (most) | + **es** | ciudad → ciudades |
| **-z** | z → c + **es** | lápiz → lápices |
