# Question Words (qué, quién, dónde, cuándo)

Every Spanish question word carries a written accent mark (tilde) — no exceptions. The inverted question mark **¿** must open every written question, and **?** closes it. These marks are mandatory in formal written Spanish and signal to the reader that a question is coming before the verb even appears.

## Question Words at a Glance

| Word | Pronunciation | Meaning | Notes |
|------|--------------|---------|-------|
| **qué** | keh | what | asks for a definition or thing |
| **quién / quiénes** | KYEHN / KYEH-nehs | who / whom | singular / plural |
| **dónde** | DOHN-deh | where | |
| **adónde** | ah-DOHN-deh | where (to) | used with verbs of motion |
| **cuándo** | KWAHN-doh | when | |
| **cuál / cuáles** | kwahl / KWAH-lehs | which (one/ones) | picking from options |
| **cómo** | KOH-moh | how | also used in greetings |
| **por qué** | pohr keh | why | two words with separate accents |
| **cuánto / cuánta** | KWAHN-toh | how much | agrees with noun gender |
| **cuántos / cuántas** | KWAHN-tohs | how many | agrees with noun gender |

## Opening and Closing Questions

Spanish wraps questions with **¿** at the start and **?** at the end. Unlike English, you know a question is coming before the verb inverts:

- **¿Cómo te llamas?** (KOH-moh teh YAH-mahs) — What is your name?
- **¿Dónde vives?** (DOHN-deh VEE-vehs) — Where do you live?
- **¿Cuándo llegaste?** (KWAHN-doh yeh-GAHS-teh) — When did you arrive?
- **¿Adónde vas?** — Where are you going?

## Qué vs Cuál — A Key Distinction

**Qué** asks for a **definition or explanation**. **Cuál / cuáles** asks you to **pick from options**:

| Use **qué** | Use **cuál / cuáles** |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Definition: ¿Qué es esto? — What is this? | Choice: ¿Cuál prefieres? — Which do you prefer? |
| Open: ¿Qué quieres? — What do you want? | Specific: ¿Cuál es tu dirección? — What is your address? |
| Before nouns: ¿Qué libro lees? — What book? | Multiple: ¿Cuáles te gustan? — Which ones do you like? |

- **¿Qué es un taco?** (keh ehs) — What is a taco? *(asking for a definition)*
- **¿Cuál es tu taco favorito?** — Which is your favorite taco? *(choosing from many)*
- **¿Qué película vemos?** (keh peh-LEE-koo-lah VEH-mohs) — What movie do we watch? *(open question)*
- **¿Cuál prefieres, la de acción o la comedia?** — Which do you prefer, the action or the comedy?

## Por Qué, Porque, Porqué, Por Que

These four look nearly identical but have very different roles:

| Form | Role | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| **por qué** | question — why? | ¿Por qué lloras? — Why are you crying? |
| **porque** | conjunction — because | Lloro porque estoy feliz. — I cry because I am happy. |
| **el porqué** | noun — the reason, the why | No entiendo el porqué. — I don't understand the reason. |
| **por que** | prep + rel. pronoun (rare) | La razón por que llegué tarde... — The reason for which I arrived late... |

The most important pair: **¿Por qué?** asks the question; **porque** gives the answer.

## More Question Words in Use

- **¿Quién es ella?** (KYEHN ehs EH-yah) — Who is she?
- **¿Quiénes son ellos?** (KYEH-nehs) — Who are they?
- **¿Cuánto cuesta esto?** (KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah EHS-toh) — How much does this cost?
- **¿Cuántos años tienes?** (KWAHN-tohs AH-nyohs TYEH-nehs) — How old are you?
- **¿Cómo se dice 'hello' en español?** — How do you say 'hello' in Spanish?
- **¿De dónde eres?** (deh DOHN-deh EH-rehs) — Where are you from?

### Cuánto — Gender Agreement

**Cuánto** agrees in gender and number with the noun it modifies:

- **¿Cuánto dinero tienes?** — How much money? (*dinero* = masc. sg.)
- **¿Cuánta agua queda?** — How much water is left? (*agua* = fem. sg.)
- **¿Cuántos libros compraste?** — How many books? (*libros* = masc. pl.)
- **¿Cuántas personas vinieron?** — How many people came? (*personas* = fem. pl.)
