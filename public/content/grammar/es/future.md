# Future Tense (ir + a + infinitive vs futuro simple)

Spanish has two ways to express the future. The **periphrastic future** (ir + a + infinitive) is by far the most common in everyday speech, while the **futuro simple** is preferred in formal writing, predictions, and expressing probability. Knowing when to use each one will make your Spanish sound natural right away.

## Periphrastic Future — ir + a + infinitive

The most common future in spoken Spanish: conjugate **ir** in the present tense, add **a**, then an infinitive. Think "I'm going to eat."

| Pronoun | ir (present) | Formula |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| yo | **voy** | voy + a + infinitivo |
| tú | **vas** | vas + a + infinitivo |
| él/ella/usted | **va** | va + a + infinitivo |
| nosotros | **vamos** | vamos + a + infinitivo |
| ustedes / ellos | **van** | van + a + infinitivo |

- **Voy a comer** (BOY ah koh-MEHR) — I'm going to eat
- **Vas a estudiar esta noche** (vahs ah es-too-DYAR) — You're going to study tonight
- **Vamos a salir juntos** (BAH-mos ah sah-LEER) — We're going to go out together

## Futuro Simple — Regular Endings

Add these endings directly to the **infinitive** (the whole word, not the stem):

| Pronoun | hablar | comer | vivir |
|---------|--------|-------|-------|
| yo | **hablaré** | comeré | viviré |
| tú | **hablarás** | comerás | vivirás |
| él/ella/usted | **hablará** | comerá | vivirá |
| nosotros | **hablaremos** | comeremos | viviremos |
| ustedes / ellos | **hablarán** | comerán | vivirán |

- **Hablaré** (ah-blah-REH) — I will speak
- **Comerás más tarde** (koh-meh-RAHS) — You will eat later
- **Viviremos allí** (vee-vee-REH-mos) — We will live there

Note: vosotros uses **hablaréis** — a Spain-only form rarely heard in Latin America.

## Irregular Stems (Futuro Simple)

A dozen common verbs use a shortened or modified stem. The endings are identical to regular verbs. These **same stems** also apply to the conditional tense.

| Infinitive | Irregular Stem | Example (yo) |
|------------|---------------|--------------|
| tener | **tendr-** | **tendré** (ten-DREH) |
| hacer | **har-** | **haré** (ah-REH) |
| poder | **podr-** | **podré** (poh-DREH) |
| decir | **dir-** | **diré** (dee-REH) |
| querer | **querr-** | **querré** (keh-RREH) |
| saber | **sabr-** | **sabré** (sah-BREH) |
| venir | **vendr-** | **vendré** (ven-DREH) |
| salir | **saldr-** | **saldré** (sal-DREH) |
| poner | **pondr-** | **pondré** (pon-DREH) |
| haber | **habr-** | **habrá** (ah-BRAH) |
| caber | **cabr-** | **cabrá** (kah-BRAH) |
| valer | **valdr-** | **valdrá** (val-DRAH) |

- **Tendré** tiempo mañana (ten-DREH) — I will have time tomorrow
- **Haremos** la tarea (ah-REH-mos) — We will do the homework
- **¿Podrás venir?** (poh-DRAHS) — Will you be able to come?

## Futuro Simple as Conjecture

The futuro simple also expresses **probability or conjecture about the present** — something English does with "must be" or "probably":

- **Serán las tres** (seh-RAHN) — It must be three o'clock / It's probably three
- **¿Dónde estará María?** — Where can María be? / Where is María, I wonder?
- **Tendrá unos cuarenta años** — He must be about forty years old

## ir + a vs futuro simple — Which to Use?

| Situation | Use | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| Near future, planned event | ir + a | *Voy a llamarte.* |
| Formal writing, speeches | Futuro simple | *El presidente hablará.* |
| Predictions far in future | Futuro simple | *En 2050 habrá robots en casa.* |
| Conjecture about present | Futuro simple | *Estará en casa.* |
| Everyday conversation | ir + a | *¿Vas a comer algo?* |
