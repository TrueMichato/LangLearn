# Cheat Sheet: Ser / Estar / Haber (Three Verbs for "To Be")

English collapses three very different ideas into a single verb: *to be*. Spanish keeps them strictly separate. **Ser** describes what something *is* in essence; **estar** describes the state or location it happens to be in; **haber** is used impersonally (*hay* = there is/are) and as the auxiliary in compound tenses.

> 📌 If you only remember one thing: **ser = identity; estar = condition; haber = existence/auxiliary.** Everything else flows from this triple.

## Quick Rule Grid

| | **SER** | **ESTAR** | **HABER (HAY)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core meaning** | Identity, essence, defining traits | Condition, location, temporary state | Existence, "there is/are" |
| **Typical complements** | Nouns, adjectives of essence, time, possession, origin | Adjectives of state, locations of objects, gerunds | Existence statements, weather, obligation, perfect tenses |
| **Key example** | *Soy profesora* — I am a teacher | *Estoy cansada* — I am tired | *Hay tres libros* — There are three books |

## Ser — Eight Core Uses

1. **Identity / who or what something is**
   - *Yo soy María.* — I am María.
   - *Esto es un libro.* — This is a book.

2. **Profession**
   - *Es médico.* — He is a doctor.
   - *Somos estudiantes.* — We are students.

3. **Nationality / origin**
   - *Soy de Argentina.* — I am from Argentina.
   - *Eres mexicano.* — You are Mexican.

4. **Material composition**
   - *La mesa es de madera.* — The table is (made) of wood.
   - *El anillo es de oro.* — The ring is gold.

5. **Possession**
   - *El libro es de Juan.* — The book belongs to Juan.
   - *Esta casa es mía.* — This house is mine.

6. **Time and date**
   - *Es la una.* — It is one o'clock.
   - *Son las tres y media.* — It's 3:30.
   - *Hoy es lunes.* — Today is Monday.

7. **Where an event takes place** *(not a thing — an event)*
   - *La fiesta es en mi casa.* — The party is at my house.
   - *La reunión es a las cinco.* — The meeting is at 5.

8. **Impersonal expressions of essence**
   - *Es importante estudiar.* — It is important to study.
   - *Es necesario que vengas.* — It's necessary that you come.

## Estar — Eight Core Uses

1. **Location of things and people** *(but not events)*
   - *Madrid está en España.* — Madrid is in Spain.
   - *El libro está en la mesa.* — The book is on the table.

2. **Temporary physical or emotional states**
   - *Estoy cansado.* — I am tired.
   - *Está triste.* — She is sad.

3. **Weather (with está)**
   - *Está nublado.* — It's cloudy.
   - *Está haciendo frío.* — It's getting cold.

4. **Present progressive (estar + gerundio)**
   - *Está comiendo.* — He is eating.
   - *Estamos estudiando.* — We are studying.

5. **Result of an action (estar + past participle)**
   - *La puerta está cerrada.* — The door is closed.
   - *El coche está roto.* — The car is broken.

6. **Past participles used as adjectives**
   - *Estoy casado.* — I am married.
   - *Está dormido.* — He is asleep.

7. **Dates with the preposition a**
   - *Estamos a 5 de marzo.* — It's March 5th.
   - *¿A cuántos estamos hoy?* — What's the date today?

8. **Fixed idioms with estar**
   - *Estar de acuerdo* — to agree
   - *Estar de pie* — to be standing
   - *Estar de vacaciones* — to be on vacation
   - *Estar a punto de* — to be about to

## Haber — Six Core Uses

1. **Existence (hay = there is/are)** — impersonal, **always singular** regardless of number
   - *Hay un libro.* — There is a book.
   - *Hay tres libros.* — There are three books. *(never "han")*
   - *Había mucha gente.* — There were lots of people.

2. **Impersonal weather statements**
   - *Hay sol.* — It's sunny.
   - *Hay niebla.* — It's foggy.
   - *Hay viento.* — It's windy.

3. **Obligation: hay que + infinitive** *(impersonal "one must")*
   - *Hay que estudiar.* — One must study / We have to study.
   - *Hay que llegar a tiempo.* — One must arrive on time.

4. **Auxiliary in compound tenses**
   - *He comido.* — I have eaten.
   - *Habíamos visto.* — We had seen.
   - *Habrá llegado.* — He will have arrived.

5. **Idiomatic existence**
   - *Hay de todo.* — There's a bit of everything.
   - *No hay de qué.* — Don't mention it / You're welcome.
   - *No hay mal que por bien no venga.* — Every cloud has a silver lining.

6. **Time elapsed (literary: ha + time)**
   - *Hace tres años* — Three years ago (the common form uses *hacer*, but archaic *ha* is sometimes seen)
   - *¿Cuántos años hace que vives aquí?* — How many years have you lived here?

> ⚠️ Common mistake: **hay** is always singular. Even with plural objects: *hay tres coches* ✓ — never *han tres coches*.

## Side-by-Side Meaning Shifts — Same Adjective, Different Verb

A small set of adjectives change meaning depending on whether they pair with **ser** (essence) or **estar** (state). These are exam favourites.

| Adjective | ser + adj | estar + adj |
|---|---|---|
| bueno | *Es bueno* — He is a good (kind) person | *Está bueno* — It tastes good / He's attractive *(colloquial)* |
| listo | *Es listo* — He is clever | *Está listo* — He is ready |
| aburrido | *Es aburrido* — He is boring | *Está aburrido* — He is bored |
| seguro | *Es seguro* — It is safe | *Está seguro* — He is certain / sure |
| rico | *Es rico* — He is rich (wealthy) | *Está rico* — It tastes delicious |
| malo | *Es malo* — He is bad (evil) | *Está malo* — He is sick / It tastes bad |
| vivo | *Es vivo* — He is sharp / clever | *Está vivo* — He is alive |
| verde | *Es verde* — It is green (color) | *Está verde* — It is unripe / inexperienced |

## Mini-Dialogue

> — ¿Dónde **está** Marta? *(DOHN-deh ehs-TAH MAHR-tah)*
> — **Está** en casa. **Es** profesora y hoy no trabaja. *(ehs-TAH ehn KAH-sah. ehs proh-feh-SOH-rah ee oy noh trah-BAH-hah)*
> — ¿Y **hay** alguien con ella? *(ee ahy AHL-gyehn kohn EH-yah)*
> — Sí, **hay** dos amigas. **Están** preparando la cena, que **es** a las ocho. La comida **está** muy rica, según dicen. *(see, ahy dohs ah-MEE-gahs. ehs-TAHN preh-pah-RAHN-doh lah SEH-nah, keh ehs ah lahs OH-choh. lah koh-MEE-dah ehs-TAH mwee REE-kah, seh-GOON DEE-sehn)*
>
> **Translation:** — Where is Marta? — She's at home. She's a teacher and today she's not working. — And is anyone with her? — Yes, there are two friends. They're preparing dinner, which is at 8. The food tastes really good, they say.
