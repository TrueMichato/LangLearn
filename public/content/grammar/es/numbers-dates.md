# Numbers, Dates & Time

Numbers in Spanish follow predictable rules with a handful of key irregularities — like *cien* vs *ciento* and *quinientos* — that reward careful attention. Days, months, and dates all follow consistent patterns once you know the building blocks, and telling time uses a simple formula with just one irregular case.

## Cardinal Numbers 0–30

| | | | | | |
|--|--|--|--|--|--|
| 0 **cero** | 1 **uno** | 2 **dos** | 3 **tres** | 4 **cuatro** | 5 **cinco** |
| 6 **seis** | 7 **siete** | 8 **ocho** | 9 **nueve** | 10 **diez** | 11 **once** |
| 12 **doce** | 13 **trece** | 14 **catorce** | 15 **quince** | 16 **dieciséis** | 17 **diecisiete** |
| 18 **dieciocho** | 19 **diecinueve** | 20 **veinte** | 21 **veintiuno** | 22 **veintidós** | 23 **veintitrés** |
| 24 **veinticuatro** | 25 **veinticinco** | 26 **veintiséis** | 27 **veintisiete** | 28 **veintiocho** | 29 **veintinueve** | 30 **treinta** |

> 📌 16–29 are written as **one word** (dieciséis, veintiuno…). From 31 onward, use **y**: treinta **y** uno, cuarenta **y** dos.

## Tens and Hundreds

| Tens | | Hundreds | |
|------|--|----------|--|
| 20 **veinte** | 70 **setenta** | 100 **cien** | 600 **seiscientos** |
| 30 **treinta** | 80 **ochenta** | 200 **doscientos/-as** | 700 **setecientos** *(irreg.)* |
| 40 **cuarenta** | 90 **noventa** | 300 **trescientos** | 800 **ochocientos** |
| 50 **cincuenta** | | 400 **cuatrocientos** | 900 **novecientos** *(irreg.)* |
| 60 **sesenta** | | 500 **quinientos** *(irreg.)* | |

- **Cien** (SYEHN) — used alone or directly before a noun: *cien personas*, *cien dólares*
- **Ciento** — used when followed by more digits: **ciento uno** (101), **ciento cincuenta** (150)
- Hundreds **agree in gender**: *doscientas páginas* (200 pages, feminine) / *doscientos libros*

## Thousands and Millions

- **mil** (1,000) — no article needed; no plural: *dos mil*, *cinco mil* (never *dos miles*)
- **un millón** (1,000,000) — takes article; plural: *dos millones*, *tres millones*
- Millones use **de** directly before a noun: **un millón de personas**, **tres millones de dólares**

## Ordinal Numbers (1st–10th)

| | | | | |
|--|--|--|--|--|
| 1st **primero** | 2nd **segundo** | 3rd **tercero** | 4th **cuarto** | 5th **quinto** |
| 6th **sexto** | 7th **séptimo** | 8th **octavo** | 9th **noveno** | 10th **décimo** |

> ⚠️ **Primero** and **tercero** drop the **-o** before a masculine singular noun: **el primer libro**, **el tercer día** (but *la primera vez*, *la tercera vez*).

## Days of the Week

Days are **masculine** and written **lowercase** in Spanish:

| lunes | martes | miércoles | jueves | viernes | sábado | domingo |
|-------|--------|----------|-------|--------|--------|--------|
| Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday | Friday | Saturday | Sunday |

- **Hoy es lunes** (OH-ee ehs LOO-nehs) — Today is Monday
- **El lunes** = on Monday (specific) / **Los lunes** = on Mondays (habitual)

## Months and Dates

Months are also **lowercase**: enero, febrero, marzo, abril, mayo, junio, julio, agosto, septiembre, octubre, noviembre, diciembre.

### Saying the Date

- **Hoy es el 5 de marzo** — Today is March 5th
- **Estamos a 5 de marzo** (ehs-TAH-mohs) — It is March 5th *(common in Latin America)*
- **El 1 de enero de 2024** — January 1st, 2024
- Always use cardinal numbers for dates: *el quince de julio*, not *el décimoquinto*

## Telling Time

| Expression | Example | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| **¿Qué hora es?** (keh OH-rah ehs) | — | What time is it? |
| Es la una | **Es la una.** | It is 1:00 *(singular)* |
| Son las + number | **Son las dos.** | It is 2:00 *(plural)* |
| y media | **Son las tres y media.** | It is 3:30 |
| y cuarto | **Es la una y cuarto.** | It is 1:15 |
| menos cuarto | **Son las cinco menos cuarto.** | It is 4:45 |
| y + minutes | **Son las tres y veinte.** | It is 3:20 |

- **¿A qué hora?** — At what time?
- **A las cinco** (ah lahs SEEN-koh) — At five o'clock
- **A la una y media** — At one-thirty
