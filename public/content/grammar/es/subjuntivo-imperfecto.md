# Imperfect Subjunctive (Subjuntivo Imperfecto)

The **imperfect subjunctive** is one of the most powerful tools in Spanish. It unlocks hypothetical statements ("If I had money..."), polite expressions ("I would like..."), and past-context wish/doubt/emotion phrases. The good news: it derives entirely from a past tense form you may already know — the **preterite third person plural**.

## Formation — The Preterite Trick

**Step 1:** Take the **ellos/ustedes** form of the **preterite indefinido** (simple past).  
**Step 2:** Drop the **-ron**.  
**Step 3:** Add the imperfect subjunctive endings.

| Infinitive | Preterite ellos | Drop -ron | Stem |
|------------|----------------|-----------|------|
| hablar | hablaron | habla- | **habla-** |
| comer | comieron | comie- | **comie-** |
| vivir | vivieron | vivie- | **vivie-** |
| tener | tuvieron | tuvie- | **tuvie-** |
| ir / ser | fueron | fue- | **fue-** |
| estar | estuvieron | estuvie- | **estuvie-** |
| poder | pudieron | pudie- | **pudie-** |
| querer | quisieron | quisie- | **quisie-** |
| poner | pusieron | pusie- | **pusie-** |
| decir | dijeron | dije- | **dije-** |
| saber | supieron | supie- | **supie-** |

## Two Sets of Endings (-ra and -se)

Both sets are correct and fully interchangeable. The **-ra** form is more common in speech throughout Latin America; **-se** appears in formal writing and in Spain.

| Pronoun | -ra endings | -se endings |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| yo | -ra | -se |
| tú | -ras | -ses |
| él/ella/usted | -ra | -se |
| nosotros | -´ramos | -´semos |
| ustedes / ellos | -ran | -sen |

Note: nosotros always carries a written accent: **habláramos / hablásemos**.

## Full Conjugation Examples

| Pronoun | hablar | comer | ir/ser |
|---------|--------|-------|--------|
| yo | **hablara** / hablase | comiera / comiese | fuera / fuese |
| tú | **hablaras** / hablases | comieras / comieses | fueras / fueses |
| él/ella | **hablara** / hablase | comiera / comiese | fuera / fuese |
| nosotros | **habláramos** / hablásemos | comiéramos / comiésemos | fuéramos / fuésemos |
| ellos | **hablaran** / hablasen | comieran / comiesen | fueran / fuesen |

## Uses of the Imperfect Subjunctive

### 1. Hypothetical si-Clauses (Contrary to Fact)

The classic use: **si + imperfect subjunctive → conditional**:

- **Si tuviera dinero, viajaría** (too-VYEH-rah / vya-ha-REE-ah) — If I had money, I would travel
- **Si pudiera volar, vería el mundo** — If I could fly, I would see the world
- **Si fuera tú, no haría eso** — If I were you, I wouldn't do that

⚠️ **Real vs hypothetical:** For real possibilities (present/future), use **present indicative + present/future**:
- **Si tengo tiempo, voy** (TE-ngo / VOY) — If I have time, I'll go ✓
- ~~Si tuviera tiempo, voy~~ — Never mix imperfect subjunctive with present in a real condition

### 2. Past-Context Subjunctive Triggers

When the main verb is in a **past tense**, subordinate clauses shift from present to imperfect subjunctive:

| Present trigger | Past trigger |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Quiero que **vengas** | Quería que **vinieras** |
| Es importante que **estudies** | Era importante que **estudiaras** |
| Espero que **puedas** | Esperaba que **pudieras** |
| Dudo que **sea** verdad | Dudaba que **fuera** verdad |

- **Quería que vinieras** (keh-REE-ah keh vee-NYEH-rahs) — She wanted you to come
- **Le pedí que me ayudara** — I asked him to help me

### 3. Polite Expressions with quisiera / pudiera

The imperfect subjunctive of **querer** and **poder** is used as an ultra-polite alternative to the conditional:

- **Quisiera un café, por favor** (kee-SYEH-rah) — I would like a coffee, please
- **¿Pudieras hablar más despacio?** — Could you speak more slowly? (very polite)
- **Quisiera reservar una mesa** — I would like to reserve a table
