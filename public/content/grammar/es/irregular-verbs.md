# Common Irregular Verbs (ir, tener, hacer)

Some of the most frequently used Spanish verbs are also the most irregular — they don't follow the standard -ar/-er/-ir patterns at all. The good news: because these verbs appear in nearly every conversation, you will master them quickly through constant exposure. This lesson covers **ir**, **tener**, and **hacer** in full, plus a set of high-frequency "yo-form irregulars."

## Ir — to go

**Ir** (eer) is completely irregular — its forms look nothing like the infinitive:

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| yo | **voy** | BOH-ee |
| tú | **vas** | bahs |
| él / ella / usted | **va** | bah |
| nosotros | **vamos** | BAH-mohs |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | **van** | bahn |

> 🌍 Spain: vosotros **vais** (BAH-ees)

- **Voy al supermercado** (BOH-ee ahl soo-pehr-mehr-KAH-doh) — I'm going to the supermarket
- **¿Vas al gimnasio hoy?** — Are you going to the gym today?
- **Vamos a la playa** (BAH-mohs ah lah PLAH-yah) — We're going to the beach

### Ir + a + infinitive = "going to"

Use **ir + a + infinitive** to express future plans (equivalent to English "going to"):

- **Voy a estudiar esta noche** — I am going to study tonight
- **Van a comer a las ocho** — They are going to eat at eight
- **¿Vas a llamar a tu madre?** — Are you going to call your mother?

## Tener — to have

**Tener** (teh-NEHR) has an irregular **yo** form (**tengo**) plus a stem change (e → ie) in tú, él, and ellos:

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| yo | **tengo** | TEHN-goh |
| tú | **tienes** | TYEH-nehs |
| él / ella / usted | **tiene** | TYEH-neh |
| nosotros | **tenemos** | teh-NEH-mohs |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | **tienen** | TYEH-nehn |

> 🌍 Spain: vosotros **tenéis** (teh-NÉHS)

- **Tengo dos hermanos** (TEHN-goh dohs ehr-MAH-nohs) — I have two siblings
- **¿Tienes hambre?** (TYEH-nehs AHM-breh) — Are you hungry? *(lit. Do you have hunger?)*
- **Tenemos mucho trabajo** — We have a lot of work

### Tener + que + infinitive = "have to"

Use **tener + que + infinitive** to express obligation:

- **Tengo que estudiar** — I have to study
- **Tienes que llamar al médico** — You have to call the doctor
- **Tienen que llegar a tiempo** — They have to arrive on time

## Hacer — to do / to make

**Hacer** (ah-SEHR) has an irregular **yo** form (**hago**); all other forms are regular:

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| yo | **hago** | AH-goh |
| tú | **haces** | AH-sehs |
| él / ella / usted | **hace** | AH-seh |
| nosotros | **hacemos** | ah-SEH-mohs |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | **hacen** | AH-sehn |

- **Hago ejercicio cada mañana** (AH-goh eh-hehr-SEE-syoh KAH-dah mah-NYAH-nah) — I exercise every morning
- **¿Qué haces?** (keh AH-sehs) — What are you doing?
- **Hace frío hoy** (AH-seh FREE-oh OH-ee) — It is cold today *(weather expressions use hace)*

## The Yo-Form Irregular Pattern

Many Spanish verbs are irregular **only in the yo (first person singular)** form. All other persons follow regular endings. This pattern is called *irregularidad en la primera persona* or the **"go verbs"** (verbs whose yo form ends in -go):

| Verb | Yo form | Rest (regular) |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **salir** (to leave/go out) | **salgo** | sales, sale, salimos, salen |
| **poner** (to put) | **pongo** | pones, pone, ponemos, ponen |
| **traer** (to bring) | **traigo** | traes, trae, traemos, traen |
| **hacer** (to do/make) | **hago** | haces, hace, hacemos, hacen |
| **conocer** (to know/be familiar with) | **conozco** | conoces, conoce, conocemos, conocen |
| **dar** (to give) | **doy** | das, da, damos, dan |
| **saber** (to know a fact) | **sé** | sabes, sabe, sabemos, saben |

- **Salgo del trabajo a las seis** (SAHL-goh) — I leave work at six
- **Pongo la mesa** (POHN-goh) — I set the table
- **Traigo el vino** (TRAH-ee-goh) — I bring the wine
- **No sé la respuesta** — I don't know the answer

## Brief Tables: Decir, Venir, Salir

### Decir — to say / to tell

| yo | tú | él/ella | nosotros | ellos |
|----|-----|---------|----------|-------|
| **digo** | dices | dice | decimos | dicen |

### Venir — to come

| yo | tú | él/ella | nosotros | ellos |
|----|-----|---------|----------|-------|
| **vengo** | vienes | viene | venimos | vienen |

### Poner — to put

| yo | tú | él/ella | nosotros | ellos |
|----|-----|---------|----------|-------|
| **pongo** | pones | pone | ponemos | ponen |

- **Digo la verdad** (DEE-goh) — I tell the truth
- **Vengo de la universidad** (BEHN-goh) — I come from the university
- **Pongo el libro en la mesa** (POHN-goh) — I put the book on the table
