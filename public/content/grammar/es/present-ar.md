# Present Tense: Regular -ar Verbs

The present tense is the most essential tense in Spanish — used constantly in everyday conversation. Regular **-ar** verbs all follow the same pattern: remove the **-ar** ending to get the stem, then add the correct ending for each person. Once you learn this pattern, you unlock hundreds of verbs immediately.

## How to Conjugate -ar Verbs: Hablar

Take **hablar** (ah-BLAR) — *to speak*. Remove **-ar** → stem **habl-**, then add:

| Pronoun | Ending | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| yo (I) | **-o** | **hablo** | AH-bloh |
| tú (you, informal) | **-as** | **hablas** | AH-blahs |
| él / ella / usted (he/she/you formal) | **-a** | **habla** | AH-blah |
| nosotros/as (we) | **-amos** | **hablamos** | ah-BLAH-mohs |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes (they/you all) | **-an** | **hablan** | AH-blahn |

> 🌍 **Spain note**: A sixth form — **vosotros habláis** (ah-BLÁHS) — is used in Spain for informal "you all." In Latin America, **ustedes hablan** covers both formal and informal plural.

## What Does the Present Tense Express?

The Spanish present tense is more versatile than English. **Hablo** can mean:

- **I speak** (habitual action)
- **I am speaking** (action happening right now)
- **I do speak** (emphatic)

Context clarifies meaning:

- **Hablo español todos los días** (AH-bloh ehs-pah-NYOHL TOH-dohs lohs DEE-ahs) — I speak Spanish every day *(habitual)*
- **Ahora hablo con mi amigo** — I am speaking with my friend right now *(ongoing)*

## Full Example: Trabajar (to work)

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---------|-------------|
| yo | **trabajo** |
| tú | **trabajas** |
| él / ella / usted | **trabaja** |
| nosotros | **trabajamos** |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | **trabajan** |

- **Trabajo en una oficina** (trah-BAH-hoh ehn OO-nah oh-FEE-see-nah) — I work in an office
- **Ella trabaja los lunes** (EH-yah trah-BAH-hah lohs LOO-nehs) — She works on Mondays
- **¿Dónde trabajas tú?** — Where do you work?

## Making Questions and Negatives

### Questions: Simply add ¿...? around the sentence

- **¿Hablas inglés?** (AH-blahs een-GLEHS) — Do you speak English?
- **¿Trabaja usted aquí?** — Do you work here?
- **¿Escuchan música?** — Do they listen to music?

### Negatives: Place **no** directly before the verb

- **No hablo chino** (noh AH-bloh CHEE-noh) — I don't speak Chinese
- **No trabajamos los domingos** — We don't work on Sundays
- **Ella no mira televisión** — She doesn't watch TV
