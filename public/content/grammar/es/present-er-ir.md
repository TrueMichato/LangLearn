# Present Tense: Regular -er/-ir Verbs

Spanish verbs come in three families: **-ar**, **-er**, and **-ir**. You've already mastered -ar. Now learn the **-er** and **-ir** patterns — they share most endings, with just two small differences in the *nosotros* and *vosotros* forms. After this lesson you'll be able to conjugate hundreds more verbs.

## Conjugating -er Verbs: Comer (to eat)

Take **comer** (koh-MEHR) — *to eat*. Remove **-er** → stem **com-**, then add:

| Pronoun | Ending | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| yo | **-o** | **como** | KOH-moh |
| tú | **-es** | **comes** | KOH-mehs |
| él / ella / usted | **-e** | **come** | KOH-meh |
| nosotros | **-emos** | **comemos** | koh-MEH-mohs |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | **-en** | **comen** | KOH-mehn |

> 🌍 **Spain note**: vosotros form is **coméis** (koh-MÉHS).

## Conjugating -ir Verbs: Vivir (to live)

Take **vivir** (bee-BEER) — *to live*. Remove **-ir** → stem **viv-**, then add:

| Pronoun | Ending | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| yo | **-o** | **vivo** | BEE-boh |
| tú | **-es** | **vives** | BEE-behs |
| él / ella / usted | **-e** | **vive** | BEE-beh |
| nosotros | **-imos** | **vivimos** | bee-BEE-mohs |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | **-en** | **viven** | BEE-behn |

> 🌍 **Spain note**: vosotros form is **vivís** (bee-BEES).

## Comparing -er and -ir Endings

The two families are nearly identical — only **nosotros** and **vosotros** differ:

| Pronoun | -er ending | -ir ending |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| yo | **-o** | **-o** |
| tú | **-es** | **-es** |
| él/ella/usted | **-e** | **-e** |
| nosotros | **-emos** | **-imos** ← different |
| ellos/ustedes | **-en** | **-en** |
| vosotros (Spain) | **-éis** | **-ís** ← different |

## Full Examples in Context

- **Como una manzana** (KOH-moh OO-nah mahn-SAH-nah) — I eat an apple
- **¿Bebes café por la mañana?** — Do you drink coffee in the morning?
- **Ellos aprenden español muy rápido** — They learn Spanish very fast
- **Vivimos en México** (bee-BEE-mohs ehn MEH-hee-koh) — We live in Mexico
- **¿Escribes correos electrónicos en español?** — Do you write emails in Spanish?
- **Ella abre la ventana** (EH-yah AH-breh lah behn-TAH-nah) — She opens the window
- **Subo las fotos a las redes** — I upload the photos to social media

## Negation and Questions

Same pattern as -ar verbs — **no** before the verb for negation:

- **No como carne** (noh KOH-moh KAHR-neh) — I don't eat meat
- **¿Lees libros en español?** — Do you read books in Spanish?
- **No vivimos lejos** — We don't live far

|---------|------------|-----------|-----------|
| yo | hablo | como | vivo |
| tú | hablas | comes | vives |
| él/ella/usted | habla | come | vive |
| nosotros | hablamos | comemos | vivimos |
