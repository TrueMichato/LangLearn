# Relative Pronouns (que, quien, cuyo, el cual)

Relative pronouns connect a main clause to a subordinate clause that describes a noun. Spanish has several relative pronouns — **que** handles most situations, while **quien**, **cuyo**, and **el cual** appear in formal writing, prepositional phrases, and formal speech.

## Que — The All-Purpose Relative Pronoun

**Que** (keh) is the most common relative pronoun. It refers to people, things, and ideas, and it never changes form.

- **El libro que leo** (ehl LEE-broh keh LEH-oh) — The book that I read
- **La mujer que conocí** (lah moo-HEHR keh koh-noh-SEE) — The woman whom I met
- **La idea que propuso** (lah ee-DEH-ah keh proh-POO-soh) — The idea that he proposed

After prepositions, **que** is retained only when the preposition is very short (a, con, de, en):

- **La ciudad en que nací** (lah syoo-DAHD ehn keh nah-SEE) — The city in which I was born

## Quien / Quienes — For People Only

**Quien** (KYEHN) and its plural **quienes** (KYEH-nehs) refer only to people. They are used mainly:

1. **After prepositions** referring to people
2. **In non-restrictive (parenthetical) clauses** as an alternative to *que*

- **La chica con quien hablo** (lah CHEE-kah kohn KYEHN AH-bloh) — The girl with whom I'm speaking
- **Mi padre, quien es médico, trabaja mucho** (mee PAH-dreh KYEHN ehs MEH-dee-koh) — My father, who is a doctor, works a lot
- **Los estudiantes a quienes enseño** (lohs ehs-too-DYAHN-tehs ah KYEH-nehs ehn-SEH-nyoh) — The students whom I teach

| Context | Use |
|---------|-----|
| After prepositions (people) | **quien/quienes** |
| Restrictive clause (people or things) | **que** |
| Non-restrictive clause (people) | **quien** or **que** |

## El cual / La cual / Los cuales / Las cuales — Formal Clarity

This series agrees in gender and number with its antecedent. Use it:

- After **long prepositions** (para, sin, durante, mediante, etc.)
- When **ambiguity** might arise in a sentence with multiple nouns

| | Singular | Plural |
|-|----------|--------|
| **Masculine** | el cual | los cuales |
| **Feminine** | la cual | las cuales |

- **La razón por la cual vine** (lah rah-SOHN pohr lah KWAHL VEE-neh) — The reason for which I came
- **El edificio frente al cual vivo** (ehl eh-dee-FEE-syoh FREHN-teh ahl KWAHL VEE-voh) — The building in front of which I live
- **Las herramientas sin las cuales no puedo trabajar** — The tools without which I cannot work

## Cuyo / Cuya / Cuyos / Cuyas — Whose

**Cuyo** (KOO-yoh) means *whose* and functions like a possessive adjective. It agrees with the **thing possessed**, not the possessor.

| | Singular | Plural |
|-|----------|--------|
| **Masculine** | cuyo | cuyos |
| **Feminine** | cuya | cuyas |

- **El hombre cuya casa vendí** (ehl OHM-breh KOO-yah KAH-sah vehn-DEE) — The man whose house I sold
- **La escritora cuyos libros leo** (lah ehs-kree-TOH-rah KOO-yohs LEE-brohs LEH-oh) — The writer whose books I read
- **El país cuyos ciudadanos emigraron** — The country whose citizens emigrated

> ⚠️ **Note:** *Cuyo* is mainly written/formal. In everyday speech, Spanish speakers often rephrase: "El hombre de quien vendí la casa" or "El hombre que le vendí la casa."

## Donde — Relative of Place

**Donde** (DOHN-deh) means *where* and acts as a relative pronoun for places.

- **La ciudad donde nací** (lah syoo-DAHD DOHN-deh nah-SEE) — The city where I was born
- **El café donde nos conocimos** — The café where we met

## Lo que / Lo cual — Neuter Relatives

These refer to an **entire idea or action** rather than a specific noun.

- **No sé lo que dijo** (noh seh loh keh DEE-hoh) — I don't know what he said
- **Llegó tarde, lo cual me molestó** — She arrived late, which bothered me

*Lo que* introduces noun clauses; *lo cual* comments on the preceding clause (cannot start a new sentence).

## Quick Reference Table

| Pronoun | Refers to | Main use |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| **que** | people, things, ideas | Everything — most common |
| **quien/quienes** | people only | After prepositions; non-restrictive |
| **el/la/los/las cual(es)** | people, things | After long prepositions; formal clarity |
| **cuyo/cuya/cuyos/cuyas** | possession (whose) | Formal/written; agrees with thing possessed |
| **donde** | places | Relative of location |
| **lo que / lo cual** | whole ideas | Neuter reference |
