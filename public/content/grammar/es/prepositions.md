# Prepositions & Contractions (a+el=al, de+el=del)

Prepositions are the small words that link nouns, pronouns, and phrases to the rest of a sentence, expressing relationships of direction, location, time, and more. Spanish shares most prepositions with English but has two important quirks: the mandatory contractions **al** and **del**, and the **personal a** used before people and pets.

## Core Prepositions at a Glance

| Preposition | Pronunciation | Meanings |
|-------------|--------------|---------|
| **a** | ah | to, at, toward |
| **de** | deh | of, from, about |
| **en** | ehn | in, on, at |
| **con** | kohn | with |
| **sin** | seen | without |
| **sobre** | SOH-breh | about, on top of |
| **entre** | EHN-treh | between, among |
| **hasta** | AHS-tah | until, up to |
| **por** | pohr | by, through, for (cause/exchange) |
| **para** | PAH-rah | for, in order to (purpose/destination) |

## Mandatory Contractions: al and del

When **a** or **de** precede the masculine singular article **el**, they **must** contract. Saying "a el" or "de el" is incorrect:

| Components | Contraction | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| a + el | **al** | Voy **al** mercado — I go to the market |
| de + el | **del** | El libro **del** profesor — The teacher's book |

> ⚠️ These contractions apply **only** to the article **el**, not the subject pronoun **él** (he):
> - Hablo **de él** (deh ehl) — I am talking about him ✓ (no contraction — él is a pronoun)

**Important:** a + la does NOT contract:
- Voy **a la** tienda ✓ — I go to the store (never *al*)
- Vengo **de la** escuela ✓ — I come from school (never *del*)

## The Personal A

Spanish uses a special **a** before direct objects that are **specific people** (or beloved pets). English has no equivalent — it is never translated:

- **Veo a María** (VEH-oh ah mah-REE-ah) — I see María
- **Llamo a mi mamá** (YAH-moh ah mee mah-MAH) — I call my mom
- **Visito a mi perro** — I visit my dog (personified pet)

No personal a with things or non-specific people:
- **Veo la casa** (VEH-oh lah KAH-sah) — I see the house
- **Busco un médico** — I am looking for a doctor (non-specific)

## Prepositions in Use

### A — destination, time, purpose

- **Voy a México** (VOH-ee ah MEH-hee-koh) — I go to Mexico
- **Llegamos a las ocho** (ah lahs OH-choh) — We arrive at eight o'clock
- **Aprendí a manejar** — I learned to drive (+ infinitive)

### De — origin, material, possession

- **Soy de Colombia** (SOH-ee deh koh-LOHM-byah) — I am from Colombia
- **Una mesa de madera** (OO-nah MEH-sah deh mah-DEH-rah) — A wooden table
- **El carro de Juan** — Juan's car

### En — location and transport

- **Estoy en casa** (ehs-TOH-ee ehn KAH-sah) — I am at home
- **Trabajo en una oficina** — I work in an office
- **Viajo en avión** (VYAH-hoh ehn ah-VYOHN) — I travel by plane

### Con / Sin — with / without

- **Café con leche** (kah-FEH kohn LEH-cheh) — Coffee with milk
- **No puedo dormir sin música** (seen MOO-see-kah) — I can't sleep without music

### Sobre — about / on top of

- **Hablamos sobre el clima** (ah-BLAH-mohs SOH-breh ehl KLEE-mah) — We talk about the weather
- **El libro está sobre la mesa** — The book is on top of the table

### Entre — between / among

- **Entre tú y yo** (EHN-treh too ee yoh) — Between you and me
- **Está entre los árboles** (AHR-boh-lehs) — It is among the trees

### Hasta — until / as far as

- **Trabajo hasta las seis** (AHS-tah lahs seh-ees) — I work until six
- **Caminé hasta el río** — I walked as far as the river

### Por vs Para — a quick preview

These two both translate as "for" in English but have distinct uses (covered in a later lesson):
- **por** → cause, exchange, duration, movement through: *Gracias por tu ayuda* — Thanks for your help
- **para** → purpose, destination, deadline: *Esto es para ti* (PAH-rah tee) — This is for you
