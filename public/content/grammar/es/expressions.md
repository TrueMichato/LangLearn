# Common Expressions & Idioms

Spanish idioms bring language to life. Knowing fixed expressions — phrases whose meaning can't be guessed word-by-word — is a hallmark of fluency. These are used daily across Latin America and Spain, and they'll make your Spanish sound genuine and natural.

## Body-Part Idioms

Many vivid idioms are built around body parts:

| Expression | Literal meaning | Idiomatic meaning | Example |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|---------|
| **tomar el pelo** | to take the hair | to tease / pull someone's leg | *¿Me estás tomando el pelo?* — Are you pulling my leg? |
| **no tener pelos en la lengua** | to have no hairs on the tongue | to speak bluntly / be outspoken | *Ella no tiene pelos en la lengua.* |
| **costar un ojo de la cara** | to cost an eye from the face | to cost an arm and a leg | *Ese coche cuesta un ojo de la cara.* |
| **meter la pata** | to put in the paw/hoof | to mess up / put one's foot in it | *Metí la pata en la reunión.* |
| **hacer la vista gorda** | to make the fat view | to turn a blind eye | *El jefe hizo la vista gorda.* |

## Feelings & State of Mind

| Expression | Literal meaning | Idiomatic meaning | Example |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|---------|
| **echar de menos** | to throw from less | to miss someone/something | *Te echo de menos.* — I miss you. |
| **estar en las nubes** | to be in the clouds | to daydream / be distracted | *Estás en las nubes otra vez.* |
| **estar hecho polvo** | to be made of dust | to be exhausted / shattered | *Después del viaje, estoy hecho polvo.* |
| **estar como una cabra** | to be like a goat | to be crazy / off one's rocker | *¡Estás como una cabra!* |
| **tener ganas de + inf** | to have desire of | to feel like doing something | *Tengo ganas de comer pizza.* |

## Effort, Worth & Action

| Expression | Literal meaning | Idiomatic meaning | Example |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|---------|
| **valer la pena** | to be worth the pain | to be worth it | *Vale la pena intentarlo.* — It's worth trying. |
| **ser pan comido** | to be eaten bread | to be a piece of cake / very easy | *El examen fue pan comido.* |
| **dar la lata** | to give the tin/can | to bother / be annoying | *Deja de dar la lata.* — Stop being a pain. |
| **cruzar los dedos** | to cross the fingers | to keep fingers crossed | *Cruza los dedos por mí.* |

## Quick Pronunciation Tips

- **tomar el pelo** (toh-MAR ehl PEH-loh)
- **echar de menos** (eh-CHAR deh MEH-nohs)
- **valer la pena** (vah-LEHR lah PEH-nah)
- **meter la pata** (meh-TEHR lah PAH-tah)
- **estar hecho polvo** (ehs-TAR EH-choh POHL-voh)

> 💡 **Tip:** The best way to internalize idioms is to hear and use them in context. When you encounter a new one, note the full sentence it appeared in — not just the expression in isolation.
