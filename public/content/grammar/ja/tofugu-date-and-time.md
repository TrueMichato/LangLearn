# Date and Time

<!-- vocab-inventory: {"mode":"explicit"} -->

> **Source:** Content from [Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese-grammar/date-and-time/). For personal study.

The date format in Japan is **Year → Month → Day → (Weekday)**, from most general to most specific. Time uses 〜時 (hour), 〜分 (minute), and 〜秒 (second).

## Date

### 〜年 (ねん) — Year
Used with numbers: 2024年 (にせんにじゅうよねん). Japan also uses the imperial calendar (e.g., 令和6年 = 2024).

Notable readings: 四時 = **よ**じ, 七時 = **しち**じ, 九時 = **く**じ.

Both 12-hour and 24-hour formats are used. 午前 (ごぜん) = AM, 午後 (ごご) = PM.

### 〜分 (ふん/ぷん) — Minute

The minute counter alternates between ふん and ぷん; some combinations also geminate the preceding consonant:
- 一分 = い**っぷん**, 六分 = ろ**っぷん**, 十分 = じゅ**っぷん**

十分 can also be read じっぷん when it means ten minutes.

## Approximate Time

### 〜くらい / 〜頃 (ころ) — "Around ~"

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"point-duration-ni","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"〜くらい / 〜頃 (ころ) — \"Around ~\"","rule":"に distinguishes the time-point reading in the paired forty-five-minute examples","example":"How does 四十五分くらいにバスに乗る differ from 四十五分くらいバスに乗る?","answer":"With に, boarding is at around :45; without it in this pair, riding lasts about forty-five minutes.","explanation":"に marks the point of boarding rather than turning a duration into a place."} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"rule-goro-point-only","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"〜くらい / 〜頃 (ころ) — \"Around ~\"","rule":"頃 approximates only a point in time; くらい can approximate a point or a duration","example":"Which 'around' word can approximate only a point in time, never a duration?","answer":"ごろ (頃).","explanation":"くらい/ぐらい can approximate either a clock time or a length of time."} -->
- 七時**くらい**にそっちに行くね。 — I'll be there **around** seven. (casual)
- 七時**頃**にそちらに伺います。 — I'll be there **around** seven. (formal)

**Key difference:** くらい can mean approximate duration OR point in time. 頃 only means a point in time.
- 四十五分くらい**に**バスに乗る — at around :45 (point in time, に required)
- 四十五分くらいバスに乗る — for about 45 minutes (duration, no に)
