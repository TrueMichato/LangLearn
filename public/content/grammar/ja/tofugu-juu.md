中（じゅう） follows a location or time-specific word to mean "everything within the area" or "all throughout the period of time," respectively.

## The Basics

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"rule-juu-reading","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"The Basics","rule":"中 read じゅう after a place or period word means everything within it / all throughout it","example":"When the suffix 中 follows a place or time-period word to mean 'all over' or 'all throughout', how is it read?","answer":"じゅう","explanation":"Answering じゅう (juu) counts; contrast ちゅう 'during' and なか 'inside'. 家中 (うちじゅう), 一晩中 (ひとばんじゅう)."} -->

The word 中, meaning "inside" or "middle," can be read なか, ちゅう, and じゅう depending on the type of word it follows, and importantly, context.

When  中(じゅう) follows a word that signifies a particular location or a particular period of time, it’s read じゅう and signifies "everything *within* that area" or "all throughout that period of time," respectively.

These are both pretty basic uses of  中(じゅう), but we’ll go over them in some detail to make sure you understand how they work.

Note: We also have separate grammar pages for 〜 中(ちゅう) and 〜 中(なか), as well as an article that compares 中 and 内. If you aren't familiar with these topics, check their dedicated pages as well!

First, let's check out the spatial use of  中(じゅう). As a quick review, when  中(じゅう) follows a word for a particular area, it indicates "everything *within* that area."

Imagine you decide to do some spring cleaning and are determined to make the entire house sparkle. In this situation, you can combine the words  家(いえ) (house) and  中(じゅう), as in  家中(うちじゅう), and say:

  - 家**中**ピッカピカに掃除するぞー！

  - I'm going to clean **the whole** house, **from top to bottom**, and make it sparkle.

Here,  中(じゅう) adds the meaning of everywhere within 家, or the house, and conveys that you're fully motivated to make the entire house sparkly clean from top to bottom.

 中(じゅう) can be used to indicate more than just physical spaces. Suppose you’re dating a celebrity. Even though you kept it a total secret, one day some of your classmates catch wind of a rumor that you’re dating someone who is a big deal. In this case, you can combine クラス (class) with  中(じゅう), as in クラス 中(じゅう), and say:

  - クラス**中**で噂された。

  - The rumor was spread **throughout** the class.

In this example,  中(じゅう) denotes a boundaried area within which the rumor has spread.

The set phrase そこら 中(じゅう) can also be used in cases like this. The first part そこら is a word indicating an approximate location, like "around there." By adding  中(じゅう), the phrase basically indicates "everywhere," as in "here and there, all over the place."

So, say you’re a lookout for a famous criminal and you spot some danger. You might call out:

  - 気をつけろ！そこら**中**に警察がいるぞ！

  - Look out! There are cops **everywhere**!

Sometimes, this expression is also written as そこいら 中(じゅう), but this is a bit old-fashioned.

Next, let's check out the temporal use of  中(じゅう). As you learned earlier, when  中(じゅう) follows a time-specific word, it signifies "all throughout that period of time."

For example, if you stayed up all night playing video games, you can combine  一晩(ひとばん) (one night) and  中(じゅう), as in  一晩中(ひとばんじゅう), and say:

  - 一晩**中**ゲームをしていた。

  - I played video games **all** night.

In the same way, if you took care of a relative's child all day, you can combine  一日(いちにち) (one day) and  中(じゅう), as in  一日中(いちにちじゅう), and say:

  - 親戚の子供を一日**中**預かった。

  - I took care of a relative's child **all** day.

Due to the nuance, 〜 中(じゅう) is often used with ずっと (all through) to emphasize that something happened from beginning to end of it. For example, if you stayed home all through your summer vacation, you can combine 夏休み (summer vacation),  中(じゅう), and ずっと and say:

  - 夏休み**中**ずっと家にいた。

  - I was home **all through the** summer vacation.

Note that 夏休み中 can be pronounced either なつやすみ**じゅう** or なつやすみ**ちゅう**. But while なつやすみ**じゅう** means "all through the summer vacation," なつやすみ**ちゅう** simply means "*during* the summer vacation."

If you aren't familiar with 〜 中(ちゅう), you can learn more about it here.

Finally, 〜 中(じゅう) can't directly follow just any word that signifies a period of time.

For example, 三時間中, セール期間中, and 映画中 are not ordinary direct-suffix expressions for "throughout" with the reading じゅう. セール期間中 is perfectly usable with ちゅう to mean "during the sale period"; that is a different reading and emphasis. To express the full duration, use 〜の間 or 〜の間中, as below.

  - 三時間**の間**(**中**)

  - throughout the three hours

  - セール**の間**(**中**)

  - throughout the sale period

  - 映画**の間**(**中**)

  - throughout the movie

Although the use of each  中(じゅう) tends to follow certain trends, the "rule" as to which 中 can stick to which word isn't really clear cut, so you'll need to learn them by heart. But don't worry! If you keep practicing, you'll eventually get used to the common patterns and memorize them without knowing. 😊

By now, you can probably guess what  一年中(いちねんじゅう) means. It's a combination of 一年 (one year) and 中, so it means "all throughout the year." For instance, if you live in a tropical location that feels like summer all year-round, you can say:

  - 私が住んでいるところは、**一年中**夏だ。

  - Where I live, it's summer all year round.

Japanese has another similar word, which is  年中(ねんじゅう). It's a combination of 年 (year) and 中, but the meaning changes slightly without "一" (one). 1

Can you guess how the meaning changes?

While  一年中(いちねんじゅう) means "all year round" or "throughout the year,"  年中(ねんじゅう) is often used to mean "all the time." This is because  年中(ねんじゅう) technically means "throughout any whole year," and as a result, it evolved into a phrase to emphasize that something is happening all the time or constantly.

So, if a clothing store is running a series of sales promotions one after another throughout the year, you can either say:

  - この服屋は、[**一年中**・**年中**]セールをしている。

  - This clothing store has sales all year round.

But in this instance,  一年中(いちねんじゅう) simply denotes that the shop is holding a sale from January 1st through December 31st.  年中(ねんじゅう), on the other hand, indicates that the store always runs sales, regardless of the time of year.

Note there is also a longer expression,  年(ねん)がら 年中(ねんじゅう), which means the same as  年中(ねんじゅう), but serves to add extra emphasis.

  

    - 
      
年中 can also be read ねんちゅう. In this case, it refers to the middle class (4-5 years old) of a three-year kindergarten program in Japan. ↩
