# こそあど言葉 (Ko-So-A-Do Words) — Overview

<!-- vocab-inventory: {"mode":"explicit"} -->

> **Source:** Content from [Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese-grammar/kosoado/). For personal study.

こそあど言葉 are sets of Japanese words beginning with こ, そ, あ, or ど. They refer to things, people, places, and manners based on **relative distance** from the speaker and listener.

## The こそあど System

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"distance-system","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"The こそあど System","rule":"こ, そ, あ, and ど encode a deictic contrast rather than interchangeable English that forms","example":"When speaker and listener face each other, what reference zones do こ, そ, and あ normally distinguish?","answer":"Near the speaker, near the listener, and far from both, respectively.","explanation":"ど supplies the question member. The exact zone depends on the participants' shared viewpoint."} -->

| Prefix | Meaning | Distance |
|---|---|---|
| こ | This (close to speaker) | Near |
| そ | That (near listener / mid-distance) | Away |
| あ | That over there (far from both) | Distant |
| ど | Question word | ? |

| Category | こ (close) | そ (away) | あ (distant) | ど (question) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Things | これ/この〜 | それ/その〜 | あれ/あの〜 | どれ/どの〜 |
| People | こいつ | そいつ | あいつ | どいつ |
| Place | ここ | そこ | あそこ | どこ |
| Direction | こちら/こっち | そちら/そっち | あちら/あっち | どちら/どっち |
| Manner | こう/こんな | そう/そんな | ああ/あんな | どう/どんな |

The こいつ/そいつ/あいつ/どいつ series is very casual and can be disrespectful. It is not the neutral person-reference equivalent of the polite こちら series.

## Three Grammar Types

### Pronouns (stand alone like nouns)
- **これ**はネコです。 — **This** is a cat.
- **ここ**に座ってください。 — Please sit **here**.

### Noun Modifiers (attach to nouns)
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"noun-modifier-kono","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Noun Modifiers (attach to nouns)","rule":"The -の members of each set modify a noun","hint":"this, directly before a noun","example":"___本(ほん)は面白(おもしろ)いです。","answer":"この","explanation":"この attaches directly to 本; これ is a standalone pronoun, not the modifier in this frame."} -->
- **その**ネコは白い。 — **That** cat is white.
- **どの**山？ — **Which** mountain?

### Adverbs (modify verbs/adjectives)
- **ああ**するのはどう？ — How about doing it **like that**?
- **こう**書いてください。 — Please write it **this way**.

## Distance Rules

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"shared-viewpoint","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Distance Rules","rule":"A shared side-by-side viewpoint changes the practical reference zones","example":"When speaker and listener stand side by side, must そ refer to something held by the listener?","answer":"No. It commonly marks a medium-distance area away from their shared position, with こ close to both and あ farther away.","explanation":"The facing-each-other diagram is not the only spatial arrangement."} -->

**When speaker and listener are side by side:**
- こ = close to both
- そ = medium distance
- あ = far from both

**When speaker and listener face each other:**
- こ = close to speaker
- そ = close to listener (or far from speaker)
- あ = far from both
