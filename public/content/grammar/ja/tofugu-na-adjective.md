# な-Adjectives — Overview

<!-- vocab-inventory: {"mode":"explicit"} -->

> **Source:** Content from [Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese-grammar/na-adjective/). For personal study.

な-adjectives are one of two adjective types in Japanese (alongside い-adjectives). They describe nouns and share many traits with nouns — they cannot be conjugated for tense and instead use だ/です.

## Present Tense

<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-benri","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Present Tense","word":"便利","reading":"べんり","meaning":"convenient"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-shizuka","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Present Tense","word":"静か","reading":"しずか","meaning":"quiet"} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"predicate-copula","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Present Tense","rule":"A な-adjective's stem stays stable while the copula carries tense and politeness","example":"Does the stem 便利 itself change when 便利だ becomes 便利です?","answer":"No. The copula changes to make the predicate polite; the adjective stem stays 便利.","explanation":"In casual speech the copula can be omitted, as in これは便利."} -->

Use だ (plain) or です (polite):

- 日本の電車はすごく便利**だ**。 — Trains in Japan are very convenient.
- 平日なら、車より電車の方が便利**です**よ。 — On weekdays, the train is more convenient than a car.

Casual speech can drop the copula entirely:
- これは便利。 — This is convenient.

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"quiet-predicate","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Present Tense","rule":"A な-adjective drops な before です","hint":"quiet","example":"この町(まち)は___です。","answer":"静か","explanation":"静か (しずか) directly precedes です; the attributive な is not used in 静かです."} -->

## Past Tense

<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-ninki","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Past Tense","word":"人気","reading":"にんき","meaning":"popularity; popular appeal"} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"plain-past","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Past Tense","rule":"The plain past copula after a な-adjective is だった","example":"This town was quiet, plain form: この街は静か___。","answer":"だった","explanation":"静かだった changes the copula rather than using the い-adjective ending かった."} -->

Use だった (plain) or でした (polite):

- この街は静か**だった**。 — This town was quiet.
- ディスコはすごく人気**でした**。 — Disco was super popular.

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"polite-past","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Past Tense","rule":"な-adjectives take でした in the polite past","hint":"polite past","example":"昔(むかし)、ここは有名(ゆうめい)___。","answer":"でした","explanation":"有名でした follows the nominal copula pattern, not the い-adjective past ending."} -->

## Negative

<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-jouzu","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Negative","word":"上手","reading":"じょうず","meaning":"skillful; good at"} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"plain-negative","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Negative","rule":"A な-adjective uses a negative copula rather than くない","example":"Use the uncontracted plain negative: チェスは上手___。","answer":"ではない","explanation":"ではない or conversational じゃない negates the predicate."} -->

Use じゃない or ではない:

- 字があまり綺麗**じゃない**ね。 — The handwriting isn't very neat.
- チェスは上手**ではない**。 — I'm not good at chess.

## Position in a Sentence

### At the End (Predicate)
Describes the subject:
- 最近、猫のビデオが人気だよね。 — Cat videos are popular lately.

### Before a Noun
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"quiet-modifier","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Before a Noun","rule":"A な-adjective takes な when it modifies a noun","hint":"quiet, with the attributive linker","example":"___町(まち)に住(す)みたい。","answer":"静かな","explanation":"静かな町 (しずかなまち) uses な before the noun, unlike the predicate 静かです."} -->
Use な to connect:
- **安全な**場所 — a safe place
- **有名な**人 — a famous person

### As a Noun
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-anzen","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"As a Noun","word":"安全","reading":"あんぜん","meaning":"safety; safe"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-jiyuu","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"As a Noun","word":"自由","reading":"じゆう","meaning":"freedom; free"} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"nominal-use","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"As a Noun","rule":"Some な-adjective stems also have noun uses","example":"What role does 自由 have in 自由を守りましょう?","answer":"It is the noun freedom, marked as the object of protecting by を.","explanation":"A word can have both noun and adjectival uses; this does not mean every な-adjective is freely a noun in every context."} -->
Some な-adjectives can function as nouns:
- **安全**が第一だ。 — **Safety** comes first.
- **自由**を守りましょう。 — Let's protect our **freedom**.

### As an Adverb
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"adverb-ni","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"As an Adverb","rule":"に makes a な-adjective adverbial","example":"I closed the door quietly: 静か___ドアを閉めた。","answer":"に","explanation":"静かに modifies how the closing happens; 静かな would modify a following noun."} -->
Combine with に:
- **静かに**ドアを閉めた。 — I closed the door **quietly**.
- バラが**綺麗に**咲いていた。 — Roses were blooming **beautifully**.

## Origins of な-adjectives

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"origin-not-class","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Origins of な-adjectives","rule":"な-adjectives have native, Sino-Japanese, and borrowed origins","example":"Does being written in katakana rule out a word being a な-adjective?","answer":"No. Loanwords such as ユニーク can be な-adjectives, alongside native 静か and Sino-Japanese 綺麗.","explanation":"Origin and script help describe the vocabulary but do not replace learning each word's grammatical behavior."} -->

Three sources:
1. **Native Japanese** — words with hiragana endings like 〜か, 〜やか: 静か, 賑やか
2. **Chinese origin** (≈ two-thirds) — written in kanji: 綺麗, 安全, 健康
3. **Foreign loanwords** — written in katakana: ユニーク, オリジナル, クレージー

## Common Mistakes

### 同じ (Same)
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-onaji","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"同じ (Same)","word":"同じ","reading":"おなじ","meaning":"same"} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"onaji-before-noun","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"同じ (Same)","rule":"同じ directly modifies an ordinary noun without な","example":"The same shoes: ___靴。 (Use the exceptional adjective for same.)","answer":"同じ","explanation":"同じ靴 is the ordinary pattern; 同じだ is possible as a predicate, and 同じなのに restores な before の."} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"onaji-nanoni","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"同じ (Same)","rule":"同じ takes な before の in なのに","example":"Even though it is the same: 同じ___のに。","answer":"な","explanation":"The special lack of な before an ordinary noun does not extend to 同じなのに."} -->
- As predicate: 同じ**だ** ✓ (normal な-adjective behavior)
- Before a noun: 同じ靴 ✓ (no な needed!)
- Before の in なのに: 同じ**な**のに ✓ (な reappears)

### Confusing な-adjectives with い-adjectives
Words like きれい **end** in い but are な-adjectives:
- ❌ きれ**かった** → ⭕ きれい**だった** (past)
- ❌ きれ**くない** → ⭕ きれい**じゃない** (negative)

**Tip:** 綺麗 spells the whole reading きれい in kanji, which can help you remember that its final /i/ is not an い-adjective ending. Treat spelling as a clue for this word, not a universal test; learn adjective class with vocabulary.
