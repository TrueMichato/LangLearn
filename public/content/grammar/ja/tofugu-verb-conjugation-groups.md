# Verb Conjugation Groups (Godan/Ichidan/Irregular)

<!-- vocab-inventory: {"mode":"explicit"} -->

> **Source:** Content from [Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese-grammar/verb-conjugation-groups/). For personal study.

Japanese verbs come in three types: **godan verbs** (五段動詞), **ichidan verbs** (一段動詞), and **irregular verbs** (変格動詞). Understanding verb types is essential because each type requires a different method of attaching conjugational verb endings.

## Godan Verbs (五段動詞)

Godan verbs are also called う-verbs, Group I verbs, or consonant-root verbs. Examples include:

- 読む (よむ) — to read
- 書く (かく) — to write
- 話す (はなす) — to speak
- 聞く (きく) — to listen

As these verbs conjugate, the final う-line character shifts through the five vowel sounds (あ, い, う, え, お). For example, with 聞く:

| Vowel Line | Japanese | Romaji | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| あ /a/ | 聞**か**ない | ki**ka**nai | not listen |
| い /i/ | 聞**き**ます | ki**ki**masu | listen (polite) |
| う /u/ | 聞**く** | ki**ku** | listen (plain) |
| え /e/ | 聞**け**る | ki**ke**ru | can listen |
| お /o/ | 聞**こ**う | ki**ko**u | let's listen |

The root of a godan verb ends in a **consonant** — hence the alternate name "consonant-root verbs."

## Ichidan Verbs (一段動詞)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"ichidan-potential","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Ichidan Verbs (一段動詞)","rule":"The standard ichidan potential replaces る with られる","example":"食べる → standard can eat: 食べ___。","answer":"られる","explanation":"食べられる retains the vowel-root 食べ."} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"ichidan-volitional","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Ichidan Verbs (一段動詞)","rule":"Ichidan volitional replaces る with よう","example":"食べる → let's eat: 食べ___。","answer":"よう","explanation":"食べよう adds the ending to the unchanged root."} -->

Ichidan verbs are also called る-verbs, Group II verbs, or vowel-root verbs. Examples include:

- 見る (みる) — to see
- 起きる (おきる) — to wake up
- 開ける (あける) — to open
- 食べる (たべる) — to eat

Conjugating these verbs is straightforward — replace る with the new ending. The character before る stays the same:

| Japanese | Romaji | English |
|---|---|---|
| 食べない | tabenai | not eat |
| 食べます | tabemasu | eat (polite) |
| 食べる | taberu | eat (plain) |
| 食べられる | taberareru | can eat |
| 食べよう | tabeyou | let's eat |

The root ends in a **vowel** — hence "vowel-root verbs."

## Godan Verbs Disguised as Ichidan

Some godan verbs end in る and look like ichidan verbs. The trick:

- If the vowel before る is **/a/**, **/u/**, or **/o/** → **definitely godan**
- If the vowel before る is **/e/** or **/i/** → **probably ichidan** (but exceptions exist)

Common exceptions (godan verbs ending in /iru/ or /eru/): 要る (いる, to need), 入る (はいる, to enter), 走る (はしる, to run), 帰る (かえる, to return), 減る (へる, to decrease), 喋る (しゃべる, to chat). 要る is not the ichidan verb いる meaning "to be present." Set aside irregular する and 来る before applying the vowel shortcut.

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"irregular-members","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Irregular Verbs (変格動詞)","rule":"する and 来る form the two basic irregular conjugation classes","example":"Which two dictionary verbs must be set aside before using the godan/ichidan shortcut?","answer":"する and 来る (くる).","explanation":"These are the two basic irregular classes, not a denial that other verbs have individual exceptional forms."} -->

The two basic irregular conjugation classes are represented by **する** (to do) and **来る (くる)** (to come), including their compounds. Other verbs can still have individual exceptional forms; this classification does not claim that every other form is perfectly regular.

## 音便 (Sound Convenience) in Conjugations

Sound changes occur in the て form and past tense for easier pronunciation:

### Double Consonant (っ)
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"te-ru","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Double Consonant (っ)","rule":"Godan verbs ending in る take って in the て-form","hint":"撮る, て-form","example":"写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)___ください。","answer":"って","explanation":"Godan 撮る (とる, to take a photo) becomes 撮って through 音便; ichidan る instead drops directly before て."} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-kau","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Double Consonant (っ)","word":"買う","reading":"かう","meaning":"to buy"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-motsu","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Double Consonant (っ)","word":"持つ","reading":"もつ","meaning":"to hold; to carry"} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"te-gemination","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Double Consonant (っ)","rule":"Godan う and つ endings join the る group in taking って","example":"買う → て-form: ___.","answer":"買って","explanation":"買って (かって) has a small っ; 持つ similarly becomes 持って. The corresponding past ending is った."} -->
When verb roots end in /w/, /t/, or /r/:
- 買う → 買って (not 買いて)
- 持つ → 持って (not 持ちて)

### Assimilation (ん)
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-nomu","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Assimilation (ん)","word":"飲む","reading":"のむ","meaning":"to drink"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-shinu","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Assimilation (ん)","word":"死ぬ","reading":"しぬ","meaning":"to die"} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"te-nasal","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Assimilation (ん)","rule":"Godan む, ぬ, and ぶ use んで in the て-form","example":"飲む → て-form: ___.","answer":"飲んで","explanation":"飲んで (のんで) uses nasal ん and voiced で; 死ぬ becomes 死んで. The corresponding past ending is んだ."} -->
When verb roots end in /m/, /n/, or /b/:
- 飲む → 飲んで (not 飲みて)
- 死ぬ → 死んで (not 死にて)

### Consonant Removal
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-isogu","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Consonant Removal","word":"急ぐ","reading":"いそぐ","meaning":"to hurry"} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"te-ku","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Consonant Removal","rule":"Ordinary godan く verbs take いて","example":"聞く → て-form: ___.","answer":"聞いて","explanation":"聞いて (きいて) removes the stem consonant. 行く is an exception, taking 行って, so this is not an exception-free rule."} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"te-gu","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Consonant Removal","rule":"Godan ぐ verbs take voiced いで","example":"急ぐ → て-form: ___.","answer":"急いで","explanation":"急いで (いそいで) retains voicing as で; the matching past is 急いだ."} -->
When verb roots end in /k/ or /g/:
- 聞く → 聞いて (not 聞きて)
- 急ぐ → 急いで (not 急ぎて)
