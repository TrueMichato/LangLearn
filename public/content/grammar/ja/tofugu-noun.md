# Nouns — Overview

<!-- vocab-inventory: {"mode":"explicit"} -->

> **Source:** Content from [Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese-grammar/noun/). For personal study.

Nouns (名詞/めいし) are words used to identify things — people, places, objects, and abstract concepts. Japanese nouns don't change form for plural, gender, or grammatical case.

## Types of Nouns

### Common Nouns
General words that label the world: 犬 (dog), 学校 (school), 石 (stone).

### Proper Nouns
Specific names of people, places, etc.: 東京 (Tokyo), 田中 (Tanaka). These do not change form.

Nouns that become verbs when combined with する:

- 勉強 (study) → 勉強**する** (to study)
- 料理 (cooking) → 料理**する** (to cook)
- 運動 (exercise) → 運動**する** (to exercise)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"residence-ni","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Nouns and Particles","rule":"に marks the place of residence with 住む","example":"Live in Tokyo: 東京___住む。","answer":"に","explanation":"に identifies the residence location, not the means of travel."} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"means-de","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Nouns and Particles","rule":"で marks a means of transport","example":"Go by train: 電車___行く。","answer":"で","explanation":"The train is the means, not the destination."} -->

Particles give nouns their grammatical role:

| Particle | Role | Example |
|---|---|---|
| が | Subject | **猫が**いる (There is a cat) |
| を | Object | **寿司を**食べる (Eat sushi) |
| は | Topic | **今日は**暑い (Today is hot) |
| に | Location/time | **東京に**住む (Live in Tokyo) |
| で | Means/location | **電車で**行く (Go by train) |
| の | Possession | **私の**本 (My book) |

## Nouns as Predicates

Nouns can serve as the predicate (main info) of a sentence:

- これは**犬**だ。 — This is a **dog**.
- あの人は**先生**です。 — That person is a **teacher**.

## Politeness with Nouns

### Polite Prefixes: 御〜

- お〜 (often native Japanese words): お水 (water), お寿司 (sushi)
- ご〜 (often Sino-Japanese words): ご飯 (meal), ご意見 (opinion)

These are tendencies, not a way to prefix any noun mechanically: established exceptions such as お寿司 must be learned with the word.

## Modifying Nouns

### With い-adjectives
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"i-modifier","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"With い-adjectives","rule":"An い-adjective directly precedes the noun it modifies","example":"A big dog: 大き___犬。","answer":"い","explanation":"大きい犬 needs no な or の between adjective and noun."} -->
Placed directly before the noun:
- **大きい**犬 — a big dog
- **美味しい**寿司 — delicious sushi

### With な-adjectives
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"na-modifier","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"With な-adjectives","rule":"A な-adjective links to a following noun with な","example":"A quiet room: 静か___部屋。","answer":"な","explanation":"静かな部屋 uses the attributive な, unlike 静かです."} -->
Use な to connect:
- **きれいな**花 — a beautiful flower
- **静かな**部屋 — a quiet room

### With Particle の

- **日本の**文化 — Japanese culture

### With Clauses
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"clause-modifier","kind":"rule","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"With Clauses","rule":"A whole clause precedes the noun it modifies","example":"What does 昨日買った modify in 昨日買った本?","answer":"本; it identifies the book as the one bought yesterday.","explanation":"No English-style relative pronoun is required between the modifier and noun."} -->
Entire clauses can modify nouns (always placed before):
- **昨日買った**本 — the book **I bought yesterday**
