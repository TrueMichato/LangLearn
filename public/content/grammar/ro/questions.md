# Asking Questions

Romanian questions are usually direct and friendly. For yes/no questions, you often keep the same word order as a statement and rely on intonation or a question mark.

## Yes/No Questions

A statement can become a question without changing the word order.

| Statement | Question | English |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| **Tu vorbești românește.** | **Tu vorbești românește?** | Do you speak Romanian? |
| **Ea este acasă.** | **Ea este acasă?** | Is she at home? |
| **Aveți timp.** | **Aveți timp?** | Do you have time? |

- **Mergi la școală?** (MERJʲ lah SHKOH-ah-luh) — Are you going to school?
- **Ai o întrebare?** (ay oh uhn-treh-BAH-reh) — Do you have a question?

You can add **oare** to make a question sound thoughtful or softer:

- **Oare vine azi?** (WAH-reh VEE-neh ahzʲ) — I wonder if he/she is coming today.

## Common Question Words

| Romanian | English | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **cine** | who | **Cine este?** — Who is it? |
| **ce** | what | **Ce faci?** — What are you doing? |
| **unde** | where | **Unde locuiești?** — Where do you live? |
| **când** | when | **Când plecăm?** — When do we leave? |
| **cum** | how | **Cum te numești?** — What is your name? |
| **de ce** | why | **De ce înveți româna?** — Why are you learning Romanian? |
| **care** | which / what | **Care carte este a ta?** — Which book is yours? |

- **Cine vine la cină?** (CHEE-neh VEE-neh lah CHEE-nuh) — Who is coming to dinner?
- **Unde este gara?** (OON-deh YES-teh GAH-rah) — Where is the train station?
- **Cum ajung la muzeu?** (koom ah-ZHOONG lah moo-ZEW) — How do I get to the museum?

## Cât, Câtă, Câți, Câte

The question word **cât** asks **how much** or **how many** and agrees with the noun.

| Form | Use | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| **cât** | masculine/neuter singular or adverbial | **Cât costă?** — How much does it cost? |
| **câtă** | feminine singular | **Câtă apă vrei?** — How much water do you want? |
| **câți** | masculine plural | **Câți băieți sunt?** — How many boys are there? |
| **câte** | feminine/neuter plural | **Câte cărți ai?** — How many books do you have? |

- **Câți ani ai?** (kuhtsʲ anʲ ay) — How old are you?
- **Câte scaune sunt în cameră?** (KUH-teh SKOW-neh soont uhn KAH-meh-ruh) — How many chairs are in the room?

## Word Order with Question Words

Question words usually come at the beginning of the sentence.

- **Ce citești?** — What are you reading?
- **Unde mergem?** — Where are we going?
- **Când începe filmul?** — When does the movie start?
- **De ce nu vii?** — Why aren't you coming?

Romanian does not need English-style auxiliary **do**:

- Correct: **Ce vrei?** — What do you want?
- Not Romanian: **Ce faci vrei?**

## Polite Questions

For politeness, use **dumneavoastră**, **vă rog** (please), or a conditional form.

- **Aveți o masă liberă?** — Do you have a free table?
- **Îmi spuneți, vă rog, unde este stația?** — Could you tell me, please, where the station is?
- **Ați putea să mă ajutați?** — Could you help me?
