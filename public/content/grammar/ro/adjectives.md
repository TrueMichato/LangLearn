# Adjective Agreement

Romanian adjectives must agree with the noun they describe in **gender** and **number**. This means the adjective changes form for masculine, feminine, neuter, singular, and plural contexts.

## The Core Rule

An adjective follows the noun most of the time and matches it.

| Noun | Adjective | English |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **băiat bun** | masculine singular | good boy |
| **fată bună** | feminine singular | good girl |
| **băieți buni** | masculine plural | good boys |
| **fete bune** | feminine plural | good girls |
| **scaun bun** | neuter singular | good chair |
| **scaune bune** | neuter plural | good chairs |

Neuter nouns use masculine adjective forms in the singular and feminine adjective forms in the plural.

## Four-Form Adjectives: bun

Many adjectives have four visible forms.

| Gender/Number | Form | Example |
|---------------|------|---------|
| masculine singular | **bun** | **un băiat bun** — a good boy |
| feminine singular | **bună** | **o fată bună** — a good girl |
| masculine plural | **buni** | **băieți buni** — good boys |
| feminine/neuter plural | **bune** | **fete bune**, **scaune bune** — good girls/chairs |

- **Cafeaua este bună.** (kah-FEAH-wah YES-teh BOO-nuh) — The coffee is good.
- **Prieteni buni sunt importanți.** (pree-EH-tenʲ boonʲ soont eem-por-TAHNTSʲ) — Good friends are important.

## Two-Form Adjectives: mare

Some adjectives have one singular form and one plural form.

| Gender/Number | Form | Example |
|---------------|------|---------|
| masculine singular | **mare** | **un oraș mare** — a big city |
| feminine singular | **mare** | **o casă mare** — a big house |
| masculine plural | **mari** | **orașe mari** — big cities |
| feminine plural | **mari** | **case mari** — big houses |

- **Camera este mare.** (KAH-meh-rah YES-teh MAH-reh) — The room is big.
- **Camerele sunt mari.** (KAH-meh-reh-leh soont marʲ) — The rooms are big.

Other common adjectives like this include **dulce / dulci** (sweet), **rece / reci** (cold), and **verde / verzi** (green).

## Position of Adjectives

Most Romanian adjectives come **after** the noun.

- **o carte interesantă** — an interesting book
- **un om bun** — a good person
- **un oraș frumos** — a beautiful city

Some adjectives can come before the noun for style or emphasis, but this may change nuance:

- **un prieten bun** — a good friend
- **un bun prieten** — a good/dear friend (more emphatic)

As a learner, put adjectives after nouns unless you have learned a fixed phrase.

## Colors and Common Adjectives

| Masculine | Feminine | Masculine Plural | Feminine/Neuter Plural | English |
|-----------|----------|------------------|------------------------|---------|
| **frumos** | frumoasă | frumoși | frumoase | beautiful |
| **nou** | nouă | noi | noi | new |
| **vechi** | veche | vechi | vechi | old |
| **roșu** | roșie | roșii | roșii | red |
| **albastru** | albastră | albaștri | albastre | blue |

- **Am o mașină roșie.** (ahm oh mah-SHEE-nuh ROH-shee-eh) — I have a red car.
- **Văd doi ochi albaștri.** (vuhd doy okʲ ahl-BAHSH-tree) — I see two blue eyes.
- **Cumpăr haine noi.** (koom-PUHR HAI-neh noy) — I am buying new clothes.
