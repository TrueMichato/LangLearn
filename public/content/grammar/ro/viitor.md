# The Future Tense (voi, o să, am să)

Romanian has several ways to talk about the future. The most useful are **voi + verb**, **o să + verb**, and **am să + verb**.

## Formal Future: voi + Infinitive

The literary or formal future uses an auxiliary plus the infinitive without **a**.

| Pronoun | Future of a merge | English |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **eu** | **voi merge** | I will go |
| **tu** | **vei merge** | you will go |
| **el / ea** | **va merge** | he / she will go |
| **noi** | **vom merge** | we will go |
| **voi** | **veți merge** | you all will go |
| **ei / ele** | **vor merge** | they will go |

- **Voi merge mâine.** (voy MER-jeh MUH-ee-neh) — I will go tomorrow.
- **Ea va lucra aici.** (yah vah loo-KRAH ah-CH) — She will work here.
- **Vom vedea.** (vom veh-DEH-ah) — We will see.

## Everyday Future: o să + Subjunctive Form

In speech, **o să** is very common. The **o** does not change, but the verb after **să** changes with the subject.

| Pronoun | Future of a merge | English |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **eu** | **o să merg** | I am going to go / I will go |
| **tu** | **o să mergi** | you will go |
| **el / ea** | **o să meargă** | he / she will go |
| **noi** | **o să mergem** | we will go |
| **voi** | **o să mergeți** | you all will go |
| **ei / ele** | **o să meargă** | they will go |

- **O să învăț româna.** (oh suh uhn-VUHTS roh-MUH-nah) — I am going to learn Romanian.
- **O să venim la timp.** (oh suh veh-NEEM lah teemp) — We will come on time.
- **O să fie bine.** (oh suh FEE-eh BEE-neh) — It will be fine.

## Intention Future: am să + Subjunctive Form

Another spoken future uses forms of **a avea** plus **să**.

| Pronoun | Example | English |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **eu** | **am să plec** | I am going to leave |
| **tu** | **ai să pleci** | you are going to leave |
| **el / ea** | **are să plece** | he / she is going to leave |
| **noi** | **avem să plecăm** | we are going to leave |
| **voi** | **aveți să plecați** | you all are going to leave |
| **ei / ele** | **au să plece** | they are going to leave |

This construction can sound intentional or emphatic, depending on context.

- **Am să citesc lecția.** — I am going to read the lesson.
- **Ai să vezi!** — You'll see!

## Negation

Put **nu** before the future marker or auxiliary.

- **Nu voi merge.** — I will not go.
- **Nu o să vin.** — I will not come.
- **Nu am să uit.** — I will not forget.

In speech, **nu o să** is often pronounced quickly, but write it clearly while learning.

## Choosing a Future Form

| Form | Style | Example |
|------|-------|---------|
| **voi merge** | formal, written, neutral | **Voi trimite documentul.** |
| **o să merg** | common spoken Romanian | **O să vin la cină.** |
| **am să merg** | spoken, intention/emphasis | **Am să termin azi.** |

All three can translate as **I will...** or **I am going to...**. For everyday conversation, **o să** is very useful.
