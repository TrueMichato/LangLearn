# Cognates & False Friends

Romanian is a Romance language, so many words look familiar to English speakers through Latin, French, or Italian. These **cognates** are helpful, but some familiar-looking words are **false friends** with different meanings.

## Helpful Cognates

Many Romanian words are close to English words in form and meaning.

| Romanian | English | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **familie** | family | **Familia mea este mare.** — My family is big. |
| **important** | important | **Este un document important.** — It is an important document. |
| **restaurant** | restaurant | **Mergem la restaurant.** — We are going to a restaurant. |
| **universitate** | university | **Studiez la universitate.** — I study at the university. |
| **doctor** | doctor | **Doctorul este aici.** — The doctor is here. |
| **problemă** | problem | **Avem o problemă.** — We have a problem. |
| **muzică** | music | **Îmi place muzica.** — I like music. |

- **Am o idee bună.** (ahm oh ee-DEH-eh BOO-nuh) — I have a good idea.
- **Situația este normală.** (see-too-AH-tsee-ah YES-teh nor-MAH-luh) — The situation is normal.

## Spelling Changes in Cognates

Cognates may have Romanian endings or diacritics.

| English | Romanian | Notes |
|---------|----------|-------|
| nation | **națiune** | English -tion often corresponds to Romanian -țiune or -ție |
| information | **informație** | feminine noun ending in -ție |
| culture | **cultură** | final -ă is common |
| traditional | **tradițional** | ț represents ts |
| discussion | **discuție** | sc + u keeps hard sk sound |

- **Informația este utilă.** — The information is useful.
- **Cultura română este bogată.** — Romanian culture is rich.

## False Friends to Learn Early

Some Romanian words look familiar but mean something else.

| Romanian | Means | Not English... | Better English Translation |
|----------|-------|----------------|----------------------------|
| **actual** | current, present-day | actual | **real**, **adevărat** |
| **eventual** | possible, if necessary | eventual | **în cele din urmă** for eventually |
| **simpatic** | nice, likeable | sympathetic | **compătimitor**, **plin de compasiune** |
| **librărie** | bookstore | library | **bibliotecă** |
| **magazin** | shop, store | magazine | **revistă** |
| **cameră** | room; also camera in compounds | camera only | **aparat foto** for photo camera |
| **sensibil** | sensitive | sensible | **rezonabil** |

- **Locuiesc în camera aceasta.** — I live in this room.
- **Cumpăr o carte la librărie.** — I buy a book at the bookstore.
- **Revista este pe masă.** — The magazine is on the table.

## Actual vs Adevărat

Romanian **actual** means **current** or **present-day**.

- **președintele actual** — the current president
- **situația actuală** — the current situation

For English **actual** meaning **real**, use **real** or **adevărat**.

- **Este o problemă reală.** — It is an actual/real problem.
- **Este adevărat?** — Is it true?

## Simpatic and Sensibil

Two common false friends involve personality.

- **simpatic** — nice, pleasant, likeable
- **sensibil** — sensitive, easily affected
- **rezonabil** — sensible, reasonable

- **Profesorul este simpatic.** — The teacher is nice.
- **Copilul este sensibil.** — The child is sensitive.
- **Este o idee rezonabilă.** — It is a sensible idea.

## Strategy for Cognates

Cognates are useful guesses, not guarantees. When you meet a familiar-looking word:

1. Check the gender and plural: **o informație, două informații**.
2. Check pronunciation and diacritics: **națiune**, **tradițional**.
3. Confirm meaning in context, especially for false friends.

- **Este un argument valid.** — It is a valid argument.
- **Am citit un articol interesant.** — I read an interesting article.
- **Am nevoie de un dicționar bun.** — I need a good dictionary.
