# Present Tense: Group 4 (-i / -î) Verbs

Group 4 verbs end in **-i** or **-î** in the infinitive. Many everyday verbs belong here: **a citi** (to read), **a vorbi** (to speak), **a dormi** (to sleep), **a hotărî** (to decide).

## Two Common Patterns

Group 4 has two very common present-tense patterns:

1. A simple pattern, as in **a dormi** — to sleep
2. An **-esc-** pattern, as in **a citi** — to read

You must learn which pattern a verb uses, but the **-esc-** pattern is extremely common.

## Pattern 1: a dormi — simple forms

| Pronoun | Form | English |
|---------|------|---------|
| **eu** | **dorm** | I sleep |
| **tu** | **dormi** | you sleep |
| **el / ea** | **doarme** | he / she sleeps |
| **noi** | **dormim** | we sleep |
| **voi** | **dormiți** | you all sleep |
| **ei / ele** | **dorm** | they sleep |

- **Eu dorm bine.** (yew dorm BEE-neh) — I sleep well.
- **Copilul doarme.** (koh-PEE-lool DOAR-meh) — The child is sleeping.
- **Noi dormim opt ore.** (noy dor-MEEM opt OH-reh) — We sleep eight hours.

Notice that **eu dorm** and **ei dorm** are identical.

## Pattern 2: a citi — with -esc-

| Pronoun | Form | English |
|---------|------|---------|
| **eu** | **citesc** | I read |
| **tu** | **citești** | you read |
| **el / ea** | **citește** | he / she reads |
| **noi** | **citim** | we read |
| **voi** | **citiți** | you all read |
| **ei / ele** | **citesc** | they read |

- **Eu citesc o carte.** (yew chee-TESK oh KAR-teh) — I read a book.
- **Ea citește ziarul.** (yah chee-TESH-teh ZYAH-rool) — She reads the newspaper.
- **Voi citiți repede.** (voy chee-TEETSʲ REH-peh-deh) — You all read quickly.

## More -esc- Verbs

| Infinitive | eu | el/ea | noi | English |
|------------|----|-------|-----|---------|
| **a vorbi** | vorbesc | vorbește | vorbim | to speak |
| **a locui** | locuiesc | locuiește | locuim | to live/reside |
| **a iubi** | iubesc | iubește | iubim | to love |
| **a primi** | primesc | primește | primim | to receive |

- **Vorbesc românește.** (vor-BESK roh-muh-NESH-teh) — I speak Romanian.
- **Locuim în București.** (loh-koo-EEM uhn boo-koo-RESH-tʲ) — We live in Bucharest.

## Verbs Ending in -î

Verbs ending in **-î** are fewer but important. The letter **î** often becomes **â** inside the word in **noi** and **voi** forms.

| Infinitive | eu | tu | el/ea | noi | voi | English |
|------------|----|----|-------|-----|-----|---------|
| **a coborî** | cobor | cobori | coboară | coborâm | coborâți | to go down |
| **a hotărî** | hotărăsc | hotărăști | hotărăște | hotărâm | hotărâți | to decide |

- **Coborâm la prima stație.** (koh-boh-RUHM lah PREE-mah STAH-tsee-eh) — We get off at the first stop.
- **Ei hotărăsc repede.** (yay hoh-tuh-RUHSK REH-peh-deh) — They decide quickly.

## Negation and Useful Sentences

- **Nu dorm mult.** — I do not sleep much.
- **Nu citesc ziarul.** — I do not read the newspaper.
- **Vorbiți engleză?** — Do you speak English?
- **Coboară aici?** — Is he/she getting off here?
