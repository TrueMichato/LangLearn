# Reflexive Verbs (a se spăla)

Reflexive verbs show that the subject is involved in the action, often doing something to or for themselves. In Romanian, reflexive verbs use short pronouns like **mă**, **te**, **se**, **ne**, and **vă**.

## The Reflexive Pronouns

| Subject | Reflexive Pronoun | Example |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **eu** | **mă** | **mă spăl** — I wash myself |
| **tu** | **te** | **te speli** — you wash yourself |
| **el / ea** | **se** | **se spală** — he/she washes |
| **noi** | **ne** | **ne spălăm** — we wash ourselves |
| **voi** | **vă** | **vă spălați** — you all wash yourselves |
| **ei / ele** | **se** | **se spală** — they wash themselves |

The infinitive includes **se** in dictionaries: **a se spăla** — to wash oneself.

## a se spăla — to wash oneself

| Pronoun | Form | English |
|---------|------|---------|
| **eu** | **mă spăl** | I wash myself |
| **tu** | **te speli** | you wash yourself |
| **el / ea** | **se spală** | he/she washes |
| **noi** | **ne spălăm** | we wash ourselves |
| **voi** | **vă spălați** | you all wash yourselves |
| **ei / ele** | **se spală** | they wash themselves |

- **Mă spăl pe mâini.** (muh spuhl peh muhnʲ) — I wash my hands.
- **Ea se spală pe față.** (yah seh SPAH-luh peh FAH-tsuh) — She washes her face.
- **Ne spălăm înainte de cină.** (neh spuh-LUHM uhn-AIN-teh deh CHEE-nuh) — We wash before dinner.

## Common Reflexive Verbs

| Infinitive | Example | English |
|------------|---------|---------|
| **a se trezi** | **Mă trezesc devreme.** | I wake up early. |
| **a se culca** | **Se culcă târziu.** | He/she goes to bed late. |
| **a se numi** | **Mă numesc Ana.** | My name is Ana. |
| **a se simți** | **Ne simțim bine.** | We feel well. |
| **a se duce** | **Se duce la magazin.** | He/she is going to the store. |
| **a se îmbrăca** | **Vă îmbrăcați repede.** | You all get dressed quickly. |

Not every reflexive verb translates with **myself** in English. **Mă numesc Ana** literally means **I call myself Ana**, but naturally means **My name is Ana**.

## Negation

Put **nu** before the reflexive pronoun.

- **Nu mă duc.** — I am not going.
- **Nu te trezești devreme.** — You don't wake up early.
- **Nu se simte bine.** — He/she does not feel well.

The order is **nu + reflexive pronoun + verb**.

## Reflexive Verbs in Perfectul Compus

In the compound past, the reflexive pronoun combines with the auxiliary.

| Present | Perfectul Compus | English |
|---------|------------------|---------|
| **mă spăl** | **m-am spălat** | I washed |
| **te trezești** | **te-ai trezit** | you woke up |
| **se duce** | **s-a dus** | he/she went |
| **ne întâlnim** | **ne-am întâlnit** | we met |
| **vă îmbrăcați** | **v-ați îmbrăcat** | you all got dressed |
| **se culcă** | **s-au culcat** | they went to bed |

- **M-am trezit la șapte.** — I woke up at seven.
- **Ne-am întâlnit în parc.** — We met in the park.
