# The Imperative

The imperative is used for commands, instructions, invitations, and requests: **Come!**, **Read!**, **Please wait!** Romanian has different patterns for informal singular **tu** and plural/formal **voi / dumneavoastră**.

## Positive Commands: tu

Many positive singular commands use a special or present-like form.

| Infinitive | Command | English |
|------------|---------|---------|
| **a vorbi** | **Vorbește!** | Speak! |
| **a citi** | **Citește!** | Read! |
| **a lucra** | **Lucrează!** | Work! |
| **a cânta** | **Cântă!** | Sing! |
| **a merge** | **Mergi!** | Go! |
| **a veni** | **Vino!** | Come! |
| **a face** | **Fă!** | Do! / Make! |
| **a da** | **Dă!** | Give! |

- **Citește textul.** (chee-TESH-teh TEKS-tool) — Read the text.
- **Vino aici!** (VEE-noh ah-CH) — Come here!
- **Fă tema!** (fuh TEH-mah) — Do the homework!

## Negative Commands: tu

Negative singular commands usually use **nu + infinitive without a**.

| Positive | Negative | English |
|----------|----------|---------|
| **Vorbește!** | **Nu vorbi!** | Don't speak! |
| **Citește!** | **Nu citi!** | Don't read! |
| **Pleacă!** | **Nu pleca!** | Don't leave! |
| **Mergi!** | **Nu merge!** | Don't go! |
| **Fă!** | **Nu face!** | Don't do it! |
| **Dă!** | **Nu da!** | Don't give! |

- **Nu vorbi atât de repede.** — Don't speak so fast.
- **Nu pleca acum.** — Don't leave now.
- **Nu face zgomot.** — Don't make noise.

## Plural and Formal Commands

For **voi** or polite **dumneavoastră**, use the second-person plural present form.

| Infinitive | Command | English |
|------------|---------|---------|
| **a vorbi** | **Vorbiți!** | Speak! |
| **a citi** | **Citiți!** | Read! |
| **a merge** | **Mergeți!** | Go! |
| **a aștepta** | **Așteptați!** | Wait! |
| **a fi** | **Fiți!** | Be! |

- **Așteptați aici, vă rog.** (ahsh-tep-TAHTSʲ ah-CH vuh rog) — Wait here, please.
- **Vorbiți mai încet.** — Speak more slowly.
- **Fiți atenți.** — Be careful / attentive.

Negative plural/formal commands keep the same verb form after **nu**:

- **Nu plecați.** — Don't leave. (plural/formal)
- **Nu vorbiți tare.** — Don't speak loudly.

## Commands with Pronouns

With positive commands, short pronouns often attach after the verb with a hyphen.

- **Ajută-mă!** (ah-ZHOO-tuh-muh) — Help me!
- **Spune-mi!** (SPOO-neh-mʲ) — Tell me!
- **Dă-mi cartea!** (duh-mʲ KAR-tyah) — Give me the book!
- **Spală-te!** (SPAH-luh-teh) — Wash yourself!

With negative commands, the pronoun comes before the verb:

- **Nu mă ajuta.** — Don't help me.
- **Nu te spăla aici.** — Don't wash yourself here.

## Making Commands Kind

Commands can sound abrupt. Add **te rog** (please, informal) or **vă rog** (please, formal/plural).

- **Deschide fereastra, te rog.** — Open the window, please.
- **Repetați, vă rog.** — Repeat, please.
- **Vorbește mai încet, te rog.** — Speak more slowly, please.
