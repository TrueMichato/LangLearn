# Present Tense: a fi & a avea

The verbs **a fi** (to be) and **a avea** (to have) are essential and irregular. Learn them early: they appear in descriptions, possession, age, and compound tenses.

## a fi — to be

| Pronoun | Romanian | English |
|---------|----------|---------|
| **eu** | **sunt** | I am |
| **tu** | **ești** | you are |
| **el / ea** | **este** / **e** | he / she is |
| **noi** | **suntem** | we are |
| **voi** | **sunteți** | you all are |
| **ei / ele** | **sunt** | they are |

**Este** is the full form. **E** is very common in speech and everyday writing.

- **Eu sunt student.** (yew soont stoo-DENT) — I am a student. (male)
- **Ea este acasă.** (yah YES-teh ah-KAH-suh) — She is at home.
- **Noi suntem prieteni.** (noy SOON-tem pree-EH-tenʲ) — We are friends.
- **Ei sunt obosiți.** (yay soont oh-boh-SEETSʲ) — They are tired.

## a avea — to have

| Pronoun | Romanian | English |
|---------|----------|---------|
| **eu** | **am** | I have |
| **tu** | **ai** | you have |
| **el / ea** | **are** | he / she has |
| **noi** | **avem** | we have |
| **voi** | **aveți** | you all have |
| **ei / ele** | **au** | they have |

- **Eu am o carte.** (yew ahm oh KAR-teh) — I have a book.
- **Tu ai timp?** (too ay teemp) — Do you have time?
- **El are un frate.** (yel AH-reh oon FRAH-teh) — He has a brother.
- **Voi aveți întrebări.** (voy ah-VETSʲ uhn-treh-BUHRʲ) — You all have questions.

## Age Uses a avea

Romanian expresses age with **a avea**, literally **to have years**.

- **Am douăzeci de ani.** (ahm DOH-uh-zech deh anʲ) — I am twenty years old.
- **Ea are treizeci de ani.** (yah AH-reh tray-ZECH deh anʲ) — She is thirty years old.
- **Câți ani ai?** (kuhtsʲ anʲ ay) — How old are you?

Do not translate English **I am twenty** word-for-word with **sunt**. Use **am**.

## Descriptions with a fi

Use **a fi** for identity, location, condition, and adjectives.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **Sunt român.** | I am Romanian. (male) |
| **Sunt româncă.** | I am Romanian. (female) |
| **Cartea este pe masă.** | The book is on the table. |
| **Cafeaua e caldă.** | The coffee is hot. |
| **Suntem gata.** | We are ready. |

Adjectives still agree with the subject:

- **El este obosit.** — He is tired.
- **Ea este obosită.** — She is tired.
- **Ei sunt obosiți.** — They are tired. (masculine/mixed)
- **Ele sunt obosite.** — They are tired. (feminine)

## Negation and Questions

Put **nu** before the verb:

- **Nu sunt acasă.** (noo soont ah-KAH-suh) — I am not at home.
- **Nu am timp.** (noo ahm teemp) — I do not have time.

For yes/no questions, Romanian usually keeps the same word order and changes intonation:

- **Ai o carte?** — Do you have a book?
- **Sunteți gata?** — Are you ready?
