# Prepositions that Take the Genitive/Dative

Many Romanian prepositions are followed by ordinary accusative forms, but some important words and prepositional phrases require the **genitive** or **dative**. These are especially common when describing location, cause, opposition, and thanks.

## Genitive Prepositional Phrases

Many location phrases are built from a noun-like word with a definite article: **fața** (front), **spatele** (back), **jurul** (around). The following noun is in the genitive.

| Phrase | Example | English |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **în fața** | **în fața casei** | in front of the house |
| **în spatele** | **în spatele școlii** | behind the school |
| **în jurul** | **în jurul orașului** | around the city |
| **deasupra** | **deasupra mesei** | above the table |
| **dedesubtul** | **dedesubtul patului** | under the bed |
| **înaintea** | **înaintea examenului** | before the exam |

- **Mașina este în fața casei.** (mah-SHEE-nah YES-teh uhn FAH-tsah KAH-sey) — The car is in front of the house.
- **Pisica doarme deasupra dulapului.** — The cat sleeps on top of the wardrobe.
- **Ne întâlnim înaintea cursului.** — We meet before the class.

## Opposition and Cause

Several common prepositions also take genitive forms.

| Preposition | Example | English |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| **împotriva** | **împotriva deciziei** | against the decision |
| **contra** | **contra timpului** | against time |
| **asupra** | **asupra problemei** | on / upon the problem |
| **din cauza** | **din cauza ploii** | because of the rain |
| **în ciuda** | **în ciuda dificultăților** | despite the difficulties |

- **Au votat împotriva propunerii.** — They voted against the proposal.
- **Am întârziat din cauza traficului.** — I was late because of the traffic.
- **În ciuda frigului, am ieșit la plimbare.** — Despite the cold, we went out for a walk.

The noun after these expressions often has a form that looks like the definite genitive-dative: **ploii**, **traficului**, **deciziei**, **dificultăților**.

## Dative Prepositions

Some prepositions take the dative. These often express benefit, conformity, or thanks.

| Preposition | Example | English |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| **datorită** | **datorită ajutorului tău** | thanks to your help |
| **mulțumită** | **mulțumită prietenilor** | thanks to the friends |
| **grație** | **grație medicilor** | thanks to the doctors |
| **conform** | **conform regulilor** | according to the rules |
| **potrivit** | **potrivit legii** | according to the law |

- **Am reușit datorită profesorului.** — I succeeded thanks to the teacher.
- **Conform regulilor, trebuie să semnăm aici.** — According to the rules, we must sign here.
- **Potrivit legii, documentul este valabil.** — According to the law, the document is valid.

## Genitive-Dative Forms to Recognize

For many nouns, the genitive and dative forms are identical. The article and endings do the work.

| Base | Genitive/Dative | Example |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| **casa** | **casei** | **în fața casei** |
| **școala** | **școlii** | **în spatele școlii** |
| **orașul** | **orașului** | **în jurul orașului** |
| **regulile** | **regulilor** | **conform regulilor** |
| **prietenii** | **prietenilor** | **datorită prietenilor** |

Do not insert **la** after these prepositions: say **datorită mamei**, not **datorită la mama**, in standard Romanian.

## Choosing din cauza or datorită

Both can translate **because of**, but the nuance differs.

- **din cauza** often introduces a negative or neutral cause: **Am întârziat din cauza ploii.** — I was late because of the rain.
- **datorită** usually introduces a positive cause: **Am reușit datorită ajutorului.** — I succeeded thanks to the help.
