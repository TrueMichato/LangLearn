# Past Tense: Perfectul Compus

**Perfectul compus** is the main conversational past tense in Romanian. It is used for completed actions: **I ate**, **she arrived**, **we saw**, **they worked**.

## Formation

Perfectul compus uses a short form of **a avea** plus the **past participle**.

**am / ai / a / am / ați / au + past participle**

| Pronoun | Auxiliary | Example with a lucra | English |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|---------|
| **eu** | **am** | **am lucrat** | I worked |
| **tu** | **ai** | **ai lucrat** | you worked |
| **el / ea** | **a** | **a lucrat** | he / she worked |
| **noi** | **am** | **am lucrat** | we worked |
| **voi** | **ați** | **ați lucrat** | you all worked |
| **ei / ele** | **au** | **au lucrat** | they worked |

- **Am lucrat ieri.** (ahm loo-KRAHT yerʲ) — I worked yesterday.
- **Ea a ajuns târziu.** (yah ah ah-ZHOONS tuhr-ZEE-oo) — She arrived late.
- **Noi am văzut filmul.** (noy ahm vuh-ZOOT FEEL-mool) — We saw the movie.

## Past Participles

Past participles must be learned, but there are common patterns.

| Infinitive | Participle | Example | English |
|------------|------------|---------|---------|
| **a lucra** | **lucrat** | **am lucrat** | I worked |
| **a cânta** | **cântat** | **ai cântat** | you sang |
| **a citi** | **citit** | **a citit** | he/she read |
| **a dormi** | **dormit** | **am dormit** | we slept |
| **a vedea** | **văzut** | **ați văzut** | you all saw |
| **a face** | **făcut** | **au făcut** | they did/made |
| **a merge** | **mers** | **am mers** | I went |
| **a spune** | **spus** | **ai spus** | you said |
| **a fi** | **fost** | **a fost** | he/she was |

- **Am citit o carte.** (ahm chee-TEET oh KAR-teh) — I read a book.
- **Ai făcut cafea?** (ay fuh-KOOT kah-FEAH) — Did you make coffee?
- **Ei au mers la mare.** (yay ow mers lah MAH-reh) — They went to the seaside.

## The Participle Does Not Agree Here

In active perfectul compus, the participle normally stays the same for all subjects.

- **El a citit.** — He read.
- **Ea a citit.** — She read.
- **Ei au citit.** — They read.
- **Ele au citit.** — They read.

Do not change **citit** to match gender or number in this active tense.

## Negation and Questions

Put **nu** before the auxiliary:

- **Nu am înțeles.** (noo ahm uhn-tseh-LES) — I did not understand.
- **Nu a venit.** (noo ah veh-NEET) — He/she did not come.
- **Nu au terminat.** (noo ow ter-mee-NAHT) — They did not finish.

Questions usually keep the same order:

- **Ai mâncat?** (ay muhn-KAHT) — Did you eat?
- **Ați văzut filmul?** (ahtsʲ vuh-ZOOT FEEL-mool) — Did you all see the movie?

In speech and informal writing, **nu am** can contract to **n-am**, **nu a** to **n-a**, and **nu au** to **n-au**.

## Time Expressions with Perfectul Compus

Perfectul compus often appears with finished-time expressions:

- **ieri** — yesterday
- **aseară** — last night
- **săptămâna trecută** — last week
- **anul trecut** — last year
- **deja** — already

- **Am fost la Cluj săptămâna trecută.** — I was in Cluj last week.
- **Ea a terminat deja.** — She has already finished.
