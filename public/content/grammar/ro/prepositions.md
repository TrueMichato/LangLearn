# Common Prepositions

Prepositions connect nouns and pronouns to the rest of the sentence: **in**, **on**, **to**, **from**, **with**, **without**, **for**. Romanian prepositions are usually followed by the plain noun phrase, not by a special case ending in beginner patterns.

## Essential Prepositions

| Romanian | English | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **în** | in | **în casă** — in the house |
| **pe** | on; direct object marker | **pe masă** — on the table |
| **la** | at, to | **la școală** — at/to school |
| **de** | of, from, by | **o carte de gramatică** — a grammar book |
| **din** | from inside, out of | **din România** — from Romania |
| **cu** | with | **cu Maria** — with Maria |
| **fără** | without | **fără zahăr** — without sugar |
| **pentru** | for | **pentru tine** — for you |

- **Cartea este pe masă.** (KAR-tyah YES-teh peh MAH-suh) — The book is on the table.
- **Locuiesc în România.** (loh-koo-YESK uhn roh-muh-NEE-ah) — I live in Romania.
- **Merg la școală.** (merg lah SHKOH-ah-luh) — I go to school.

## în vs la

Both **în** and **la** can relate to location, but they are not identical.

### Use în for inside or within an area

- **Sunt în casă.** — I am in the house.
- **Locuiesc în București.** — I live in Bucharest.
- **Merg în parc.** — I am going into/to the park.

### Use la for at or to a place, institution, or event

- **Sunt la școală.** — I am at school.
- **Merg la restaurant.** — I am going to the restaurant.
- **Ne vedem la ora opt.** — We see each other at eight o'clock.

## pe: On and Direct Object Marker

**Pe** means **on** with locations:

- **Paharul este pe masă.** — The glass is on the table.
- **Tabloul este pe perete.** — The picture is on the wall.

**Pe** also marks many definite human direct objects.

- **Îl văd pe Andrei.** (uhl vuhd peh ahn-DRAY) — I see Andrei.
- **O aștept pe Maria.** (oh ahsh-TEPT peh mah-REE-ah) — I am waiting for Maria.

But ordinary inanimate direct objects usually do not take **pe**:

- **Văd cartea.** — I see the book.
- **Cumpăr pâinea.** — I buy the bread.

## More Location Prepositions

| Romanian | English | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **lângă** | next to | **lângă ușă** — next to the door |
| **între** | between | **între două case** — between two houses |
| **sub** | under | **sub masă** — under the table |
| **peste** | over, across, in after time | **peste drum** — across the road |
| **după** | after, behind | **după masă** — after lunch / behind the table by context |
| **înainte de** | before | **înainte de cină** — before dinner |

- **Pisica este sub scaun.** — The cat is under the chair.
- **Farmacia este lângă bancă.** — The pharmacy is next to the bank.

## About, From, and For

- **Vorbim despre România.** — We are talking about Romania.
- **Sunt din Canada.** — I am from Canada.
- **Cadoul este pentru tine.** — The gift is for you.
- **Beau ceai cu miere.** — I drink tea with honey.
