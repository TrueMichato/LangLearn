# Comparatives & Superlatives (mai, cel mai)

Romanian comparisons are built with **mai** (more), **mai puțin** (less), and **cel mai / cea mai / cei mai / cele mai** (the most). These patterns are regular and very useful.

## Comparative: mai + adjective/adverb

Use **mai** before an adjective or adverb to mean **more** or **-er**.

- **mai mare** — bigger
- **mai mic** — smaller
- **mai frumos** — more beautiful
- **mai repede** — faster
- **mai bine** — better

- **Bucureștiul este mai mare decât Clujul.** (boo-koo-RESH-tyool YES-teh mai MAH-reh deh-KUHT KLOO-zhool) — Bucharest is bigger than Cluj.
- **Ea vorbește mai repede.** (yah vor-BESH-teh mai REH-peh-deh) — She speaks faster.

## Than: decât

Use **decât** to introduce the thing being compared.

- **Maria este mai înaltă decât Ana.** — Maria is taller than Ana.
- **Trenul este mai rapid decât autobuzul.** — The train is faster than the bus.
- **Cafeaua este mai caldă decât ceaiul.** — The coffee is hotter than the tea.

In everyday speech, you may also hear **ca**, but **decât** is the standard comparative marker after **mai**.

## Less: mai puțin

Use **mai puțin** for **less**.

- **mai puțin scump** — less expensive
- **mai puțin interesant** — less interesting
- **mai puțin repede** — less quickly

- **Acest hotel este mai puțin scump decât acela.** — This hotel is less expensive than that one.
- **Lecția aceasta este mai puțin dificilă.** — This lesson is less difficult.

## Equality: la fel de ... ca

To say two things are equal, use **la fel de ... ca**.

- **El este la fel de înalt ca fratele lui.** — He is as tall as his brother.
- **Cartea este la fel de interesantă ca filmul.** — The book is as interesting as the movie.
- **Alerg la fel de repede ca tine.** — I run as fast as you.

## Superlative: cel mai, cea mai, cei mai, cele mai

The relative superlative means **the most** or **the -est**. The first word agrees with the noun.

| Noun Type | Superlative | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| masculine singular | **cel mai** | **cel mai bun student** — the best student |
| feminine singular | **cea mai** | **cea mai bună studentă** — the best female student |
| masculine plural | **cei mai** | **cei mai buni studenți** — the best students |
| feminine/neuter plural | **cele mai** | **cele mai bune cărți** — the best books |

- **Acesta este cel mai frumos oraș.** — This is the most beautiful city.
- **Aceasta este cea mai bună cafea.** — This is the best coffee.
- **Acestea sunt cele mai importante lucruri.** — These are the most important things.

## Very and Extremely

For an absolute superlative, use intensifiers such as **foarte** (very), **extrem de** (extremely), or **deosebit de** (particularly).

- **foarte bun** — very good
- **extrem de frumos** — extremely beautiful
- **deosebit de important** — particularly important

These do not compare one thing to another; they simply intensify.
