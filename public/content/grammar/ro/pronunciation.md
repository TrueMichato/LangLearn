# Alphabet, Diacritics & Pronunciation

Romanian is written with the Latin alphabet, and spelling is usually close to pronunciation. Once you know the five Romanian letters with diacritics, you can read new words with confidence.

## The Romanian Alphabet

Romanian uses 31 letters. The core Latin letters are joined by **ă**, **â**, **î**, **ș**, and **ț**. The letters **k**, **q**, **w**, and **y** appear mostly in loanwords and names.

| Letter | Sound | Example |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **a** | like "a" in father | **apă** (AH-puh) — water |
| **e** | usually "eh"; sometimes "ye" in pronouns/forms of a fi | **elev** (eh-LEV) — pupil |
| **i** | like "ee"; final i can be very short | **mic** (meek) — small |
| **o** | like "o" in more, shorter | **om** (ohm) — person |
| **u** | like "oo" in moon | **bun** (boon) — good |

Romanian stress is not always marked, so learn the stress with each word. In the hints here, capital letters show the stressed syllable.

## The Five Diacritics

These letters are not optional decoration. They distinguish words and must be typed correctly.

| Letter | Name | Pronunciation | Example |
|--------|------|---------------|---------|
| **ă** | a-breve | a reduced "uh" sound | **casă** (KAH-suh) — house |
| **â** | a-circumflex | central vowel, like **î** | **român** (roh-MUHN) — Romanian man |
| **î** | i-circumflex | same sound as **â** | **în** (uhn) — in |
| **ș** | s-comma | "sh" in shoe | **șapte** (SHAHP-teh) — seven |
| **ț** | t-comma | "ts" in cats | **țară** (TSAH-ruh) — country |

Use **ș** and **ț** with comma below, not the older cedilla forms. In modern Romanian, **â** is used inside words, while **î** is used at the beginning and end of words: **încep** (uhn-CHEP) — I begin, **a hotărî** (ah hoh-tuh-RUH) — to decide.

## C and G Before E or I

The letters **c** and **g** change sound before **e** and **i**:

| Spelling | Sound | Example |
|----------|-------|---------|
| **ca, co, cu** | k | **casă** (KAH-suh) — house |
| **ce, ci** | ch | **ceai** (chai) — tea, **cinci** (cheench) — five |
| **che, chi** | k | **cheie** (KEH-yeh) — key, **chitară** (kee-TAH-ruh) — guitar |
| **ga, go, gu** | g as in go | **gară** (GAH-ruh) — station |
| **ge, gi** | j as in judge | **genunchi** (jeh-NOONK) — knee, **gingie** (JEEN-jee-eh) — gum |
| **ghe, ghi** | hard g | **gheață** (GYAH-tsuh) — ice, **ghid** (geed) — guide |

- **Citesc o carte.** (chee-TESK oh KAR-teh) — I read a book.
- **Gheorghe are o cheie.** (GYOR-geh AH-reh oh KEH-yeh) — Gheorghe has a key.

## Final I and Palatalization

A final **i** after a consonant is often very short. It may soften the consonant rather than make a full syllable.

- **buni** (boonʲ) — good, masculine plural
- **români** (roh-MUHNʲ) — Romanians
- **frați** (frahtsʲ) — brothers

But when there are two written **i**, you hear a clearer vowel:

- **copii** (koh-PEE) — children
- **fiii** (FEE-ee) — the sons

## Other Useful Sounds

| Spelling | Sound | Example |
|----------|-------|---------|
| **j** | like French j or English s in measure | **joc** (zhok) — game |
| **r** | tapped or lightly rolled | **repede** (REH-peh-deh) — fast |
| **h** | pronounced, as in hotel | **hartă** (HAR-tuh) — map |
| **x** | ks or gz | **taxi** (TAK-see) — taxi, **examen** (eg-ZAH-men) — exam |

Romanian has no English-style "th" sound. Keep vowels clean and short.

## Stress and Accent

Romanian stress can fall on different syllables and is not written with an accent mark. Compare:

- **copil** (koh-PEEL) — child
- **masă** (MAH-suh) — table
- **mașină** (mah-SHEE-nuh) — car
- **universitate** (oo-nee-ver-see-TAH-teh) — university

When in doubt, check a dictionary or listen to a native speaker. Stress matters, but Romanian listeners can usually understand learners who pronounce each letter clearly.
