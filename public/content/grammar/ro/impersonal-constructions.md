# Impersonal Constructions (îmi place, îmi trebuie, mi-e frig)

Romanian often describes feelings, needs, and impressions with a **dative experiencer**. English says **I like**, **I need**, or **I am cold**; Romanian often says literally **to me is pleasing**, **to me is necessary**, or **to me is cold**.

## Dative Experiencer Pronouns

The person who feels, likes, or needs something is marked with a dative pronoun.

| Person | Before consonant | Before e / vowel in common contractions | English Idea |
|--------|------------------|------------------------------------------|--------------|
| eu | **îmi** | **mi-e** | to me |
| tu | **îți** | **ți-e** | to you |
| el / ea | **îi** | **îi e** | to him / her |
| noi | **ne** | **ne e** | to us |
| voi / dvs. | **vă** | **vă e** | to you |
| ei / ele | **le** | **le e** | to them |

- **Îmi place muzica.** (uhmʲ PLAH-cheh MOO-zee-kah) — I like the music.
- **Ți-e frig?** (tsyeh freeg) — Are you cold?
- **Le e foame.** — They are hungry.

## îmi place / îmi plac

With **a plăcea**, the thing liked controls the verb. Use **place** for one thing or an infinitive idea, and **plac** for plural things.

| Romanian | Natural English |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Îmi place cartea.** | I like the book. |
| **Îmi plac cărțile.** | I like the books. |
| **Îți place cafeaua?** | Do you like the coffee? |
| **Ne plac filmele românești.** | We like Romanian films. |
| **Îmi place să citesc.** | I like to read. |

- **Îmi place orașul.** (uhmʲ PLAH-cheh oh-RAH-shool) — I like the city.
- **Îmi plac orașele vechi.** (uhmʲ plahk oh-RAH-sheh-leh vekʲ) — I like old cities.
- **Vă place aici?** — Do you like it here?

## îmi trebuie

**Îmi trebuie** means **I need**. In everyday standard Romanian, **trebuie** usually stays the same, even when the thing needed is plural.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **Îmi trebuie un bilet.** | I need a ticket. |
| **Îmi trebuie două bilete.** | I need two tickets. |
| **Îți trebuie ajutor?** | Do you need help? |
| **Ne trebuie mai mult timp.** | We need more time. |
| **Vă trebuie pașaportul.** | You need the passport. |

- **Îmi trebuie o hartă.** (uhmʲ TREH-boo-yeh oh HAR-tuh) — I need a map.
- **Ne trebuie trei scaune.** — We need three chairs.
- **Nu îmi trebuie nimic.** — I don't need anything.

## mi-e frig, foame, somn

Many physical states use **mi-e** plus a noun or adjective. **Mi-e** is a contraction of **îmi este**.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **Mi-e frig.** | I am cold. |
| **Mi-e cald.** | I am hot. |
| **Mi-e foame.** | I am hungry. |
| **Mi-e sete.** | I am thirsty. |
| **Mi-e somn.** | I am sleepy. |
| **Mi-e dor de tine.** | I miss you. |

- **Mi-e foame după curs.** (myeh FOH-ah-meh DUH-puh koers) — I am hungry after class.
- **Ți-e somn?** — Are you sleepy?
- **Nu ne e frig.** — We are not cold.

## mi se pare and îmi vine să

Use **mi se pare** for impressions and **îmi vine să** for an impulse or urge.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **Mi se pare interesant.** | It seems interesting to me. |
| **Cartea mi se pare bună.** | The book seems good to me. |
| **Cărțile mi se par bune.** | The books seem good to me. |
| **Îmi vine să râd.** | I feel like laughing. |
| **Îmi vine să plec.** | I feel like leaving. |

- **Mi se pare că ai dreptate.** (mee seh PAH-reh kuh ay drehp-TAH-teh) — It seems to me that you are right.
- **Filmul mi se pare lung.** — The film seems long to me.
- **Îmi vine să plâng.** — I feel like crying.
