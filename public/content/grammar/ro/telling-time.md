# Telling Time & Dates

Romanian time expressions use **ora** for the hour, **la ora** for at a time, and everyday words such as **jumătate** (half), **un sfert** (a quarter), **și** (past), and **fără** (to / minus). Dates use lowercase days and months: **luni**, **martie**, **august**.

## Asking and Giving the Time

The most common question is **Cât e ceasul?** You will also hear **Ce oră este?**.

| Question / Answer | English |
|-------------------|---------|
| **Cât e ceasul?** | What time is it? |
| **Ce oră este?** | What time is it? |
| **E ora unu.** | It is one o'clock. |
| **E ora trei.** | It is three o'clock. |
| **Este ora douăsprezece.** | It is twelve o'clock. |

- **Cât e ceasul? E ora trei.** (kuht eh CHAH-sool eh OH-rah tray) — What time is it? It is three o'clock.
- **Este ora unu fix.** (YES-teh OH-rah OO-noo feeks) — It is exactly one o'clock.
- **E ora două.** — It is two o'clock.

## At a Time: la ora

Use **la ora** for scheduled times: at three o'clock, at eight thirty, at noon.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **la ora trei** | at three o'clock |
| **la ora opt dimineața** | at eight in the morning |
| **la ora șase seara** | at six in the evening |
| **la prânz** | at noon |
| **la miezul nopții** | at midnight |

- **Cursul începe la ora nouă.** (KOOR-sool ihn-CHEH-peh lah OH-rah NOH-uh) — The class starts at nine.
- **Trenul pleacă la ora șapte seara.** — The train leaves at seven in the evening.
- **Ne vedem la prânz.** — We will see each other at noon.

## Half, Quarter, Past, and To

For conversational time, Romanian often uses **și** for minutes after the hour and **fără** for minutes before the next hour.

| Time | Romanian | English |
|------|----------|---------|
| 3:15 | **trei și un sfert** | quarter past three |
| 3:30 | **trei și jumătate** | half past three |
| 3:45 | **patru fără un sfert** | quarter to four |
| 4:50 | **cinci fără zece** | ten to five |
| 6:05 | **șase și cinci** | five past six |

- **E trei și jumătate.** (eh tray shee zhoo-muh-TAH-teh) — It is half past three.
- **E patru fără un sfert.** (eh PAH-troo FUH-ruh oon sfert) — It is quarter to four.
- **Ajung la două și zece.** — I arrive at ten past two.

## Days and Months

Days and months are not capitalized unless they begin a sentence. Use the day name alone for on that day, and **în** with months.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **luni** | Monday / on Monday |
| **marți** | Tuesday / on Tuesday |
| **miercuri** | Wednesday / on Wednesday |
| **joi** | Thursday / on Thursday |
| **vineri** | Friday / on Friday |
| **sâmbătă** | Saturday / on Saturday |
| **duminică** | Sunday / on Sunday |

- **Plec luni.** (plek loonʲ) — I leave on Monday.
- **Avem examen vineri.** — We have an exam on Friday.
- **Mergem în iulie.** — We are going in July.

Common months include **ianuarie**, **februarie**, **martie**, **aprilie**, **mai**, **iunie**, **iulie**, **august**, **septembrie**, **octombrie**, **noiembrie**, **decembrie**.

## Dates and Years

Use **pe** before a calendar date when you mean on that date. Use **în** for years.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **pe 3 martie** | on March 3 |
| **pe 15 august** | on August 15 |
| **în 2024** | in 2024 |
| **din 2020** | since / from 2020 |
| **până în 2026** | until 2026 |

- **M-am născut pe 3 martie.** (mahm nuhs-KOOT peh tray mar-TEE-eh) — I was born on March 3.
- **Festivalul este în august.** — The festival is in August.
- **Locuiesc aici din 2020.** — I have lived here since 2020.
