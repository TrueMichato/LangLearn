# Word Order & Clitic Pronoun Placement

Romanian object pronouns such as **îl**, **o**, **îi**, **le**, **îmi**, and **îți** are clitics: short words that attach closely to the verb. Their position changes with tense, negation, and commands.

## Before Finite Verbs

With most finite verb forms, object clitics come before the verb.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **Îl văd.** | I see him/it. |
| **O cunosc.** | I know her. |
| **Îi scriu.** | I write to him/her/them. |
| **Le trimit un mesaj.** | I send them a message. |
| **Te aștept.** | I am waiting for you. |

- **Îl văd pe Mihai.** (uhl vuhd pe mee-HAY) — I see Mihai.
- **O citesc în fiecare seară.** — I read it every evening. (about a feminine noun such as **cartea**)
- **Îți mulțumesc.** — I thank you.

## With perfectul compus

In the compound past, the clitic usually attaches to the auxiliary with a hyphen when vowel contact requires it.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **L-am văzut.** | I saw him/it. |
| **Am văzut-o.** | I saw her/it. |
| **I-am spus adevărul.** | I told him/her the truth. |
| **Le-am cumpărat.** | I bought them. |
| **Nu l-am găsit.** | I did not find him/it. |

- **L-am întâlnit ieri.** — I met him yesterday.
- **Am citit-o deja.** — I have already read it. (about a feminine noun)
- **Nu i-am răspuns încă.** — I have not answered him/her yet.

## Double Clitics: Indirect Before Direct

When both an indirect and a direct object clitic appear, the indirect clitic comes first.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **Mi-l dă.** | He/she gives it to me. |
| **Ți-o aduc.** | I bring it to you. |
| **I le arată.** | He/she shows them to him/her. |
| **Ni le trimite.** | He/she sends them to us. |
| **Vi-i prezint.** | I introduce them to you all. |

- **Profesorul mi-l explică.** — The teacher explains it to me.
- **Maria ți-o trimite azi.** — Maria sends it to you today.
- **Nu ni le arată.** — He/she does not show them to us.

With the compound past, the cluster is split differently: **Mi l-a dat**, **Ți le-a arătat**, **Nu ni le-a trimis**.

## Negation

**Nu** comes before the clitic cluster.

- **Nu îl văd.** / **Nu-l văd.** — I do not see him/it.
- **Nu o cunosc.** / **N-o cunosc.** — I do not know her.
- **Nu mi-l dă.** — He/she does not give it to me.
- **Nu i-am spus nimic.** — I did not tell him/her anything.

Both full and contracted spellings occur, but learners should recognize the common hyphenated forms: **nu-l**, **n-o**, **nu i-am**.

## Commands: After Affirmative Imperatives

In affirmative commands, clitics usually attach after the imperative verb.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **Dă-mi cartea!** | Give me the book! |
| **Dă-mi-l!** | Give it to me! |
| **Adu-mi-o!** | Bring it to me! |
| **Spune-i adevărul!** | Tell him/her the truth! |
| **Trimite-le mesajul!** | Send them the message! |

- **Ajută-mă, te rog!** — Help me, please!
- **Sună-l mâine!** — Call him tomorrow!
- **Arată-ni-le!** — Show them to us!

In negative commands, the clitics go before the verb: **Nu mi-l da!**, **Nu o suna!**, **Nu le spune!**

## Infinitives and Gerunds

With infinitives and gerunds, clitics can attach after the verb form.

- **Pentru a-l vedea, am venit devreme.** — In order to see him, I came early.
- **Văzându-l, am zâmbit.** — Seeing him, I smiled.
- **Spunându-i adevărul, am fost sincer.** — Telling him/her the truth, I was honest.

For everyday speech, master these first: **îl văd**, **l-am văzut**, **mi-l dă**, **mi l-a dat**, **dă-mi-l**.
