# Demonstratives (acesta, acela, ăsta)

Demonstratives point to things: **this**, **that**, **these**, **those**. Romanian demonstratives agree in gender and number with the noun they refer to.

## This and That as Pronouns

When the demonstrative stands alone, use these forms:

| Meaning | Masculine Singular | Feminine Singular | Masculine Plural | Feminine/Neuter Plural |
|---------|--------------------|-------------------|------------------|------------------------|
| **this / these** | **acesta** | **aceasta** | **aceștia** | **acestea** |
| **that / those** | **acela** | **aceea** | **aceia** | **acelea** |

- **Acesta este fratele meu.** (ah-CHEH-stah YES-teh FRAH-teh-leh myew) — This is my brother.
- **Aceasta este cartea mea.** (ah-CHAS-tah YES-teh KAR-tyah myah) — This is my book.
- **Acelea sunt case vechi.** (ah-CHEH-lyah soont KAH-seh vekʲ) — Those are old houses.

## Before a Noun: acest, această, acești, aceste

Before a noun, Romanian uses shorter adjective forms for **this/these**:

| Noun Type | Form | Example |
|-----------|------|---------|
| masculine singular | **acest** | **acest băiat** — this boy |
| feminine singular | **această** | **această fată** — this girl |
| masculine plural | **acești** | **acești băieți** — these boys |
| feminine/neuter plural | **aceste** | **aceste fete**, **aceste scaune** — these girls/chairs |

- **Acest oraș este frumos.** — This city is beautiful.
- **Această lecție este utilă.** — This lesson is useful.
- **Aceste scaune sunt noi.** — These chairs are new.

## Before a Noun: acel, acea, acei, acele

For **that/those** before a noun, use:

| Noun Type | Form | Example |
|-----------|------|---------|
| masculine singular | **acel** | **acel băiat** — that boy |
| feminine singular | **acea** | **acea fată** — that girl |
| masculine plural | **acei** | **acei băieți** — those boys |
| feminine/neuter plural | **acele** | **acele case**, **acele lucruri** — those houses/things |

- **Acel tren pleacă acum.** — That train leaves now.
- **Acea casă este veche.** — That house is old.
- **Acele lucruri sunt importante.** — Those things are important.

## After a Noun: Noun + Demonstrative

Romanian very often places the demonstrative after a **definite** noun.

| Before Noun | After Noun | English |
|-------------|------------|---------|
| **acest băiat** | **băiatul acesta** | this boy |
| **această fată** | **fata aceasta** | this girl |
| **acel om** | **omul acela** | that man |
| **acele case** | **casele acelea** | those houses |

Both patterns are correct. The after-noun pattern is very common in speech.

- **Îmi place cartea aceasta.** (eemʲ PLAH-cheh KAR-tyah ah-CHAS-tah) — I like this book.
- **Nu cunosc omul acela.** (noo koo-NOSK OH-mool ah-CHEH-lah) — I don't know that man.

## Colloquial Forms: ăsta, asta, ăștia, astea

In everyday speech, Romanians often use shorter colloquial forms:

| Standard | Colloquial | English |
|----------|------------|---------|
| **acesta** | **ăsta** | this one (masc.) |
| **aceasta** | **asta** | this one (fem.) |
| **aceștia** | **ăștia** | these ones (masc.) |
| **acestea** | **astea** | these ones (fem./neut.) |

- **Ce e asta?** (cheh yeh AHS-tah) — What is this?
- **Ăsta este telefonul meu.** (UHS-tah YES-teh teh-leh-FOH-nool myew) — This is my phone.

Use standard forms in formal writing; understand colloquial forms in conversation.
