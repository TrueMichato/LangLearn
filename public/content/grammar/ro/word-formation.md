# Word Formation: Prefixes & Suffixes

Romanian builds many words with **prefixes** at the beginning and **suffixes** at the end. Learning common pieces helps you guess new words and recognize word families.

## Prefixes: Changing the Meaning

A **prefix** attaches before a base word. It can negate, repeat, reverse, or create a verb.

| Prefix | Main idea | Example | Meaning |
|--------|-----------|---------|---------|
| **ne-** | not / un- | **fericit → nefericit** | happy → unhappy |
| **re-** | again / back | **a face → a reface** | to do/make → to remake |
| **în- / îm-** | into / make | **bogat → a îmbogăți** | rich → to enrich |
| **des- / dez-** | undo / remove | **a face → a desface** | to do/make → to undo |

- **cunoscut → necunoscut** — known → unknown
- **a citi → a reciti** — to read → to reread
- **roșu → a înroși** — red → to redden / blush
- **a lega → a dezlega** — to tie → to untie

## ne- and re-

**Ne-** is one of the easiest prefixes to recognize. It often makes adjectives negative.

| Base | Derived word | English |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **fericit** | **nefericit** | unhappy |
| **sigur** | **nesigur** | unsure / unsafe |
| **cunoscut** | **necunoscut** | unknown |

**Re-** usually means doing something again or restoring a state.

- **a face → a reface** — to remake / restore
- **a vedea → a revedea** — to see again
- **a scrie → a rescrie** — to rewrite

<!-- grammar-card: {"rule":"The prefix ne- often makes adjectives negative","hint":"fericit -> nefericit","example":"sigur -> ___","answer":"nesigur","explanation":"Adding ne- to sigur gives nesigur, meaning unsure or unsafe."} -->

## în-, îm-, des-, dez-

Romanian writes **îm-** before **b** or **p**, and **în-** in many other contexts.

| Base | Derived word | English |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **bogat** | **a îmbogăți** | to enrich |
| **piatră** | **a împietri** | to turn to stone / become hardened |
| **roșu** | **a înroși** | to redden |
| **cald** | **a încălzi** | to warm |

**Des-** and **dez-** often show reversal, separation, or removal.

- **a face → a desface** — to undo / unwrap
- **a lega → a dezlega** — to untie
- **a lipi → a dezlipi** — to unstick / detach

## Agent Suffix -tor

The suffix **-tor** often forms a person or thing that does an action. Feminine forms commonly use **-toare**.

| Verb | Noun | English |
|------|------|---------|
| **a învăța** | **învățător / învățătoare** | teacher |
| **a conduce** | **conducător / conducătoare** | leader / driver |
| **a vâna** | **vânător / vânătoare** | hunter |

- **Învățătorul explică lecția.** — The teacher explains the lesson.
- **Un conducător bun ascultă.** — A good leader listens.

## Nominal Suffixes: -ție and -are

Romanian often turns verbs into nouns with **-ție** or **-are**. The exact suffix depends on the word family, so learn common examples as vocabulary.

| Verb | Noun | English |
|------|------|---------|
| **a educa** | **educație** | education |
| **a informa** | **informație** | information |
| **a organiza** | **organizare** | organization / organizing |
| **a lucra** | **lucrare** | work / paper |

- **Educația este importantă.** — Education is important.
- **Organizarea cere timp.** — Organizing takes time.

## Adjective and Collective Suffixes

The suffix **-esc** often makes adjectives meaning **related to** or **characteristic of**.

| Base | Derived adjective | English |
|------|-------------------|---------|
| **român** | **românesc** | Romanian / Romanian-style |
| **copil** | **copilăresc** | childish / childlike |
| **student** | **studențesc** | student-related |

The suffix **-ime** can make collective or abstract nouns.

- **om → omenime** — human being → humankind
- **mulți → mulțime** — many → crowd / multitude
- **tânăr → tinerime** — young person → youth / young people

## Diminutive Suffixes: -uț, -uță, -aș

Diminutives can show small size, affection, or a softer tone.

| Base | Diminutive | English |
|------|------------|---------|
| **casă** | **căsuță** | little house |
| **băiat** | **băiețaș** | little boy |
| **copil** | **copilaș** | little child |
| **drag** | **drăguț** | cute / dear |

Diminutives are common in speech, but their tone depends on context. **Căsuță** can sound warm; overusing diminutives can sound overly cute.
