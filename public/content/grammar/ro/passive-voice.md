# The Passive Voice (a fi + participle & the se-passive)

The passive voice focuses on what happens to the subject: **Cartea este citită** means **The book is read / is being read**. Romanian has two common passive patterns: **a fi + participle** and the very frequent **se-passive**.

## a fi + Past Participle

The formal passive is built with a form of **a fi** plus the past participle. The participle agrees with the subject in gender and number.

**a fi + participle**

| Subject | Passive | English |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **romanul** | **este citit** | the novel is read |
| **cartea** | **este citită** | the book is read |
| **biletele** | **sunt vândute** | the tickets are sold |
| **casele** | **sunt construite** | the houses are built |

- **Cartea este citită de Ana.** (KAR-teh-ah YES-teh chee-TEE-tuh) — The book is read by Ana.
- **Ușa este închisă.** (OO-shah YES-teh uhn-KEE-suh) — The door is closed.
- **Documentele sunt semnate.** — The documents are signed.

## Passive in Different Tenses

The verb **a fi** carries the tense. The participle still agrees with the subject.

| Tense | Romanian | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| present | **Podul este construit.** | The bridge is built / is being built. |
| perfect compus | **Podul a fost construit.** | The bridge was built. |
| imperfect | **Podul era construit.** | The bridge was being built / used to be built. |
| future | **Podul va fi construit.** | The bridge will be built. |

- **Scrisoarea a fost trimisă ieri.** — The letter was sent yesterday.
- **Blocurile vor fi renovate anul viitor.** — The apartment blocks will be renovated next year.
- **Regulile au fost explicate clar.** — The rules were explained clearly.

## Expressing the Agent

The person or force doing the action is introduced by **de** or, more explicitly, **de către**.

- **Casa a fost construită de bunicul meu.** — The house was built by my grandfather.
- **Legea a fost aprobată de către parlament.** — The law was approved by parliament.
- **Problema a fost rezolvată de o echipă mică.** — The problem was solved by a small team.

**De către** sounds more formal and is often used with people or institutions. In everyday speech, Romanian often avoids a heavy passive and uses an active sentence instead.

## The se-Passive

Romanian frequently uses **se + verb** when English uses a passive or an impersonal **one/people** construction.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **Aici se vorbește românește.** | Romanian is spoken here. |
| **Se vând bilete online.** | Tickets are sold online. |
| **Se caută voluntari.** | Volunteers are wanted. |
| **Așa se face mămăliga.** | This is how polenta is made. |

- **În această cameră nu se fumează.** — Smoking is not allowed in this room.
- **La ghișeu se plătește în numerar.** — At the counter, payment is made in cash.
- **Aceste cuvinte se folosesc des.** — These words are used often.

## Choosing Between the Two

Use **a fi + participle** when you need a clear passive subject and often an agent. Use **se + verb** for signs, rules, general customs, and impersonal statements.

- **Contractul a fost semnat de director.** — The contract was signed by the director.
- **Contractele se semnează la birou.** — Contracts are signed at the office.
- **Muzeul este închis luni.** — The museum is closed on Mondays.
- **Luni nu se lucrează.** — People do not work on Monday / Work is not done on Monday.

Be careful: **se** can also mark reflexive verbs, so meaning depends on the verb and context.
