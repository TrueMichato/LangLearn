# Noun Gender (Masculine, Feminine, Neuter)

Romanian nouns have **gender**. Every noun is **masculine**, **feminine**, or **neuter**, and this gender affects articles, adjectives, pronouns, and numbers.

## The Three Genders

| Gender | Singular Article | Plural Behavior | Examples |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|----------|
| **Masculine** | un | masculine plural | **un băiat** — a boy, **doi băieți** — two boys |
| **Feminine** | o | feminine plural | **o fată** — a girl, **două fete** — two girls |
| **Neuter** | un | feminine-like plural | **un scaun** — a chair, **două scaune** — two chairs |

The special Romanian feature is **neuter**: it behaves like masculine in the singular and like feminine in the plural. A useful learner test is **un / două**: **un scaun, două scaune**.

- **Un băiat citește.** (oon buh-YAHT chee-TESH-teh) — A boy is reading.
- **O fată cântă.** (oh FAH-tuh KUHN-tuh) — A girl is singing.
- **Un scaun este aici.** (oon skown YES-teh ah-CH) — A chair is here.

## Natural Gender

For people and many animals, grammatical gender usually follows natural sex:

| Masculine | Feminine | English |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| **băiat** | **fată** | boy / girl |
| **bărbat** | **femeie** | man / woman |
| **frate** | **soră** | brother / sister |
| **profesor** | **profesoară** | teacher |
| **student** | **studentă** | student |

- **Profesorul este român.** (proh-feh-SOH-rool yes-teh roh-MUHN) — The teacher is Romanian. (male)
- **Profesoara este româncă.** (proh-feh-soh-AH-rah yes-teh roh-MUHN-kuh) — The teacher is Romanian. (female)

## Common Gender Clues

Gender is partly predictable from endings, but there are exceptions. Learn nouns with their article.

### Often Feminine

| Ending | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| **-ă** | **casă** — house, **masă** — table, **școală** — school |
| **-ie** | **familie** — family, **lecție** — lesson |
| **-ție / -siune** | **stație** — station, **televiziune** — television |

### Often Masculine

| Ending | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| consonant for persons | **băiat** — boy, **student** — student |
| **-or** | **doctor** — doctor, **scriitor** — writer |
| **-e** for some people | **frate** — brother, **câine** — dog |

### Often Neuter

| Ending | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| consonant for objects | **scaun** — chair, **drum** — road |
| **-u** | **lucru** — thing, **teatru** — theater |
| **-ment** | **apartament** — apartment, **document** — document |

## Agreement Depends on Gender

Adjectives and some numbers change with the noun.

| Noun | Correct Phrase | English |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **băiat** (masc.) | **băiat bun** | good boy |
| **fată** (fem.) | **fată bună** | good girl |
| **scaun** (neut. sg.) | **scaun bun** | good chair |
| **scaune** (neut. pl.) | **scaune bune** | good chairs |

- **Am un telefon nou.** (ahm oon teh-leh-FON noh-oo) — I have a new phone.
- **Am două telefoane noi.** (ahm DOH-uh teh-leh-FOH-ah-neh noy) — I have two new phones.

## Why Gender Matters

If you know gender, you can choose the correct article and adjective:

- **un băiat înalt** — a tall boy
- **o fată înaltă** — a tall girl
- **un bloc înalt** — a tall apartment building
- **două blocuri înalte** — two tall apartment buildings

Notice again that **bloc** is neuter: **un bloc**, but **două blocuri înalte**.
