# The Conditional Mood (aș, ai, ar...)

The Romanian conditional expresses **would**, **could**, **would like**, and hypothetical situations. It is built with a short auxiliary plus the infinitive form without **a**.

## Present Conditional Forms

Use **aș, ai, ar, am, ați, ar + verb**.

| Pronoun | a merge | English |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **eu** | **aș merge** | I would go |
| **tu** | **ai merge** | you would go |
| **el / ea** | **ar merge** | he / she would go |
| **noi** | **am merge** | we would go |
| **voi** | **ați merge** | you all would go |
| **ei / ele** | **ar merge** | they would go |

- **Aș merge cu tine.** (ahsh MER-jeh koo TEE-neh) — I would go with you.
- **Noi am sta acasă.** (noy ahm stah ah-KAH-suh) — We would stay at home.
- **Ei ar veni mâine.** (yay ar veh-NEE MUH-ee-neh) — They would come tomorrow.

## Polite Requests: aș vrea and aș dori

The conditional makes requests softer and more polite.

- **Aș vrea o cafea, vă rog.** (ahsh vreh-ah oh kah-FEAH vuh rog) — I would like a coffee, please.
- **Aș dori o cameră.** (ahsh doh-REE oh KAH-meh-ruh) — I would like a room.
- **Ați putea să mă ajutați?** (ahtsʲ poo-TEH-ah suh muh ah-zhoo-TAHTSʲ) — Could you help me?

**Aș vrea** is one of the most useful Romanian phrases for travel and everyday politeness.

## Hypothetical Sentences

Romanian often uses **dacă** (if) with the conditional.

- **Dacă aș avea timp, aș merge.** — If I had time, I would go.
- **Dacă ar ploua, am rămâne acasă.** — If it rained, we would stay home.
- **Dacă ai învăța mai mult, ai vorbi mai bine.** — If you studied more, you would speak better.

These sentences often pair an imperfect-like or conditional idea in the if-clause with a conditional result.

## Conditional of Common Verbs

| Infinitive | eu | el/ea | noi | English |
|------------|----|-------|-----|---------|
| **a fi** | aș fi | ar fi | am fi | would be |
| **a avea** | aș avea | ar avea | am avea | would have |
| **a putea** | aș putea | ar putea | am putea | could / would be able |
| **a vrea** | aș vrea | ar vrea | am vrea | would want / would like |
| **a face** | aș face | ar face | am face | would do/make |

- **Ar fi bine.** — It would be good.
- **Am putea pleca acum.** — We could leave now.
- **Ce ai face?** — What would you do?

## Negation

Put **nu** before the conditional auxiliary.

- **Nu aș merge.** — I would not go.
- **Nu ar fi corect.** — It would not be fair.
- **Nu am putea termina azi.** — We could not finish today.

In informal writing you may see contractions like **n-aș** and **n-ar**, but the full forms are clearer for learners.
