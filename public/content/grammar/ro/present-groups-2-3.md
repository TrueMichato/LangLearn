# Present Tense: Group 2 (-ea) & Group 3 (-e) Verbs

Romanian Group 2 verbs end in **-ea**, and Group 3 verbs end in **-e**. There are fewer of them than Group 1 and Group 4 verbs, but many are extremely common.

## Group 2: a vedea — to see

| Pronoun | Form | English |
|---------|------|---------|
| **eu** | **văd** | I see |
| **tu** | **vezi** | you see |
| **el / ea** | **vede** | he / she sees |
| **noi** | **vedem** | we see |
| **voi** | **vedeți** | you all see |
| **ei / ele** | **văd** | they see |

- **Văd un câine.** (vuhd oon KUH-ee-neh) — I see a dog.
- **Tu vezi munții?** (too vezʲ MOON-tsee) — Do you see the mountains?
- **Noi vedem orașul.** (noy veh-DEM oh-RAH-shool) — We see the city.

## More Group 2 Verbs

Several Group 2 verbs are irregular but very useful.

| Infinitive | eu | tu | el/ea | noi | English |
|------------|----|----|-------|-----|---------|
| **a putea** | pot | poți | poate | putem | to be able/can |
| **a bea** | beau | bei | bea | bem | to drink |
| **a avea** | am | ai | are | avem | to have |
| **a plăcea** | îmi place | îți place | îi place | ne place | to like / to please |

**A putea** is followed by a subjunctive with **să** or sometimes by an infinitive-style construction in fixed contexts. A beginner-safe pattern is **pot să...**

- **Pot să vin.** (pot suh veen) — I can come.
- **Poți să ajuți?** (potsʲ suh ah-ZOOTSʲ) — Can you help?
- **Bem apă rece.** (bem AH-puh REH-cheh) — We drink cold water.

## Group 3: a merge — to go

| Pronoun | Form | English |
|---------|------|---------|
| **eu** | **merg** | I go |
| **tu** | **mergi** | you go |
| **el / ea** | **merge** | he / she goes |
| **noi** | **mergem** | we go |
| **voi** | **mergeți** | you all go |
| **ei / ele** | **merg** | they go |

- **Merg la școală.** (merg lah SHKOH-ah-luh) — I go to school.
- **Ea merge acasă.** (yah MER-jeh ah-KAH-suh) — She is going home.
- **Mergem cu trenul.** (MER-jem koo TREH-nool) — We go by train.

## More Group 3 Verbs

| Infinitive | eu | tu | el/ea | noi | English |
|------------|----|----|-------|-----|---------|
| **a face** | fac | faci | face | facem | to do/make |
| **a spune** | spun | spui | spune | spunem | to say/tell |
| **a scrie** | scriu | scrii | scrie | scriem | to write |
| **a începe** | încep | începi | începe | începem | to begin |

- **Fac cafea.** (fahk kah-FEAH) — I am making coffee.
- **Spui adevărul.** (spooy ah-deh-VUH-rool) — You tell the truth.
- **Scriem un mesaj.** (skree-YEM oon meh-SAHZH) — We write a message.

## Why These Verbs Feel Irregular

Groups 2 and 3 contain many old, high-frequency verbs. They often have stem changes:

- **a vedea → văd** — to see → I see
- **a putea → pot** — to be able → I can
- **a face → fac** — to do/make → I do/make
- **a spune → spun** — to say → I say

Learn them as complete mini-patterns instead of forcing one regular ending system.
