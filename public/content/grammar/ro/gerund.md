# The Gerund / Present Participle (-ând, -ind)

The Romanian **gerund** describes an action in progress or an action that happens while another action happens. It often translates English **while doing**, **by doing**, or simply **doing**.

## Forming the Gerund

Common endings are **-ând** and **-ind**. The exact stem must sometimes be learned with the verb.

| Infinitive | Gerund | English |
|------------|--------|---------|
| **a merge** | **mergând** | going |
| **a cânta** | **cântând** | singing |
| **a citi** | **citind** | reading |
| **a face** | **făcând** | doing / making |
| **a fi** | **fiind** | being |
| **a avea** | **având** | having |
| **a lua** | **luând** | taking |

- **Mergând spre casă, am întâlnit un prieten.** (mer-GUHN(d) spre KAH-suh) — While going home, I met a friend.
- **Citind în fiecare zi, înveți mai repede.** — By reading every day, you learn faster.
- **Făcând exerciții, mi-am amintit regula.** — By doing exercises, I remembered the rule.

## Meaning: While or By

The gerund does not show person or number. The subject is usually understood from the main clause.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **Ascultând muzică, a adormit.** | Listening to music, he/she fell asleep. |
| **Lucrând împreună, terminăm mai repede.** | By working together, we finish faster. |
| **Văzând ploaia, am luat umbrela.** | Seeing the rain, I took the umbrella. |
| **Fiind obosit, a plecat devreme.** | Being tired, he left early. |

- **Vorbind încet, profesorul a explicat regula.** — Speaking slowly, the teacher explained the rule.
- **Având timp liber, am citit un roman.** — Having free time, I read a novel.

The gerund is concise and common in writing. In conversation, Romanian can also use a full clause with **când**: **Când mergeam spre casă, am întâlnit un prieten.**

## Important Irregular-Looking Forms

Some frequent verbs have short or changed stems.

| Verb | Gerund | Example |
|------|--------|---------|
| **a sta** | **stând** | **Stând aici, văd muntele.** — Sitting/standing here, I see the mountain. |
| **a da** | **dând** | **Dând un exemplu, a clarificat ideea.** — Giving an example, he/she clarified the idea. |
| **a spune** | **spunând** | **Spunând adevărul, a câștigat încredere.** — Telling the truth, he/she gained trust. |
| **a scrie** | **scriind** | **Scriind des, faci progrese.** — By writing often, you make progress. |
| **a ști** | **știind** | **Știind răspunsul, a ridicat mâna.** — Knowing the answer, he/she raised a hand. |

## Gerund with Pronouns

Object pronouns attach after the gerund, usually with linking sounds and hyphens.

- **Văzându-l pe Mihai, l-am salutat.** — Seeing Mihai, I greeted him.
- **Auzind-o pe Maria, am deschis ușa.** — Hearing Maria, I opened the door.
- **Spunându-i adevărul, l-am ajutat.** — Telling him/her the truth, I helped him/her.
- **Citindu-le copiilor, mama a zâmbit.** — Reading to the children, the mother smiled.

These forms are common but advanced. First learn the basic gerunds: **mergând**, **citind**, **făcând**, **fiind**, **având**.

## Gerund vs. Supine

Do not confuse the gerund with the supine.

| Form | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **citind** | reading / while reading |
| **de citit** | to read / for reading |
| **făcând** | doing / by doing |
| **de făcut** | to do / to be done |

- **Citind, am găsit ideea principală.** — While reading, I found the main idea.
- **Am ceva de citit.** — I have something to read.
