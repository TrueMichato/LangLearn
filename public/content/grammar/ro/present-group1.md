# Present Tense: Group 1 (-a) Verbs

Romanian verbs are traditionally grouped by their infinitive ending. **Group 1** verbs end in **-a**: **a cânta** (to sing), **a lucra** (to work), **a învăța** (to learn/study). This is a large and useful group.

## The Infinitive and the Stem

Romanian dictionary forms usually include **a** before the verb:

- **a cânta** (ah KUHN-tah) — to sing
- **a lucra** (ah loo-KRAH) — to work
- **a asculta** (ah ahs-kool-TAH) — to listen

To conjugate, remove the infinitive marker **a** and the ending **-a**, then add present-tense endings. Some verbs also insert **-ez-** in several forms.

## Pattern 1: a cânta — no -ez-

| Pronoun | Form | English |
|---------|------|---------|
| **eu** | **cânt** | I sing |
| **tu** | **cânți** | you sing |
| **el / ea** | **cântă** | he / she sings |
| **noi** | **cântăm** | we sing |
| **voi** | **cântați** | you all sing |
| **ei / ele** | **cântă** | they sing |

- **Eu cânt încet.** (yew kuhnt uhn-CHET) — I sing quietly.
- **Tu cânți foarte bine.** (too kuhntsʲ FOAR-teh BEE-neh) — You sing very well.
- **Noi cântăm la petrecere.** (noy kuhn-TUHM lah peh-treh-CHEH-reh) — We sing at the party.

The third-person singular and plural are the same: **el cântă**, **ei cântă**.

## More Verbs Like a cânta

| Infinitive | eu | tu | el/ea | noi | English |
|------------|----|----|-------|-----|---------|
| **a asculta** | ascult | asculți | ascultă | ascultăm | to listen |
| **a întreba** | întreb | întrebi | întreabă | întrebăm | to ask |
| **a ajuta** | ajut | ajuți | ajută | ajutăm | to help |
| **a pleca** | plec | pleci | pleacă | plecăm | to leave |

Some stems change slightly: **întreba → întreabă**, **pleca → pleacă**. These alternations are common and become familiar through exposure.

- **Întreb profesorul.** (uhn-TREB proh-feh-SOH-rool) — I ask the teacher.
- **Ei pleacă mâine.** (yay PLEH-ah-kuh MUH-ee-neh) — They leave tomorrow.

## Pattern 2: a lucra — with -ez-

Many Group 1 verbs insert **-ez-** in **eu**, **tu**, **el/ea**, and **ei/ele** forms.

| Pronoun | Form | English |
|---------|------|---------|
| **eu** | **lucrez** | I work |
| **tu** | **lucrezi** | you work |
| **el / ea** | **lucrează** | he / she works |
| **noi** | **lucrăm** | we work |
| **voi** | **lucrați** | you all work |
| **ei / ele** | **lucrează** | they work |

- **Eu lucrez acasă.** (yew loo-KREZ ah-KAH-suh) — I work at home.
- **Ea lucrează la spital.** (yah loo-KRYAH-zuh lah spee-TAHL) — She works at the hospital.
- **Voi lucrați mult.** (voy loo-KRAHTSʲ moolt) — You all work a lot.

## More -ez- Verbs

| Infinitive | eu | tu | el/ea | noi | English |
|------------|----|----|-------|-----|---------|
| **a dansa** | dansez | dansezi | dansează | dansăm | to dance |
| **a studia** | studiez | studiezi | studiază | studiem | to study |
| **a fuma** | fumez | fumezi | fumează | fumăm | to smoke |
| **a desena** | desenez | desenezi | desenează | desenăm | to draw |

The **noi** and **voi** forms usually keep the simple stem: **lucrăm**, **lucrați**; **dansăm**, **dansați**.

## Negatives and Questions

Use **nu** before the conjugated verb:

- **Nu lucrez azi.** — I am not working today.
- **Ei nu cântă aici.** — They do not sing here.

Questions usually keep the same word order:

- **Tu studiezi româna?** — Do you study Romanian?
- **Voi ascultați muzică?** — Do you all listen to music?
