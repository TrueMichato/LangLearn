# Indefinite & Negative Pronouns (cineva, ceva, nimeni, nimic, tot, fiecare, oricine)

Romanian has a rich set of **indefinite pronouns** for talking about unknown people, things, places, and choices. Many are built from short pieces: **cine** (who), **ce** (what), **unde** (where), **ori-** (any-/ever-), and **alt-** (other).

## Basic Indefinites: cineva, ceva, undeva

Use these when you mean **someone**, **something**, or **somewhere**.

| Romanian | English | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **cineva** | someone / somebody | **Cineva bate la ușă.** — Someone is knocking at the door. |
| **ceva** | something | **Vreau ceva rece.** — I want something cold. |
| **undeva** | somewhere | **Locuiește undeva aproape.** — He/she lives somewhere nearby. |

- **A venit cineva?** (ah veh-NEET chee-neh-VAH) — Did someone come?
- **Am găsit ceva interesant.** — I found something interesting.
- **Ne vedem undeva în centru.** — We will meet somewhere downtown.

## Negative Pronouns: nimeni, nimic, niciunul

Romanian uses **negative concord**: a negative pronoun normally appears with **nu**. English says **I do not see anybody** or **I see nobody**; Romanian says literally **I do not see nobody**.

| Romanian | English | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **nimeni** | nobody / no one | **Nu văd pe nimeni.** — I do not see anyone. |
| **nimic** | nothing / anything | **Nu am nimic.** — I do not have anything. |
| **niciunul** | none / not one (masc.) | **Niciunul nu răspunde.** — None of them answers. |
| **niciuna** | none / not one (fem.) | **Niciuna nu știe.** — None of them knows. |

Even when the negative word comes first, Romanian still keeps **nu**:

- **Nimeni nu știe.** — Nobody knows.
- **Nimic nu este ușor.** — Nothing is easy.
- **Niciunul nu a venit.** — Not one of them came.

Use **pe nimeni** for a human direct object, just like **pe Maria** or **pe Andrei**.

## Tot, toată, toți, toate

**Tot** means **all**, **every**, or **the whole**, and it agrees with the noun it modifies.

| Form | Used with | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **tot** | masculine/neuter singular | **tot orașul** — the whole city |
| **toată** | feminine singular | **toată ziua** — all day |
| **toți** | masculine plural | **toți prietenii** — all the friends |
| **toate** | feminine/neuter plural | **toate cărțile** — all the books |

As pronouns, these forms can stand alone:

- **Totul este gata.** — Everything is ready.
- **Toți au plecat.** — Everyone / all of them left.
- **Le-am citit pe toate.** — I read all of them. (feminine/neuter plural objects)

## Fiecare: each / every

**Fiecare** means **each** or **every**. It usually stays singular, so the verb is singular too.

- **Fiecare student are o carte.** — Every student has a book.
- **Fiecare dintre noi învață.** — Each of us is studying.
- **Vorbesc cu fiecare copil.** — I speak with every child.

Do not use a plural noun directly after **fiecare**: say **fiecare student**, not **fiecare studenți**.

## Oricine, orice, oricare

The prefix **ori-** gives the idea of **any-**, **whoever**, or **whichever**.

| Romanian | English | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **oricine** | anyone / whoever | **Oricine poate învăța.** — Anyone can learn. |
| **orice** | anything / whatever | **Poți întreba orice.** — You can ask anything. |
| **oricare** | any one / whichever | **Alege oricare.** — Choose whichever one. |

- **Oricine știe răspunsul poate vorbi.** — Whoever knows the answer may speak.
- **Mănânc orice este proaspăt.** — I eat whatever is fresh.
- **Oricare dintre aceste cărți e bună.** — Any one of these books is good.

## Altcineva and altceva

Add **alt-** when you mean **someone else** or **something else**.

- **A sunat altcineva.** — Someone else called.
- **Vreau altceva.** — I want something else.
- **Mai este altcineva aici?** — Is anyone else here?

These forms are useful for gentle corrections:

- **Nu asta. Altceva.** — Not this. Something else.
- **Nu eu am spus asta; altcineva a spus-o.** — I did not say that; someone else said it.
