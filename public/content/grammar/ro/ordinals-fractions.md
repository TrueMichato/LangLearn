# Ordinal Numbers & Fractions

Romanian ordinal numbers answer **which one in order?**: **primul** (the first), **al doilea** (the second), **a treia** (the third, feminine). Fractions express parts: **o jumătate**, **un sfert**, **două treimi**.

## First and Second

The first two ordinals are extremely common and have gender forms.

| Masculine / Neuter | Feminine | English |
|--------------------|----------|---------|
| **primul** | **prima** | the first |
| **întâiul** | **întâia** | the first, more formal/literary |
| **al doilea** | **a doua** | the second |

- **primul tren** — the first train
- **prima lecție** — the first lesson
- **etajul al doilea** — the second floor
- **a doua zi** — the second day / the next day

**Întâi** is also used as an adverb: **Mai întâi bem cafea.** — First, we drink coffee.

## al treilea, a treia and Beyond

From **third** onward, Romanian usually uses **al** for masculine/neuter and **a** for feminine.

| Number | Masculine / Neuter | Feminine |
|--------|--------------------|----------|
| 3rd | **al treilea** | **a treia** |
| 4th | **al patrulea** | **a patra** |
| 5th | **al cincilea** | **a cincea** |
| 6th | **al șaselea** | **a șasea** |
| 7th | **al șaptelea** | **a șaptea** |
| 8th | **al optulea** | **a opta** |
| 9th | **al nouălea** | **a noua** |
| 10th | **al zecelea** | **a zecea** |

- **capitolul al treilea** — the third chapter
- **pagina a treia** — the third page
- **exercițiul al cincilea** — the fifth exercise
- **întrebarea a zecea** — the tenth question

## Position with Nouns

Ordinals usually follow a definite noun, especially in formal labels.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **lecția a patra** | the fourth lesson |
| **rândul al doilea** | the second row |
| **ziua a șaptea** | the seventh day |
| **secolul al nouăsprezecelea** | the nineteenth century |

They can also come before the noun in everyday expressions: **prima zi**, **a doua săptămână**, **al treilea copil**.

- **Locuiesc la etajul al treilea.** — I live on the third floor.
- **Aceasta este a doua încercare.** — This is the second attempt.
- **A câștigat al patrulea meci.** — He/she won the fourth match.

## Fractions: Common Forms

Fractions are often expressed with special nouns.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **o jumătate** | one half |
| **un sfert** | one quarter |
| **trei sferturi** | three quarters |
| **o treime** | one third |
| **două treimi** | two thirds |
| **o cincime** | one fifth |
| **două cincimi** | two fifths |

- **Am băut o jumătate de litru de apă.** — I drank half a liter of water.
- **Ne vedem într-un sfert de oră.** — We will see each other in a quarter of an hour.
- **Două treimi dintre studenți au trecut examenul.** — Two thirds of the students passed the exam.

## de and din / dintre with Fractions

Use **de** with measured quantities and **din** or **dintre** when you mean **of** a whole group.

- **o jumătate de oră** — half an hour
- **un sfert de kilogram** — a quarter of a kilogram
- **o treime din buget** — one third of the budget
- **două treimi dintre copii** — two thirds of the children

Decimals use **virgulă** in Romanian: **1,5** is read **unu virgulă cinci**.
