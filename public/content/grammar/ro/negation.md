# Negation (nu, niciodată, nimic)

Romanian negation is built around **nu**, placed before the verb. Romanian also uses negative words like **nimic** (nothing), **nimeni** (nobody), and **niciodată** (never).

## Basic Negation with nu

Put **nu** directly before the conjugated verb.

| Affirmative | Negative | English |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| **Vorbesc românește.** | **Nu vorbesc românește.** | I do not speak Romanian. |
| **Am timp.** | **Nu am timp.** | I do not have time. |
| **Ea este acasă.** | **Ea nu este acasă.** | She is not at home. |

- **Nu înțeleg.** (noo uhn-tseh-LEG) — I don't understand.
- **Nu merg azi.** (noo merg ahzʲ) — I am not going today.
- **Nu avem cafea.** (noo ah-VEM kah-FEAH) — We don't have coffee.

## Negative Words

| Romanian | English | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **nimic** | nothing / anything in negatives | **Nu văd nimic.** — I don't see anything. |
| **nimeni** | nobody / anybody in negatives | **Nu vine nimeni.** — Nobody is coming. |
| **niciodată** | never | **Nu uit niciodată.** — I never forget. |
| **nicăieri** | nowhere / anywhere in negatives | **Nu merg nicăieri.** — I am not going anywhere. |
| **niciun / nicio** | no, not any | **Nu am niciun frate.** — I have no brother. |

Romanian standard grammar uses a kind of **negative concord**: the negative word appears with **nu**.

- **Nu știu nimic.** (noo shtyoo nee-MEEK) — I don't know anything.
- **Nu cunosc pe nimeni aici.** (noo koo-NOSK peh nee-MENʲ ah-CH) — I don't know anyone here.

## Never: niciodată

**Niciodată** can appear after the verb phrase or at the beginning for emphasis.

- **Nu beau cafea niciodată.** — I never drink coffee.
- **Niciodată nu beau cafea.** — Never do I drink coffee.

Both are correct. The second is more emphatic.

## No Longer and Not Yet

Two useful negative expressions are **nu mai** and **încă nu**.

| Romanian | English | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **nu mai** | no longer / not anymore | **Nu mai locuiesc aici.** — I no longer live here. |
| **încă nu** | not yet | **Încă nu știu.** — I don't know yet. |

- **Nu mai am timp.** (noo mai ahm teemp) — I don't have time anymore.
- **Încă nu am terminat.** (UHN-kuh noo ahm ter-mee-NAHT) — I haven't finished yet.

## Negation with Pronouns

When object or reflexive pronouns appear before the verb, **nu** comes before the pronoun.

- **Mă duc.** — I am going.
- **Nu mă duc.** — I am not going.
- **Îl văd.** — I see him.
- **Nu îl văd.** — I do not see him.

In fast speech, forms may contract, but the written beginner pattern is clear: **nu + pronoun + verb**.
