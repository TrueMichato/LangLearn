# Formal Address & Politeness (dumneavoastră)

Romanian distinguishes informal **tu** from formal **dumneavoastră**. Choosing the right form helps you sound respectful in shops, offices, classrooms, and first meetings.

## tu vs. dumneavoastră

Use **tu** with close friends, family, children, and people who invite you to be informal. Use **dumneavoastră** for respectful singular **you** and for formal plural **you**.

| Informal | Formal | English |
|----------|--------|---------|
| **Tu ești gata?** | **Dumneavoastră sunteți gata?** | Are you ready? |
| **Ai timp?** | **Aveți timp?** | Do you have time? |
| **Te rog.** | **Vă rog.** | Please. |
| **Îți mulțumesc.** | **Vă mulțumesc.** | Thank you. |

- **Dumneavoastră sunteți profesorul?** (doom-nyah-voh-AHS-truh soon-TETSʲ) — Are you the teacher?
- **Aveți o rezervare?** — Do you have a reservation?
- **Vă rog să intrați.** — Please come in.

## Verb Agreement

**Dumneavoastră** uses second-person plural verb forms, even when you address one person.

| Verb | Informal Singular | Formal |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| **a fi** | **ești** | **sunteți** |
| **a avea** | **ai** | **aveți** |
| **a putea** | **poți** | **puteți** |
| **a dori** | **dorești** | **doriți** |
| **a intra** | **intri** | **intrați** |

- **Doriți cafea sau ceai?** — Would you like coffee or tea?
- **Puteți repeta, vă rog?** — Can you repeat, please?
- **Unde locuiți?** — Where do you live?

The subject pronoun is often omitted: **Aveți timp?** is already formal because of **aveți**.

## Polite Pronouns and Short Forms

You will see several polite pronouns. They differ in formality and region.

| Pronoun | Use |
|---------|-----|
| **dumneavoastră** | standard polite you, singular or plural |
| **dvs.** | written abbreviation of dumneavoastră |
| **dumneata** | semi-formal you, less common and sometimes old-fashioned |
| **dumnealui** | polite he / him |
| **dumneaei** | polite she / her |
| **dumnealor** | polite they / them |

- **Vă pot ajuta?** — Can I help you?
- **Am primit mesajul dumneavoastră.** — I received your message.
- **Dumneaei este directoarea.** — She is the director. (polite)

## Titles and Vocatives

Politeness often combines **dumneavoastră** with titles.

| Title | Addressing Someone | English |
|-------|--------------------|---------|
| **domnul** | **domnule** | Mr. / sir |
| **doamna** | **doamnă** | Mrs./Ms. / ma'am |
| **domnișoara** | **domnișoară** | Miss |
| **profesorul** | **domnule profesor** | professor, sir |
| **profesoara** | **doamnă profesoară** | professor, ma'am |

- **Domnule Popescu, aveți un mesaj.** — Mr. Popescu, you have a message.
- **Doamnă Ionescu, vă rog să semnați aici.** — Ms./Mrs. Ionescu, please sign here.
- **Domnule profesor, puteți explica din nou?** — Professor, can you explain again?

Use **bună ziua** rather than **salut** in formal situations.

## Adjective Agreement with a Formal You

The verb stays plural with **dumneavoastră**, but adjectives can reflect the real person or group.

- To one man: **Dumneavoastră sunteți foarte amabil.** — You are very kind.
- To one woman: **Dumneavoastră sunteți foarte amabilă.** — You are very kind.
- To a mixed group or men: **Dumneavoastră sunteți foarte amabili.** — You are very kind.
- To a group of women: **Dumneavoastră sunteți foarte amabile.** — You are very kind.

In many service situations, you can avoid the issue by using polite verbs and nouns: **Vă pot ajuta?**, **Doriți o chitanță?**

## Useful Polite Phrases

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **Vă rog.** | Please. |
| **Vă mulțumesc.** | Thank you. |
| **Cu plăcere.** | You are welcome. |
| **Mă scuzați.** | Excuse me. |
| **Îmi pare rău.** | I am sorry. |
| **Puteți să repetați?** | Can you repeat? |

- **Mă scuzați, unde este gara?** — Excuse me, where is the train station?
- **Vă mulțumesc pentru ajutor.** — Thank you for the help.
