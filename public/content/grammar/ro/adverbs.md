# Adverbs & Adverbs of Manner

Adverbs describe verbs, adjectives, or whole sentences. They answer questions like **how?**, **when?**, **where?**, and **how much?** Romanian adverbs are often simple and do not agree with gender or number.

## Common Adverbs of Manner

Adverbs of manner answer **how?**

| Romanian | English | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **bine** | well | **Vorbești bine.** — You speak well. |
| **rău** | badly | **Mă simt rău.** — I feel bad. |
| **repede** | quickly | **Aleargă repede.** — He/she runs quickly. |
| **încet** | slowly, quietly | **Vorbește încet.** — Speak slowly/quietly. |
| **clar** | clearly | **Explică clar.** — He/she explains clearly. |
| **frumos** | nicely, beautifully | **Cântă frumos.** — He/she sings beautifully. |

- **Citesc încet.** (chee-TESK uhn-CHET) — I read slowly.
- **Ea dansează frumos.** (yah dahn-SYAH-zuh froo-MOS) — She dances beautifully.

## Adjectives Used as Adverbs

Many Romanian adverbs look like the masculine singular adjective form.

| Adjective | Adverb Example | English |
|-----------|----------------|---------|
| **frumos** | **scrie frumos** | writes beautifully/neatly |
| **clar** | **vorbește clar** | speaks clearly |
| **corect** | **răspunde corect** | answers correctly |
| **rapid** | **merge rapid** | goes quickly |

The adverb does **not** agree with the subject.

- **El cântă frumos.** — He sings beautifully.
- **Ea cântă frumos.** — She sings beautifully.
- **Ele cântă frumos.** — They sing beautifully.

Do not change **frumos** to **frumoasă** when it describes the verb.

## Time and Frequency Adverbs

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **acum** | now |
| **azi / astăzi** | today |
| **ieri** | yesterday |
| **mâine** | tomorrow |
| **mereu** | always, continually |
| **adesea** | often |
| **uneori** | sometimes |
| **niciodată** | never |
| **deja** | already |
| **încă** | still, yet |

- **Mâine plecăm.** — Tomorrow we leave.
- **Uneori citesc seara.** — Sometimes I read in the evening.
- **Nu beau cafea niciodată.** — I never drink coffee.

## Place Adverbs

| Romanian | English | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **aici** | here | **Stau aici.** — I stay here. |
| **acolo** | there | **Merg acolo.** — I go there. |
| **aproape** | near | **Locuiesc aproape.** — I live nearby. |
| **departe** | far | **Gara este departe.** — The station is far. |
| **sus** | up, upstairs | **Urc sus.** — I go upstairs. |
| **jos** | down, downstairs | **Cobor jos.** — I go downstairs. |

## Language Adverbs in -ește

Romanian often uses **-ește** adverbs to mean **in a language** or **in a certain manner**.

- **românește** — in Romanian
- **englezește** — in English
- **franțuzește** — in French
- **prietenește** — in a friendly way

- **Vorbesc românește puțin.** (vor-BESK roh-muh-NESH-teh poo-TSIN) — I speak a little Romanian.
- **Poți explica englezește?** — Can you explain in English?

## Comparative Adverbs

Use **mai** with adverbs just as with adjectives.

- **mai bine** — better
- **mai repede** — faster
- **mai încet** — more slowly
- **cel mai bine** — the best

- **Vorbește mai încet, te rog.** — Speak more slowly, please.
- **Ea aleargă cel mai repede.** — She runs the fastest.
