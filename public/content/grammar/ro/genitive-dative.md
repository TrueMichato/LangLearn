# The Genitive & Dative Case

Romanian has case forms. The two most important for learners after the basic subject/object form are the **genitive** and **dative**. They often share the same noun endings.

## What They Mean

| Case | Main Use | English Equivalent |
|------|----------|-------------------|
| **Genitive** | possession or relationship | of, 's |
| **Dative** | indirect object, recipient | to, for |

- **cartea profesorului** — the teacher's book / the book of the teacher
- **dau cartea profesorului** — I give the book to the teacher

The form **profesorului** can be genitive or dative depending on the sentence.

## Masculine and Neuter Singular: -ului

For many masculine and neuter singular nouns, the genitive-dative definite form ends in **-ului**.

| Basic Definite | Genitive-Dative | Example |
|----------------|-----------------|---------|
| **băiatul** | **băiatului** | **cartea băiatului** — the boy's book |
| **profesorul** | **profesorului** | **îi scriu profesorului** — I write to the teacher |
| **scaunul** | **scaunului** | **piciorul scaunului** — the chair's leg |
| **orașul** | **orașului** | **centrul orașului** — the city center |

- **Telefonul băiatului este nou.** (teh-leh-FOH-nool buh-YAHT-oo-looy YES-teh noh-oo) — The boy's phone is new.
- **Îi dau profesorului tema.** (ee dow proh-feh-SOH-roo-looy TEH-mah) — I give the homework to the teacher.

## Feminine Singular: often -ei

Feminine singular genitive-dative forms often end in **-ei**, with possible stem changes.

| Basic Definite | Genitive-Dative | Example |
|----------------|-----------------|---------|
| **fata** | **fetei** | **cartea fetei** — the girl's book |
| **casa** | **casei** | **ușa casei** — the house's door |
| **mama** | **mamei** | **îi telefonez mamei** — I phone mother |
| **cartea** | **cărții** | **coperta cărții** — the book's cover |

- **Mașina fetei este roșie.** (mah-SHEE-nah FEH-tey YES-teh ROH-shee-eh) — The girl's car is red.
- **Ușa casei este deschisă.** (OO-shah KAH-sey YES-teh deh-SKEE-suh) — The door of the house is open.

## Plural: -lor

Plural genitive-dative forms use **-lor**.

| Definite Plural | Genitive-Dative Plural | English |
|-----------------|------------------------|---------|
| **băieții** | **băieților** | of/to the boys |
| **fetele** | **fetelor** | of/to the girls |
| **scaunele** | **scaunelor** | of/to the chairs |
| **copiii** | **copiilor** | of/to the children |

- **Jucăriile copiilor sunt pe jos.** (zhoo-kuh-REE-ee-leh koh-PEE-ee-lor soont peh zhos) — The children's toys are on the floor.
- **Le dau fetelor flori.** (leh dow FEH-teh-lor florʲ) — I give flowers to the girls.

## Dative with Verbs of Giving and Saying

The dative marks the recipient or beneficiary.

- **Îi dau Mariei o carte.** — I give Maria a book.
- **Îi scriu profesorului un mesaj.** — I write the teacher a message.
- **Le spun copiilor o poveste.** — I tell the children a story.

The short pronouns **îi** (to him/her) and **le** (to them) often appear together with the full dative noun for clarity or emphasis.

## When al, a, ai, ale Appears

When the possessed noun is indefinite, separated, or omitted, Romanian often uses **al / a / ai / ale** before the genitive.

- **cartea profesorului** — the teacher's book
- **o carte a profesorului** — a book of the teacher's
- **cărțile profesorului** — the teacher's books
- **două cărți ale profesorului** — two books of the teacher's
