# The Imperfect Tense

The Romanian **imperfect** describes ongoing, repeated, habitual, or background actions in the past. It often corresponds to English **used to**, **would**, or **was/were doing**.

## When to Use the Imperfect

Use the imperfect for:

1. **Habitual past actions** — what used to happen
2. **Background descriptions** — what things were like
3. **Ongoing past actions** — what was happening
4. **Age, time, weather, and states** in the past

- **Când eram copil, mergeam des la bunici.** (kuhnd eh-RAHM koh-PEEL, mer-JAHM des lah boo-NEECHʲ) — When I was a child, I often went to my grandparents.
- **Ploua și era frig.** (PLOH-wah shee EH-rah freeg) — It was raining and it was cold.

## a fi in the Imperfect

The verb **a fi** (to be) is essential in the imperfect.

| Pronoun | Form | English |
|---------|------|---------|
| **eu** | **eram** | I was |
| **tu** | **erai** | you were |
| **el / ea** | **era** | he / she was |
| **noi** | **eram** | we were |
| **voi** | **erați** | you all were |
| **ei / ele** | **erau** | they were |

- **Eram obosit.** — I was tired. (male)
- **Ea era profesoară.** — She was a teacher.
- **Ei erau acasă.** — They were at home.

## Group 1 (-a) Imperfect

Group 1 verbs typically use **-am, -ai, -a, -am, -ați, -au**.

| Pronoun | a cânta | English |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **eu** | **cântam** | I was singing / used to sing |
| **tu** | **cântai** | you were singing |
| **el / ea** | **cânta** | he / she was singing |
| **noi** | **cântam** | we were singing |
| **voi** | **cântați** | you all were singing |
| **ei / ele** | **cântau** | they were singing |

- **Lucram în fiecare zi.** (loo-KRAHM uhn fee-EH-kah-reh zee) — I used to work every day.
- **Copiii cântau în curte.** (koh-PEE-ee kuhn-TAW uhn KOOR-teh) — The children were singing in the yard.

## Other Verbs: -eam, -eai, -ea...

Many non-Group-1 verbs use endings with **-e-**.

| Pronoun | a merge | a citi | English |
|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| **eu** | **mergeam** | **citeam** | I was going / reading |
| **tu** | **mergeai** | **citeai** | you were going / reading |
| **el / ea** | **mergea** | **citea** | he/she was going / reading |
| **noi** | **mergeam** | **citeam** | we were going / reading |
| **voi** | **mergeați** | **citeați** | you all were going / reading |
| **ei / ele** | **mergeau** | **citeau** | they were going / reading |

- **Mergeam la școală pe jos.** — I used to walk to school.
- **Citeam seara.** — I used to read in the evening.

## Imperfect vs Perfectul Compus

| Perfectul Compus | Imperfect | Difference |
|------------------|-----------|------------|
| **Am citit cartea.** | **Citeam cartea.** | I read/finished it vs I was reading it |
| **Am mers la mare.** | **Mergeam la mare.** | I went once vs I used to go |
| **A plouat.** | **Ploua.** | It rained vs it was raining |

- **Citeam când ai sunat.** — I was reading when you called.
- **Am citit cartea ieri.** — I read the book yesterday.
