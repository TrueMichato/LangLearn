# Possessives (al, a, ai, ale + meu/tău...)

Romanian possessives must agree with the **thing possessed**, not with the owner. The forms **al, a, ai, ale** are especially important when the possessed noun is omitted or indefinite.

## Possessive Forms with a Definite Noun

After a definite noun, Romanian usually places the possessive adjective after the noun.

| Possessed Noun | Phrase | English |
|----------------|--------|---------|
| **fratele** (the brother) | **fratele meu** | my brother |
| **sora** (the sister) | **sora mea** | my sister |
| **frații** (the brothers) | **frații mei** | my brothers |
| **surorile** (the sisters) | **surorile mele** | my sisters |

- **Cartea mea este aici.** (KAR-tyah myah YES-teh ah-CH) — My book is here.
- **Prietenii mei sunt acasă.** (pree-EH-teh-nee may soont ah-KAH-suh) — My friends are at home.

## meu, tău, său, nostru, vostru

These forms agree with the possessed noun.

| Possessed Thing | my | your (sg.) | his/her own | our | your (pl.) |
|-----------------|----|------------|-------------|-----|------------|
| masculine sg. | **meu** | **tău** | **său** | **nostru** | **vostru** |
| feminine sg. | **mea** | **ta** | **sa** | **noastră** | **voastră** |
| masculine pl. | **mei** | **tăi** | **săi** | **noștri** | **voștri** |
| feminine/neuter pl. | **mele** | **tale** | **sale** | **noastre** | **voastre** |

- **telefonul meu** — my phone
- **mașina ta** — your car
- **copiii noștri** — our children
- **cărțile voastre** — your books

## lui, ei, lor

For **his**, **her**, and **their**, Romanian often uses invariable forms after the noun:

- **cartea lui** — his book
- **cartea ei** — her book
- **cartea lor** — their book
- **copiii lor** — their children

These do not change for the possessed noun.

## al, a, ai, ale

Use **al / a / ai / ale** when the possessive stands alone, follows an indefinite noun, or is separated from the definite noun.

| Possessed Thing | Form | Example |
|-----------------|------|---------|
| masculine singular | **al meu** | **Acesta este al meu.** — This one is mine. |
| feminine singular | **a mea** | **Cartea este a mea.** — The book is mine. |
| masculine plural | **ai mei** | **Aceștia sunt ai mei.** — These are mine. |
| feminine/neuter plural | **ale mele** | **Cărțile sunt ale mele.** — The books are mine. |

The small word **al/a/ai/ale** agrees with the possessed thing:

- **un prieten al meu** — a friend of mine
- **o prietenă a mea** — a female friend of mine
- **niște prieteni ai mei** — some friends of mine
- **două cărți ale mele** — two books of mine

## Possessives with Family and People

- **mama mea** — my mother
- **tatăl tău** — your father
- **fratele ei** — her brother
- **sora lui** — his sister
- **prietenii noștri** — our friends

Romanian often uses the definite noun plus possessive: **mama mea**, literally **the mother my**.

## Common Mistakes

Do not make **al/a/ai/ale** agree with the owner. It agrees with the possessed noun.

- **Cartea lui Andrei este a mea.** — Andrei's book is mine. (**cartea** is feminine singular → **a**)
- **Pantofii Mariei sunt ai mei.** — Maria's shoes are mine. (**pantofii** is masculine plural → **ai**)
- **Scaunele sunt ale noastre.** — The chairs are ours. (**scaunele** is neuter plural → **ale**)
