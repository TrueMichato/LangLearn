# Direct & Indirect Object Pronouns (clitics)

Romanian uses short object pronouns, called **clitics**, that attach closely to the verb. They can mean **me**, **you**, **him**, **her**, **it**, **us**, **them**, or **to me**, **to you**, **to them**.

## Direct Object Pronouns

Direct object pronouns answer **whom?** or **what?**

| English | Romanian | Example |
|---------|----------|---------|
| me | **mă** | **Mă vezi?** — Do you see me? |
| you | **te** | **Te aud.** — I hear you. |
| him / it (masc./neut. sg.) | **îl** | **Îl văd.** — I see him/it. |
| her / it (fem. sg.) | **o** | **O cunosc.** — I know her. |
| us | **ne** | **Ne ajută.** — He/she helps us. |
| you all | **vă** | **Vă aștept.** — I am waiting for you all. |
| them (masc.) | **îi** | **Îi văd.** — I see them. |
| them (fem./neut.) | **le** | **Le citesc.** — I read them. |

- **Îl văd pe Andrei.** (uhl vuhd peh ahn-DRAY) — I see Andrei.
- **O aștept pe Maria.** (oh ahsh-TEPT peh mah-REE-ah) — I am waiting for Maria.
- **Le cumpăr.** (leh koom-PUHR) — I buy them. (feminine/neuter plural things)

## Pe with Human Direct Objects

When the direct object is a specific person, Romanian often uses both a clitic and **pe**.

- **Îl cunosc pe profesor.** — I know the teacher. (male)
- **O cunosc pe profesoară.** — I know the teacher. (female)
- **Îi văd pe copii.** — I see the children.

For ordinary objects, do not use **pe**:

- **Citesc cartea.** — I read the book.
- **O citesc.** — I read it. (cartea is feminine singular)

## Indirect Object Pronouns

Indirect object pronouns answer **to whom?** or **for whom?**

| English | Romanian | Example |
|---------|----------|---------|
| to me | **îmi** | **Îmi dai?** — Will you give me? |
| to you | **îți** | **Îți scriu.** — I write to you. |
| to him/her | **îi** | **Îi dau o carte.** — I give him/her a book. |
| to us | **ne** | **Ne trimite un mesaj.** — He/she sends us a message. |
| to you all | **vă** | **Vă spun adevărul.** — I tell you the truth. |
| to them | **le** | **Le dau apă.** — I give them water. |

- **Îmi place muzica.** (eemʲ PLAH-cheh MOO-zee-kah) — I like the music. (literally: the music is pleasing to me)
- **Îi scriu mamei.** (ee SKREE-oo MAH-mey) — I write to my mother.
- **Le spun copiilor o poveste.** (leh spoon koh-PEE-ee-lor oh poh-VES-teh) — I tell the children a story.

## Placement Before the Verb

In simple present sentences, clitics usually come before the verb.

- **Mă vede.** — He/she sees me.
- **Te sun mâine.** — I will call you tomorrow.
- **Îl ajut.** — I help him.
- **Îi dau cheia.** — I give him/her the key.

With **nu**, the order is **nu + clitic + verb**:

- **Nu îl văd.** — I do not see him.
- **Nu îți scriu azi.** — I am not writing to you today.

## Clitics with Past and Commands

Before vowel auxiliaries, some clitics shorten and attach with a hyphen:

- **L-am văzut pe Andrei.** — I saw Andrei.
- **I-am dat cartea Mariei.** — I gave Maria the book.
- **Mi-a spus adevărul.** — He/she told me the truth.
- **Ne-a trimis un mesaj.** — He/she sent us a message.

The feminine direct object **o** often appears after the participle:

- **Am văzut-o pe Maria.** — I saw Maria.

With positive commands, pronouns often attach after the verb:

- **Ajută-mă!** — Help me!
- **Dă-mi cartea!** — Give me the book!
