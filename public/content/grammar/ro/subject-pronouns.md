# Subject Pronouns (eu, tu, el, ea...)

Subject pronouns tell us who does the action: **I**, **you**, **he**, **she**, **we**, **they**. Romanian often drops them because the verb ending already shows the subject.

## The Basic Subject Pronouns

| Romanian | English | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| **eu** | I | pronounced roughly yew |
| **tu** | you | singular informal |
| **el** | he | masculine singular |
| **ea** | she | feminine singular |
| **noi** | we | all genders |
| **voi** | you all | plural informal |
| **ei** | they | masculine or mixed group |
| **ele** | they | feminine-only group |

- **Eu sunt aici.** (yew soont ah-CH) — I am here.
- **Tu vorbești românește.** (too vor-BESH-tʲ roh-muh-NESH-teh) — You speak Romanian.
- **Ele citesc.** (EH-leh chee-TESK) — They are reading. (all female)

## Romanian Is a Pro-Drop Language

Because verb endings are informative, Romanian often omits the subject pronoun.

| With Pronoun | Natural Short Form | English |
|--------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Eu vorbesc.** | **Vorbesc.** | I speak. |
| **Tu citești.** | **Citești.** | You read. |
| **Noi mergem.** | **Mergem.** | We go. |
| **Ei lucrează.** | **Lucrează.** | They work. |

Use the pronoun when you want clarity, contrast, or emphasis:

- **Eu sunt profesor, dar el este medic.** — I am a teacher, but he is a doctor.
- **Noi mergem azi; voi mergeți mâine.** — We go today; you all go tomorrow.

## Formal You: dumneavoastră

Romanian has a polite pronoun **dumneavoastră**, used for one person formally or for several people formally. It takes the same verb form as **voi**.

- **Dumneavoastră sunteți profesor?** (doom-neh-ah-VOAH-struh soon-TETSʲ proh-feh-SOR) — Are you a teacher? (formal)
- **Dumneavoastră aveți timp?** — Do you have time? (formal)

In service situations, with strangers, older people, or professional settings, **dumneavoastră** is safer than **tu**.

## Gender in the Third Person

Romanian distinguishes masculine and feminine third-person pronouns.

| Singular | Plural | Use |
|----------|--------|-----|
| **el** | **ei** | masculine nouns/people; mixed groups |
| **ea** | **ele** | feminine nouns/people; feminine-only groups |

- **Băieții sunt aici. Ei așteaptă.** — The boys are here. They are waiting.
- **Fetele sunt aici. Ele așteaptă.** — The girls are here. They are waiting.
- **Maria și Andrei sunt aici. Ei așteaptă.** — Maria and Andrei are here. They are waiting. (mixed group)

## Pronouns and Verb Agreement

The verb must match the subject, whether or not the pronoun is spoken.

| Subject | a fi | a avea | a vorbi |
|---------|------|--------|---------|
| **eu** | sunt | am | vorbesc |
| **tu** | ești | ai | vorbești |
| **el/ea** | este/e | are | vorbește |
| **noi** | suntem | avem | vorbim |
| **voi** | sunteți | aveți | vorbiți |
| **ei/ele** | sunt | au | vorbesc |

- **Ea are o întrebare.** — She has a question.
- **Ei au multe întrebări.** — They have many questions.
