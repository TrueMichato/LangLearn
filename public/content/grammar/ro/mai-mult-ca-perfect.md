# The Past Perfect (Mai-mult-ca-perfectul)

**Mai-mult-ca-perfectul** describes an action that had already happened before another past action. English often uses **had done**, **had gone**, or **had seen**.

## Core Meaning

Use this tense when one past event is earlier than another past event.

- **Când am ajuns, filmul începuse.** (kuhnd ahm ah-ZHOONS, FEEL-mool uhn-cheh-POO-seh) — When I arrived, the film had started.
- **Mâncasem deja când m-ai sunat.** — I had already eaten when you called me.
- **Ei plecaseră înainte să înceapă ploaia.** — They had left before the rain started.

The later past event is often in **perfectul compus**: **am ajuns**, **m-ai sunat**, **a început**.

## Common Endings

The endings of mai-mult-ca-perfectul are recognizable. Here is **a lucra**.

| Pronoun | Form | English |
|---------|------|---------|
| **eu** | **lucrasem** | I had worked |
| **tu** | **lucraseși** | you had worked |
| **el / ea** | **lucrase** | he/she had worked |
| **noi** | **lucraserăm** | we had worked |
| **voi** | **lucraserăți** | you all had worked |
| **ei / ele** | **lucraseră** | they had worked |

- **Eu terminasem tema.** — I had finished the homework.
- **Noi ajunseserăm devreme.** — We had arrived early.
- **Voi cumpăraserăți biletele.** — You all had bought the tickets.

## Frequent Verbs

Many forms are built from the past stem plus the endings. Learn common verbs as whole forms.

| Infinitive | eu | el / ea | ei / ele | English |
|------------|----|---------|----------|---------|
| **a face** | **făcusem** | **făcuse** | **făcuseră** | had done / made |
| **a merge** | **mersesem** | **mersese** | **merseseră** | had gone |
| **a vedea** | **văzusem** | **văzuse** | **văzuseră** | had seen |
| **a spune** | **spusesem** | **spusese** | **spuseseră** | had said |
| **a fi** | **fusesem** | **fusese** | **fuseseră** | had been |
| **a citi** | **citisem** | **citise** | **citiseră** | had read |

- **Nu știam că tu văzuseși filmul.** — I did not know that you had seen the film.
- **Ea îmi spusese adevărul.** — She had told me the truth.
- **Fuseserăm acolo de două ori.** — We had been there twice.

## Negation and Word Order

Put **nu** before the verb form.

- **Nu citisem cartea înainte de curs.** — I had not read the book before class.
- **El nu plecase încă.** — He had not left yet.
- **Noi nu înțeleseserăm explicația.** — We had not understood the explanation.

Object pronouns come before the verb:

- **Îl văzusem deja.** — I had already seen him/it.
- **Le cumpărasem ieri.** — I had bought them yesterday.
- **Nu o întâlnisem niciodată.** — I had never met her.

## When to Use It

This tense is common in narration, stories, explanations, and written Romanian. In casual conversation, speakers sometimes use **perfectul compus** plus words like **deja** or **înainte**, but mai-mult-ca-perfectul is precise and elegant.

| Romanian | English |
|----------|---------|
| **După ce terminasem, am ieșit.** | After I had finished, I went out. |
| **Când a venit ea, noi plecaserăm.** | When she came, we had left. |
| **Credeam că uitase cheia.** | I thought he/she had forgotten the key. |
