# Future Tense (Futuro)

Portuguese has two main ways to express the future. The most common in everyday Brazilian Portuguese is **ir + infinitive** (going to...), which works just like English "going to." The second is the **futuro simples** (simple future), which is more formal and used in writing, news, and certain fixed expressions. Knowing both will make you sound natural in any situation.

## Ir + Infinitive: The Everyday Future

This is the future tense you'll use 90% of the time in Brazilian Portuguese. It's formed with the verb **ir** (to go) conjugated in the present tense, followed by the infinitive of the main verb.

### Formation

| Pronoun | Ir (present) | + Infinitive |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| eu | vou | falar / comer / abrir |
| você / ele / ela | vai | falar / comer / abrir |
| nós | vamos | falar / comer / abrir |
| vocês / eles / elas | vão | falar / comer / abrir |

### Examples with -AR verbs:

- **Eu vou falar com ele amanhã** (eh-oo voh fah-lar kong eh-lee ah-mahn-yang) — I'm going to talk to him tomorrow
- **Ela vai estudar à noite** (eh-lah vah-ee esh-too-dar ah noy-chee) — She's going to study at night
- **Nós vamos viajar em dezembro** (nohs vah-mooz vee-ah-zhar eng deh-zem-broo) — We're going to travel in December
- **Eles vão trabalhar amanhã** (eh-leez vow trah-bah-lyar ah-mahn-yang) — They're going to work tomorrow

### Examples with -ER verbs:

- **Eu vou comer feijoada** (eh-oo voh koh-mer fay-zhoh-ah-dah) — I'm going to eat feijoada
- **Você vai aprender rápido** (voh-seh vah-ee ah-pren-der hah-pee-doo) — You're going to learn quickly
- **Nós vamos beber suco** (nohs vah-mooz beh-ber soo-koo) — We're going to drink juice

### Examples with -IR verbs:

- **Eu vou sair cedo** (eh-oo voh sah-eer seh-doo) — I'm going to leave early
- **Ela vai abrir uma loja** (eh-lah vah-ee ah-breer oo-mah loh-zhah) — She's going to open a shop
- **Eles vão partir amanhã** (eh-leez vow par-cheer ah-mahn-yang) — They're going to leave tomorrow

### Questions:

- **Você vai sair hoje?** (voh-seh vah-ee sah-eer oh-zhee) — Are you going to go out today?
- **O que vocês vão fazer?** (oo kee voh-sehz vow fah-zer) — What are you all going to do?
- **Quando nós vamos comer?** (kwan-doo nohs vah-mooz koh-mer) — When are we going to eat?

### Negative:

- **Eu não vou ir** (eh-oo now voh eer) — I'm not going to go
- **Ela não vai poder** (eh-lah now vah-ee poh-der) — She's not going to be able to
- **Nós não vamos trabalhar amanhã** (nohs now vah-mooz trah-bah-lyar ah-mahn-yang) — We're not going to work tomorrow

## Futuro Simples: The Formal Future

The futuro simples is formed by adding endings directly to the infinitive. It's used in formal writing, news reports, speeches, and certain fixed phrases.

### Regular Conjugation

The endings are the same for all three verb groups (-ar, -er, -ir):

| Pronoun | Ending | Falar | Comer | Abrir |
|---------|--------|-------|-------|-------|
| eu | -ei | falarei | comerei | abrirei |
| você / ele / ela | -á | falará | comerá | abrirá |
| nós | -emos | falaremos | comeremos | abriremos |
| vocês / eles / elas | -ão | falarão | comerão | abrirão |

- **Eu falarei com o diretor** (eh-oo fah-lah-ray kong oo jee-reh-tor) — I will speak with the director
- **Ela estudará medicina** (eh-lah esh-too-dah-rah meh-jee-see-nah) — She will study medicine
- **Nós comeremos juntos** (nohs koh-meh-reh-mooz zhoon-tooz) — We will eat together
- **Eles abrirão a loja às nove** (eh-leez ah-bree-row ah loh-zhah ahz noh-vee) — They will open the store at nine

> **Tip:** The futuro simples adds endings to the **full infinitive**, not the stem. Think of it as: infinitive + ending.

### Irregular Verbs in the Futuro Simples

Only three verbs are irregular in the futuro simples. They shorten their infinitive before adding the endings:

#### Fazer (to do/make) → far-

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---------|-------------|
| eu | farei |
| você / ele / ela | fará |
| nós | faremos |
| vocês / eles / elas | farão |

- **Eu farei o possível** (eh-oo fah-ray oo poh-see-vew) — I will do everything possible
- **O que você fará?** (oo kee voh-seh fah-rah) — What will you do?

#### Dizer (to say) → dir-

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---------|-------------|
| eu | direi |
| você / ele / ela | dirá |
| nós | diremos |
| vocês / eles / elas | dirão |

- **Eu direi a verdade** (eh-oo jee-ray ah vehr-dah-jee) — I will tell the truth
- **Ela dirá o que pensa** (eh-lah jee-rah oo kee pen-sah) — She will say what she thinks

#### Trazer (to bring) → trar-

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---------|-------------|
| eu | trarei |
| você / ele / ela | trará |
| nós | traremos |
| vocês / eles / elas | trarão |

- **Eu trarei o bolo** (eh-oo trah-ray oo boh-loo) — I will bring the cake
- **Eles trarão as bebidas** (eh-leez trah-row ahz beh-bee-dahz) — They will bring the drinks

## Comparing the Two Futures

| Ir + Infinitive | Futuro Simples |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Everyday speech | Formal writing / speeches |
| Plans and intentions | Certainties and predictions |
| Most common in Brazil | News, literature, laws |
| "Eu vou falar" | "Eu falarei" |

Both mean essentially the same thing — the choice depends on context and formality:

- **Casual:** Eu vou te ligar depois — I'm going to call you later
- **Formal:** Eu te ligarei posteriormente — I will call you subsequently

### When Brazilians use the Futuro Simples:

1. **News and reporting:** "O presidente falará à nação" — The president will address the nation
2. **Promises and commitments:** "Eu nunca te esquecerei" — I will never forget you
3. **Laws and regulations:** "O contrato terminará em dezembro" — The contract will end in December
4. **Fixed expressions:** "Será que...?" — Could it be that...? / I wonder if...

### Common expression with futuro simples:

- **Será que vai chover?** (seh-rah kee vah-ee shoh-ver) — I wonder if it's going to rain?
- **Será que ele vem?** (seh-rah kee eh-lee veng) — I wonder if he's coming?

## Time Expressions for the Future

- **Amanhã** (ah-mahn-yang) — Tomorrow
- **Depois de amanhã** (deh-poysh jee ah-mahn-yang) — The day after tomorrow
- **Na semana que vem** (nah seh-mah-nah kee veng) — Next week
- **No mês que vem** (noo mesh kee veng) — Next month
- **No ano que vem** (noo ah-noo kee veng) — Next year
- **Daqui a pouco** (dah-kee ah poh-koo) — In a little while
- **Em breve** (eng breh-vee) — Soon
- **No futuro** (noo foo-too-roo) — In the future

- **Amanhã eu vou acordar cedo** (ah-mahn-yang eh-oo voh ah-kor-dar seh-doo) — Tomorrow I'm going to wake up early
- **Na semana que vem, nós vamos sair** (nah seh-mah-nah kee veng nohs vah-mooz sah-eer) — Next week, we're going to go out
- **Daqui a pouco eu volto** (dah-kee ah poh-koo eh-oo vohl-too) — I'll be back in a little while
