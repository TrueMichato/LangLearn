# Present Subjunctive (Subjuntivo Presente)

<!-- vocab-inventory: {"mode":"explicit"} -->

The subjunctive mood expresses wishes, doubts, possibilities, and emotions — things that aren't certain facts. While English rarely uses the subjunctive (compare "I suggest he **go**" vs. "he **goes**"), Portuguese uses it constantly. Mastering the present subjunctive is essential because it appears after dozens of common triggers in everyday conversation.

## Formation

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"subjunctive-formation","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Formation","rule":"The present subjunctive normally uses the eu indicative base and opposite vowel endings","example":"How is the present subjunctive normally built from the present indicative eu form?","answer":"Drop final -o and add -e endings for -ar verbs or -a endings for -er/-ir verbs; exceptional forms must be learned separately.","explanation":"The opposite-vowel guide is useful, but does not derive exceptional forms such as seja and vá."} -->

The present subjunctive is normally formed by taking the **eu** form of the present indicative, dropping the **-o**, and adding the opposite vowel endings. Exceptional forms such as **seja** and **vá** must be learned separately:

- **-ar verbs** → use **-e** endings
- **-er/-ir verbs** → use **-a** endings

### -AR Verbs (falar → fal-)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"subjunctive-ar","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"-AR Verbs (falar → fal-)","rule":"Present-subjunctive -ar verbs use -e endings","example":"É necessário que nós ___. (falar)","answer":"falemos","explanation":"Present-subjunctive -ar verbs use -e endings: nós falemos."} -->

| Pronoun | Ending | Subjunctive |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| que eu | -e | fale |
| que você / ele / ela | -e | fale |
| que nós | -emos | falemos |
| que vocês / eles / elas | -em | falem |

### -ER Verbs (comer → com-)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"subjunctive-er","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"-ER Verbs (comer → com-)","rule":"Present subjunctive uses the opposite vowel: -ar verbs → -e endings, -er/-ir verbs → -a endings","example":"Espero que ela ___ bem (comer)","answer":"coma","explanation":"Comer uses -a in the present subjunctive after espero que."} -->

| Pronoun | Ending | Subjunctive |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| que eu | -a | coma |
| que você / ele / ela | -a | coma |
| que nós | -amos | comamos |
| que vocês / eles / elas | -am | comam |

### -IR Verbs (abrir → abr-)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"subjunctive-ir","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"-IR Verbs (abrir → abr-)","rule":"Abrir has plural present subjunctive abram","example":"Quero que eles ___ a porta. (abrir)","answer":"abram","explanation":"Abrir forms its plural present subjunctive with -am: abram."} -->

| Pronoun | Ending | Subjunctive |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| que eu | -a | abra |
| que você / ele / ela | -a | abra |
| que nós | -amos | abramos |
| que vocês / eles / elas | -am | abram |

> **Key insight:** The subjunctive uses the **opposite** vowel from the indicative. -AR verbs use -e (instead of -a), and -ER/-IR verbs use -a (instead of -e/-i).

- **Espero que você fale devagar** (esh-peh-roo kee voh-seh fah-lee deh-vah-gar) — I hope you speak slowly
- **É importante que ela coma bem** (eh een-por-tan-chee kee eh-lah koh-mah beng) — It's important that she eat well
- **Quero que eles abram a porta** (keh-roo kee eh-leez ah-brang ah por-tah) — I want them to open the door

## Irregular Verbs

Several common verbs are irregular in the present subjunctive. These must be memorized:

### Ser (to be)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"subjunctive-ser","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Ser (to be)","rule":"Ser has the exceptional subjunctive stem sej-","example":"Espero que ele ___ feliz. (ser)","answer":"seja","explanation":"Ser has the exceptional subjunctive stem sej-: seja."} -->

| Pronoun | Subjunctive |
|---------|-------------|
| que eu | seja |
| que você / ele / ela | seja |
| que nós | sejamos |
| que vocês / eles / elas | sejam |

- **Espero que ele seja feliz** (esh-peh-roo kee eh-lee seh-zhah feh-leez) — I hope he is happy
- **É preciso que nós sejamos pacientes** (eh preh-see-zoo kee nohs seh-zhah-mooz pah-see-en-cheez) — It's necessary that we be patient

### Estar (to be — temporary)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"subjunctive-estar","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Estar (to be — temporary)","rule":"Key irregular present subjunctives: ser→seja, estar→esteja, ir→vá, ter→tenha, saber→saiba","example":"Tomara que tudo ___ bem (estar)","answer":"esteja","explanation":"Estar uses esteja after a hope expression such as tomara que."} -->

| Pronoun | Subjunctive |
|---------|-------------|
| que eu | esteja |
| que você / ele / ela | esteja |
| que nós | estejamos |
| que vocês / eles / elas | estejam |

- **Espero que você esteja bem** (esh-peh-roo kee voh-seh esh-teh-zhah beng) — I hope you are well
- **Duvido que ele esteja em casa** (doo-vee-doo kee eh-lee esh-teh-zhah eng kah-zah) — I doubt he is at home

### Ir (to go)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"subjunctive-irregular-ir","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Ir (to go)","rule":"Ir has singular present subjunctive vá","example":"Talvez eu ___ amanhã. | Pronoun | Subjunctive |
|---------|-------------|
| que eu | vá |
| que você / ele / ela | vá |
| que nós | vamos |
| que vocês / eles / elas | vão |

- **Espero que ela vá à festa** (esh-peh-roo kee eh-lah vah ah fesh-tah) — I hope she goes to the party
- **Talvez eu vá amanhã** (tow-vez eh-oo vah ah-mahn-yang) — Maybe I'll go tomorrow

### Ter (to have)

| Pronoun | Subjunctive |

| que eu | tenha |

- **Espero que você tenha um bom dia** (esh-peh-roo kee voh-seh ten-yah ung bong jee-ah) — I hope you have a good day
- **É importante que nós tenhamos paciência** (eh een-por-tan-chee kee nohs ten-yah-mooz pah-see-en-see-ah) — It's important that we have patience

### Saber (to know)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"subjunctive-saber","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Saber (to know)","rule":"Saber has singular present subjunctive saiba","example":"Duvido que ele ___ a resposta. ## Triggers: When to Use the Subjunctive

The present subjunctive is triggered by specific words and expressions. Here are the main categories:

### 1. Wishes and Desires (Querer que, Esperar que, Desejar que)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"wish-trigger","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"1. Wishes and Desires (Querer que, Esperar que, Desejar que)","rule":"Tomara que introduces a hope with the subjunctive","example":"___ que não chova. (I hope; invariable expression, not a form of esperar)","answer":"Tomara","explanation":"Tomara que introduces a hope and takes the subjunctive.","legacyAliases":[{"rule":"Tomara que introduces a hope with the subjunctive","example":"___ que não chova. (I hope)"}]} -->

- **Eu quero que você venha** (eh-oo keh-roo kee voh-seh ven-yah) — I want you to come
- **Espero que tudo dê certo** (esh-peh-roo kee too-doo deh ser-too) — I hope everything works out
- **Desejo que vocês sejam felizes** (deh-zeh-zhoo kee voh-sehz seh-zhang feh-lee-zeez) — I wish you all to be happy
- **Tomara que não chova** (toh-mah-rah kee now shoh-vah) — I hope it doesn't rain

### 2. Impersonal Expressions (É importante que, É preciso que)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"impersonal-trigger","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"2. Impersonal Expressions (É importante que, É preciso que)","rule":"These evaluative and possibility expressions take subjunctive clauses","example":"Why do é importante que and é possível que take the subjunctive?","answer":"They express evaluation, necessity, or possibility rather than stating a fact.","explanation":"Any answer naming evaluation, necessity, or possibility counts; a plain statement of fact, as in É certo que ela vem, takes the indicative.","legacyAliases":[{"rule":"These evaluative and possibility expressions take subjunctive clauses","example":"Which mood follows é importante que and é possível que in this lesson?"}]} -->

- **É importante que você estude** (eh een-por-tan-chee kee voh-seh esh-too-jee) — It's important that you study
- **É necessário que nós falemos** (eh neh-seh-sah-ree-oo kee nohs fah-leh-mooz) — It's necessary that we talk
- **É possível que ele venha** (eh poh-see-vew kee eh-lee ven-yah) — It's possible that he comes
- **É bom que ela saiba** (eh bong kee eh-lah sah-ee-bah) — It's good that she knows
- **É provável que chova** (eh proh-vah-vew kee shoh-vah) — It's likely that it'll rain

### 3. Doubt (Duvidar que, Não acreditar que)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"doubt-trigger","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"3. Doubt (Duvidar que, Não acreditar que)","rule":"Não acreditar que and duvidar que introduce subjunctive doubt clauses here","example":"Não acredito que isso ___ verdade. (ser)","answer":"seja","explanation":"Não acreditar que and duvidar que introduce doubt and take the subjunctive in these examples."} -->

- **Duvido que ele passe na prova** (doo-vee-doo kee eh-lee pah-see nah proh-vah) — I doubt he passes the test
- **Não acredito que isso seja verdade** (now ah-kreh-jee-too kee ee-soo seh-zhah vehr-dah-jee) — I don't believe that's true

### 4. Talvez (Maybe)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"talvez-trigger","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"4. Talvez (Maybe)","rule":"Clause-initial talvez normally selects the subjunctive in standard usage","example":"___ ele venha amanhã (Maybe he'll come tomorrow)","answer":"Talvez","explanation":"Clause-initial talvez normally selects the subjunctive in standard usage; this is not an absolute rule for every position or register.","legacyAliases":[{"rule":"'Talvez' always triggers the subjunctive in Portuguese","example":"___ ele venha amanhã (Maybe he'll come tomorrow)"}]} -->

Clause-initial **talvez** normally takes the subjunctive in standard usage. This is not an absolute rule for every position or register.

- **Talvez eu vá** (tow-vez eh-oo vah) — Maybe I'll go
- **Talvez ela saiba** (tow-vez eh-lah sah-ee-bah) — Maybe she knows
- **Talvez chova amanhã** (tow-vez shoh-vah ah-mahn-yang) — Maybe it'll rain tomorrow

### 5. Conjunctions (Quando, Embora, Para que, Antes que)

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"conjunction-tense-contrast","kind":"rule","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"5. Conjunctions (Quando, Embora, Para que, Antes que)","rule":"Future quando and concessive embora select different subjunctive tenses here","example":"How do chegar in Quando ele chegar and seja in Embora seja difícil differ in tense?","answer":"Chegar is future subjunctive after future-oriented quando; seja is present subjunctive after embora.","explanation":"Conjunction and meaning select the mood and tense; future reference alone does not require the future subjunctive after every conjunction.","legacyAliases":[{"rule":"Future quando and concessive embora select different subjunctive tenses here","example":"In Quando ele chegar and Embora seja difícil, are chegar and seja the same subjunctive tense?"}]} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"purpose-trigger","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"5. Conjunctions (Quando, Embora, Para que, Antes que)","rule":"Para que introduces a subjunctive purpose clause","example":"Falo devagar para que você ___. (entender)","answer":"entenda","explanation":"Para que introduces a purpose clause with the subjunctive."} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"rule-quando-future","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"5. Conjunctions (Quando, Embora, Para que, Antes que)","rule":"Quando referring to a future event takes the future subjunctive","example":"Which subjunctive follows quando when it refers to a future event?","answer":"The future subjunctive, as in quando ele chegar.","explanation":"Any answer naming the future subjunctive counts; embora, para que, and antes que take the present subjunctive here."} -->

**Embora**, **para que**, and **antes que** introduce subjunctive clauses in the examples below. **Quando** referring to a future event instead uses the **future subjunctive**: **chegar** below is not present subjunctive. Present subjunctive can also refer to the future, as in **antes que chova**:

- **Quando ele chegar, me avise** (kwan-doo eh-lee sheh-gar mee ah-vee-zee) — When he arrives, let me know
- **Embora seja difícil, eu vou tentar** (en-boh-rah seh-zhah jee-fee-see-oo eh-oo voh ten-tar) — Although it's difficult, I'm going to try
- **Vou sair antes que chova** (voh sah-eer an-cheez kee shoh-vah) — I'll leave before it rains
- **Falo devagar para que você entenda** (fah-loo deh-vah-gar pah-rah kee voh-seh en-ten-dah) — I speak slowly so that you understand

## Subjunctive vs. Indicative

<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"indicative-subjunctive-choice","kind":"rule","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Subjunctive vs. Indicative","rule":"Sei que presents a fact; espero que introduces a wish","example":"Why do sei que ele está aqui and espero que ele esteja aqui use different moods?","answer":"Sei que presents a fact with the indicative; espero que expresses a wish with the subjunctive.","explanation":"The speaker's construction and attitude matter, not merely whether the event is objectively certain."} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"rule-subjunctive-meaning","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Subjunctive vs. Indicative","rule":"The subjunctive expresses uncertainty, wishes, or subjectivity","example":"What does the subjunctive express, as opposed to the indicative's statements of fact?","answer":"Uncertainty, wishes, or subjectivity.","explanation":"Any answer naming uncertainty, wishes, doubt, emotion, or subjectivity counts."} -->

The key difference: the **indicative** states facts; the **subjunctive** expresses uncertainty, wishes, or subjectivity.

| Indicative (fact) | Subjunctive (uncertainty) |
|---|---|
| Eu sei que ele **fala** português | Espero que ele **fale** português |
| É certo que ela **vem** | É possível que ela **venha** |
| Eu acho que ele **sabe** | Duvido que ele **saiba** |

- **Eu sei que ele está aqui** (fact — indicative) — I know he is here
- **Espero que ele esteja aqui** (wish — subjunctive) — I hope he is here
