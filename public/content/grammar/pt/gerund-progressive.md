# The Gerund and Progressive Tenses (Gerúndio e Tempos Progressivos)

In Brazilian Portuguese, the gerund is used to express ongoing actions, similar to the English "-ing" form. This is one of the key differences between Brazilian and European Portuguese — Brazilians say "estou falando" (I am speaking) while the Portuguese say "estou a falar."

## Gerund Formation

The gerund is formed by replacing the infinitive ending with the appropriate gerund ending:

| Conjugation | Infinitive | Gerund | Example |
|-------------|-----------|--------|---------|
| -ar verbs | falar | fal**ando** | speaking |
| -er verbs | comer | com**endo** | eating |
| -ir verbs | partir | part**indo** | leaving |

- **falando** (fah-LAN-doo) — speaking
- **comendo** (koh-MEN-doo) — eating
- **partindo** (par-CHIN-doo) — leaving
- **dormindo** (dor-MEEN-doo) — sleeping
- **escrevendo** (esh-kreh-VEN-doo) — writing
- **ouvindo** (oh-VEEN-doo) — listening

### Irregular Gerunds

Most verbs form the gerund regularly, but a few have spelling changes:

- **dizer → dizendo** (jee-ZEN-doo) — saying
- **fazer → fazendo** (fah-ZEN-doo) — making/doing
- **trazer → trazendo** (trah-ZEN-doo) — bringing
- **pôr → pondo** (PON-doo) — putting
- **vir → vindo** (VEEN-doo) — coming

## Present Progressive: estar + gerund

The most common progressive form combines **estar** with the gerund to express what is happening right now:

- **Eu estou estudando português.** (eu es-TOH es-too-DAN-doo) — I am studying Portuguese.
- **Ela está cozinhando o jantar.** (EH-lah es-TAH koh-zee-NYAN-doo) — She is cooking dinner.
- **Nós estamos assistindo um filme.** (nois es-TAH-mohs ah-sees-CHIN-doo) — We are watching a movie.
- **Eles estão trabalhando muito.** (EH-lees es-TOWN trah-bah-LYAN-doo) — They are working a lot.
- **Vocês estão aprendendo rápido!** (vo-SEHS es-TOWN ah-pren-DEN-doo) — You all are learning fast!

### Contracted Forms (Very Common in Speech)

In spoken Brazilian Portuguese, **estar** is almost always contracted:

- **Tô estudando.** (toh es-too-DAN-doo) — I'm studying.
- **Tá chovendo.** (tah shoh-VEN-doo) — It's raining.
- **Tamos indo.** (TAH-mohs IN-doo) — We're going.
- **Cê tá brincando?** (seh tah breen-KAN-doo) — Are you kidding?

## Past Progressive: estava + gerund

To express what was happening at a specific moment in the past:

- **Eu estava dormindo quando você ligou.** (eu es-TAH-vah dor-MEEN-doo) — I was sleeping when you called.
- **Ela estava lendo um livro.** (EH-lah es-TAH-vah LEN-doo) — She was reading a book.
- **Nós estávamos jantando às oito.** (nois es-TAH-vah-mohs jan-TAN-doo) — We were having dinner at eight.
- **Eles estavam conversando no parque.** (EH-lees es-TAH-vahn con-ver-SAN-doo) — They were talking in the park.

## Other Progressive Constructions

### continuar + gerund (to keep doing)

- **Ele continua estudando mesmo cansado.** (EH-lee con-CHEE-noo-ah es-too-DAN-doo) — He keeps studying even though he's tired.
- **Continuamos tentando até conseguir.** (con-chee-noo-AH-mohs ten-TAN-doo) — We keep trying until we succeed.

### ficar + gerund (to stay doing / end up doing)

- **Ela ficou esperando por horas.** (EH-lah fee-KOH es-peh-RAN-doo) — She stayed waiting for hours.
- **Não fica falando isso!** (nown FEE-kah fah-LAN-doo) — Don't keep saying that!

### ir + gerund (gradual action)

- **A situação vai melhorando aos poucos.** (ah see-too-ah-SOWN vai meh-lyo-RAN-doo) — The situation keeps getting better little by little.
- **O dia foi escurecendo.** (oo JEE-ah foy es-koo-reh-SEN-doo) — The day gradually got dark.

### andar + gerund (to go around doing)

- **Ele anda trabalhando demais.** (EH-lee AN-dah trah-bah-LYAN-doo) — He's been working too much (lately).
- **Ando pensando em mudar de cidade.** (AN-doo pen-SAN-doo) — I've been thinking about moving to another city.

## Brazilian vs European Portuguese

| Brazilian Portuguese | European Portuguese | English |
|---------------------|--------------------| --------|
| Estou falando | Estou a falar | I am speaking |
| Estava comendo | Estava a comer | I was eating |
| Fico pensando | Fico a pensar | I keep thinking |
