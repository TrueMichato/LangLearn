# Common Irregular Verbs (ir, ter, fazer)

Many of the most frequently used verbs in Portuguese are irregular — they don't follow the standard conjugation patterns. The good news is that you use these verbs so often that they'll become second nature quickly. Let's learn the six most essential irregular verbs.

## Ir (eer) — To Go

**Ir** is one of the most important verbs in Portuguese. It's also used to form the informal future tense.

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|------------|---------------|
| Eu | **vou** | voh |
| Você / Ele / Ela | **vai** | vai |
| Nós | **vamos** | vah-mooz |
| A gente | **vai** | vai |
| Vocês / Eles / Elas | **vão** | vow |

- **Eu vou ao mercado** (eh-oo voh ow mehr-kah-doo) — I go to the market
- **Você vai para casa?** (voh-say vai pah-rah kah-zah) — Are you going home?
- **Nós vamos à praia** (nohz vah-mooz ah prah-yah) — We go to the beach
- **Eles vão ao cinema** (eh-leez vow ow see-neh-mah) — They go to the movies

### Ir + Infinitive = Informal Future

This is the most common way to express the future in spoken Brazilian Portuguese:

- **Eu vou estudar** (eh-oo voh esh-too-dahr) — I'm going to study
- **Ela vai viajar** (eh-lah vai vee-ah-zhahr) — She's going to travel
- **Nós vamos comer pizza** (nohz vah-mooz koh-mehr pee-zah) — We're going to eat pizza
- **Vocês vão gostar** (voh-sehz vow gosh-tahr) — You're going to like it

## Ter (tehr) — To Have

**Ter** expresses possession and is also used in many expressions where English uses "to be":

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|------------|---------------|
| Eu | **tenho** | teh-nyoo |
| Você / Ele / Ela | **tem** | tayn |
| Nós | **temos** | teh-mooz |
| A gente | **tem** | tayn |
| Vocês / Eles / Elas | **têm** | tayn |

> **Note:** **tem** and **têm** are pronounced the same. The circumflex on **têm** only appears in the plural form in writing.

- **Eu tenho dois irmãos** (eh-oo teh-nyoo doysh eer-mowz) — I have two brothers
- **Você tem um carro?** (voh-say tayn oon kah-hoo) — Do you have a car?
- **Nós temos uma casa grande** (nohz teh-mooz oo-mah kah-zah grahn-jee) — We have a big house
- **Eles têm muitos amigos** (eh-leez tayn mween-tooz ah-mee-gooz) — They have many friends

### Ter + Noun = Common Expressions

Portuguese uses **ter** (to have) where English uses **to be** in many expressions:

- **Eu tenho fome** (eh-oo teh-nyoo foh-mee) — I am hungry (literally: I have hunger)
- **Eu tenho sede** (eh-oo teh-nyoo seh-jee) — I am thirsty
- **Eu tenho sono** (eh-oo teh-nyoo soh-noo) — I am sleepy
- **Eu tenho medo** (eh-oo teh-nyoo meh-doo) — I am scared
- **Eu tenho razão** (eh-oo teh-nyoo hah-zow) — I am right
- **Ela tem vinte anos** (eh-lah tayn veen-chee ah-nooz) — She is twenty years old
- **Eu tenho pressa** (eh-oo teh-nyoo preh-sah) — I am in a hurry

## Fazer (fah-zehr) — To Do / To Make

**Fazer** is extremely versatile and appears in many everyday expressions:

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|------------|---------------|
| Eu | **faço** | fah-soo |
| Você / Ele / Ela | **faz** | fahz |
| Nós | **fazemos** | fah-zeh-mooz |
| A gente | **faz** | fahz |
| Vocês / Eles / Elas | **fazem** | fah-zayn |

- **Eu faço exercícios** (eh-oo fah-soo eh-zehr-see-see-ooz) — I do exercises
- **Ela faz um bolo** (eh-lah fahz oon boh-loo) — She makes a cake
- **Nós fazemos o jantar** (nohz fah-zeh-mooz oo zhahn-tahr) — We make dinner
- **O que você faz?** (oo kee voh-say fahz) — What do you do?

### Fazer in Weather Expressions

- **Faz calor** (fahz kah-lohr) — It's hot (weather)
- **Faz frio** (fahz free-oo) — It's cold (weather)
- **Faz sol** (fahz sow) — It's sunny

### Fazer in Time Expressions

- **Faz dois anos** (fahz doysh ah-nooz) — It's been two years
- **Faz tempo** (fahz tehn-poo) — It's been a while

## Poder (poh-dehr) — To Be Able To / Can

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|------------|---------------|
| Eu | **posso** | poh-soo |
| Você / Ele / Ela | **pode** | poh-jee |
| Nós | **podemos** | poh-deh-mooz |
| A gente | **pode** | poh-jee |
| Vocês / Eles / Elas | **podem** | poh-dayn |

- **Eu posso ajudar?** (eh-oo poh-soo ah-zhoo-dahr) — Can I help?
- **Você pode repetir?** (voh-say poh-jee heh-peh-cheer) — Can you repeat?
- **Nós podemos ir amanhã** (nohz poh-deh-mooz eer ah-mah-nyah) — We can go tomorrow
- **Eles não podem ficar** (eh-leez now poh-dayn fee-kahr) — They can't stay

## Querer (keh-rehr) — To Want

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|------------|---------------|
| Eu | **quero** | keh-roo |
| Você / Ele / Ela | **quer** | kehr |
| Nós | **queremos** | keh-reh-mooz |
| A gente | **quer** | kehr |
| Vocês / Eles / Elas | **querem** | keh-rayn |

- **Eu quero água** (eh-oo keh-roo ah-gwah) — I want water
- **Você quer café?** (voh-say kehr kah-feh) — Do you want coffee?
- **Nós queremos viajar** (nohz keh-reh-mooz vee-ah-zhahr) — We want to travel
- **Eles querem sair** (eh-leez keh-rayn sah-eer) — They want to go out

## Saber (sah-behr) — To Know (facts/skills)

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|------------|---------------|
| Eu | **sei** | say |
| Você / Ele / Ela | **sabe** | sah-bee |
| Nós | **sabemos** | sah-beh-mooz |
| A gente | **sabe** | sah-bee |
| Vocês / Eles / Elas | **sabem** | sah-bayn |

- **Eu sei falar português** (eh-oo say fah-lahr por-too-gesh) — I know how to speak Portuguese
- **Você sabe nadar?** (voh-say sah-bee nah-dahr) — Do you know how to swim?
- **Eu não sei** (eh-oo now say) — I don't know
- **Ela sabe cozinhar** (eh-lah sah-bee koh-zee-nyahr) — She knows how to cook

> **Note:** **Saber** is for knowing facts and skills. For knowing people and places, use **conhecer**: **Eu conheço São Paulo** (I know São Paulo).
