# Advanced Prepositions and Conjunctions (Preposições e Conjunções Avançadas)

As you progress in Portuguese, you'll need more sophisticated ways to connect ideas, express cause and effect, and build complex sentences. This lesson covers compound prepositions and subordinating conjunctions that are essential for intermediate and advanced communication.

## Compound Prepositions

### Apesar de (despite / in spite of)

- **Apesar do frio, fomos à praia.** (ah-peh-ZAR doo FREE-oo) — Despite the cold, we went to the beach.
- **Apesar de estar cansada, ela continuou trabalhando.** (ah-peh-ZAR jee es-TAR) — Despite being tired, she kept working.
- **Apesar de tudo, estou feliz.** (ah-peh-ZAR jee TOO-doo) — Despite everything, I'm happy.
- **Ele passou na prova apesar de não ter estudado.** (EH-lee pah-SOH) — He passed the test despite not having studied.

### Através de (through / by means of)

- **Conheci ela através de um amigo.** (koh-nyeh-SEE EH-lah ah-trah-VEHS jee) — I met her through a friend.
- **Aprendemos muito através da leitura.** (ah-pren-DEH-mohs MOO-ee-too ah-trah-VEHS) — We learn a lot through reading.
- **O acordo foi feito através de negociação.** (oo ah-KOR-doo foy FAY-too) — The agreement was made through negotiation.

### Devido a (due to)

- **Devido à chuva, o jogo foi cancelado.** (deh-VEE-doo ah SHOO-vah) — Due to the rain, the game was cancelled.
- **Devido ao trânsito, cheguei atrasado.** (deh-VEE-doo ow TRAHN-zee-too) — Due to traffic, I arrived late.
- **A escola foi fechada devido ao calor.** (ah es-KOH-lah foy feh-SHAH-dah) — The school was closed due to the heat.

### Em vez de (instead of)

- **Em vez de reclamar, faça algo.** (ehn vehs jee heh-klah-MAR) — Instead of complaining, do something.
- **Ele foi ao cinema em vez de estudar.** (EH-lee foy ow see-NEH-mah) — He went to the movies instead of studying.
- **Em vez de carro, fui de ônibus.** (ehn vehs jee KAH-hoo) — Instead of (going by) car, I went by bus.

### Além de (besides / in addition to)

- **Além de bonita, ela é inteligente.** (ah-LEHN jee boo-NEE-tah) — Besides being pretty, she's intelligent.
- **Além do português, ele fala inglês e espanhol.** (ah-LEHN doo por-too-GEHZ) — Besides Portuguese, he speaks English and Spanish.
- **Além de trabalhar, ela estuda à noite.** (ah-LEHN jee trah-bah-LYAR) — In addition to working, she studies at night.

### De acordo com (according to)

- **De acordo com a pesquisa, 70% aprovaram.** (jee ah-KOR-doo con ah pes-KEE-zah) — According to the survey, 70% approved.
- **De acordo com o professor, a prova será difícil.** (jee ah-KOR-doo con oo pro-feh-SOR) — According to the teacher, the test will be difficult.
- **Tudo foi feito de acordo com o plano.** (TOO-doo foy FAY-too) — Everything was done according to plan.

### Por causa de (because of)

- **Fiquei em casa por causa da chuva.** (fee-KAY ehn KAH-zah por KOW-zah) — I stayed home because of the rain.
- **Ele está feliz por causa da promoção.** (EH-lee es-TAH feh-LEEZ) — He's happy because of the promotion.
- **Por causa do barulho, não consegui dormir.** (por KOW-zah doo bah-ROO-lyoo) — Because of the noise, I couldn't sleep.

### Other Useful Compound Prepositions

- **Diante de** (in front of / faced with): **Diante da situação, decidimos agir.** — Faced with the situation, we decided to act.
- **A respeito de** (regarding): **Quero falar a respeito do projeto.** — I want to talk about the project.
- **Em relação a** (in relation to): **Em relação ao preço, está bom.** — In relation to the price, it's fine.
- **A partir de** (starting from): **A partir de amanhã, vou acordar cedo.** — Starting from tomorrow, I'll wake up early.
- **Por meio de** (by means of): **Entrei em contato por meio de e-mail.** — I got in touch by email.

## Subordinating Conjunctions

### Embora (although)

Takes the **subjunctive**:

- **Embora esteja chovendo, vou sair.** (en-BOH-rah es-TEH-jah shoh-VEN-doo) — Although it's raining, I'll go out.
- **Embora ele seja rico, é humilde.** (en-BOH-rah EH-lee SEH-jah HEE-koo) — Although he's rich, he's humble.
- **Embora eu não concorde, respeito sua opinião.** (en-BOH-rah eu nown con-KOR-jee) — Although I don't agree, I respect your opinion.

### Enquanto (while)

- **Enquanto eu cozinho, você lava a louça.** (en-KWAN-too eu koh-ZEEN-yoo) — While I cook, you wash the dishes.
- **Enquanto estava no Brasil, aprendi muito.** (en-KWAN-too es-TAH-vah) — While I was in Brazil, I learned a lot.
- **Enquanto houver esperança, não desisto.** (en-KWAN-too oh-VEHR) — As long as there's hope, I won't give up.

### Assim que (as soon as)

- **Assim que eu chegar, te ligo.** (ah-SEEN kee eu sheh-GAR) — As soon as I arrive, I'll call you.
- **Assim que soubemos, ficamos felizes.** (ah-SEEN kee soh-BEH-mohs) — As soon as we found out, we were happy.
- **Assim que terminar, me avisa.** (ah-SEEN kee tehr-mee-NAR) — As soon as you finish, let me know.

### Desde que (since / provided that)

Has two meanings depending on context:

- **Desde que cheguei, não parei de trabalhar.** (DEHS-jee kee sheh-GAY) — Since I arrived, I haven't stopped working. *(temporal)*
- **Você pode ir, desde que volte cedo.** (DEHS-jee kee VOW-chee) — You can go, provided that you come back early. *(conditional — subjunctive)*
- **Desde que ele se mudou, não o vi mais.** (DEHS-jee kee EH-lee see moo-DOH) — Since he moved, I haven't seen him anymore.

### A menos que (unless)

Takes the **subjunctive**:

- **A menos que chova, vamos ao parque.** (ah MEH-nohs kee SHOH-vah) — Unless it rains, we'll go to the park.
- **Não vou sair a menos que você venha comigo.** (ah MEH-nohs kee vo-SEH VEN-yah) — I won't go out unless you come with me.
- **A menos que ele mude de ideia, o projeto continua.** (ah MEH-nohs kee EH-lee MOO-jee) — Unless he changes his mind, the project continues.

### Caso (in case)

Takes the **subjunctive**:

- **Caso você precise, me liga.** (KAH-zoo vo-SEH preh-SEE-zee) — In case you need, call me.
- **Leve um guarda-chuva, caso chova.** (LEH-vee oon GWAR-dah-SHOO-vah KAH-zoo SHOH-vah) — Take an umbrella, in case it rains.
- **Caso ele não venha, começamos sem ele.** (KAH-zoo EH-lee nown VEN-yah) — In case he doesn't come, we'll start without him.

### Para que (so that / in order that)

Takes the **subjunctive**:

- **Estudo para que eu possa passar na prova.** (es-TOO-doo PAH-rah kee eu POH-sah) — I study so that I can pass the test.
- **Fale mais alto para que todos ouçam.** (FAH-lee maish OW-too) — Speak louder so that everyone can hear.
