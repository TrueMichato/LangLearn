# Perfeito vs. Imperfeito: Choosing the Right Past Tense

One of the biggest challenges for Portuguese learners is knowing when to use the pretérito perfeito and when to use the pretérito imperfeito. Both describe the past, but they paint very different pictures. Think of it this way: the **perfeito** is a camera snapshot — a single, completed moment. The **imperfeito** is a video camera — recording an ongoing scene, a background, or a repeated routine.

## Quick Comparison

| Pretérito Perfeito | Pretérito Imperfeito |
|---|---|
| Completed actions | Ongoing/habitual actions |
| Specific moments | Background descriptions |
| "What happened" | "What was happening" |
| Snapshots | Video footage |
| One-time events | Repeated routines |

## When to Use the Pretérito Perfeito

Use the perfeito when the action is **completed** and has a clear beginning and/or end:

- **Eu comi pizza ontem** (eh-oo koh-mee pee-zah on-teng) — I ate pizza yesterday
- **Ela chegou às três horas** (eh-lah sheh-goh ahz trehz oh-rahz) — She arrived at three o'clock
- **Nós compramos uma casa** (nohs kon-prah-mooz oo-mah kah-zah) — We bought a house
- **De repente, ele gritou** (jee heh-pen-chee eh-lee gree-toh) — Suddenly, he shouted
- **O jogo começou e terminou rápido** (oo zhoh-goo koh-meh-soh ee ter-mee-noh hah-pee-doo) — The game started and ended quickly

**Key signals for perfeito:** ontem, na semana passada, de repente, uma vez, naquele dia, às três horas

## When to Use the Pretérito Imperfeito

Use the imperfeito for **ongoing states**, **habitual actions**, and **descriptions**:

- **Eu comia pizza toda sexta** (eh-oo koh-mee-ah pee-zah toh-dah sess-tah) — I used to eat pizza every Friday
- **Ela sempre chegava atrasada** (eh-lah sem-pree sheh-gah-vah ah-trah-zah-dah) — She always arrived late
- **A casa era grande e bonita** (ah kah-zah eh-rah gran-jee ee boh-nee-tah) — The house was big and beautiful
- **Ele dormia tranquilamente** (eh-lee dor-mee-ah tran-kwee-lah-men-chee) — He was sleeping peacefully
- **Nós morávamos perto da praia** (nohs moh-rah-vah-mooz per-too dah prah-yah) — We used to live near the beach

**Key signals for imperfeito:** sempre, todo dia, toda semana, geralmente, antigamente, quando era criança, naquela época

## Using Both Together: Narrating Stories

The real magic happens when you combine both tenses in the same sentence. This is how native speakers tell stories naturally.

### Pattern 1: Background (imperfeito) + Interruption (perfeito)

The imperfeito sets the scene, and the perfeito interrupts it with a sudden event:

- **Eu dormia quando o telefone tocou** (eh-oo dor-mee-ah kwan-doo oo teh-leh-foh-nee toh-koh) — I was sleeping when the phone rang
- **Chovia muito quando nós saímos** (shoh-vee-ah mween-too kwan-doo nohs sah-ee-mooz) — It was raining a lot when we left
- **Ele estudava quando a luz apagou** (eh-lee esh-too-dah-vah kwan-doo ah looz ah-pah-goh) — He was studying when the lights went out
- **As crianças brincavam quando começou a chover** (ahz kree-an-sahz breen-kah-vang kwan-doo koh-meh-soh ah shoh-ver) — The children were playing when it started to rain

> **Visualize it:**
> ```
> imperfeito: ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
> perfeito:              |  (interrupts!)
> ```

### Pattern 2: Scene Description (imperfeito) + Action (perfeito)

First describe the setting, then tell what happened:

- **O dia estava lindo. Eu decidi ir à praia** (oo jee-ah esh-tah-vah leen-doo. eh-oo deh-see-jee eer ah prah-yah) — The day was beautiful. I decided to go to the beach
- **A rua estava vazia. Um carro apareceu** (ah hoo-ah esh-tah-vah vah-zee-ah. ung kah-hoo ah-pah-reh-seh-oo) — The street was empty. A car appeared
- **Ela usava um vestido vermelho. Todos olharam** (eh-lah oo-zah-vah ung vesh-chee-doo ver-meh-lyoo. toh-dooz oh-lyah-rang) — She was wearing a red dress. Everyone looked

### Pattern 3: Two Simultaneous Background Actions (imperfeito + imperfeito)

When two ongoing actions happen at the same time:

- **Enquanto eu cozinhava, ela estudava** (en-kwan-too eh-oo koh-zeen-yah-vah eh-lah esh-too-dah-vah) — While I was cooking, she was studying
- **Ele cantava enquanto tomava banho** (eh-lee kan-tah-vah en-kwan-too toh-mah-vah bahn-yoo) — He was singing while he was taking a shower

### Pattern 4: Sequence of Completed Actions (perfeito + perfeito)

A chain of events that happened one after another:

- **Eu acordei, tomei café e saí** (eh-oo ah-kor-day toh-may kah-feh ee sah-ee) — I woke up, had coffee, and left
- **Ela entrou, sentou e começou a falar** (eh-lah en-troh sen-toh ee koh-meh-soh ah fah-lar) — She entered, sat down, and started to talk

## Practice: Choosing the Right Tense

Consider these pairs and notice how the meaning changes:

| Perfeito (completed) | Imperfeito (ongoing/habitual) |
|---|---|
| **Eu morei em Curitiba** — I lived in Curitiba (and moved away) | **Eu morava em Curitiba** — I used to live in Curitiba (describing a past period) |
| **Eu soube a resposta** — I found out the answer (at that moment) | **Eu sabia a resposta** — I knew the answer (ongoing knowledge) |
| **Ele foi gentil** — He was kind (on that occasion) | **Ele era gentil** — He was kind (characteristically) |
| **Eu tive medo** — I got scared (at a specific moment) | **Eu tinha medo** — I was scared / I used to be scared |
| **Ela pode sair** — She was able to leave (and did) | **Ela podia sair** — She could leave (ability, not necessarily did) |

- **Eu conheci minha esposa em 2010** (eh-oo koh-nyeh-see meen-yah esh-poh-zah eng doysh meel ee dez) — I met my wife in 2010 (first time meeting)
- **Eu conhecia minha esposa há muitos anos** (eh-oo koh-nyeh-see-ah meen-yah esh-poh-zah ah mween-tooz ah-nooz) — I knew my wife for many years (ongoing familiarity)

## A Full Narrative Example

Read this short story and notice how the tenses work together:

> **Era** (imperfeito) um dia bonito. O sol **brilhava** (imperfeito) e os pássaros **cantavam** (imperfeito). Maria **estava** (imperfeito) no jardim — ela **usava** (imperfeito) um chapéu grande. De repente, **começou** (perfeito) a chover. Maria **correu** (perfeito) para dentro de casa e **fechou** (perfeito) a porta. Ela **pegou** (perfeito) um livro que **estava** (imperfeito) na mesa e **começou** (perfeito) a ler.

Translation: It was a beautiful day. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Maria was in the garden — she was wearing a big hat. Suddenly, it started to rain. Maria ran inside and closed the door. She grabbed a book that was on the table and started to read.

> **Notice the pattern:** The imperfeito paints the background scene. The perfeito moves the story forward with actions.
