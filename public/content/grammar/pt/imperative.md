# Imperative (Imperativo)

The imperative mood is how you give commands, make requests, offer advice, and give instructions in Portuguese. In Brazilian Portuguese, the imperative is simpler than in European Portuguese because it's based on the **você** form. You'll hear it everywhere — from recipes and street directions to parenting and advertising.

## Affirmative Commands

In Brazilian Portuguese, the affirmative imperative for **você** is formed from the present subjunctive. In practice, this means:

- **-ar verbs** → change to **-e** ending
- **-er verbs** → change to **-a** ending
- **-ir verbs** → change to **-a** ending

| Verb | Infinitive | Imperative (você) |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| falar | to speak | **Fale!** |
| comer | to eat | **Coma!** |
| abrir | to open | **Abra!** |
| esperar | to wait | **Espere!** |
| beber | to drink | **Beba!** |
| escrever | to write | **Escreva!** |
| dormir | to sleep | **Durma!** |
| ouvir | to hear | **Ouça!** |

### Common -AR verb commands:

- **Fale mais devagar** (fah-lee mahyz deh-vah-gar) — Speak more slowly
- **Olhe para mim** (oh-lyee pah-rah meeng) — Look at me
- **Espere um momento** (esh-peh-ree ung moh-men-too) — Wait a moment
- **Feche a porta** (feh-shee ah por-tah) — Close the door
- **Ligue para mim** (lee-gee pah-rah meeng) — Call me

### Common -ER verb commands:

- **Coma tudo!** (koh-mah too-doo) — Eat everything!
- **Beba água** (beh-bah ah-gwah) — Drink water
- **Escreva seu nome aqui** (esh-kreh-vah seh-oo noh-mee ah-kee) — Write your name here
- **Corra!** (koh-hah) — Run!

### Common -IR verb commands:

- **Abra a janela** (ah-brah ah zhah-neh-lah) — Open the window
- **Durma bem** (door-mah beng) — Sleep well
- **Suba as escadas** (soo-bah ahz esh-kah-dahz) — Go up the stairs
- **Descubra a verdade** (desh-koo-brah ah vehr-dah-jee) — Discover the truth

## Negative Commands

To make a negative command, simply add **não** before the same form:

- **Não fale alto** (now fah-lee ow-too) — Don't speak loudly
- **Não coma isso** (now koh-mah ee-soo) — Don't eat that
- **Não abra a porta** (now ah-brah ah por-tah) — Don't open the door
- **Não se preocupe** (now see preh-oh-koo-pee) — Don't worry
- **Não esqueça** (now esh-keh-sah) — Don't forget
- **Não toque nisso** (now toh-kee nee-soo) — Don't touch that
- **Não saia sem guarda-chuva** (now sah-ee-ah seng gwahr-dah-shoo-vah) — Don't go out without an umbrella

> **Simple rule:** Affirmative and negative commands use the same verb form in Brazilian Portuguese. Just add **não** in front for negation.

## Irregular Imperatives

Some very common verbs have irregular imperative forms:

| Verb | Meaning | Affirmative | Negative |
|------|---------|-------------|----------|
| ser | to be | **Seja!** | **Não seja!** |
| estar | to be | **Esteja!** | **Não esteja!** |
| ir | to go | **Vá!** | **Não vá!** |
| ter | to have | **Tenha!** | **Não tenha!** |
| dar | to give | **Dê!** | **Não dê!** |
| saber | to know | **Saiba!** | **Não saiba!** |
| fazer | to do/make | **Faça!** | **Não faça!** |
| dizer | to say | **Diga!** | **Não diga!** |
| trazer | to bring | **Traga!** | **Não traga!** |
| vir | to come | **Venha!** | **Não venha!** |

- **Seja paciente** (seh-zhah pah-see-en-chee) — Be patient
- **Vá embora!** (vah en-boh-rah) — Go away!
- **Tenha cuidado** (ten-yah kwee-dah-doo) — Be careful (have care)
- **Faça a lição de casa** (fah-sah ah lee-sow jee kah-zah) — Do your homework
- **Diga a verdade** (jee-gah ah vehr-dah-jee) — Tell the truth
- **Venha cá!** (ven-yah kah) — Come here!
- **Traga seu passaporte** (trah-gah seh-oo pah-sah-por-chee) — Bring your passport
- **Dê uma chance** (deh oo-mah shan-see) — Give (it) a chance

## Plural Commands (Vocês)

For commands directed at multiple people, the form is the same — just make sure the context is clear:

- **Falem mais baixo** (fah-leng mahyz bah-ee-shoo) — Speak more quietly (you all)
- **Comam tudo** (koh-mang too-doo) — Eat everything (you all)
- **Venham aqui** (ven-yang ah-kee) — Come here (you all)
- **Não façam barulho** (now fah-sang bah-roo-lyoo) — Don't make noise (you all)
- **Sentem-se, por favor** (sen-teng-see por fah-vor) — Sit down, please (you all)

## Polite Requests with "Por Favor"

Adding **por favor** softens any command into a polite request:

- **Feche a porta, por favor** (feh-shee ah por-tah por fah-vor) — Close the door, please
- **Me passe o sal, por favor** (mee pah-see oo sow por fah-vor) — Pass me the salt, please
- **Fale mais devagar, por favor** (fah-lee mahyz deh-vah-gar por fah-vor) — Speak more slowly, please
- **Espere um momento, por favor** (esh-peh-ree ung moh-men-too por fah-vor) — Wait a moment, please

You can also soften commands with other expressions:

- **Você pode fechar a porta?** (voh-seh poh-jee feh-shar ah por-tah) — Can you close the door?
- **Você poderia me ajudar?** (voh-seh poh-deh-ree-ah mee ah-zhoo-dar) — Could you help me?
- **Será que você pode...** (seh-rah kee voh-seh poh-jee) — Would you be able to...

## Common Imperatives in Daily Life

### At home:

- **Acorda!** (ah-kor-dah) — Wake up!
- **Toma café** (toh-mah kah-feh) — Have your coffee
- **Arruma seu quarto** (ah-hoo-mah seh-oo kwar-too) — Clean up your room
- **Desliga a TV** (dez-lee-gah ah teh-veh) — Turn off the TV
- **Vai dormir** (vah-ee dor-meer) — Go to sleep

> **Note:** In very casual speech, Brazilians often use the present tense form (você present indicative) as an informal imperative: "Fala!" instead of "Fale!", "Olha!" instead of "Olhe!". Both are widely understood.

### On the street:

- **Vire à direita** (vee-ree ah jee-ray-tah) — Turn right
- **Siga em frente** (see-gah eng fren-chee) — Go straight ahead
- **Pare!** (pah-ree) — Stop!
- **Cuidado!** (kwee-dah-doo) — Be careful! / Watch out!
- **Atravesse a rua** (ah-trah-veh-see ah hoo-ah) — Cross the street

### At a restaurant:

- **Me traz um café** (mee trahz ung kah-feh) — Bring me a coffee (informal)
- **Traga a conta, por favor** (trah-gah ah kon-tah por fah-vor) — Bring the check, please
- **Escolha seu prato** (esh-kol-yah seh-oo prah-too) — Choose your dish
