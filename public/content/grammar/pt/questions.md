# Question Words (quem, o que, onde, quando)

Asking questions is one of the most important skills in any language. In Brazilian Portuguese, questions can be formed simply by changing your intonation, or you can use specific question words to ask for particular information. Let's learn all the essential question words!

## How to Form Questions

There are two main ways to ask questions in Brazilian Portuguese:

### 1. Intonation (Yes/No Questions)

Simply raise your voice at the end of a statement:

- **Você fala inglês.** → **Você fala inglês?** (voh-say fah-lah een-glesh) — Do you speak English?
- **Ele mora aqui.** → **Ele mora aqui?** (eh-lee moh-rah ah-kee) — Does he live here?
- **Está chovendo.** → **Está chovendo?** (esh-tah shoh-vehn-doo) — Is it raining?

### 2. Question Words (Information Questions)

Use a question word at the beginning of the sentence:

- **Onde você mora?** (ohn-jee voh-say moh-rah) — Where do you live?
- **O que você faz?** (oo kee voh-say fahz) — What do you do?

## The Question Words

### Quem (kayn) — Who

- **Quem é você?** (kayn eh voh-say) — Who are you?
- **Quem fez isso?** (kayn fez ee-soo) — Who did this?
- **Com quem você mora?** (kohn kayn voh-say moh-rah) — Who do you live with?
- **De quem é esse livro?** (jee kayn eh eh-see lee-vroo) — Whose book is this?
- **Para quem é o presente?** (pah-rah kayn eh oo preh-zehn-chee) — Who is the gift for?

### O que / Que (oo kee / kee) — What

**O que** and **que** both mean "what." **O que** is more common in Brazilian Portuguese:

- **O que é isso?** (oo kee eh ee-soo) — What is this?
- **O que você quer?** (oo kee voh-say kehr) — What do you want?
- **O que aconteceu?** (oo kee ah-kohn-teh-seh-oo) — What happened?
- **Que horas são?** (kee oh-rahz sow) — What time is it?
- **O que você faz no fim de semana?** (oo kee voh-say fahz noo feen jee seh-mah-nah) — What do you do on the weekend?

### Onde (ohn-jee) — Where

- **Onde você mora?** (ohn-jee voh-say moh-rah) — Where do you live?
- **Onde fica o banheiro?** (ohn-jee fee-kah oo bah-nyay-roo) — Where is the bathroom?
- **Onde você trabalha?** (ohn-jee voh-say trah-bah-lyah) — Where do you work?
- **De onde você é?** (jee ohn-jee voh-say eh) — Where are you from?

**Aonde** (ah-ohn-jee) is used with verbs of motion (going somewhere):

- **Aonde você vai?** (ah-ohn-jee voh-say vai) — Where are you going?
- **Aonde nós vamos?** (ah-ohn-jee nohz vah-mooz) — Where are we going?

> **Tip:** In everyday speech, many Brazilians use **onde** for both static location and motion. **Aonde** is more grammatically precise.

### Quando (kwahn-doo) — When

- **Quando você chega?** (kwahn-doo voh-say sheh-gah) — When do you arrive?
- **Quando é a festa?** (kwahn-doo eh ah fehs-tah) — When is the party?
- **Quando começa a aula?** (kwahn-doo koh-meh-sah ah ow-lah) — When does class start?
- **Desde quando você mora aqui?** (dehs-jee kwahn-doo voh-say moh-rah ah-kee) — Since when have you lived here?

### Como (koh-moo) — How

- **Como você está?** (koh-moo voh-say esh-tah) — How are you?
- **Como se chama?** (koh-moo see shah-mah) — What's your name? (literally: How do you call yourself?)
- **Como é o seu apartamento?** (koh-moo eh oo seh-oo ah-pahr-tah-mehn-too) — What's your apartment like?
- **Como você vai ao trabalho?** (koh-moo voh-say vai ow trah-bah-lyoo) — How do you go to work?
- **Como se diz "thank you" em português?** (koh-moo see jeez "thank you" ayn por-too-gesh) — How do you say "thank you" in Portuguese?

### Por que / Porque / Por quê (pohr kee / pohr-kee / pohr keh) — Why / Because

This is one of the trickiest aspects of Portuguese spelling. There are **four** forms:

| Form | Usage | Example |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Por que** | In questions (beginning or middle) | **Por que você estuda?** — Why do you study? |
| **Por quê?** | At the end of a question | **Você não vai? Por quê?** — You're not going? Why? |
| **Porque** | In answers (because) | **Porque eu gosto.** — Because I like it. |
| **Porquê** | As a noun (the reason) | **Qual é o porquê?** — What is the reason? |

- **Por que você gosta de português?** (pohr kee voh-say gosh-tah jee por-too-gesh) — Why do you like Portuguese?
- **Porque é uma língua bonita** (pohr-kee eh oo-mah leen-gwah boo-nee-tah) — Because it's a beautiful language
- **Você não veio? Por quê?** (voh-say now vay-oo pohr keh) — You didn't come? Why?

### Quanto / Quanta / Quantos / Quantas (kwahn-too) — How much / How many

This question word agrees in gender and number with the noun:

- **Quanto custa?** (kwahn-too koohs-tah) — How much does it cost?
- **Quantos anos você tem?** (kwahn-tooz ah-nooz voh-say tayn) — How old are you?
- **Quantas pessoas vão?** (kwahn-tahz peh-soh-ahz vow) — How many people are going?
- **Quanto tempo demora?** (kwahn-too tehn-poo deh-moh-rah) — How long does it take?
- **Quantos irmãos você tem?** (kwahn-tooz eer-mowz voh-say tayn) — How many siblings do you have?

### Qual / Quais (kwaw / kwai-eez) — Which / What

**Qual** is used when choosing from options, and when asking for specific information:

- **Qual é o seu nome?** (kwaw eh oo seh-oo noh-mee) — What is your name?
- **Qual você prefere?** (kwaw voh-say preh-feh-ree) — Which do you prefer?
- **Qual é o seu telefone?** (kwaw eh oo seh-oo teh-leh-foh-nee) — What is your phone number?
- **Quais são os seus hobbies?** (kwai-eez sow ooz seh-ooz oh-beez) — What are your hobbies?

> **Qual vs O que:** Use **qual** when asking to identify/choose from options. Use **o que** when asking for a definition or general "what."

- **O que é isso?** — What is this? (asking for a definition)
- **Qual é o problema?** — What is the problem? (asking to identify)

## Practice Conversations

**At a café:**

- **Garçom: O que você vai querer?** (oo kee voh-say vai keh-rehr) — Waiter: What will you have?
- **Eu quero um café, por favor. Quanto custa?** (eh-oo keh-roo oon kah-feh pohr fah-vohr. kwahn-too koohs-tah) — I want a coffee, please. How much is it?

**Meeting someone:**

- **Como você se chama?** (koh-moo voh-say see shah-mah) — What's your name?
- **De onde você é?** (jee ohn-jee voh-say eh) — Where are you from?
- **O que você faz?** (oo kee voh-say fahz) — What do you do (for work)?
