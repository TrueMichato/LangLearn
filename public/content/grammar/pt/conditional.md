# Conditional (Futuro do Pretérito)

The conditional mood in Portuguese — called **futuro do pretérito** (literally "future of the past") — expresses hypothetical situations, polite requests, wishes, and what would happen under certain conditions. It's equivalent to "would" in English: "I would go," "she would like," "we would buy."

## Regular Conjugation

Like the futuro simples, the conditional adds endings to the **full infinitive**. The endings are the same for all verb groups (-ar, -er, -ir):

| Pronoun | Ending | Falar | Comer | Abrir |
|---------|--------|-------|-------|-------|
| eu | -ia | falaria | comeria | abriria |
| você / ele / ela | -ia | falaria | comeria | abriria |
| nós | -íamos | falaríamos | comeríamos | abriríamos |
| vocês / eles / elas | -iam | falariam | comeriam | abririam |

> **Note:** The eu and você forms are identical, just like in the imperfeito.

- **Eu falaria com ele, mas não tive tempo** (eh-oo fah-lah-ree-ah kong eh-lee mahz now chee-vee tem-poo) — I would talk to him, but I didn't have time
- **Ela comeria pizza, mas está de dieta** (eh-lah koh-meh-ree-ah pee-zah mahz esh-tah jee jee-eh-tah) — She would eat pizza, but she's on a diet
- **Nós viajaríamos juntos** (nohs vee-ah-zhah-ree-ah-mooz zhoon-tooz) — We would travel together
- **Eles comprariam a casa** (eh-leez kon-prah-ree-ang ah kah-zah) — They would buy the house

## Polite Requests: Eu Gostaria

One of the most common uses of the conditional is making polite requests. **Gostaria** (would like) is essential for everyday politeness:

- **Eu gostaria de um café** (eh-oo gosh-tah-ree-ah jee ung kah-feh) — I would like a coffee
- **Eu gostaria de fazer uma reserva** (eh-oo gosh-tah-ree-ah jee fah-zer oo-mah heh-zer-vah) — I would like to make a reservation
- **Eu gostaria de saber o preço** (eh-oo gosh-tah-ree-ah jee sah-ber oo preh-soo) — I would like to know the price
- **Você gostaria de ir ao cinema?** (voh-seh gosh-tah-ree-ah jee eer ow see-neh-mah) — Would you like to go to the movies?

Other polite conditionals:

- **Você poderia me ajudar?** (voh-seh poh-deh-ree-ah mee ah-zhoo-dar) — Could you help me?
- **Seria possível trocar de mesa?** (seh-ree-ah poh-see-vew troh-kar jee meh-zah) — Would it be possible to change tables?
- **Você se importaria de fechar a janela?** (voh-seh see een-por-tah-ree-ah jee feh-shar ah zhah-neh-lah) — Would you mind closing the window?
- **Eu preferiria ficar em casa** (eh-oo preh-feh-ree-ree-ah fee-kar eng kah-zah) — I would prefer to stay home

## Hypothetical Situations

The conditional is used for things that would happen under certain conditions, often paired with **se** (if):

### Se + Imperfeito do Subjuntivo + Conditional

- **Se eu tivesse dinheiro, eu compraria uma casa** (see eh-oo chee-veh-see jeen-yay-roo eh-oo kon-prah-ree-ah oo-mah kah-zah) — If I had money, I would buy a house
- **Se eu pudesse, eu iria para a praia** (see eh-oo poo-deh-see eh-oo ee-ree-ah pah-rah ah prah-yah) — If I could, I would go to the beach
- **Se ele estudasse mais, passaria na prova** (see eh-lee esh-too-dah-see mahyz pah-sah-ree-ah nah proh-vah) — If he studied more, he would pass the test
- **Se nós morássemos no Brasil, falaríamos português todo dia** (see nohs moh-rah-seh-mooz noo brah-zeel fah-lah-ree-ah-mooz por-too-gesh toh-doo jee-ah) — If we lived in Brazil, we would speak Portuguese every day
- **Se eu fosse você, eu aceitaria** (see eh-oo foh-see voh-seh eh-oo ah-say-tah-ree-ah) — If I were you, I would accept

> **Important pattern:** The "if" clause uses the **imperfeito do subjuntivo** (se eu tivesse, se eu pudesse), and the result clause uses the **conditional** (compraria, iria).

## Wishes and Desires

The conditional expresses what you wish were true:

- **Eu adoraria morar na praia** (eh-oo ah-doh-rah-ree-ah moh-rar nah prah-yah) — I would love to live at the beach
- **Eu daria tudo para voltar no tempo** (eh-oo dah-ree-ah too-doo pah-rah vohl-tar noo tem-poo) — I would give anything to go back in time
- **Nós gostaríamos de viajar pelo mundo** (nohs gosh-tah-ree-ah-mooz jee vee-ah-zhar peh-loo moon-doo) — We would like to travel around the world
- **Seria incrível morar em Paris** (seh-ree-ah een-kree-vew moh-rar eng pah-reez) — It would be amazing to live in Paris

## Irregular Verbs

The same three verbs that are irregular in the futuro simples are also irregular in the conditional:

### Fazer (to do/make) → far-

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---------|-------------|
| eu | faria |
| você / ele / ela | faria |
| nós | faríamos |
| vocês / eles / elas | fariam |

- **Eu faria diferente** (eh-oo fah-ree-ah jee-feh-ren-chee) — I would do it differently
- **O que você faria?** (oo kee voh-seh fah-ree-ah) — What would you do?

### Dizer (to say) → dir-

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---------|-------------|
| eu | diria |
| você / ele / ela | diria |
| nós | diríamos |
| vocês / eles / elas | diriam |

- **Eu não diria isso** (eh-oo now jee-ree-ah ee-soo) — I wouldn't say that
- **Ele diria a mesma coisa** (eh-lee jee-ree-ah ah mez-mah koy-zah) — He would say the same thing

### Trazer (to bring) → trar-

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---------|-------------|
| eu | traria |
| você / ele / ela | traria |
| nós | traríamos |
| vocês / eles / elas | trariam |

- **Eu traria comida** (eh-oo trah-ree-ah koh-mee-dah) — I would bring food
- **Eles trariam os documentos** (eh-leez trah-ree-ang ooz doh-koo-men-tooz) — They would bring the documents

## Conditional in Reported Speech

The conditional is also used to report what someone said they would do (indirect speech):

- **Ele disse que viria** (eh-lee jee-see kee vee-ree-ah) — He said he would come
- **Ela prometeu que ligaria** (eh-lah proh-meh-teh-oo kee lee-gah-ree-ah) — She promised she would call
- **Eles avisaram que chegariam tarde** (eh-leez ah-vee-zah-rang kee sheh-gah-ree-ang tar-jee) — They warned that they would arrive late
- **O professor disse que a prova seria difícil** (oo proh-feh-sor jee-see kee ah proh-vah seh-ree-ah jee-fee-see-oo) — The teacher said the test would be hard

## Common Expressions with the Conditional

- **Eu diria que sim** (eh-oo jee-ree-ah kee seeng) — I would say yes
- **Seria bom** (seh-ree-ah bong) — That would be good
- **Não faria sentido** (now fah-ree-ah sen-chee-doo) — That wouldn't make sense
- **Valeria a pena** (vah-leh-ree-ah ah pen-ah) — It would be worth it
- **Eu no seu lugar não faria isso** (eh-oo noo seh-oo loo-gar now fah-ree-ah ee-soo) — In your place, I wouldn't do that
