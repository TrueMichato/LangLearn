# Adverbs (Advérbios)

Adverbs modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. In Portuguese, many adverbs are formed from adjectives, but many of the most common adverbs are standalone words. Learning adverbs will make your sentences richer and more natural — they tell you *how*, *when*, *where*, and *how much*.

## Formation from Adjectives (-mente)

The most productive way to form adverbs is adding **-mente** to the feminine form of an adjective. This is similar to adding "-ly" in English:

| Adjective (m/f) | Feminine | Adverb | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| rápido / rápida | rápida | rapidamente | quickly |
| feliz / feliz | feliz | felizmente | happily |
| claro / clara | clara | claramente | clearly |
| fácil / fácil | fácil | facilmente | easily |
| lento / lenta | lenta | lentamente | slowly |
| triste / triste | triste | tristemente | sadly |

- **Ela fala rapidamente.** (eh-lah fah-lah hah-pee-dah-men-chee) — She speaks quickly.
- **Felizmente, tudo deu certo.** (feh-leez-men-chee, too-doo deh-oo sehr-too) — Happily, everything worked out.
- **Ele explicou claramente.** (eh-lee esh-plee-koh klah-rah-men-chee) — He explained clearly.
- **O professor falou lentamente.** (oo proh-feh-sohr fah-loh len-tah-men-chee) — The teacher spoke slowly.

> **Tip:** If the adjective has no separate feminine form (like *feliz* or *fácil*), just add **-mente** directly.

## Adverbs of Time (Advérbios de Tempo)

These tell you *when* something happens:

| Adverb | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| agora | ah-goh-rah | now |
| hoje | oh-zhee | today |
| amanhã | ah-mahn-yah | tomorrow |
| ontem | ohn-teng | yesterday |
| sempre | sem-pree | always |
| nunca | noon-kah | never |
| já | zhah | already / right now |
| ainda | ah-een-dah | still / yet |
| logo | loh-goo | soon |
| cedo | seh-doo | early |
| tarde | tar-jee | late |
| depois | deh-poysh | after / later |
| antes | ahn-chees | before |

- **Eu vou ao mercado agora.** (eh-oo voh ow mehr-kah-doo ah-goh-rah) — I'm going to the market now.
- **Ontem eu comi pizza.** (ohn-teng eh-oo koh-mee pee-tsah) — Yesterday I ate pizza.
- **Ela sempre estuda à noite.** (eh-lah sem-pree esh-too-dah ah noy-chee) — She always studies at night.
- **Eu nunca fui ao Japão.** (eh-oo noon-kah foo-ee ow zhah-pow) — I've never been to Japan.
- **Ele já chegou.** (eh-lee zhah sheh-goh) — He already arrived.
- **Ainda estou esperando.** (ah-een-dah esh-toh esh-peh-rahn-doo) — I'm still waiting.

## Adverbs of Place (Advérbios de Lugar)

These tell you *where* something happens:

| Adverb | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| aqui | ah-kee | here (near speaker) |
| aí | ah-ee | there (near listener) |
| ali | ah-lee | there (away from both) |
| lá | lah | there (far away) |
| perto | pehr-too | nearby / close |
| longe | lohn-zhee | far away |
| dentro | den-troo | inside |
| fora | foh-rah | outside |
| acima | ah-see-mah | above |
| abaixo | ah-bai-shoo | below |
| atrás | ah-trahsh | behind |
| em frente | eng fren-chee | in front |

- **Venha aqui, por favor.** (veh-nyah ah-kee, por fah-vor) — Come here, please.
- **O restaurante fica ali.** (oo heh-stow-rahn-chee fee-kah ah-lee) — The restaurant is over there.
- **Ele mora longe daqui.** (eh-lee moh-rah lohn-zhee dah-kee) — He lives far from here.
- **As crianças estão fora.** (ahz kree-ahn-sahz esh-tow foh-rah) — The children are outside.
- **O gato está dentro da caixa.** (oo gah-too esh-tah den-troo dah kai-shah) — The cat is inside the box.

## Adverbs of Manner (Advérbios de Modo)

These tell you *how* something is done. Some are standalone words (not formed with -mente):

| Adverb | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| bem | beng | well |
| mal | maw | badly / poorly |
| assim | ah-seen | like this / so |
| devagar | deh-vah-gar | slowly |
| depressa | deh-preh-sah | fast / quickly |
| melhor | meh-lyor | better |
| pior | pee-or | worse |

- **Ela canta muito bem.** (eh-lah kahn-tah moo-ee-too beng) — She sings very well.
- **Ele está se sentindo mal.** (eh-lee esh-tah see sen-cheen-doo maw) — He is feeling poorly.
- **Fale devagar, por favor.** (fah-lee deh-vah-gar, por fah-vor) — Speak slowly, please.
- **Ela cozinha melhor que eu.** (eh-lah koh-zee-nyah meh-lyor kee eh-oo) — She cooks better than me.

### Bom vs Bem — A Common Mistake!

This is one of the most common errors for learners:

- **Bom** is an adjective — it describes *nouns*: **O filme é bom.** (The movie is good.)
- **Bem** is an adverb — it describes *verbs*: **Ela dança bem.** (She dances well.)

- **Ele é um bom professor.** (eh-lee eh oon bong proh-feh-sohr) — He is a good teacher. (adjective)
- **Ele ensina bem.** (eh-lee en-see-nah beng) — He teaches well. (adverb)

## Adverbs of Quantity (Advérbios de Intensidade)

These tell you *how much*:

| Adverb | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| muito | moo-ee-too | very / a lot |
| pouco | poh-koo | little / not much |
| bastante | bahs-tahn-chee | quite / enough |
| demais | deh-maish | too much |
| mais | maish | more |
| menos | meh-noosh | less |
| tão | tow | so (intensifier) |
| tanto | tahn-too | so much |
| quase | kwah-zee | almost |

- **Eu gosto muito de chocolate.** (eh-oo gosh-too moo-ee-too jee shoh-koh-lah-chee) — I like chocolate a lot.
- **Ele come pouco.** (eh-lee koh-mee poh-koo) — He eats little.
- **Está quente demais!** (esh-tah ken-chee deh-maish) — It's too hot!
- **Ela é tão inteligente!** (eh-lah eh tow een-teh-lee-zhen-chee) — She is so smart!
- **Eu quase esqueci.** (eh-oo kwah-zee esh-keh-see) — I almost forgot.

## Adverbs of Affirmation & Negation

| Adverb | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| sim | seen | yes |
| não | now | no / not |
| certamente | sehr-tah-men-chee | certainly |
| realmente | heh-ow-men-chee | really |
| claro | klah-roo | of course |
| com certeza | kohm sehr-teh-zah | for sure |

- **Sim, eu quero ir.** (seen, eh-oo keh-roo eer) — Yes, I want to go.
- **Ela realmente gosta de música.** (eh-lah heh-ow-men-chee gosh-tah jee moo-zee-kah) — She really likes music.
- **Claro que sim!** (klah-roo kee seen) — Of course!
- **Com certeza vou ajudar.** (kohm sehr-teh-zah voh ah-zhoo-dar) — I will definitely help.

## Position in Sentences

Adverbs are relatively flexible in Portuguese, but here are the main patterns:

1. **After the verb** (most common): **Ela fala bem.** — She speaks well.
2. **Before the adjective**: **Ela é muito bonita.** — She is very beautiful.
3. **At the beginning for emphasis**: **Ontem eu fui ao cinema.** — Yesterday I went to the movies.
4. **Before another adverb**: **Ele corre muito depressa.** — He runs very fast.
