# The Pluperfect Tense (Mais-que-Perfeito)

The pluperfect (mais-que-perfeito) expresses an action that happened **before** another past action — the "past of the past." In English, this corresponds to "had done," "had spoken," etc. Brazilian Portuguese has two forms: the **compound** (much more common in speech) and the **simple** (literary/formal).

## Compound Pluperfect: tinha + Past Participle

The compound form is what you'll hear and use in everyday Brazilian Portuguese. It combines **tinha** (imperfect of *ter*) with the **past participle**:

| Person | ter (imperfect) | + participle |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| eu | tinha | falado |
| você/ele/ela | tinha | falado |
| nós | tínhamos | falado |
| vocês/eles/elas | tinham | falado |

### Examples with Regular Participles

- **Eu já tinha comido quando ela chegou.** (eu jah CHEEN-yah koh-MEE-doo) — I had already eaten when she arrived.
- **Nós tínhamos estudado a lição antes da prova.** (nois CHEEN-yah-mohs es-too-DAH-doo) — We had studied the lesson before the test.
- **Eles tinham saído quando eu liguei.** (EH-lees CHEEN-yahn sah-EE-doo) — They had left when I called.
- **Você já tinha viajado para o Brasil antes?** (vo-SEH jah CHEEN-yah vee-ah-JAH-doo) — Had you traveled to Brazil before?
- **Ela tinha morado em São Paulo por cinco anos.** (EH-lah CHEEN-yah moh-RAH-doo) — She had lived in São Paulo for five years.

### Examples with Irregular Participles

Remember to use the **regular** participle form with *ter*:

- **Eu tinha feito o dever de casa.** (eu CHEEN-yah FAY-too) — I had done the homework.
- **Ela tinha dito a verdade.** (EH-lah CHEEN-yah JEE-too) — She had told the truth.
- **Nós tínhamos visto o filme antes.** (nois CHEEN-yah-mohs VEES-too) — We had seen the movie before.
- **Eles tinham escrito a carta.** (EH-lees CHEEN-yahn es-KREE-too) — They had written the letter.
- **Eu tinha aberto a janela antes de sair.** (eu CHEEN-yah ah-BEHR-too) — I had opened the window before leaving.
- **Você já tinha posto a mesa?** (vo-SEH jah CHEEN-yah POHS-too) — Had you already set the table?

## Usage: The Past Before the Past

The pluperfect establishes a timeline — one action happened **before** another in the past:

- **Quando eu cheguei, a festa já tinha acabado.** (KWAN-doo eu sheh-GAY ah FES-tah jah CHEEN-yah) — When I arrived, the party had already ended.
- **Ele não sabia que ela já tinha ido embora.** (EH-lee nown sah-BEE-ah) — He didn't know that she had already left.
- **A gente percebeu que tinha esquecido as chaves.** (ah JEN-chee pehr-seh-BEH-oo) — We realized that we had forgotten the keys.
- **Eles disseram que já tinham terminado o trabalho.** (EH-lees jee-SEH-rahn) — They said they had already finished the work.
- **Eu nunca tinha visto algo tão bonito.** (eu NOON-kah CHEEN-yah VEES-too) — I had never seen something so beautiful.

## Simple Pluperfect (Mais-que-Perfeito Simples)

The simple form is based on the preterite (pretérito perfeito) stem. It's used almost exclusively in **literary** and very **formal** writing — you rarely hear it in conversation:

| Person | falar | comer | partir |
|--------|-------|-------|--------|
| eu | falara | comera | partira |
| você/ele/ela | falara | comera | partira |
| nós | faláramos | comêramos | partíramos |
| vocês/eles/elas | falaram | comeram | partiram |

- **Ele chegara antes de todos.** (EH-lee sheh-GAH-rah) — He had arrived before everyone. *(literary)*
- **Nunca vira algo tão belo.** (NOON-kah VEE-rah) — (He/She) had never seen something so beautiful. *(literary)*
- **A notícia que recebêramos era falsa.** (heh-seh-BEH-rah-mohs) — The news we had received was false. *(literary)*

### Irregular Simple Pluperfect Forms

| Infinitive | Simple Pluperfect |
|-----------|------------------|
| ser | fora |
| ter | tivera |
| fazer | fizera |
| dizer | dissera |
| ver | vira |
| vir | viera |
| pôr | pusera |
| ir | fora |

- **Ele fora o melhor aluno da turma.** (EH-lee FOH-rah) — He had been the best student in class. *(literary)*
- **Ela dissera que viria.** (EH-lah jee-SEH-rah) — She had said she would come. *(literary)*

## Compound vs. Simple: When to Use Each

| Form | Usage | Register |
|------|-------|----------|
| Compound (tinha + participle) | Everyday speech, informal writing | Spoken + written |
| Simple (-ra form) | Literature, formal texts, news | Written only |

In everyday Brazilian Portuguese, **always use the compound form**:

- ✅ **Eu já tinha feito isso.** (everyday)
- 📖 **Eu já fizera isso.** (literary)
- ✅ **Ela tinha chegado cedo.** (everyday)
- 📖 **Ela chegara cedo.** (literary)

## Common Time Markers

These words and phrases often appear with the pluperfect:

- **já** (already): **Eu já tinha saído.**
- **nunca** (never): **Eu nunca tinha visto isso.**
- **antes** (before): **Antes disso, eu tinha morado em Recife.**
- **quando** (when): **Quando cheguei, ela já tinha ido.**
- **ainda não** (not yet): **Eu ainda não tinha terminado.**
