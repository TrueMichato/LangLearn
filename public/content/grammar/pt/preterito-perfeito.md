# Pretérito Perfeito (Simple Past)

The pretérito perfeito is one of the most important tenses in Portuguese. It describes completed actions in the past — things that happened and are finished. If you want to tell someone what you did yesterday, last week, or at any specific moment in the past, this is the tense you need.

## Regular Verbs: -AR

The largest group of Portuguese verbs ends in **-ar**. To conjugate in the pretérito perfeito, remove the **-ar** and add these endings:

| Pronoun | Ending | Example: falar (to speak) |
|---------|--------|--------------------------|
| eu | -ei | falei |
| você / ele / ela | -ou | falou |
| nós | -amos | falamos |
| vocês / eles / elas | -aram | falaram |

- **Eu falei com ela ontem** (eh-oo fah-lay kong eh-lah on-teng) — I spoke with her yesterday
- **Você comprou um carro novo** (voh-seh kon-proh ung kah-hoo noh-voo) — You bought a new car
- **Nós estudamos muito** (nohs esh-too-dah-mooz mween-too) — We studied a lot
- **Eles viajaram para o Brasil** (eh-leez vee-ah-zhah-rang pah-rah oo brah-zeel) — They traveled to Brazil
- **Ela trabalhou o dia inteiro** (eh-lah trah-bah-lyoh oo jee-ah een-tay-roo) — She worked the whole day

> **Note:** The **nós** form in the pretérito perfeito is the same as in the present tense for -ar verbs (falamos). Context tells you which tense is meant.

## Regular Verbs: -ER

For verbs ending in **-er**, remove the ending and add:

| Pronoun | Ending | Example: comer (to eat) |
|---------|--------|------------------------|
| eu | -i | comi |
| você / ele / ela | -eu | comeu |
| nós | -emos | comemos |
| vocês / eles / elas | -eram | comeram |

- **Eu comi pizza ontem** (eh-oo koh-mee pee-zah on-teng) — I ate pizza yesterday
- **Você bebeu muita água** (voh-seh beh-beh-oo mween-tah ah-gwah) — You drank a lot of water
- **Nós aprendemos português** (nohs ah-pren-deh-mooz por-too-gesh) — We learned Portuguese
- **Eles correram no parque** (eh-leez koh-heh-rang noo par-kee) — They ran in the park

## Regular Verbs: -IR

For verbs ending in **-ir**, remove the ending and add:

| Pronoun | Ending | Example: partir (to leave) |
|---------|--------|---------------------------|
| eu | -i | parti |
| você / ele / ela | -iu | partiu |
| nós | -imos | partimos |
| vocês / eles / elas | -iram | partiram |

- **Eu abri a porta** (eh-oo ah-bree ah por-tah) — I opened the door
- **Ela decidiu sair cedo** (eh-lah deh-see-jee-oo sah-eer seh-doo) — She decided to leave early
- **Nós assistimos ao filme** (nohs ah-sees-chee-mooz ow feel-mee) — We watched the movie
- **Eles partiram de manhã** (eh-leez par-chee-rang jee mahn-yang) — They left in the morning

## Irregular Verbs

Many common Portuguese verbs are irregular in the pretérito perfeito. These must be memorized individually.

### Ser / Ir (to be / to go)

These two verbs share the same conjugation in the pretérito perfeito:

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---------|-------------|
| eu | fui |
| você / ele / ela | foi |
| nós | fomos |
| vocês / eles / elas | foram |

- **Eu fui ao mercado** (eh-oo foo-ee ow mer-kah-doo) — I went to the market
- **Ela foi professora** (eh-lah foy proh-feh-soh-rah) — She was a teacher
- **Nós fomos à praia** (nohs foh-mooz ah prah-yah) — We went to the beach
- **Eles foram muito gentis** (eh-leez foh-rang mween-too zhen-cheez) — They were very kind

> **Tip:** Context tells you whether **fui/foi** means "went" or "was." If followed by a destination (ao mercado, à praia), it means "went." If followed by a description (professora, gentis), it means "was."

### Ter (to have)

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---------|-------------|
| eu | tive |
| você / ele / ela | teve |
| nós | tivemos |
| vocês / eles / elas | tiveram |

- **Eu tive uma ideia** (eh-oo chee-vee oo-mah ee-day-ah) — I had an idea
- **Ele teve sorte** (eh-lee teh-vee sor-chee) — He was lucky (had luck)

### Fazer (to do/make)

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---------|-------------|
| eu | fiz |
| você / ele / ela | fez |
| nós | fizemos |
| vocês / eles / elas | fizeram |

- **Eu fiz o jantar** (eh-oo feez oo zhan-tar) — I made dinner
- **O que você fez ontem?** (oo kee voh-seh fez on-teng) — What did you do yesterday?

### Estar (to be — temporary)

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---------|-------------|
| eu | estive |
| você / ele / ela | esteve |
| nós | estivemos |
| vocês / eles / elas | estiveram |

- **Eu estive em São Paulo** (eh-oo esh-chee-vee eng sow pow-loo) — I was in São Paulo
- **Ela esteve doente** (eh-lah esh-teh-vee doh-en-chee) — She was sick

### More Important Irregulars

| Verb | eu | você | nós | eles |
|------|-----|------|-----|------|
| poder (can) | pude | pôde | pudemos | puderam |
| saber (to know) | soube | soube | soubemos | souberam |
| dizer (to say) | disse | disse | dissemos | disseram |
| trazer (to bring) | trouxe | trouxe | trouxemos | trouxeram |
| ver (to see) | vi | viu | vimos | viram |
| vir (to come) | vim | veio | viemos | vieram |

- **Eu não pude ir** (eh-oo now poo-jee eer) — I couldn't go
- **Ela soube a verdade** (eh-lah soh-bee ah vehr-dah-jee) — She found out the truth
- **Ele disse que sim** (eh-lee jee-see kee seeng) — He said yes
- **Eu trouxe um presente** (eh-oo troh-see ung preh-zen-chee) — I brought a gift
- **Nós vimos o filme** (nohs vee-mooz oo feel-mee) — We saw the movie
- **Eles vieram ontem** (eh-leez vee-eh-rang on-teng) — They came yesterday

## Time Expressions with Pretérito Perfeito

These words and phrases signal that you should use the pretérito perfeito:

- **Ontem** (on-teng) — Yesterday
- **Anteontem** (an-chee-on-teng) — The day before yesterday
- **Na semana passada** (nah seh-mah-nah pah-sah-dah) — Last week
- **No mês passado** (noo mesh pah-sah-doo) — Last month
- **No ano passado** (noo ah-noo pah-sah-doo) — Last year
- **Há dois dias** (ah doysh jee-ahz) — Two days ago
- **De repente** (jee heh-pen-chee) — Suddenly

- **Ontem eu acordei cedo** (on-teng eh-oo ah-kor-day seh-doo) — Yesterday I woke up early
- **Na semana passada, nós viajamos** (nah seh-mah-nah pah-sah-dah nohs vee-ah-zhah-mooz) — Last week, we traveled
- **De repente, ele apareceu** (jee heh-pen-chee eh-lee ah-pah-reh-seh-oo) — Suddenly, he appeared
