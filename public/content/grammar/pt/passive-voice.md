# The Passive Voice (Voz Passiva)

The passive voice in Portuguese shifts focus from who performs the action to what receives the action. Portuguese has two main passive constructions: the **analytical passive** (with ser) and the **pronominal passive** (with se). Brazilian Portuguese also uses past participles that must agree with the subject.

## Analytical Passive: ser + Past Participle

The most straightforward passive construction uses **ser** (conjugated) + **past participle**. The participle must agree in gender and number with the subject:

- **O livro foi escrito por Machado de Assis.** (oo LEE-vroo foy es-KREE-too) — The book was written by Machado de Assis.
- **A carta foi enviada ontem.** (ah KAR-tah foy en-vee-AH-dah) — The letter was sent yesterday.
- **Os prédios foram construídos em 2010.** (ooz PREH-jee-ohs FOH-rahn cons-troo-EE-dohs) — The buildings were built in 2010.
- **As casas foram vendidas rapidamente.** (ahz KAH-zahs FOH-rahn ven-JEE-dahz) — The houses were sold quickly.
- **O problema será resolvido amanhã.** (oo pro-BLEH-mah seh-RAH heh-sow-VEE-doo) — The problem will be solved tomorrow.

### Agent with "por" / "pelo" / "pela"

The agent (who does the action) is introduced by **por** (or its contractions **pelo/pela/pelos/pelas**):

- **A música foi composta por Tom Jobim.** (ah MOO-zee-kah foy com-POHS-tah) — The song was composed by Tom Jobim.
- **O gol foi marcado pelo atacante.** (oo gowl foy mar-KAH-doo PEH-loo) — The goal was scored by the striker.
- **As leis foram aprovadas pelo congresso.** (ahz lays FOH-rahn ah-pro-VAH-dahz) — The laws were approved by congress.

## Regular Past Participles

| Conjugation | Formation | Example |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| -ar → -ado | falar → fal**ado** | spoken |
| -er → -ido | comer → com**ido** | eaten |
| -ir → -ido | partir → part**ido** | left |

- **A comida foi preparada com carinho.** (ah koh-MEE-dah foy preh-pah-RAH-dah) — The food was prepared with care.
- **O texto foi lido pela turma toda.** (oo TEKS-too foy LEE-doo) — The text was read by the whole class.
- **As portas foram fechadas às dez.** (ahz POR-tahz FOH-rahn feh-SHAH-dahz) — The doors were closed at ten.

## Irregular Past Participles

Many common verbs have irregular past participles that you must memorize:

| Infinitive | Regular | Irregular | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|-----------|---------|
| abrir | — | **aberto** | opened |
| escrever | — | **escrito** | written |
| fazer | — | **feito** | done/made |
| dizer | — | **dito** | said |
| ver | — | **visto** | seen |
| pôr | — | **posto** | put |
| vir | — | **vindo** | come |
| cobrir | — | **coberto** | covered |
| descobrir | — | **descoberto** | discovered |

Some verbs have **both** regular and irregular forms. Use the **irregular** with ser/estar (passive/state) and the **regular** with ter/haver (compound tenses):

| Infinitive | Regular (with ter) | Irregular (with ser/estar) |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| pagar | pagado | **pago** |
| aceitar | aceitado | **aceito** |
| entregar | entregado | **entregue** |
| prender | prendido | **preso** |
| eleger | elegido | **eleito** |

- **O trabalho foi feito com cuidado.** (oo trah-BAH-lyoo foy FAY-too) — The work was done carefully.
- **A porta estava aberta.** (ah POR-tah es-TAH-vah ah-BEHR-tah) — The door was open.
- **O presidente foi eleito pelo povo.** (oo preh-zee-DEN-chee foy eh-LAY-too) — The president was elected by the people.

## Pronominal Passive with "se"

The passive with **se** is very common in Brazilian Portuguese, especially in signs and general statements:

- **Fala-se português aqui.** (FAH-lah see por-too-GEHZ) — Portuguese is spoken here.
- **Vende-se esta casa.** (VEN-dee see) — This house is for sale. (lit. "This house sells itself")
- **Alugam-se apartamentos.** (ah-LOO-gahn see) — Apartments for rent.
- **Come-se bem neste restaurante.** (KOH-mee see behn) — One eats well at this restaurant.
- **Precisa-se de funcionários.** (preh-SEE-zah see) — Employees needed.

Note: In formal grammar, the verb agrees with the subject (**Vendem-se carros** = Cars are sold), but in spoken Brazilian Portuguese, singular is often used regardless (**Vende-se carros**).

## Participle Agreement

The past participle in passive constructions **must agree** with the subject in gender and number:

- **O relatório foi terminado.** (masculine singular) — The report was finished.
- **A pesquisa foi terminada.** (feminine singular) — The research was finished.
- **Os relatórios foram terminados.** (masculine plural) — The reports were finished.
- **As pesquisas foram terminadas.** (feminine plural) — The researches were finished.
