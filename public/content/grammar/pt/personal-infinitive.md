# The Personal Infinitive (Infinitivo Pessoal)

The personal infinitive is one of the most distinctive features of the Portuguese language — no other Romance language has it! While a regular infinitive is impersonal (like "to speak"), the personal infinitive adds personal endings so you know *who* is performing the action. This is especially useful when the subject of the infinitive clause differs from the main clause.

## Formation

The personal infinitive is formed by adding personal endings to the regular infinitive. For regular verbs:

| Person | falar (speak) | comer (eat) | partir (leave) |
|--------|--------------|-------------|-----------------|
| eu | falar | comer | partir |
| você/ele/ela | falar | comer | partir |
| nós | falarmos | comermos | partirmos |
| vocês/eles/elas | falarem | comerem | partirem |

Notice that the first and third person singular forms are identical to the regular infinitive. The key forms to remember are **nós** (-mos) and **vocês/eles** (-em).

## Usage After Prepositions

The personal infinitive is used after prepositions when the subject of the infinitive is different from the subject of the main verb:

- **Para eu entender a matéria, preciso estudar mais.** (PAH-rah eu en-ten-DEHR) — For me to understand the subject, I need to study more.
- **Antes de nós sairmos, vamos conferir tudo.** (AN-tesh jee nois sah-EER-mohs) — Before we leave, let's check everything.
- **Depois de vocês chegarem, começamos a festa.** (de-POYS jee vo-SEHS sheh-GAH-rehn) — After you all arrive, we'll start the party.
- **Apesar de eles serem jovens, são muito responsáveis.** (ah-peh-ZAR jee EH-lees SEH-rehn) — Despite them being young, they are very responsible.
- **Sem vocês ajudarem, não vamos terminar a tempo.** (sehn vo-SEHS ah-joo-DAH-rehn) — Without you all helping, we won't finish in time.

## Usage After Certain Expressions

Some expressions and constructions naturally trigger the personal infinitive:

- **É importante nós estudarmos todos os dias.** (eh een-por-TAN-chee nois es-too-DAR-mohs) — It's important for us to study every day.
- **É difícil eles entenderem essa regra.** (eh jee-FEE-sew EH-lees en-ten-DEH-rehn) — It's difficult for them to understand this rule.
- **É melhor vocês saírem agora.** (eh meh-LYOR vo-SEHS sah-EE-rehn) — It's better for you all to leave now.
- **É necessário nós confirmarmos a reserva.** (eh neh-seh-SAH-ree-oo nois con-feer-MAR-mohs) — It's necessary for us to confirm the reservation.

## Contrast with the Regular Infinitive

When the subject of the infinitive is the **same** as the main verb, use the regular infinitive:

- **Eu quero falar com você.** (eu KEH-roo fah-LAR) — I want to speak with you. *(regular infinitive — same subject)*
- **Para eu falar melhor, preciso praticar.** (PAH-rah eu fah-LAR) — For me to speak better, I need to practice. *(personal infinitive — emphasizes "eu")*

When the subject is **different**, use the personal infinitive:

- **Eu pedi para eles virem mais cedo.** (eu peh-JEE PAH-rah EH-lees VEE-rehn) — I asked for them to come earlier.
- **Ela saiu sem nós percebermos.** (EH-lah sah-EE-oo sehn nois pehr-seh-BEH-mohs) — She left without us noticing.
- **O professor explicou para os alunos entenderem.** (oo pro-feh-SOR esh-plee-KOH PAH-rah ooz ah-LOO-nohs en-ten-DEH-rehn) — The teacher explained so the students would understand.

## Common Mistakes

Be careful not to confuse the personal infinitive with the future subjunctive — they look similar but serve different purposes:

- **Quando eles falarem** — When they speak *(future subjunctive)*
- **Para eles falarem** — For them to speak *(personal infinitive)*
- **Nós decidimos sair antes de vocês chegarem.** (nois deh-see-JEE-mohs sah-EER) — We decided to leave before you arrived.
- **É hora de nós irmos embora.** (eh OH-rah jee nois EER-mohs) — It's time for us to leave.
