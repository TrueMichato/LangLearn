# Essential Brazilian Expressions (Expressões Brasileiras)

<!-- vocab-inventory: {"mode":"explicit"} -->

Brazilian Portuguese is rich with idiomatic expressions, slang, and informal phrases that you won't find in textbooks. These expressions are the key to sounding natural and understanding everyday Brazilian conversation. Let's explore the most essential ones!

## Greetings and Responses

### Tudo bem? / Tudo bom?
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"greeting-question-response","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Tudo bem? / Tudo bom?","rule":"Tudo bem can be a wellbeing question or a positive response","example":"How can the same everyday Brazilian phrase ask how someone is and also answer that question?","answer":"Tudo bem? asks How are you?; Tudo bem! answers All good!","explanation":"Intonation and conversational role distinguish the question from its response.","legacyAliases":[{"rule":"'Tudo bem' as greeting and response","example":"— ___ ? — Tudo bem, e você?"}]} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-tudo-bem","word":"tudo bem","meaning":"how are you?; all good (question or response)","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Tudo bem? / Tudo bom?"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-tudo-bom","word":"tudo bom","meaning":"how are you?; all good (casual greeting or response)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Tudo bem? / Tudo bom?"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-tudo-otimo","word":"tudo ótimo","meaning":"everything is great","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Tudo bem? / Tudo bom?"} -->

The universal Brazilian greeting — much more common than "Como vai?"

- **Tudo bem?** (TOO-doo behn) — How are you? (lit. "Everything well?")
- **Tudo bem! E você?** (TOO-doo behn ee vo-SEH) — All good! And you?
- **Tudo bom?** (TOO-doo bohn) — How are you? (slightly more casual)
- **Tudo ótimo!** (TOO-doo OH-chee-moo) — Everything's great!

### Beleza!
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-beleza","word":"beleza","meaning":"cool; alright; OK (informal Brazilian conversation)","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Beleza!"} -->

One of the most versatile Brazilian expressions:

- **Beleza!** (beh-LEH-zah) — Cool! / Alright! / OK! (lit. "Beauty!")
- **Beleza, então a gente se vê amanhã.** (beh-LEH-zah en-TOWN) — Cool, so we'll see each other tomorrow.
- **Beleza?** (beh-LEH-zah) — Is that OK? / Deal?

### E aí?
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-e-ai","word":"e aí","meaning":"what's up? (casual Brazilian greeting)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"E aí?"} -->

Casual greeting, like "What's up?":

- **E aí, tudo certo?** (ee ah-EE TOO-doo SEHR-too) — What's up, everything alright?
- **E aí, beleza?** (ee ah-EE beh-LEH-zah) — What's up, all good?

## Reactions and Exclamations

### Que legal!
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-que-legal","word":"que legal","meaning":"how cool; that's awesome (Brazilian informal use)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Que legal!"} -->

- **Que legal!** (kee leh-GOW) — How cool! / That's awesome!
- **Que legal que você veio!** (kee leh-GOW kee vo-SEH VAY-oo) — How cool that you came!

### Nossa!
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-nossa","word":"nossa","meaning":"wow; oh my (interjection)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Nossa!"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-nossa-senhora","word":"Nossa Senhora","meaning":"oh my (exclamation; literally Our Lady)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Nossa!"} -->

An exclamation of surprise, equivalent to "Wow!" or "Oh my!":

- **Nossa, que bonito!** (NOH-sah kee boo-NEE-too) — Wow, how beautiful!
- **Nossa Senhora!** (NOH-sah seh-NYOH-rah) — Oh my! (stronger, lit. "Our Lady!")
- **Nossa, que calor!** (NOH-sah kee kah-LOR) — Wow, it's so hot!

### Caramba!
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-caramba","word":"caramba","meaning":"geez; wow (mild exclamation)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Caramba!"} -->

Mild exclamation of surprise:

- **Caramba, não sabia disso!** (kah-RAM-bah nown sah-BEE-ah) — Geez, I didn't know that!

## Agreement and Confirmation

### Com certeza
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-com-certeza","word":"com certeza","meaning":"certainly; absolutely; of course","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Com certeza"} -->

- **Com certeza!** (con sehr-TEH-zah) — Of course! / Absolutely!
- **Você vai à festa? Com certeza!** — Are you going to the party? Absolutely!

### Pois é
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-pois-e","word":"pois é","meaning":"that's right; indeed; yeah (often resigned)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Pois é"} -->

Expresses agreement or resignation — "That's right" / "Indeed" / "Yeah...":

- **Pois é, a vida é assim.** (poysh eh ah VEE-dah eh ah-SEEN) — Yeah, life is like that.
- **Pois é, não tem jeito.** (poysh eh nown tehn JAY-too) — Indeed, there's no way around it.

### Tá bom / Tá
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-ta-bom","word":"tá bom","meaning":"OK; alright (informal Brazilian speech)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Tá bom / Tá"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-ta","word":"tá","meaning":"OK (informal acknowledgement; shortened está)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Tá bom / Tá"} -->

Informal "OK" — extremely common:

- **Tá bom.** (tah bohn) — OK. / Alright.
- **Tá.** (tah) — OK. (even more casual)
- **Tá bom, eu vou.** (tah bohn eu voh) — Alright, I'll go.

## Time Expressions

### Faz tempo
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"elapsed-faz-tempo","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Faz tempo","rule":"Elapsed-time fazer stays in the third-person singular","example":"___ ___ que não te vejo!","answer":"Faz tempo","explanation":"Elapsed-time fazer is impersonal and stays singular: faz tempo, not fazem tempo.","legacyAliases":[{"rule":"'Faz tempo' for elapsed time","example":"___ ___ que não te vejo!"}]} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-faz-tempo","word":"faz tempo","meaning":"it has been a long time; for a long time","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Faz tempo"} -->

- **Faz tempo que não te vejo!** (fahz TEN-poo kee nown chee VEH-joo) — It's been a while since I've seen you!
- **Faz tempo que moro aqui.** (fahz TEN-poo kee MOH-roo ah-KEE) — I've been living here for a long time.

### Daqui a pouco
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-daqui-pouco","word":"daqui a pouco","meaning":"in a little while","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Daqui a pouco"} -->

- **Daqui a pouco eu chego.** (dah-KEE ah POH-koo eu SHEH-goo) — I'll be there in a bit.
- **Daqui a pouco a gente sai.** (dah-KEE ah POH-koo) — We'll leave in a little while.

## Essential Idiomatic Expressions

### Dar um jeito
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"dar-jeito-application","kind":"rule","format":"cloze","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Dar um jeito","rule":"'Dar um jeito' — finding a solution","example":"Não se preocupe, a gente ___ um jeito. (dar)","answer":"dá","explanation":"Dar um jeito means to find a way; a gente takes third-person singular dá.","legacyAliases":[{"rule":"'Dar um jeito' — finding a solution","example":"Não se preocupe, a gente ___ um ___."}]} -->
<!-- grammar-card: {"id":"jeitinho-context","kind":"concept","format":"qa","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Dar um jeito","rule":"Jeitinho names a cultural concept rather than a universal personal trait","example":"What idea does jeitinho brasileiro refer to in the discussion of finding a solution?","answer":"A culturally familiar idea of improvising or finding a creative way around a difficulty.","explanation":"It names a cultural concept, not a claim that every Brazilian always behaves the same way."} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-dar-jeito","word":"dar um jeito","meaning":"to find a way; to figure it out","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Dar um jeito"} -->

One of the most Brazilian expressions — to find a way, to figure it out, to improvise a solution:

- **A gente dá um jeito.** (ah JEN-chee dah oon JAY-too) — We'll figure it out.
- **Ele sempre dá um jeito em tudo.** (EH-lee SEN-pree dah oon JAY-too) — He always finds a way for everything.
- **Vou dar um jeito nisso.** (voh dar oon JAY-too NEE-soo) — I'll sort this out.

The concept of "jeitinho brasileiro" (the Brazilian way of finding creative solutions) is deeply cultural.

### Ficar de boa
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-ficar-boa","word":"ficar de boa","meaning":"to chill; to relax (informal Brazilian use)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Ficar de boa"} -->

- **Fica de boa!** (FEE-kah jee BOH-ah) — Chill out! / Relax!
- **Hoje vou ficar de boa em casa.** (OH-jee voh fee-KAR jee BOH-ah) — Today I'll just chill at home.

### Dar certo / Dar errado
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-dar-certo","word":"dar certo","meaning":"to work out; to go right","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"Dar certo / Dar errado"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-dar-errado","word":"dar errado","meaning":"to go wrong","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Dar certo / Dar errado"} -->

- **Vai dar certo!** (vai dar SEHR-too) — It'll work out!
- **Deu errado.** (deu eh-HAH-doo) — It went wrong.
- **Tomara que dê certo.** (toh-MAH-rah kee deh SEHR-too) — I hope it works out.

### Fazer falta
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-fazer-falta","word":"fazer falta","meaning":"to be missed; to be lacking","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Fazer falta"} -->

- **Você faz muita falta.** (vo-SEH fahz MOO-ee-tah FOW-tah) — I miss you a lot. (lit. "You make a lot of lack")
- **Minha família faz falta.** (MEEN-yah fah-MEE-lee-ah) — I miss my family.

### Ter a ver com
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-ter-ver","word":"ter a ver com","meaning":"to have to do with","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Ter a ver com"} -->

- **Isso não tem nada a ver!** (EE-soo nown tehn NAH-dah ah vehr) — That has nothing to do with it!
- **O que isso tem a ver comigo?** (oo kee EE-soo tehn ah vehr) — What does this have to do with me?

### Deixa pra lá
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-deixa-la","word":"deixa pra lá","meaning":"forget about it; let it go (informal)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"Deixa pra lá"} -->

- **Deixa pra lá.** (DAY-shah prah lah) — Forget about it. / Let it go.
- **Não se preocupe, deixa pra lá.** (nown see preh-OH-koo-pee) — Don't worry, let it go.

## More Useful Expressions

<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-saudade","word":"saudade","meaning":"nostalgic longing; missing someone or something","priority":"essential","sourceSection":"More Useful Expressions"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-tomara","word":"tomara","meaning":"I hope so; let's hope","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"More Useful Expressions"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-imagina","word":"imagina","meaning":"don't mention it; no problem (response to thanks)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"More Useful Expressions"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-pode-crer","word":"pode crer","meaning":"you bet; totally (informal agreement)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"More Useful Expressions"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-sei-la","word":"sei lá","meaning":"I dunno; I don't know (informal)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"More Useful Expressions"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-falou","word":"falou","meaning":"OK then; see you (casual farewell)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"More Useful Expressions"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-valeu","word":"valeu","meaning":"thanks (informal Brazilian use)","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"More Useful Expressions"} -->
<!-- vocab-card: {"id":"vocab-isso-ai","word":"é isso aí","meaning":"that's it; exactly","priority":"optional","sourceSection":"More Useful Expressions"} -->

- **Que saudade!** (kee sow-DAH-jee) — I miss it/you so much! (*saudade* names nostalgic longing or missing someone or something; there is no single English equivalent for every context)
- **Tomara!** (toh-MAH-rah) — I hope so! / Let's hope!
- **Imagina!** (ee-mah-JEE-nah) — Don't mention it! / No problem! (response to "obrigado")
- **Pode crer!** (POH-jee krehr) — You bet! / Totally!
- **Sei lá!** (say lah) — I dunno!
- **Falou!** (fah-LOH) — OK then! / See ya! (casual farewell)
- **Valeu!** (vah-LEH-oo) — Thanks! (informal)
- **É isso aí!** (eh EE-soo ah-EE) — That's it! / Exactly!
