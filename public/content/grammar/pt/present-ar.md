# Present Tense: Regular -ar Verbs

The present tense is the first verb tense you need to learn. In Portuguese, verbs are grouped into three conjugation families based on their infinitive ending: **-ar**, **-er**, and **-ir**. The **-ar** verbs are the largest and most common group. Once you learn this pattern, you can conjugate hundreds of verbs!

## How Conjugation Works

To conjugate a verb, remove the infinitive ending (-ar) to get the **stem**, then add the appropriate ending for each pronoun:

**Falar** (fah-lahr) — to speak → Stem: **fal-**

| Pronoun | Ending | Conjugation | Pronunciation |
|---------|--------|------------|---------------|
| Eu | -o | **falo** | fah-loo |
| Você / Ele / Ela | -a | **fala** | fah-lah |
| Nós | -amos | **falamos** | fah-lah-mooz |
| A gente | -a | **fala** | fah-lah |
| Vocês / Eles / Elas | -am | **falam** | fah-lown |

> **Note:** In Brazilian Portuguese, **a gente** (literally "the people") is used interchangeably with **nós** (we). It conjugates like **você** (third person singular).

## Example Sentences with Falar

- **Eu falo português** (eh-oo fah-loo por-too-gesh) — I speak Portuguese
- **Você fala inglês?** (voh-say fah-lah een-glesh) — Do you speak English?
- **Nós falamos espanhol** (nohz fah-lah-mooz esh-pah-nyow) — We speak Spanish
- **A gente fala todo dia** (ah zhen-chee fah-lah toh-doo jee-ah) — We speak every day
- **Eles falam muito rápido** (eh-leez fah-lown mween-too hah-pee-doo) — They speak very fast

## Common -ar Verbs

Here are the most useful -ar verbs you should know:

### Trabalhar (trah-bah-lyahr) — to work

- **Eu trabalho em casa** (eh-oo trah-bah-lyoo ayn kah-zah) — I work from home
- **Ela trabalha no hospital** (eh-lah trah-bah-lyah noo ohsh-pee-taw) — She works at the hospital

### Estudar (esh-too-dahr) — to study

- **Eu estudo português** (eh-oo esh-too-doo por-too-gesh) — I study Portuguese
- **Vocês estudam na universidade** (voh-sehz esh-too-down nah oo-nee-vehr-see-dah-jee) — You (all) study at the university

### Morar (moh-rahr) — to live (reside)

- **Eu moro no Brasil** (eh-oo moh-roo noo brah-zeew) — I live in Brazil
- **Nós moramos em São Paulo** (nohz moh-rah-mooz ayn sow pow-loo) — We live in São Paulo

### Gostar (gosh-tahr) — to like

**Gostar** always requires **de** (of) after it:

- **Eu gosto de música** (eh-oo gosh-too jee moo-zee-kah) — I like music
- **Você gosta de café?** (voh-say gosh-tah jee kah-feh) — Do you like coffee?
- **Eles gostam de futebol** (eh-leez gosh-town jee foo-chee-bow) — They like soccer

### Comprar (kohn-prahr) — to buy

- **Eu compro frutas no mercado** (eh-oo kohn-proo froo-tahz noo mehr-kah-doo) — I buy fruit at the market
- **Ela compra roupas novas** (eh-lah kohn-prah hoh-pahz noh-vahz) — She buys new clothes

### Viajar (vee-ah-zhahr) — to travel

- **Nós viajamos todo ano** (nohz vee-ah-zhah-mooz toh-doo ah-noo) — We travel every year
- **Vocês viajam de avião?** (voh-sehz vee-ah-zhown jee ah-vee-ow) — Do you travel by plane?

### Cozinhar (koh-zee-nyahr) — to cook

- **Eu cozinho todos os dias** (eh-oo koh-zee-nyoo toh-dooz ooz jee-ahz) — I cook every day
- **Ele cozinha muito bem** (eh-lee koh-zee-nyah mween-too bayn) — He cooks very well

### Ajudar (ah-zhoo-dahr) — to help

- **Você me ajuda?** (voh-say mee ah-zhoo-dah) — Can you help me?
- **Nós ajudamos os vizinhos** (nohz ah-zhoo-dah-mooz ooz vee-zee-nyooz) — We help the neighbors

## Asking Questions

In spoken Brazilian Portuguese, questions are formed simply by **changing your intonation** (raising your voice at the end). The word order stays the same:

- **Você fala inglês.** (statement) — You speak English.
- **Você fala inglês?** (question) — Do you speak English?

- **Ela mora aqui.** (statement) — She lives here.
- **Ela mora aqui?** (question) — Does she live here?

## Negative Sentences

To make a sentence negative, simply add **não** before the verb:

- **Eu não falo japonês** (eh-oo now fah-loo zhah-poh-nesh) — I don't speak Japanese
- **Ela não trabalha hoje** (eh-lah now trah-bah-lyah oh-zhee) — She doesn't work today
- **Nós não moramos aqui** (nohz now moh-rah-mooz ah-kee) — We don't live here

## Practice: Complete Conjugation

Let's conjugate **comprar** (to buy) completely:

| Pronoun | Form | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Eu | compro | Eu compro pão. (I buy bread.) |
| Você | compra | Você compra leite. (You buy milk.) |
| Ele/Ela | compra | Ela compra frutas. (She buys fruit.) |
| Nós | compramos | Nós compramos café. (We buy coffee.) |
| A gente | compra | A gente compra tudo aqui. (We buy everything here.) |
| Vocês | compram | Vocês compram muito. (You all buy a lot.) |
| Eles/Elas | compram | Eles compram roupas. (They buy clothes.) |
