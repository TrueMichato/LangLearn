# Comparatives and Superlatives (Comparativos e Superlativos)

Comparatives and superlatives let you compare things — saying something is bigger, better, more interesting, or the most beautiful. Portuguese has regular patterns for most adjectives and a few important irregular forms that you'll use every day.

## Comparatives of Superiority: Mais...do que

To say something is "more...than," use **mais + adjective + do que**:

- **O Brasil é mais grande do que Portugal** (oo brah-zeel eh mahyz gran-jee doo kee por-too-gow) — Brazil is bigger than Portugal
- **Ela é mais alta do que eu** (eh-lah eh mahyz ow-tah doo kee eh-oo) — She is taller than me
- **Este livro é mais interessante do que aquele** (esh-chee lee-vroo eh mahyz een-teh-reh-san-chee doo kee ah-keh-lee) — This book is more interesting than that one
- **São Paulo é mais populosa do que o Rio** (sow pow-loo eh mahyz poh-poo-loh-zah doo kee oo hee-oo) — São Paulo is more populated than Rio
- **Português é mais difícil do que espanhol** (por-too-gesh eh mahyz jee-fee-see-oo doo kee esh-pan-yol) — Portuguese is more difficult than Spanish

> **Note:** In spoken Brazilian Portuguese, **do que** is often shortened to just **que**: "Ela é mais alta que eu."

You can also compare actions with **mais...do que**:

- **Eu estudo mais do que ele** (eh-oo esh-too-doo mahyz doo kee eh-lee) — I study more than him
- **Ela trabalha mais do que todo mundo** (eh-lah trah-bah-lyah mahyz doo kee toh-doo moon-doo) — She works more than everyone

## Comparatives of Inferiority: Menos...do que

To say something is "less...than," use **menos + adjective + do que**:

- **Este filme é menos interessante do que o outro** (esh-chee feel-mee eh meh-nooz een-teh-reh-san-chee doo kee oo oh-troo) — This movie is less interesting than the other one
- **O inverno aqui é menos frio do que lá** (oo een-ver-noo ah-kee eh meh-nooz free-oo doo kee lah) — The winter here is less cold than there
- **Eu como menos do que você** (eh-oo koh-moo meh-nooz doo kee voh-seh) — I eat less than you
- **Ele é menos paciente do que a irmã** (eh-lee eh meh-nooz pah-see-en-chee doo kee ah eer-mah) — He is less patient than his sister

## Comparatives of Equality: Tão...quanto

To say something is "as...as," use **tão + adjective + quanto**:

- **Ela é tão inteligente quanto ele** (eh-lah eh tow een-teh-lee-zhen-chee kwan-too eh-lee) — She is as intelligent as him
- **Este restaurante é tão bom quanto aquele** (esh-chee hes-tow-ran-chee eh tow bong kwan-too ah-keh-lee) — This restaurant is as good as that one
- **Eu sou tão alto quanto meu pai** (eh-oo soh tow ow-too kwan-too meh-oo pah-ee) — I'm as tall as my father
- **O exame foi tão difícil quanto eu esperava** (oo eh-zah-mee foy tow jee-fee-see-oo kwan-too eh-oo esh-peh-rah-vah) — The exam was as hard as I expected

For quantities, use **tanto/tanta/tantos/tantas...quanto**:

- **Eu tenho tantos livros quanto ela** (eh-oo ten-yoo tan-tooz lee-vrooz kwan-too eh-lah) — I have as many books as her
- **Ele bebe tanta água quanto eu** (eh-lee beh-bee tan-tah ah-gwah kwan-too eh-oo) — He drinks as much water as me

## Irregular Comparatives

Some common adjectives have irregular comparative forms — they don't use **mais**:

| Adjective | Meaning | Comparative | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|-------------|---------|
| bom | good | **melhor** | better |
| mau/ruim | bad | **pior** | worse |
| grande | big | **maior** | bigger |
| pequeno | small | **menor** | smaller |

- **Este café é melhor do que aquele** (esh-chee kah-feh eh meh-lyor doo kee ah-keh-lee) — This coffee is better than that one
- **A situação está pior do que antes** (ah see-too-ah-sow esh-tah pee-or doo kee an-cheez) — The situation is worse than before
- **Minha casa é maior do que a dela** (meen-yah kah-zah eh mah-ee-or doo kee ah deh-lah) — My house is bigger than hers
- **O preço é menor do que eu pensava** (oo preh-soo eh meh-nor doo kee eh-oo pen-sah-vah) — The price is smaller than I thought
- **Ele canta melhor do que eu** (eh-lee kan-tah meh-lyor doo kee eh-oo) — He sings better than me
- **Hoje eu me sinto pior do que ontem** (oh-zhee eh-oo mee seen-too pee-or doo kee on-teng) — Today I feel worse than yesterday

> **Important:** Never say "mais melhor" or "mais pior" — the comparative is already built into the word. It's like saying "more better" in English.

## Superlatives

### Relative Superlative: o/a mais...

To say something is "the most..." or "the ...est," use **o/a mais + adjective**:

- **Ela é a mais inteligente da turma** (eh-lah eh ah mahyz een-teh-lee-zhen-chee dah toor-mah) — She is the most intelligent in the class
- **Este é o restaurante mais caro da cidade** (esh-chee eh oo hes-tow-ran-chee mahyz kah-roo dah see-dah-jee) — This is the most expensive restaurant in the city
- **O Everest é a montanha mais alta do mundo** (oo eh-veh-rest eh ah mon-tahn-yah mahyz ow-tah doo moon-doo) — Everest is the tallest mountain in the world
- **Foi o dia mais feliz da minha vida** (foy oo jee-ah mahyz feh-leez dah meen-yah vee-dah) — It was the happiest day of my life

For "the least...":

- **Este é o filme menos interessante** (esh-chee eh oo feel-mee meh-nooz een-teh-reh-san-chee) — This is the least interesting movie

### Irregular Superlatives

| Adjective | Superlative |
|-----------|-------------|
| bom | o/a melhor |
| mau/ruim | o/a pior |
| grande | o/a maior |
| pequeno | o/a menor |

- **Ela é a melhor aluna** (eh-lah eh ah meh-lyor ah-loo-nah) — She is the best student
- **Este foi o pior dia da minha vida** (esh-chee foy oo pee-or jee-ah dah meen-yah vee-dah) — This was the worst day of my life
- **São Paulo é a maior cidade do Brasil** (sow pow-loo eh ah mah-ee-or see-dah-jee doo brah-zeel) — São Paulo is the biggest city in Brazil

### Absolute Superlative: -íssimo

The suffix **-íssimo/a** adds emphasis, meaning "very" or "extremely." It agrees in gender:

| Adjective | Superlative | Meaning |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| lindo | lindíssimo/a | extremely beautiful |
| fácil | facilíssimo/a | extremely easy |
| difícil | dificílimo/a | extremely difficult |
| bom | boníssimo/a or ótimo/a | extremely good |
| mau | malíssimo/a or péssimo/a | extremely bad |
| rico | riquíssimo/a | extremely rich |
| caro | caríssimo/a | extremely expensive |
| frio | friíssimo/a | extremely cold |

- **A comida estava boníssima** (ah koh-mee-dah esh-tah-vah boh-nee-see-mah) — The food was extremely good
- **O hotel é caríssimo** (oo oh-tew eh kah-ree-see-moo) — The hotel is extremely expensive
- **Ela é lindíssima** (eh-lah eh leen-dee-see-mah) — She is extremely beautiful
- **O exame foi dificílimo** (oo eh-zah-mee foy jee-fee-see-lee-moo) — The exam was extremely difficult
