# English-Portuguese Cognates & False Friends

If you speak English, you already know hundreds of Portuguese words — you just don't realize it yet! **Cognates** are words that share a common origin and look (or sound) similar across languages. Portuguese and English share thousands of cognates through Latin and French. But beware of **false friends** (*falsos amigos*) — words that look familiar but mean something completely different!

## What Are Cognates?

Cognates are words in two languages that have similar spelling, pronunciation, and meaning because they share a common etymological origin. Portuguese and English both borrowed heavily from Latin, so many academic, scientific, and everyday words are recognizable across both languages.

- **Música** (moo-zee-kah) — Music
- **Hospital** (osh-pee-taw) — Hospital
- **Telefone** (teh-leh-foh-nee) — Telephone

## Common Suffix Patterns

One of the best tricks for expanding your Portuguese vocabulary is learning how English suffixes transform into Portuguese ones:

### -tion → -ção

- **Nation** → **nação** (nah-sow) — nation
- **Action** → **ação** (ah-sow) — action
- **Education** → **educação** (eh-doo-kah-sow) — education
- **Information** → **informação** (een-for-mah-sow) — information
- **Situation** → **situação** (see-too-ah-sow) — situation

### -ty → -dade

- **University** → **universidade** (oo-nee-vehr-see-dah-jee) — university
- **City** → **cidade** (see-dah-jee) — city
- **Society** → **sociedade** (soh-see-eh-dah-jee) — society
- **Quality** → **qualidade** (kwah-lee-dah-jee) — quality
- **Reality** → **realidade** (heh-ah-lee-dah-jee) — reality

### -ment → -mento

- **Moment** → **momento** (moh-men-too) — moment
- **Document** → **documento** (doh-koo-men-too) — document
- **Movement** → **movimento** (moh-vee-men-too) — movement
- **Apartment** → **apartamento** (ah-par-tah-men-too) — apartment

### -ous → -oso

- **Famous** → **famoso** (fah-moh-zoo) — famous
- **Nervous** → **nervoso** (nehr-voh-zoo) — nervous
- **Curious** → **curioso** (koo-ree-oh-zoo) — curious
- **Delicious** → **delicioso** (deh-lee-see-oh-zoo) — delicious
- **Generous** → **generoso** (zheh-neh-roh-zoo) — generous

### -al → -al

- **Natural** → **natural** (nah-too-raw) — natural
- **Animal** → **animal** (ah-nee-maw) — animal
- **Normal** → **normal** (nor-maw) — normal
- **Hospital** → **hospital** (osh-pee-taw) — hospital
- **Cultural** → **cultural** (kool-too-raw) — cultural

### -ible/-able → -ível/-ável

- **Possible** → **possível** (poh-see-vew) — possible
- **Comfortable** → **confortável** (kon-for-tah-vew) — comfortable
- **Responsible** → **responsável** (heh-spon-sah-vew) — responsible
- **Incredible** → **incrível** (een-kree-vew) — incredible

## Direct Cognates

Many words are nearly identical in both languages. Here are common ones you can start using immediately:

| English | Portuguese | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| hotel | hotel | oh-tew |
| area | área | ah-reh-ah |
| problem | problema | proh-bleh-mah |
| telephone | telefone | teh-leh-foh-nee |
| radio | rádio | hah-jee-oo |
| music | música | moo-zee-kah |
| hospital | hospital | osh-pee-taw |
| restaurant | restaurante | heh-stow-rahn-chee |
| chocolate | chocolate | shoh-koh-lah-chee |
| computer | computador | kohm-poo-tah-dor |
| internet | internet | een-ter-neh-chee |
| taxi | táxi | tah-ksee |
| pizza | pizza | pee-tsah |
| banana | banana | bah-nah-nah |

- **O hotel fica perto do restaurante.** (oo oh-tew fee-kah pehr-too doo heh-stow-rahn-chee) — The hotel is near the restaurant.
- **Eu tenho um problema com o computador.** (eh-oo teh-nyoo oon proh-bleh-mah kohm oo kohm-poo-tah-dor) — I have a problem with the computer.
- **A música brasileira é famosa.** (ah moo-zee-kah brah-zee-lay-rah eh fah-moh-zah) — Brazilian music is famous.

> **Note:** Even with true cognates, pronunciation differs significantly! Pay attention to the Portuguese sounds.

## False Friends (Falsos Amigos) — Watch Out!

These words look like English words but mean something **completely different**. Mixing them up can lead to embarrassing misunderstandings!

| Portuguese | Looks like | Actually means | English equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **pretender** | pretend | to intend / to plan | fingir = to pretend |
| **puxar** | push | to pull | empurrar = to push |
| **atualmente** | actually | currently / nowadays | na verdade = actually |
| **eventualmente** | eventually | occasionally / perhaps | finalmente = eventually |
| **parentes** | parents | relatives | pais = parents |
| **costume** | costume | habit / custom | fantasia = costume |
| **livraria** | library | bookstore | biblioteca = library |
| **fábrica** | fabric | factory | tecido = fabric |
| **sensível** | sensible | sensitive | sensato = sensible |
| **resumo** | resume | summary | currículo = resume/CV |
| **pasta** | pasta | folder / briefcase | massa/macarrão = pasta |
| **exquisito** | exquisite | strange / weird | requintado = exquisite |

- **Eu pretendo viajar no próximo ano.** (eh-oo preh-ten-doo vee-ah-zhar noo proh-see-moo ah-noo) — I intend to travel next year. (NOT: I pretend to travel)
- **Puxe a porta, não empurre!** (poo-shee ah por-tah, now en-poo-hee) — Pull the door, don't push!
- **Atualmente, eu moro em São Paulo.** (ah-too-aw-men-chee, eh-oo moh-roo eng sow pow-loo) — Currently, I live in São Paulo. (NOT: Actually)
- **Meus parentes moram no sul.** (meh-oosh pah-ren-cheez moh-rahm noo soo) — My relatives live in the south. (NOT: My parents)
- **Vou à livraria comprar um livro.** (voh ah lee-vrah-ree-ah kohm-prar oon lee-vroo) — I'm going to the bookstore to buy a book. (NOT: library)

## Tips for Guessing Portuguese Words

1. **Try the suffix conversion:** English "-tion" → Portuguese "-ção" works about 90% of the time.
2. **Watch for gender:** Many cognates that end in *-a* are feminine (música, área), and those ending in *-o* or a consonant are often masculine (momento, hospital).
3. **Remember the pronunciation shifts:** English "th" → Portuguese "t"; English "ph" → Portuguese "f"; silent letters change.
4. **When in doubt about a false friend, check!** If a Portuguese word looks too much like an English word, it might mean something different.
5. **Academic and scientific vocabulary** is your best bet for cognates — everyday casual words are more likely to diverge.

- **A tecnologia é essencial na educação moderna.** (ah tek-noh-loh-zhee-ah eh eh-sen-see-aw nah eh-doo-kah-sow moh-dehr-nah) — Technology is essential in modern education.
- **O professor é responsável pela informação.** (oo proh-feh-sohr eh heh-spon-sah-vew peh-lah een-for-mah-sow) — The professor is responsible for the information.
